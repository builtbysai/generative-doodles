# No.338 Double Inset

## Concept

After MyClayCo's Double Ellipse Inset Hoopla and Scalloped Fridge Frame cutters: nested-outline product language as clean boolean linework. Three nested ellipses on near-black, the middle ring scalloped in gold, and a margin ring of eight small inset studies, each one the same three-ring system frozen at its own inset preset, like the size variants pinned around a product photo. The only motion is slow: the scallop phase turns on a 30 second cycle, and the middle ring itself can be slid sideways between its neighbours and let go, easing home.

## Construction

One canvas, everything as parametric data, nothing pre-rendered and no offscreen layer. One base ellipse (semi-axes 445 by 308 design px, centred) generates every ring as its parallel curve: a point at parameter theta is offset along the ellipse's inward unit normal by the ring's distance. Exactly 3 nested ellipses, asserted in code: outer at distance 0 in cream, inner at distance 100 in cream, and the middle at the designed inset of 50 in gold. The middle ring's scallop is a radius modulation of that distance, sin(K*theta + phase) with K fixed at 24 in code and a depth of 9 design px; the phase advances through exactly one full turn per 30 second cycle, so the loop is seamless and frame 30s matches frame 0. The 8 margin studies are asserted in code as well: same construction at 56 by 40 semi-axes with an inner distance of 22, scallop depth 2, and fixed middle presets of 7, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15 and 17 design px, placed on an elliptical margin ring; none of them is interactive.

Drag is horizontal only. Pointer coordinates are converted to design coords through the stored centred-fit transform (S, OX, OY); the drag maps the pointer x delta onto the middle inset distance d, clamped to 18..82, which with the 9 px scallop swing keeps the middle ring's nearest approach at about 9 px from either neighbour's centreline, so the rings never cross. On release the inset eases home with an exponential approach (rate 3.0 per second, snapping the final 0.05 px), which is monotone and cannot overshoot past a neighbour. Palette is exactly three colours: ground near-black #101314, line cream #F2EBD8, scallop accent gold #D9A441; strokes only, no fill, no gradients, no shadowBlur, no filters, no text or UI. House rules throughout: Visual Viewport sizing with resize on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned every frame via style width and height in px, phone detection via window.screen min dimension under 700px with aspect fallback (h > w*1.15), design space 1280x900 with centred fit and the full canvas filled in the piece's own near-black so the page ground matches, synchronous first frame with the clock offset 7.5s into the cycle so frame one is developed at the designed inset, typeof-guarded rAF, fixed values only, no runtime random.
