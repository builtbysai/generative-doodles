# No.176 Paper Tree

## Concept
Fathauer's fractal trees: one small photographic building block, a cluster of curved branch strokes, iterated past what nature would do. Here a single ink block of seven strokes is stamped at its own three tips, generation after generation, one copy rotated differently, one reflected, one lightened for depth, until the envelope reads unmistakably as a tree: black ink on warm paper, no leaves, no green, no sky. Double tap grows a different tree.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The block is pure path data: seven quadratic branch strokes in unit space (base at the origin, growth upward) with three designated tips, each tip carrying its outgoing direction. Iteration composes affine transforms: at each tip, three child stamps at 0.55 scale (seeded jitter 0.94 to 1.06), aligned to the tip direction, with roles rotating by generation: one rotated by an extra seeded angle, one reflected across its growth axis, one drawn in the lightened ink #3A3A3A at lower alpha, Fathauer's depth trick. Eight generations give 3280 stamps and about 23000 stroke entries, flattened once per seed into screen independent entries (transformed points, accumulated stroke width, colour, generation), then replayed each frame under the fit transform. The prompt's offscreen canvas stamp is deliberately not used: the build harness has no document.createElement, so the block is replayed as path data under transforms instead, which is the same discipline with a different storage medium. Pencil marks, two short jittered #6B6B6B strokes per stamp on generations 2 to 5, are seeded where stamps pile up. The 30 second loop grows one generation every 2 seconds (per entry stagger inside each generation), holds the full tree 8 seconds with a faint paper grain shimmer (700 precomputed grain specks breathing at low alpha), then fades over 6 seconds and regrows. Double tap reseeds (new angles, reflection choices, grain) and restarts growth; otherwise there is no hint and no visible interaction. First frame is offset to 20 seconds, the full tree in its hold. Palette exactly per spec: paper #F2EDE1, ink #141414, lightened #3A3A3A, pencil #6B6B6B. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, paper full bleed; on portrait phones the tree scales up and the canopy bleeds off both sides rather than shrinking into a corner.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. A precedence bug in the loop time expression was caught before first render. The desktop frame shows the full fractal envelope: bold trunk strokes stepping down through visibly self similar branching to a fine twig fuzz, with lightened depth copies reading as grey sub trees inside the canopy. The first phone render shrank the wide tree into the bottom quarter of the tall screen; the portrait fit now scales by frame height with the canopy bleeding off the sides, trunk grounded at the bottom. One honest trait: the lowest generations carry a few long bare twig strokes from the block's minor strokes, which read as winter branches rather than artifacts at full size.
