# Research and playback findings — connected Marble Atlas

## Primary references

- [Exploratorium: Chain Reaction](https://www.exploratorium.edu/tinkering/projects/chain-reaction): each construction must set off its neighbor. Applied by positioning a receiver from the preceding exit and requiring a contact-operated handoff, rather than placing disconnected acts into fixed slots.
- [Rube Goldberg Institute activity cards](https://s3.amazonaws.com/www.rubegoldberg.org/documents/Rube_Goldberg_Activity_Cards_R2.pdf): simple machines, stored energy, triggers, storytelling, and repeated testing. Applied through a spring-pinned loading plank, linked gate, visible pressure key and mechanism-level seed regressions.
- [PBS Design Squad: Chain Reaction Machine](https://www.pbs.org/video/design-squad-nation-chain-reaction-machine/): its documented construction combines ramps, strings, levers and sequential triggers, with iteration on failed connections. Applied to explicit docking, rolling, unlocking and release states. The article/transcript was consulted; its footage and soundtrack were not used.

These are engineering and composition references. No footage, music or copied
illustrations are shipped. The private Stephen Wu MP4 remains unavailable in the
workspace; the checked-in creative brief preserves its scene inventory.

## Findings from browser playback

Inspected timed screenshots and simulation events during real Chromium playback,
including the opening sequence and garden, rowing/submarine and carnival sequences.
The later-world views advanced the real simulation to a particular capture, then
resumed ordinary browser playback; they were not replacements for the production
physics route.

The repeated intake/catch V shapes dominated the old film. Every prop returned the
marble to the same central downward lane. Fixed chapter openings and finales also
reduced surprise across seeds. The new generator varies devices and geometry,
spends more time moving horizontally or upward, and joins two devices with one
working transfer. A lower rolling route powers the exit through a real load.

Two concrete discontinuities were in the code: a carrier adopted the impact point
as its new origin at capture, and a stage advanced with the previous stage's final
progress still set. Capture now preserves both positions and settles the marble;
new stages draw their beginning. Coaster tangents match across the loop joins so
its carriage can follow the rail continuously. The lens no longer changes when a
carrier captures/releases.

The first gate prototype obstructed the approach when it opened beside the throat.
It now hinges outside the approach and swings down. A seed-33 pause exposed a
return plank lifting too far into its inlet; asymmetric mechanical stops limit
that receiving edge. The second-cycle regression additionally separates the
feed rail from the full swept deck by more than one marble diameter; a short
visible jump closes the handoff. These are geometry fixes, not hidden ball nudges
or recovery.

## What remains an approximation

Transporters, bridge extension and exit-gate actuation use explicit constrained
motion after their specified triggers. Ropes and link lines explain the coupling;
they are not a tension/cable solver. The boat's oars are coupled to its authored
propulsion; buoyancy and fluid dynamics are not simulated. The balance plank,
rolling marble, collisions, springs and hinge stops use the rigid-body solver.

The generator currently has one connected assembly grammar with many device and
geometry combinations. Further topology variation is the next compositional step;
this is not an unlimited catalog of unique inventions.
