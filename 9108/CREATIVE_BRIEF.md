## Creative direction

A marble is the protagonist, never lost in busy decoration. Its movement triggers the next cause-and-effect reaction. Render thoughtfully designed, immediately legible, 2D mechanical constructions. A continuous piano arrangement shifts harmony through four distinct worlds. New forms, colors, materials and devices should grow from the machine's environment. The journey runs forever: after the fourth world, return to the first with fresh structure and musical variation. Aim for four chapters of roughly the proportions below, **not** scripted copies of fixed five-minute choreography.

Physical credibility matters. Motors, elevators, teleporters, flying animals and other events can become special authored machines with clearly defined animation states/sensors if necessary, but never present arbitrary timed movement as a true physics reaction. Preserve a clear camera, readable scale, working route, continuous ball identity and rescue paths. Avoid generic game HUDs, cards, pills, text over the machine and generic glow effects.

## Improvement standard

Research real kinetic sculptures, domino dynamics, pinball geometry, pulleys, cams, tension mechanisms, and music synchronization. First ensure that each individual prop mechanically works and the marble can pass; then compose transitions. Environments must look designed, sparse and purposeful. Every unusual lift/transporter should have clear authored state transitions and graceful fallbacks. Avoid pure random clutter, pre-fallen dominoes, particle spam and generic AI UI.

Make the animation replayable and unbounded. Seed controls reproducibility; multiple runs should visibly differ. The melody must be an original composition that follows the world mood, with audible collision accents and a safe mute/unlock interaction. Keep the art responsive and stable on mobile. Test the machine's progression across all worlds, memory boundedness, and real browser audio before asserting release readiness.
