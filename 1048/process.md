# No.1048 "Five White Cubes"

Study #129 katsuhiko-harada, PP209 "The Omni-Wheel Origin" — the border
cubes' holonomic grammar as a pure piece.

## Concept

Five identical white cubes stand on a dark floor. Nothing distinguishes
them but their motion, and the motion is the whole piece: it is the
vocabulary of the omni wheel, which no car can speak. A cube strafes
sideways while slowly rotating. Another translates in a straight line
without turning at all. A third stops dead, rotates in place (the omni
signature), then sets off sideways. Position and orientation are
independent schedules, so a cube's heading almost never agrees with its
direction of travel, and that disagreement is the drawing. Each cube
leaves a cool trail exactly where it has been; the trails cross and
accumulate into the composition. At every waypoint correction (a stop, a
rotation, a set-off) a small warm tick flashes at the cube's corner: the
feedback, biting. Forward is optional.

## Construction

- **The holonomic leg model.** Each cube's route is a seeded list of
  legs covering exactly 60 s, generated by mulberry32 from the piece
  seed. Two leg types:
  - *move*: position lerps from waypoint to waypoint at constant
    velocity (leg durations come from distance / 52-84 units per
    second); orientation drifts linearly by a leg delta (about 45% of
    move legs carry a drift of up to plus/minus 0.7 rad). The drift is
    defined on the orientation schedule only, never derived from the
    travel direction: strafing is structural, not animated.
  - *spin*: position fixed; orientation turns by a multiple of 90
    degrees (1-3 quarter turns, either sign) over 1.1-2.2 s with a
    smoothstep ease, so the motors ramp, hold, and settle.
  After the last waypoint the route returns to its start waypoint; all
  durations are then scaled so the legs total exactly 60.000 s, and a
  closure correction (the difference between total rotation and the
  nearest whole number of turns) is distributed across the legs in
  proportion to duration, so orientation also closes exactly at the
  loop. State at any time is a closed-form lookup: find the leg,
  interpolate. No integration anywhere.
- **Trails.** The record is recomputed analytically each frame: 39
  samples of the same closed-form position function, from now back over
  a 7 s window, stroked as segments with alpha 0.35 * (1 - age/7)^1.3
  and width tapering 2.5 to 0.7. Length and brightness therefore follow
  the path exactly; while a cube spins in place its trail collapses to
  a point, which is the truth of its record. No accumulation, no canvas
  fade.
- **Ticks.** Leg boundaries are the correction points. Boundary age is
  computed cyclically from the leg start times; a tick inside 1.1 s of
  a boundary draws a warm diamond at the cube's front corner (rotated
  with the cube), alpha 1 - age/1.1.
- **The cubes.** Identical 48-unit matte white squares with a dark
  wheel-shadow plate offset beneath, four dark corner wheel housings
  and one front edge housing mark, all rotating with the body. The
  housings are the only asymmetry, and they exist so the heading is
  legible against the trail: without them a rotating square reads as a
  still one.
- **Frame one.** Drawn synchronously at the fixed phase T0 = 18 s. The
  base seed (2718) was chosen with a numeric probe over the pure model:
  at t = 18 s two cubes are mid-spin (center and upper left), one cube
  travels with its heading about 76 degrees off its travel direction,
  and three correction ticks are lit (ages 0.27, 0.24, 0.77 s). Minimum
  pairwise cube distance at frame one is 195 world units.
- **Interaction (recorded events only).** Drag = a current: pointer
  motion stamps events (throttled to 0.09 s) carrying a per-cube push
  vector, computed at stamp time for cubes within 330 units, in the
  drag direction, weighted by (1 - d/330)^1.5. A cube's drawn position
  is its route position plus the sum of live event pushes under an
  attack/decay envelope (0.25 s attack, exp decay tau 1.3 s, dead after
  6 s): paths bend while you drag and relax after; trails, drawn from
  the same position function, bend with them. Tap a cube = hold: the
  cube freezes for 3 s and its schedule shifts by the held time
  (schedule time = piece time minus the sum of clamped hold overlaps,
  so resume is exact, not a jump). Double-tap = new routes from the
  next seed.
- **Sound (first gesture only, lazy, typeof-guarded).** A 44 Hz floor
  hum; a triangle servo whine pitched by the fastest cube's measured
  speed (90 + 1.7 * v Hz, gain only while something moves); short
  1900 Hz clicks every 0.14 s while any cube is in a spin leg, the
  omni turning made audible. Silent and complete without audio.
- **House rules.** Single self-contained file, canvas 2D, Visual
  Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window,
  canvas px-pinned in draw(), phone detection via window.screen
  (min < 700) with aspect fallback, rAF typeof-guarded, no gradients /
  shadowBlur / ctx.filter / offscreen canvas / createElement, no
  microphone, no visible text besides the title, no Date.now-derived
  state (grep-verified clean).

## QA log

- Rendered with render-piece.js at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844
  (qa-mobile.png), 720x720 (thumb.png). All three render cleanly in
  silence (no AudioContext, no rAF in the QA renderer).
- **Determinism:** thumb.png rendered twice and byte-compared with cmp:
  identical. Duplicate deleted. Frame one is the synchronous draw at
  T0 = 18 s; all pre-input state is a closed-form function of piece
  time and the base seed.
- **Desktop read-back:** dark floor, five identical white cubes spread
  across the frame, dark wheel housings visible on each. The center
  cube is mid-spin, nearly axis-aligned over its own vertical trail;
  the left cube sits at a full 45-degree diamond across its trail; the
  lower center cube's heading is visibly rotated against its diagonal
  trail. Cool trails cross between cubes in long straight runs. Warm
  tick diamonds show at the corners of the upper-left and center
  cubes, as the probe predicted.
- **Thumb read-back:** same composition; housings, ticks, and the
  crossing trails all legible at 720px.
- **Mobile read-back:** the square world fits the 390 width and centers
  vertically on the floor tone; all five cubes and their trails are
  whole, nothing clipped.
- **Deviations:**
  1. The seed is 2718 rather than a piece-number-derived value: the
     first candidate seed put two cubes 39 units apart at frame one
     (nearly touching). Seed choice was made by probing the shipped
     model code, not by altering the model, and the frame-one
     conditions it buys (two mid-spin cubes, three lit ticks, 195-unit
     minimum separation) are recorded above.
  2. Spin legs ease with a smoothstep rather than turning at constant
     angular velocity: real omni drives ramp, and the brief's ban is
     on car-like turning arcs in translation, which the move legs
     honor strictly (constant velocity, heading independent).
  3. Each cube carries one front edge housing mark in addition to the
     four corner housings, identical on all five cubes. A perfectly
     four-fold-symmetric square cannot show slow rotation in a still
     or at a glance; the single extra mark is what makes the heading,
     and therefore the strafe, readable. Recorded here rather than
     hidden.
