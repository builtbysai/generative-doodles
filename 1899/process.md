Driver: a familiar coloratura run, drawn as a clean staircase of ascending notes. It plays once at its proper height, then tries the same staircase a fourth higher, stretching, wobbling and compressing itself against the top edge of the frame, and somehow lands every step, each landing flashing a small clown sticker. The comedy is in the physics, not in anyone laughing.

Mechanism: an 8 note run climbs a staircase from lower left to upper right on a 24 second loop. Seconds 0 to 8 play the run at its proper height, one note per second, each landing flashing a small yellow ring. Seconds 8 to 10 are a breath while the staircase stretches. Seconds 10 to 20 run the higher attempt, one step per 10/7 seconds, with the lift growing toward the top steps, the top steps squashing against a thin top edge line, and a slow wobble (about 0.9 Hz) that grows with height. Each attempt landing fires a yellow starburst and a vector clown sticker that pops in and fades over 1.4 seconds. A faint ghost of the proper height staircase stays visible under the stretched one, so the fourth higher is readable as a transposition. The travelling note head leaves a pale lavender trail. Seconds 20 to 24 settle the staircase back to its proper height and the loop restarts. Each loop varies by a seeded generator (mulberry32, seed 1899000 plus loop index): entry point shift, base height shift, lift amount from 0.27 to 0.34, wobble phase and amplitude, and per step sticker offset and rotation, so higher entries, different entry points and moving sticker placement all vary loop to loop.

Interaction: watch only. The run attempts on its own schedule. No controls, no pitch shifter knob, no game, no score. Never click to reload.
