# No.171 Ripple Emergence

## Concept
Robert Fathauer's Fractal Ripples recipe, translated literally: concentric ripple rings on black, copied at one third scale around themselves generation after generation, until a Koch snowflake stands visible across the field, though no line of it was ever drawn. Watch only.

## Construction
Ripple centres are computed from the Koch construction itself. For each side of the snowflake, the seed point is the side's midpoint; applying all words of the four Koch IFS maps (each a one third scaling) gives the midpoints of every sub-segment at generations 1 to 5: 4, 16, 64, 256, 1024 centres per side, deduplicated at the corners, plus one master ripple at the centroid. A generation g ripple has outer radius 0.52 x (1/3)^g of the side length, exact one third scaling between generations, with ring counts per ripple falling from 16 at the centre to 2 at generation 5. Rings are stroked in #DCE9F5 with alpha falling outward, composited with 'lighter' on the #0A0A0C ground, so overlapping rings brighten into interference bands and the snowflake curve, where overlaps concentrate, emerges on its own. The curve is never stroked as a polyline anywhere in the code. The 40 second loop follows the spec's choreography: the central ripple expands over 8 seconds, generations 1 to 5 arrive one per 4 seconds, an 8 second hold with the curve fully legible, then a soft 4 second fade resets the loop. Frame one is offset to 30 seconds, mid hold. On narrow screens the snowflake's height target (85 percent of viewport height) yields to its width so the whole figure always fits.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. The first pass, with a one third smaller reach per ripple and fewer rings, read as separate ripple flowers with dark gaps; raising the reach to 0.52 and densifying the rings fused the field, and the snowflake now blazes continuously out of the overlaps at every size, with the calm central bullseye binding the middle. Roughly 13,000 ring arcs in the hold frame render in about a second in the node harness and are mostly small paths in a browser. Renderer reports resize listeners only, confirming watch-only.
