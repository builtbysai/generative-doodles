# No.326 Swirling Vines

After Debbie Sharman's Swirling Vines, this piece trails six jungle vines from the top edge down across a cream ground until they fill the whole frame. Each vine is a chain of eighteen segments, and each segment takes its bend from an authored swirl table read at that stem's own shift and direction, so no two vines follow the same path. The palette holds to five colours only: a vine green for the stems, a leaf teal for the leaves, a daisy cream and a gold for the daisies, and the warm cream of the ground itself. Leaves sit at authored nodes along every stem, six to a stem, and exactly three daisies sit at authored nodes on each stem, for eighteen daisies in all. Both counts are asserted in the code.

The idle motion is a forty second seamless loop. The stems are fully grown in the first frame, and the loop animates only the curl at the vine tips and a gentle tremble in the leaves. The curl grows stronger toward each tip, so the ends slowly curl and unfurl while the body of each vine stays where it grew, and the tremble runs six small cycles inside the loop. Because every moving part is a whole number of cycles in forty seconds, the frame at forty seconds matches the frame at zero.

Dragging through the vines is a wind. Each segment carries its own spring, and a segment near the pointer bends away from it, further the closer the pointer gets, then springs back once the pointer has passed. The daisies tremble harder while the wind is on their stem. No stem is ever uprooted, nothing reloads and nothing is reseeded. All marks are drawn directly on one canvas each frame from the authored stem tables.
