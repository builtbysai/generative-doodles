# No.252 Day Five

## Concept
After Day 5 (Nov 2020) by tanzinchospel.art, Stanzin Chospel, the first entry of his public Blender learning diary: a lone figure standing on a rocky ridge, a huge dome looming behind, teal water glow everywhere, a hard red light raking the rocks from the left. The piece is presented as his log presents the study, captioned in the corner like his own: "Day 5". The atmosphere breathes on a twenty second loop, light shafts drifting, the water glow pulsing. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, a flat 2.5D parallax scene in a 1280 by 800 design space, cover-fitted so the composition holds on any screen with the teal ground filling the rest. The dome is a great grey-blue circle in the upper right crossed by flat facet bands, drifting a few pixels against the nearer layers. The ridge is a seeded crag line, its left third rearing higher; a paler far ridge slides behind it at a slower rate. The red rake is drawn facet by facet along the left third of the crest, up-slopes brightest, with short facet ticks dropping from the vertices and a low red wash over the left rock mass, all breathing with the scene. Three translucent shafts slant down through the water and drift; a soft glow ellipse pulses low in the frame on the same twenty second breath. The figure is a small near-black silhouette standing on the ridge line itself, so it sits honestly on the rock at any size. The caption is positioned from the visible slice of the cover-fit, so cropping never eats it. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, synchronous first frame from an offset clock (six seconds in, mid breath), rAF typeof guarded, no interaction handlers at all.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Two fixes. First pass: the cover-fit cropped the caption off the left edge of the desktop frame and the red rake was only a few faint strokes; the caption now anchors to the visible slice and every left-third facet catches the red, up-slopes at full strength, so the rake reads on desktop, thumb and phone. Frame one reads as the study should: teal water, the banded dome looming, the little figure on the ridge, the red light raking in from the left, and his caption in the corner.
