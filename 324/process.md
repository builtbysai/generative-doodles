# No.324 Thirty Days, One Palette

After Debbie Sharman's thirty day fixed palette collab, this piece lays out thirty tiles in a grid six across and five down, filling the whole frame with only thin snow coloured gaps between them. Each tile holds one small flat motif from her Natural Christmas prompt list, in tile order from day one to day thirty: foxes, holly, a wreath, birds, pine cones, dried fruits, snow, berries, owls, twigs, deer, cosy, bears, a tree, a sleigh, gift wrap, starlight, christmas cards, mushrooms, garland, a snowman, presents, a squirrel, a poinsettia, ribbons, ornaments, fire and yarn. The prompt list named twenty eight prompts, so days twenty nine and thirty revisit foxes and holly as second showings, which keeps the grid at exactly thirty tiles. Every tile is drawn in the same five colours and nothing else: forest, berry, twig brown, snow and ochre. The small parametric generators keep each motif doodle simple and flat, a fox from triangle ears and a curved tail, holly from spiked leaves and berries, a wreath from a circle of leaves, and so on through the list. The only lettering in the piece is a small day number on each tile.

The watch only life of the piece is a schedule. Exactly one tile redraws per two second beat, in a fixed seeded order that covers all thirty tiles in sixty seconds, and each redraw advances that tile to the next of its three authored variants, so a fox shifts its tail and markings, a garland gains leaves, an ornament drops a little lower, and then the cycle repeats. No tile is ever chosen at random in the moment. The first frame opens with all thirty tiles already developed at mixed variants, and one tile is caught mid redraw near its beat.

Tapping a tile enlarges it for exactly one point six seconds. The motif shows large at the centre of the enlarged tile, its prompt name sits under it in the same flat lettering as the day numbers, and its day number stays in the corner. Then the tile settles back into the grid. The two second redraw schedule keeps running underneath and does not restart when a tile is tapped. All thirty tiles, their thirty prompts and the thirty step schedule are counted and asserted in the code, and all marks are drawn directly on one canvas each frame from authored data.
