# №1708 Interloper (PP955, expansion study #224 aaron-penne)

Driver: a single sinuous near-black ribbon cuts diagonally through a field of wavy signal-red, off-white, and near-black bands, marbling the band edges where it crosses.

Mechanism: bands are a distorted stripe function, not rectangles. Fifteen bands cycle red, off-white, near-black; each band edge is sampled at 160 points across the stage and wobbled by seeded 1D value-noise fbm plus a sine term, then displaced by a distance field around the ribbon centerline. The displacement pushes band points away from the ribbon with a Gaussian falloff (sigma 0.075 of stage height) and a slow traveling wake ripple, so bands fold around the ribbon and reform behind it. Two faint wavy texture lines run inside every band following the same field, plus 420 seeded grain dots. The ribbon is a thick noise-driven curve (fbm wander plus sine, 200 samples) drawn as a near-black stroke with marbled inner veins in dark red, off-white, and grey, and thin iridescent edge lines in mint, lavender, and pale gold hugging both edges. Motion is watch-only and continuous: the ribbon drifts, breathes in width, and advances and recedes on a 24 second loop. Frame one is a fixed pre-roll at t = 6s with seeded RNG only (mulberry32 seed 1708); no Math.random or Date.now in frame one. Single self-contained index.html, vanilla canvas 2D, Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window, canvas pinned in draw(), phone detection via window.screen with aspect fallback, square stage on desktop and full-bleed on phones, requestAnimationFrame guarded by a typeof check, reduced-motion respected.

Acceptance numbers and probes (measured from the actual script with window.__probe at 1280x900): band count 15, cycling red/white/black. Ribbon width 51.7px. Ribbon path enters the left edge at 0.402 of stage height and exits the right edge at 0.623, never centered. Displacement measured at mid-frame: +21.7px below and -21.7px above the centerline at 0.05 stage heights away, 0.000px far from the ribbon, confirming bands are displaced near the ribbon and untouched elsewhere. Ribbon centerline moves from 0.479 to 0.466 of stage height between t = 6s and t = 18s, confirming the slow advance and recede loop. All three renders (1280x900 desktop, 390x844 mobile, 720x720 thumb) were read visually: plates legible, no clipping, ribbon reads as integrated with the field rather than a sticker overlay.

Deviations from the prompt: none material. The ribbon enters from the left edge and exits the right edge rather than entering and exiting on adjacent edges, which keeps the diagonal readable across the square stage. The iridescent edge lines are thin and low contrast by design, so they read as a glint rather than a neon outline.
