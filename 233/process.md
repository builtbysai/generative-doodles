# No.233 Radio Free Santa Fe

## Concept
After Kluber's 2019 title work: rhythmic horizontal stripes of lime green and cyan running full bleed across a 2:1 panel, warm yellow green tones softening the top and bottom edges, and six rectangular blocks of colour, orange and violet, interrupting the flow, three at the left and three at the right. The blocks are signal bars breathing at different rates: each one inhales and exhales in opacity and scale on its own prime period clock, twenty three, thirty one, thirty seven, forty three, forty seven and fifty nine seconds, so the six never synchronise and never repeat their combined pattern in a sitting. They never blink or flash. There is no radio imagery of any kind; the title is the only nod. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The stripes are seeded layout data (mulberry32) drawn flat each frame, alternating lime and cyan in thin seeded heights. The warm edges are wavy filled bands, a double sine wobble giving them their organic edge. The blocks sit in staggered seeded slots, each exactly one sixteenth of the panel wide, their colours alternating orange and violet; per frame each block's breath is a sine on its own period, shaped with a 1.3 power so the low end of the breath lingers, driving alpha between 0.30 and 1.0 and scale between 0.94 and 1.0. No additive compositing: these are painted blocks breathing in opacity, per the work. The opening clock is offset and two block phases are nudged deterministically so that frame one already shows the system mid breath, one block at full opacity and one faded low. The panel is 2:1, centred, the page ground white. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Two fixes after the first render round: the seeded stripes were too chunky (seventeen to forty four pixels each), so the height range was halved and the field now carries roughly twice as many stripes with a proper rhythm; and two of the left blocks landed on top of each other in the lower corner, so block positions moved to staggered slots with small seeded jitter and an overlap is no longer possible. The final renders show six separated blocks at clearly differing breath phases, the solid orange block reading at full strength against the faded ones, warm wavy edges top and bottom, and on the phone frame the panel holds its shape and the blocks stay legible at 390 wide.
