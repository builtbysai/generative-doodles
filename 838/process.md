# No.838 The Spinning Collage

EXPANSION #102 john-cale, PP7 "Inverted Illusion". Shipped title is the prompt's Title suggestion.

## Concept

The POPtical Illusion deluxe LP as a generative method: Bjorn Copeland's cut-paper collage turned into a spinning collaged-paper disc. Forty torn paper fragments (eye-irises, lens rings, architectural window grids, fabric stripes, plain scraps) are layered at rotations on a cream disc over a sky ground. One fragment, a cream label in the lower-left quadrant, carries the word ILLUSION printed upside-down, the single inversion the rule allows. The disc spins slowly; a tap shears it into displaced vertical bands for three seconds, the inner-sleeve moment; a circular drag spins it by hand; a double-tap reseeds the collage. Flat primitives only, no photorealism, no sound.

## Construction

- Palette (strict): sky #7FB6C9, collage pink #E84A8C, vinyl turquoise #3FD0C0, paper cream #F2EAD8, displacement red #C9563E, ink #141414. Page ground is the sky tone; the disc keeps its shape at every size.
- Fragment generator: seeded mulberry32 (base seed 838102). All fragments are data in disc coordinates (unit circle, y down): position, rotation, scale (0.13 to 0.30 of the disc radius), a torn polygon, a motif, and palette colors. A torn polygon is 5 to 7 corners at jittered radii (0.78 to 1.12) with a torn midpoint pulled inward (0.60 to 0.82) between each pair of corners, so every silhouette reads as ripped paper rather than a clean vector shape; about a third of fragments are elongated. Motifs are drawn flat and clipped to the polygon: stripes (alternating bands), grid (cream window cells on a dark or saturated base), iris (ink ring, colored iris, ink pupil, cream glint), lens (alternating concentric rings), plain (flat fill with a smaller accent copy of the same torn shape at 45 percent alpha). Each fragment gets a faint ink edge stroke (alpha 0.16) to separate the layers.
- The word fragment is generated first and separately: a torn rectangle (half extents 1.18 by 0.46, jittered) in cream, placed with cx in [-0.50, -0.30] and cy in [0.28, 0.46] so it always sits in the disc's lower-left quadrant. Its rotation carries an extra half-turn, so the ink word ILLUSION (the only text in the piece besides the title) reads upside-down at frame one. Exactly one inverted word exists in any seed.
- Spin model: the disc angle is a closed-form function of recorded segments, never frame-integrated. A 'decay' segment gives theta(t) = th0 + OMEGA0*dt + (v0 - OMEGA0)*TAU*(1 - exp(-dt/TAU)) with resting rate OMEGA0 = 2pi/45 rad/s (one turn in 45 s) and TAU = 1.8 s. A circular drag records a 'drag' segment of timestamped pointer angles (unwrapped around the disc center); theta during the drag is piecewise-linear over those points. On release, the velocity from the last 0.15 s of points (clamped to plus or minus 7 rad/s) seeds a new decay segment, so a thrown disc eases back to the resting spin.
- Shear model: a tap records a time; shearAt(t) evaluates the latest tap as a 3 s envelope (smoothstep in over 0.25 s, hold to 2.2 s, smoothstep out by 3 s). While the envelope is positive, the disc is drawn in six horizontal bands: each band is clipped to its strip and to the disc circle, then translated horizontally by envelope * R * 0.10 * BAND_VAR[i] * (alternating sign), with BAND_VAR = [1.0, 0.8, 1.15, 0.9, 1.05, 0.75]. Displacement-red lines are stroked along the five band boundaries at alpha 0.85 * envelope. No offscreen canvas is used; the banding is done with clip paths per draw, per house renderer limits.
- Double-tap reseeds: fragments regenerate from base seed plus reseed count times 7919; the word fragment is rebuilt by the same generator, so the lower-left rule and the single inversion hold for every seed.
- Determinism: frame one is the synchronous draw(0); the initial segment is a decay segment with th0 = 0 and v0 = OMEGA0, so the disc sits at angle 0 and all fragment data comes from the one seeded stream. No Date.now anywhere; the clock is elapsed performance.now. Canvas size comes from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; the canvas is pinned in px inside draw(); phone detection uses window.screen (min dimension under 700) with the h > w*1.15 aspect as fallback only. No gradients, no shadowBlur (the contact shadow is a flat ink circle at alpha 0.12), no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas, no audio, no microphone. rAF is typeof-guarded.
