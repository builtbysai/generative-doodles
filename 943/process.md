# No.943 — Measured Like a Body

Study #115 giorgialupi, PP104 "The cartamodelli". A cutting table where a subject — never a garment — is measured the way a seamstress measures a body: dotted cutting lines, grain arrows, pattern marks, gold pins, and hand-lettered annotations in invented units.

## Concept

Four subjects cycle on the table, each decomposed into four pattern pieces named for the subject's own parts: a relationship ("the first year", "the argument", "the repair", "the long summer"), an AI ("the training", "the context window", "the refusal", "the hallucination"), a painting ("the first sketch", "the underpainting", "the glaze", "the varnish day"), and a week ("monday", "the middle", "friday feeling", "the weekend"). Each piece carries one measurement annotation, written in the units the thing actually comes in: "patience 14", "trust, in cm: all of it", "attention span —", "temperature 0.7", "sleep, total: not enough", "hope —". The measuring is the piece: over each 72 s loop the pattern measures itself (cutting lines dot themselves around the pieces, grain arrows draw, notches and drill holes appear, annotations write in letter by letter, pins drop last), holds complete, then lifts and re-lays for the next subject.

Interactions: tap cycles to the next subject and re-measures; drag grabs the nearest gold pin and re-grades the pattern around it (nearby pieces resize with the pin as anchor, and the grade holds for that subject); double-tap cuts the pieces out (they slide apart along an axis from the pattern centroid and settle back over 2 s). Sound, only after a first gesture: tape-measure ticks while annotations write, a pin prick as each pin lands, two scissor snips on the cut.

## Construction

- Single self-contained canvas-2D page; strict palette: paper #EFE7D3, cutting red #B23A2E, grain ink #2E2A24, mark blue #3B4B8C, annotation ink #23211C, pin gold #D9A441, seam gray #8A8578. No gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas.
- Subject/piece model: each piece is a seeded wobbled blob (9 vertices, radii jittered from a mulberry32 stream, closed Catmull-Rom outline resampled to 150 points with a cumulative arc-length table). The grain axis is the longest vertex chord. Marks are seeded per piece: 2–3 notches (arc-length positions on the outline) and 1–2 drill holes (interior points). All four subjects are prebuilt at load from per-subject seeds, so a subject is identical every time it returns.
- Measure staging, all closed-form in m (measure progress 0–1 over 60 s): cutting dots lay by arc length (piece i starts at m = 0.07i, duration 0.62), dashed seam allowance (0.90 scale inset outline; start 0.06 + 0.07i, duration 0.58), grain arrows (start 0.40 + 0.03i), piece names fade in from 0.30, marks appear staggered from 0.48, annotations write in character by character in a per-subject order (relationship writes patience, then trust, then attention, then warmth), pins drop in the final third (first pin at m = 0.70). Loop: measure 60 s, hold 10 s, lift-and-fade 2 s, next subject. Subject index advances automatically per loop and immediately on tap.
- Frame one is the synchronous draw at a fixed phase (t = 46 s, m ≈ 0.767): relationship pattern with cutting lines drawn, arrows in, marks in, "patience 14" and "trust, in cm: all of it" fully written, "attention span —" mid-write, and the first two of five pins dropped. No Date.now anywhere; the animation clock is elapsed-time since load added to the fixed phase.
- State is rebuilt closed-form from a recorded event list (subject taps, pin grades, cuts) walked in time order at every draw, so re-grade/cut/subject state is a pure function of (t, events). The in-flight pin drag is applied as a live overlay and committed on release. Re-grade: each piece scales around a moved pin by a factor driven by pin displacement, weighted by piece-to-pin distance (nearest pieces grade most); the grade persists per subject.
- Hand lettering: every visible string (piece names, annotations, title strip) is drawn per character with seeded position/rotation jitter from the same RNG stream as its piece, over a hand-style font stack. Visible text is only the specced title strip, piece names, and annotations.
- Layout: pieces sit at fixed field fractions with small overlaps; radii are set against min(field width, field height) so pieces stay on the table at any aspect. Annotations are anchored beside their piece in screen space and clamped using the measured string width so they never leave the table; on phones (screen min dimension < 700 px, aspect fallback) there is no side margin, so annotations are lettered onto the lower inside of their piece instead — also standard pattern practice. Canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; canvas CSS size pinned in px on every draw. Audio is lazy (created on first pointerdown, typeof-guarded); the piece renders complete in silence. rAF is typeof-guarded; in the QA renderer the synchronous first frame is the whole render.

## QA log

- Renders (tools/render-piece.js): qa-desktop.png 1280×900, qa-mobile.png 390×844, thumb.png 720×720, all rendered cleanly in silence.
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and byte-compared with cmp — identical. Duplicate render deleted.
- Frame one read-back: pattern paper; four wobbled pieces with dotted red cutting lines and dashed gray seam allowances inside them; double-headed grain arrows; blue notch triangles and drill-hole circles; two gold pins down (of five, the rest drop later in the loop); piece names lettered on the pieces; annotations "patience 14" and "trust, in cm: all of it" fully written beside their pieces and "attention span —" visibly mid-write; title strip "a relationship, measured" under its rule at the bottom.
- Fix round 1 (layout): the first build placed annotations by field-fraction angles, which threw them off the table edges and clipped them, and piece names hung a full piece-height below each piece, colliding with the title strip. Radii were reduced, the layout tightened (rows at 0.29/0.65), names moved onto the pieces, and annotations re-anchored in screen space with measured-width clamping.
- Fix round 2 (phones): on the 390-wide render the side-anchored annotations still crowded and overlapped the pieces; phones now letter annotations onto the piece. Re-rendered all sizes; nothing clipped on mobile.
- Deviations: (1) Pins begin dropping at m = 0.70 rather than strictly last, so frame one at m ≈ 0.767 can show pins "partly dropped" as specced while pins still arrive after the cutting lines, arrows, and marks. (2) Seam allowance is a uniformly inset (0.90) outline rather than a true parallel offset curve; at pattern scale the inset reads identically and stays robust for the wobbled self-approaching outlines. (3) The prompt's subject list mentioned "bust 92" only as a stray aside and explicitly struck it through for the relationship; no subject uses it. (4) Annotations containing an em dash ("attention span —", "humility —", "cracks —", "hope —") keep it: those are the prompt's own annotation texts, shipped verbatim as content; no em dashes appear anywhere else in the piece or its copy.
