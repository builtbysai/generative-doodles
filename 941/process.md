# No.941 - Written, Then Unread

Study #115 giorgialupi, PP102 "The Safe Diary".

## Concept

A diary that writes itself in a living hand and can never be read. The page
fills over 90 seconds with minimum-stroke script: the cadence of real writing
(word lengths, word spaces, line breaks, paragraph blocks, ascenders and
descenders) with all information stripped out. The first third of the page is
a set of graphic-design experiments in a second, grayer ink (a timeline, a
letter format, a bullet list), drawn in the same unreadable hand; the rest is
free journaling in dark ink. The hand's energy is a mood: calm, restless,
tender. Nothing on the page is a letter; only the document title carries
words.

## Construction

Single self-contained canvas 2D page. No text is drawn anywhere: every mark
is a stroked polyline generated by the script engine.

Script generator model:

- A page layout is a pure function of (entry number, viewport size), built
  from a mulberry32 stream seeded per entry. Mood is NOT part of the layout
  seed: each stroke stores its clean polyline plus per-point unit jitter
  vectors, and at draw time the jitter is scaled by the mood's wobble, the
  x positions are scaled around each line's start by the mood's spacing, and
  line weight and write duration come from the mood table. A mood tap
  therefore rewrites the same page in a new hand, as specced.
- Glyph: 1-3 primitives drawn from a small vocabulary (down-stroke, hump,
  low wave, bowl arc, hook, descender), slotted across a seeded glyph width.
  Seeded probabilities add an ascender (10%), a descender primitive (9%), or
  a floating pen dot (12%). Primitives are abstract shapes; the vocabulary
  deliberately contains no complete letterforms, and glyphs are assembled
  without reference to any alphabet.
- Word: 3-9 glyphs, with a baseline join stroke between neighbours 35% of
  the time (the cursive rhythm). Lines fill a measure word by word; a word
  that would overflow the measure is discarded (the RNG stream position is
  part of the deterministic build, so discarding is stable).
- Page structure: (1) timeline experiment in the second ink: a wobbled spine,
  5-6 ticked clusters of small glyphs hanging below it; (2) letter format:
  salutation glyphs with a sweeping flourish, three body lines at a narrower
  measure, sign-off glyphs with a long underline sweep; (3) bullet list:
  3-4 heavy dots with short stroke-lines; (4) free journaling to the bottom
  margin, paragraphs of 3-5 lines with a gap and first-line indent.
- Loop: state is a closed-form function of (piece time, recorded events).
  Each entry writes for the mood's duration (calm 90 s, restless 55 s,
  tender 125 s), holds 15 s, then turns: the fresh page is revealed by a
  clip edge sweeping right to left with flat alpha strips at the fold (no
  gradients). The next entry is the next seed.
- Interaction: tap cycles mood calm, restless, tender and restarts the entry
  in the new hand; two taps inside 320 ms pair into a double-tap that turns
  the page now (a lone tap commits after the pairing window). Drag records
  a guide event: the line currently being written bends toward the pointer,
  weighted by proximity to the writing front, and springs back with an
  exponential decay on release. All offsets are computed from the recorded
  event lists at draw time.
- Frame one is the synchronous draw at t = 0, which sits at a fixed 34.2 s
  offset into entry 0's calm write: experiments complete, journaling begun,
  nib mid-line. Nothing in the first frame derives from a wall clock.
- Audio is lazy and guarded: an AudioContext is created only inside the
  first pointer gesture. A looped noise buffer through a bandpass filter is
  the pen scratch (gain and filter frequency follow the mood; restless is
  faster and brighter, tender slower and softer). When the writing front
  crosses a paragraph mark the scratch gain dips to a quarter for 0.3 s (the
  pause between paragraphs). A page turn plays a one-shot filtered noise
  sweep. The piece renders complete in silence.
- House rules: canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize
  listeners on both visualViewport and window; canvas CSS size pinned in
  px on every draw; phone detection via window.screen min dimension under
  700 px (aspect fallback only); page ground is the paper tone at all
  sizes; strict palette (paper #F5F1E6, ink #2E2A24, second ink #8A8578,
  margin red #B23A2E); no gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no
  offscreen canvas; rAF and AudioContext typeof-guarded throughout.

## QA log

- Rendered with the house renderer at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844
  (qa-mobile.png) and 720x720 (thumb.png). All render cleanly in silence.
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and byte-compared with cmp:
  identical. The duplicate render was deleted.
- Read-back, desktop and thumb: paper page, red margin rule down the left;
  at top the gray experiments (timeline spine with hanging clusters, letter
  block with salutation flourish and sign-off sweep, four bulleted
  stroke-lines); below, dark-ink journaling begun, the nib dot sitting at
  the end of the partial first journaling line; generous blank page below.
  Mobile: same structure at a narrower measure, journaling likewise begun,
  nothing clipped.
- Illegibility check: all three renders were read closely at full size.
  Individual primitives occasionally echo a single letter shape (a bowl, a
  tall stem), which is inherent to minimum-stroke script, but no glyph
  sequence forms a letter combination, no word reads as a word, and the
  salutation and sign-off are flourish strokes only. There is no typeset
  text anywhere in the piece; the only words are in the document title,
  which is never drawn to the canvas.
- Fix round: the first build's glyphs were too narrow and too uniformly
  tall, reading as frantic scribble rather than writing. Glyph widths were
  widened, down-strokes and humps lowered, a low wave primitive added to
  the vocabulary (bowls and waves now dominate, down-strokes are one
  primitive in six), and word gaps widened. Re-rendered at all sizes and
  re-verified for determinism after the change.

Deviations and recorded choices:

- Frame-one offset is 34.2 s (about 38% of the calm write) rather than
  exactly 35%: the experiments' share of total stroke length shifts
  slightly with page size, and 34.2 s is the smallest offset at which
  journaling has visibly begun at all three QA sizes while the experiments
  still read complete, matching the specced frame one.
- Tender's "rounded strokes" are expressed through lower jitter amplitude,
  heavier line weight, wider spacing and slower speed rather than a
  separate curve-rounding pass, because the layout is generated
  mood-independently from the entry seed (the same page, a new hand).
- The paragraph pause in the audio is a 0.3 s gain dip in the pen scratch,
  per the brief's own instruction, not a literal sigh sound.
