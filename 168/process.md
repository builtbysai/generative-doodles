# No.168 Dichromatic Garden

## Concept
An Ishihara plate as a garden: 2704 dots in reds, greens and tans on a cream ground, with a large eight pointed star hidden in the red-green confusion channel. It cannot be picked out in the default view. Tap the small eye at bottom centre and the simulated deuteranope view collapses the colour riot; the star resolves. The eye glyph is the only control and the only mark besides the dots.

## Construction
Dot colours were solved at build time against the Machado et al. (2009) deuteranopia simulation matrix. Each dot starts from one of three palette segments (reds in the #C0392B to #D35400 range, greens in the #229954 to #27AE60 range, tans around #D4A373); a bisection finds the shade on the segment whose simulated luminance equals a common background level (0.56), or 0.10 lower for dots inside the star. Every dot is then jittered along the matrix's near-null confusion direction (0.922, -0.386, 0.028), which a trichromat sees as red-green riot and the simulation barely registers (simulated step under 0.0001 per unit). Dot families are stratified three-by-three per block so family mix is uniform at every scale and only luminance can form a regional signal. Star dots share the background's size, family and jitter distributions exactly; measured simulated luminance: background mean 0.561 (sd 0.013), star mean 0.460 (sd 0.013). At run time the toggle applies the same Machado matrix to the baked colours and crossfades the two tables over 600 ms. A ?seed=deut query starts the piece in the simulated view for QA. One honest limit, recorded rather than hidden: simulated and trichromat luminance are near-locked through this matrix, so a heavy blur of the default frame shows a faint darkening where the star sits, as on real Ishihara plates; at native scale the chroma riot hides it.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 in both views (the simulated frames are saved beside the standard QA as qa-deut.png and qa-mobile-deut.png) and inspected all. Default view: full red-green riot, eye glyph legible at bottom centre, no star shape pickable at a glance on desktop, phone or thumb. Simulated view: the riot collapses to golds and olives and the darker eight pointed star resolves in the middle, clearly on the phone frame. Two iterations were needed: the first encoding leaked the star through plain luminance (equal in both views), and unstratified family sampling left gold patches that masked the star in the simulated view; both are fixed in the shipped build. The red segment's light end was extended slightly past #D35400 so red shades can reach the common simulated luminance at all.
