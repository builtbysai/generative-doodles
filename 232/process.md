# No.232 Parsing the Lingo

## Concept
After Kluber's 2019 work of the same name: wide horizontal bands of turquoise and chartreuse run through the panel, the left and right fifths are given over to darker blue vertical sections, and the centre holds a large rectangle of ninety fine horizontal lines, alternating blue and green on deep blue, an optical field that suggests data visualisation. This is the one Kluber in the set where the viewer disturbs the system. A drag across the fine field scrambles the lines locally under the finger; left alone, the field relaxes back to perfect order over about four seconds and heals itself. At rest, a faint data shimmer runs through the lines, so the field is never quite still even when nobody is touching it.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The wide bands are seeded layout data (mulberry32, seed 232) drawn flat each frame; the blue sections are flat fills with a single thin turquoise divider each. The fine field is ninety polylines of sixty points held in flat arrays; every point carries a vertical offset and velocity. A pointer drag kicks the points inside a radius around the finger, the kick scaled by the drag's vertical speed and by distance falloff, with velocities clamped. Each frame a spring damper (stiffness 22, damping 1.5) integrates all points back toward their rest positions, underdamped enough to wobble visibly and settle in roughly four seconds. The shimmer is added at draw time only, a small double sine per point, so it never feeds the physics. There is no click handler and no tap action: a press that does not move does nothing. The panel is 2:1, centred, and the page ground takes the field's own deep blue. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no text in the piece.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The composition reads exactly as specified: blue fifths with their dividers, the turquoise and chartreuse band margin showing around the fine rectangle on all four sides, and the ninety lines in order with the shimmer's gentle waviness visible across them. On the phone frame the panel goes full width, keeps its 2:1 shape on the deep blue ground, and the fine lines stay distinct at 390 wide. The disturbance itself cannot be exercised by the headless renderer (it fires no pointer events), so the kick and relaxation constants were set from the spring math and verified by the velocity clamp and the settle time the damping ratio implies; the at-rest state the renderer can show is correct.
