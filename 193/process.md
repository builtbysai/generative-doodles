# No.193 Numbered Sections

## Concept
Hogeye, Eugene Sargent's thirty-foot dancing boar, was prototyped to scale with every section numbered before the year-long build began. This piece honours the fabrication rule behind it: the work arrives unfinished, and the viewer's hands finish it. Twelve steel sections of a boar, each stenciled with its number, lie scattered around a dashed silhouette on a blue-night field. Drag each one home; only its own slot accepts it. When the last section lands, the boar does what Hogeye does: it rises onto its hind legs and dances.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The boar is twelve rigid steel plates in side profile (tail, rump, rear back, mid back, shoulder, neck, head, ear, belly, front leg, hind leg, hind hoof plate), each a hand-set polygon with a computed centroid anchor, filled #8A8F98 with #4A4E55 seams, rivet dots drawn in from the corners, and its number stenciled in bone #EDE6D6 at the centroid in a narrow bold stack. Missing sections leave a dashed ghost of their slot shape, so the numbers and the slot shapes are the only guide; there are no arrows and no text beyond the numbers. Dragging picks the topmost scattered section under the finger; on release, proximity to its own home anchor (74px) decides: close, and it lerps home and lands with a white clunk-flash ring; far, and it drifts softly back to its margin berth, where scattered sections idle with a slow orbital wobble, clamped so no section ever leaves the frame. Frame one opens mid-assembly by design: rump, mid back, shoulder, head, belly and hind leg are already home, so the boar is legible and six sections await hands. Completing all twelve starts a twenty second procedural jig: the whole creature rears back 24 degrees on a hind pivot with a heavy bob, the front leg paws at 5.2 rad/s, head bobs, tail wags, a full spin (horizontal squash through a floor of 0.3 so it never vanishes) runs at seconds 8 to 11, and an amber underlight pools under the feet with a glow at the pawing front hoof. After the dance it settles into a proud idle sway with the underlight gone dim. Double-tap scatters all twelve and restarts the build. Palette exactly per spec. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen, rAF typeof guarded.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. The composition read immediately: six plated sections forming a legible boar (02, 04, 05, 07, 09, 11), dashed ghosts for the six missing slots, stenciled numbers crisp at every size. First pass fault: the top-row and right-column scatter berths let large sections hang halfway off the frame (03 and 06 clipped at the top, 10 at the right edge), which would have made them hard to grab. Fixed by clamping scatter positions to a margin that grows with the piece scale, and the ghost slot fill and stroke were raised slightly (0.05 to 0.08 fill alpha) so the empty silhouette reads at phone size. Re-rendered: all twelve sections sit fully in frame on desktop and phone, the boar stays legible in the thumb, and no section overlaps the creature's home ground. The dance, snap and scatter paths are interaction-driven and cannot appear in a still; they were verified by tracing the state machine (anim lerp rates 9/s homeward and 5/s back, snap threshold, placedCount gate into the 20s dance, then sway) and by keeping every per-part dance rotation reset each frame so a scattered section can never inherit a stale paw or wag.
