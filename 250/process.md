# No.250 Sets of Three

## Concept
After Bawa's Blue Scaffolds in Zbayul and her Ladakh rule: a climbable scaffold structure on a hillside, modules grouped in threes, each trio smaller than the last as they ascend, deep blue against pale rock. The structure invites occupation: it is a system to explore. The topmost trio sways gently in the wind. Dragging across it pushes the poles aside on their springs and they settle back; double tap reseeds the trio layout. The poles never detach or break.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1200 by 800 scene: a flat pale rock sky, a hillside in a darker rock tone rising diagonally from lower left to upper right with a near black crest line, and small ground pads at the column feet. The layout is seeded: three or four trios step up the slope, each trio a four column scaffold frame whose bay width and level height scale by 0.82 per trio, with seeded column height patterns, rails at every shared level and alternating pale blue diagonals. Every coupler node is a spring body anchored at its home: a stiff damped spring pulls it back, displacement is clamped so poles bend but the grid can never tear, and edges are drawn between the live node positions each frame. The topmost trio's anchors sway sideways on a seven second sine, weighted by height, so its tops lean in the wind. Dragging injects velocity into nodes within a radius, both along the drag and radially away from the pointer; a second tap within a third of a second reseeds the layout. The opening clock starts mid sway with the full staircase built. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, shaped scene centred on the rock ground, rAF typeof guarded, no text, no score, no chrome.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; it passed on the first pass. Frame one reads as the full staircase: the big trio at the lower left climbing four levels, then the 0.82 trio, then the smallest at the upper right, all feet planted on the crest line. The seeded layout for the gallery seed draws three trios; a fourth appears on some reseeds. The phone frame centres the scene on the rock ground, the slope and the stepping still legible at 390 wide. Drag and double tap are registered as pointer handlers only; the spring and clamp constants were set so a hard fling bends the nearest poles well past their neighbours and still settles home.
