№8975 · Euclid Necklace

One cycle of a euclidean rhythm, strung as beads on a ring. The outer ring plays the first rhythm; the inner ring plays the second, turned a few steps against it. Filled beads sound, open beads rest. A drummer will spot the tresillo. Click the plate to re-string the necklace.
