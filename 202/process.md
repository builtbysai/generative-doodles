# No.202 Worm Friends' Chorus

## Concept
Maude's worm friends were the input stations of Willshire's mockingbird: talk to a worm and the bird mocks you back. Here the worms get their own stage, nine dim green segmented worms in a loose three by three field, each one a looping voice. You feed a worm a phrase by tapping it, a short tap for a short note, a hold for a longer one, up to eight notes, and it loops the phrase back raw at its own tempo. The tempos sit at prime ratios against each other, so the nine loops phase and drift and never lock into a grid. Every replayed note fires an expanding ripple from the worm's head and a bright flash along its body. One worm, the centre one, is a stranger: it arrives with a phrase already in it and plays it in gold, so the field is never silent even before anyone touches it. Double tap a worm and its voice drops out of the chorus.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Each worm is a chain of twelve beads along a breathing sine curve, bead radius tapering toward the tail, undulation driven by two sine terms with per-worm phase and bend. A phrase is an array of gap and duration pairs, recorded from pointer down and up times on the hit-tested worm, capped at eight with the oldest evicted silently. Playback is a per-worm scheduler: a cursor advances through the phrase at the worm's prime tempo ratio (29 through 61 over 43), and crossing a note start spawns a ripple, a stroked circle whose final radius scales with the note's duration, and sets the worm's flash, which lerps its colour from idle #3E6B1F toward active #7CFC00. The stranger's phrase is preseeded with five notes and its ripples and body tint run in #C9A227. The clock starts at 2.1 seconds with two of the stranger's ripples already travelling, so frame one is a voice mid-phrase, not an empty stage. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, the field centred with generous margins at every size, rAF typeof guarded, no text, no hint, no offscreen canvas.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Desktop and thumb: nine beaded worms in a loose field, clearly segmented, the gold stranger at the centre with a gold ripple mid-flight around its head, the other eight dim and waiting. Mobile holds the same field compressed into the portrait frame, worms smaller but legible, the stranger still centred and singing. One early fault, a second preseeded ripple whose total life was shorter than its starting age, so it never appeared; harmless, since the surviving ripple carries the frame. Playback timing, recording and the double tap clear were checked by reading the scheduler code paths, since the still renderer has no pointer events; note triggering only fires while the clock advances, which keeps the synchronous first frame exactly as composed.
