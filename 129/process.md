# №129 · The Crescent Shore - process notes

## Concept
A Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion field growing live on warm paper, read as a shoreline in cross-section. Where you stand on the feed/kill parameter square decides what the shore looks like: spots near one end of the crescent, crawling worms along the middle, coral labyrinths at the far end. The field is hard-thresholded to two values only, ink or paper, so the living simulation reads as a printed pattern that never stops moving.

## Construction
- Single self-contained page, no libraries. 2D canvas. Gray-Scott on two Float32Arrays (U and V), grid adapted to the field aspect with the long side capped at 144 cells, 9-point laplacian, 7 iterations per frame, wrap-around edges. Rendered as thresholded cells (V above 0.22 is ink) batched into horizontal fillRect runs on the main context; no offscreen canvas and no ImageData, so the node-canvas QA shim runs the identical script.
- Palette is exactly two colors: ground warm paper #F2EDE3, pattern ink #1A1A1A. Nothing else.
- Composition: the field fills the frame edge to edge with a small margin and a thin ink border at every size. Along the bottom edge a minimal parameter strip holds the f/k square (feed 0.010 to 0.080, kill 0.040 to 0.075) with the crescent zone drawn through the three Pearson points, a dot at each point, a ring marker at the current point, and only tiny f and k axis ticks. The hint sits in the strip to the left of the square so nothing overlaps.
- Pearson points: spots F=0.035/k=0.065, worms F=0.048/k=0.065, coral F=0.0545/k=0.062. The piece opens at coral, the densest and most reliable labyrinth regime. Diffusion is Du=0.2097, Dv=0.105: with the classic 0.16/0.08 scaling the worms point froze into stable spots on this grid, while at 0.2097/0.105 all three points land in their named regimes (verified by rendering each point separately during QA).
- Seed: a dense jittered grid of small noisy blobs across the whole frame (one blob every 15 cells with position and radius jitter, roughly 50 blobs, each an irregular disc with radius wobbled by angle, never a clean square), plus one larger central blob, scattered noise specks and faint grain, from a seeded PRNG (mulberry32, seed 322, advanced per reseed). Growth starts everywhere at once instead of spreading from a single blob. Pre-warm: 1200 simulation iterations before the first frame so the piece opens as a grown labyrinth, not a seed state.
- Visual Viewport sizing with resize listened to on visualViewport and window; canvas pinned by style width and height in px inside draw; phone detection via window.screen with aspect fallback; a synchronous first frame is drawn at load and requestAnimationFrame is guarded so node-canvas QA works. Time is read through a guarded helper (performance.now when present, Date.now otherwise).

## Interaction
- The strip is the instrument. Tap on the f/k square sets feed and kill from the tap position and regrows the field from a fresh blob seed. Drag on the strip scrubs f/k continuously and the field re-seeds on release. Drag on the field itself scrubs along the crescent by horizontal travel (spots toward worms toward coral) and also re-seeds on release.
- Untouched for 30 seconds, a slow auto-scrub drifts the parameters back and forth along the crescent, so spots become worms become coral and back without any reseed shock.
- Never click-to-reload: taps and drags are parameter changes, not restarts of the page.

## QA log
- Frames rendered from the actual page script (node-canvas) at 1280x900 desktop, 390x844 mobile and 720x720 thumbnail size and inspected before shipping. First pass failed review twice: with diffusion 0.16/0.08 the default worms point settled into frozen square spots and a lone blob seed died outright; and at the classic scaling the coral point read as fat blobs. Recalibrated diffusion to 0.2097/0.105 and re-rendered all three Pearson points separately: spots point gives clean spots, worms point gives elongated crawling squiggles, coral point gives a true labyrinth. Second failure: the HTML hint overlapped the centered f/k square and the f tick sat on the frame edge; the hint moved into the strip as canvas text left of the square and the square shrank slightly for tick clearance.
- Gate rework pass: the parent review found the single-blob seeding too sparse at the worms default, reading as scattered isolated squares rather than a grown pattern. Fixed by seeding a dense jittered grid of blobs across the whole frame, defaulting the piece to the coral end of the crescent, pre-warming 1200 iterations, and lowering the render threshold to 0.22.
- Final pass: all three sizes show a dense thresholded coral labyrinth in ink on paper filling the field edge to edge, the strip with crescent, marker at the coral point and ticks fully inside the frame, nothing clipped or degenerate on desktop, mobile or thumb. Hans's real-device check is the final gate.
- Visible copy check: no em dashes, no date strings, no absolute URLs; hint is lowercase and short ("drag to scrub the shore"); the only other on-canvas text is the tiny f and k ticks the spec allows.
