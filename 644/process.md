# No.644 - Mapa-Guía

EXPANSION #78 paulo-cox, prompt block 4 "Contour Tramo". His Andeshandbook cartography as a living map: the terrain breathes, the contours re-render, the route holds its line.

## Concept

A paper map sheet with generous margins holds a generative contour map: nested closed contour lines in ink form a mountain massif, with the confident wobble of a hand-drafted map. A flat rust route switchbacks up the massif from the bottom-left corner to a small black summit triangle. One small blue lake sits in a carved saddle basin. Five plain dark folio tabs with single tick marks hang along the right edge. On a slow 60-second loop the heightfield morphs, the massif reshapes, and the route re-fits itself to the new summit while the lake stays in its saddle. Line map only: no satellite look, no legend text, no numbered scale bars, no text anywhere.

## Construction

- Palette (strict, nothing else): paper #F4EFE2, contour ink #2A3340, route rust #B4572E, summit black #14110D, water blue #3A4E6B.
- Heightfield: sum of 5 seeded gaussians (one dominant massif peak, amp 1.0; four satellites 0.5-0.85) sampled on a 104-column grid (rows follow the sheet aspect, clamped 56-130). Morph: each gaussian's centre drifts on a small circle and its amplitude breathes +/-13%, all as pure functions of the 60 s loop phase, so the field at any moment is a pure function of (seed, phase) and the loop is seamless. A fixed low-frequency trigonometric wobble (+/-0.06 total) is baked into the field so the contour lines wander like hand-drafted ink.
- Closed contours, guaranteed: a smoothstep falloff envelope multiplies the field to zero at the sheet edges, so every contour level sits above the border field and every loop closes inside the sheet. Contours are extracted with marching squares at 8 levels (0.16 to 0.86 of the current field maximum); interpolated edge points are cached per grid edge so adjacent cells share identical points, and the segments are then joined into paths through their shared edge keys (each key has degree at most 2) and stroked as single polylines, closed paths getting closePath. Contours are also clipped to the sheet rect. Stroke about 1.5 px, scaled to the frame.
- Lake: 14 seeded candidate points are ranked by their un-morphed field (away from the main peak); the lowest becomes the lake centre. A gaussian depression (depth 0.5, sigma 0.06) is permanently carved there, so the basin and its ringing contours persist through the morph and the lake keeps its saddle. The lake itself is a flat blue blob (28-point outline, seeded 3- and 5-cycle radius wobble) drawn over the inner contours with a thin ink outline. Its centre and size change only on reseed.
- Route fit (deterministic, recomputed every frame from the current field): 7 waypoints are laid from the base point (9% / 90% of the sheet) to the current summit (grid argmax, border excluded), with alternating perpendicular offsets (decaying toward the summit) producing the switchback zigzag. The polyline is densified to about 12 px steps, given two light smoothing passes with endpoints fixed, then deflected radially out of a clearance disc around the lake (blob radius x 1.22 + 9 px) and clamped inside the sheet. No RNG at fit time: same field, same route.
- Summit: small filled black triangle at the current field maximum; it rides the morph.
- Folio tabs: five ink rectangles protruding from the right border at fixed fractions of the sheet height, each with a single paper-coloured tick stroke. Tick marks only, no numerals.
- Interaction: DRAG across the map traces the route: the nearest point on the current route polyline to the pointer is computed per move and a small tracer dot (paper halo, rust core) rides the line while dragging. DOUBLE-TAP (two short, unmoving taps within 350 ms and 48 px) reseeds: the seed advances deterministically and a new massif, lake, route and summit are generated. Watch-only otherwise. Never click-to-reload.
- House rules: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; canvas style width/height pinned in px inside draw(); phone detection via window.screen min dimension under 700 px with aspect h > w*1.15 fallback; frame one is the synchronous draw at morph phase 0; the rAF loop is typeof-guarded and anchored to its first timestamp; the morph clock never reads Date.now (event timestamps use performance.now only for tap timing, never for frame one); no Math.random, no createElement, no AudioContext, no offscreen canvas, no ctx.filter, no external assets, no visible text beyond the title element.
- QA hook: `?seed=NNNN` overrides the terrain seed; `?seed=tNN` freezes the morph clock at NN seconds for verification renders only. Production loads have no query string and are unaffected.

## Deviations

- Edge falloff envelope: the prompt asks for contours filling the frame edge to edge AND for contours that close; a field that stays high at the border cannot produce closed loops. The envelope resolves the conflict in favour of the explicit prohibition (no broken lines): the massif still fills most of the sheet, and its outermost loops close just inside the border.
- Lake fixed per seed: the brief's morph covers the heightfield and route; the lake's position is a per-seed terrain feature (it moves on reseed, as specced) while its carved basin keeps it seated as the gaussians drift around it.
- Lake candidate ranking uses the un-morphed gaussian sum (no wobble, envelope or carve) for speed and stability; the ranking is only a heuristic for "low area", and the carve guarantees the dip at the chosen point.
- The drag tracer dot is drawn only while the pointer is down and moving over the sheet; the prompt describes the dot travelling under the finger during a drag, so it is not left on the map after release.
