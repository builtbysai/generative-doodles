# No.934: Fifteen Years of Branching

Study #114 stefpos, PP95 "The Tree Diagram". Stefanie Posavec's signature form: the hand-drawn tree diagram as data practice, grown here as a living loop.

## Concept

A tree diagram drawn by an unsteady hand on a herbarium sheet. A gold root dot at bottom-center; from it, a wobbled trunk and branches climb into a canopy where every split carries a name: the dataset's categories lettered along the branches, and the terminals carry the data itself as small colored dots, one leaf per observation, in three class colors (blue, red, violet). The tree is never simply shown: it grows, leaf by leaf, holds in full leaf, sheds, and regrows on a 90-second loop. Growth is the piece.

Three taxonomies cycle on tap: (a) "projects": root "work" into data / drawing / books, then dear data, listening, tree diagrams, walking, i am a book, weather; (b) "a dear data week": root "week" into clocks, thanks, sounds, envy, food and their subthemes; (c) "a word tree": root "branch" into twig, leaf, root, bark, canopy and their playful children (twiglet, leaflet, rootlet, understory...). Dragging is wind: the crown bends and a few leaves shake loose. Double-tap is winter: eight seconds of bare structure.

## Construction

- **Geometry (seeded, fixed at build).** Each dataset is built once at load from its own mulberry32 stream (seeds 93411 / 93422 / 93433) in unit space, root at (0,0), growth toward −y. The labeled taxonomy is laid out recursively: children fan across 1.35 rad at depth 1 and 1.0 rad at depth 2, lengths shrinking ×0.74–0.92 per level. From each taxonomy terminal, an organic twig fan (2–3 branches, fan 0.85 rad, ×0.6–0.78 length) recurses to depth 5, giving branch depths 0–5 overall. Labeled nodes per tree: 10 / 16 / 16, all ≤ 20.
- **The wobble.** Every branch is a 7-segment polyline whose interior points are offset perpendicular to the chord by sin(s·π)·amp·sin(s·fr + ph), with amp, fr, ph drawn from the branch's seeded stream. Endpoints are exact (the sine envelope is zero at both ends), so nodes join cleanly while no branch is straight. Twigs (depth ≥ 3) wobble at half amplitude and lower frequency so they read as drawn, not as lightning.
- **Leaves.** Every twig tip is a leaf site, weighted 1 / 2 / 3 by depth (outer canopy heavier). Per-site leaf counts come from the weights by seeded stochastic rounding against a total of ~250, with a guaranteed minimum of one leaf on every first-order twig; the three trees land inside the 150–300 band. A leaf's class is its cluster's class with 78% probability, otherwise a random class. A leaf pops in over 0.6 s once its twig's growth has passed its attach point (attach at 55–100% of the twig's draw window).
- **The loop (90 s).** Branch growth is scheduled by depth: start = 2 + 7.5·depth s, duration 11 s, smootherstep easing: so the whole crown is drawn by ~51 s, inside the grow window (0–65 s); leaves complete it. Hold in full leaf to 75 s. Shed (75–83 s): each leaf falls on its own seeded delay (0–5.5 s) with an accelerating closed-form drop and a sideways sway, fading as it falls. Bare branches hold to 90 s, with a short paper fade across the wrap (87.5–90 s out, 0–2 s in) so regrowth never snaps. The loop clock is offset +41 s, so frame one is mid-growth: taxonomy and first twig orders complete, lower and mid canopy in leaf, depth-5 twigs still extending.
- **Projection.** The tree's unit bounding box is fitted, centered, into the frame with paper margins on all sides, recomputed per resize. Wind enters the projection itself: each point's screen x is offset by sway · (height fraction)^1.3 · 0.16 units, leaves at 1.35× that, so the crown bends more than the trunk.
- **Interaction (all closed-form over recorded events).** Tap: registered after a 0.38 s disambiguation window, advances the dataset (a → b → c). Double-tap: winter: all leaves hidden for 8 s, branches untouched. Drag: horizontal pointer velocity injects impulses into an impulse list; sway(t) = Σ amp·e^(−(t−tᵢ)·1.1)·sin((t−tᵢ)·5.5), a damped spring with no integration state. A fast drag (combined speed > 520 px/s, throttled to twice a second) shakes loose the up-to-three nearest visible leaves; a shaken leaf falls in real time over 1.6 s and stays gone until the loop wraps or the dataset changes.
- **Sound (lazy, guarded, gesture-born).** First pointerdown builds the AudioContext (typeof-guarded, try/caught; the piece renders complete in silence). Two looped noise chains through one master: a bandpass at 1700 Hz whose gain follows how many branches are mid-growth (the pencil scratch), and a lowpass at 420 Hz whose gain follows sway velocity plus an active drag (the wind). Leaf pops fire a tiny triangle tick (2100–2600 Hz, 50 ms), throttled to one per 90 ms and triggered only when the visible pop count rises. The tick's exact pitch uses Math.random: sound only, never a drawn pixel; all drawn state is seeded.
- **House mechanics.** Canvas size from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; canvas.style.width/height pinned in px inside draw() every frame; backing store scaled by devicePixelRatio (clamped 1–3) with the transform set per frame. Phone detection via window.screen (min dimension < 700 px), aspect h > w·1.15 as fallback only. Page ground is the paper color, so the light-ground phone rule is satisfied by construction. No gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas. The animation loop is typeof-guarded; frame one is the synchronous draw(0).

- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and cmp byte-compared **identical**. The first pass failed this by 40 anti-aliased pixels (±1 RGB along mid-growth twig edges): the initial frame had been drawn at draw(elapsed()), and the sub-millisecond clock epsilon moved the growth-clipped endpoints. Fixed by drawing frame one at exactly t = 0; the fix is recorded below.
- Fix rounds: (1) labels repositioned above their nodes with a thin paper halo (the first pass set them beside the node and branch lines struck through "books", "weather", "dear data"); (2) fans narrowed (depth 1: 1.8 → 1.35 rad, depth 2: 1.3 → 1.0) and trunk widths reduced so the tree reads upright rather than candelabra; (3) leaves moved from final terminals only to every twig tip, weighted outward, after the first pass showed a leafless frame one (the old terminal-only sites did not complete until ~48 s); (4) leaf attach window widened to 55–100% of each twig's draw so leaves ride the growth instead of waiting for its end; (5) loop offset settled at +41 s.
- Read-backs: desktop and thumb show the paper sheet, the wobbled tree grown from the gold "work" root, bark-brown trunk and first branches, all ten labels legible, roughly fifty blue/red/violet leaf dots through the grown canopy, the outermost twigs still bare and extending. Mobile shows the whole tree centered in the tall frame, labels small but unclipped, nothing cut off.

## Deviations

- **Root label.** The brief pictures the gold root dot "labeled 'root'", and also names each dataset's root ("work", "week", "branch"). The dot carries the dataset root's name, since the root is the first named decision and the piece's rule is that every split is named; the dot's gold is what marks it as the root.
- **Frame one at exactly t = 0.** draw(0) rather than draw(elapsed()) for the first frame, so the gate render is byte-stable (see QA log). In the browser the first rAF frame continues from the piece clock; the difference is a few milliseconds of growth.
- **Leaf placement.** Leaves hang at every twig tip, weighted toward the outer canopy, rather than only at final terminals: same total band, but leaf then arrives through the crown as it grows, which is what the growth loop is about.
- **Branch schedule compressed.** Branches finish by ~51 s inside the specced 65 s grow window (start 2 + 7.5·depth, 11 s each), so the full-leaf hold is genuinely full; the phase boundaries (grow / hold / shed / bare) are unchanged.
- **Phone labels.** All labels are taxonomy nodes at depth ≤ 2, so the phone fallback (labels only to depth 3) never engages; on phones the labels simply shrink to 9.5 px and the tree is fitted whole. Recorded here because the brief asked for the choice to be noted.
