# No.199 Peroxide

## Concept
For the Carp Slam trophies, Willshire paints a peroxide mix onto plasma-cut steel carp tails to force the rust. The piece is that bench process as an instrument: one raw steel tail on a black field, and the visitor's drag is the brush. Where peroxide lands, rust blooms, spreads and darkens over the following minute, fresh orange bloom going to mature rust and finally deep umber, and it keeps deepening for as long as the piece is open. The rust history is the whole composition. Double-tap and the next trophy comes off the rack, a fresh un-rusted tail in a slightly different cut.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The tail is a hand-set forked polygon, stalk at lower left, notch opening up-right, rotated per variant with a mirrored flip on alternate reseeds. The chemistry lives on a 128 by 96 grid laid over the tail's bounds, three Float32Arrays (rust, peroxide mask, cell noise) plus an inside mask computed by inverse-transforming cell centres into tail space; there are no offscreen canvas elements anywhere, the field is drawn cell by cell as filled rects clipped to the tail path. Each sim step diffuses rust toward the neighbourhood mean, grows rust where the mask lies (mottled by the cell noise), consumes the mask with a twenty five second time constant, and creeps every existing rust value upward so old work keeps darkening. The colour ramp runs clean shaded steel #6B7280 below the threshold, then bloom #C4551B, mature #8B2500 and umber #3B1F0E. Dragging stamps the mask through a soft round brush, about 24px, with a bone-coloured ring shown at forty percent alpha while the hand is down. Frame one is pre-aged honestly: two strokes are laid programmatically and the sim is run forward, the older one dosed twice so its heart reaches the dark end of the ramp, the fresher one still orange at the edges, before the first frame is drawn. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen (brush grows to 30px there), rAF typeof guarded, no text, no clear button, the double-tap is the only reset.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three, through four rounds. Round one faults: the fork read as a triangular shard, the grid did not cover the rotated tips so the lobes ended in black triangles, and the peroxide barely consumed during pre-aging, so the strokes saturated into two hard horizontal sausages. Round two rebuilt the sim rates (growth slowed, mask time constant shortened, diffusion softened) and widened the grid, but the silhouette still read as a shark fin. Rounds three and four reworked the polygon twice, fleshing the fan, deepening the notch and easing the rotation to about seventeen degrees; the final frames show a forked steel tail with the notch plainly cut, a mature rust band with a dark heart across its middle and a fresher bloom low on the lower lobe, mottled and feathered at every edge, and clean shaded steel everywhere the brush has not been.
