# No.235 Pulse

## Concept
After Woodfill's Pulse, 2003, the lamp installation in the stairwells of the 11th and Oak parking garage: a long horizontal array of square lamp dots, nine rows of sixty, five hundred and forty exactly, across near black. Brightness travels through the array in long packets, waves that rise in a row, hold, and release, each row answering a beat after the one above, and because every row is offset by half a dot the chase drifts diagonally as it goes. A second packet runs against the first, and where the two cross they interfere and brighten instead of cancelling. The light is flat throughout: these are on and off devices, lamps, not atmospheres, so there is no glow, no falloff, no gradient anywhere. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The array is drawn fresh each frame as flat squares, dot size two thirds of the smaller cell dimension, rows offset by half a cell. Brightness per dot is the sum of two gaussians (sigma 4.2 columns) centred on the two packet positions, the centres shifted per row by a fixed lag of 1.15 columns from the middle row outward, plus a small seeded hash term and a faint slow sine so the dark field is not dead. Packet A travels right at 2.4 columns a second on an 88 column loop, packet B travels left at 1.8 on a 96 column loop; the incommensurate loops mean crossings happen at different places each pass. Brightness maps to colour by a two stage lerp through the palette's three states only: off grey, dim amber, full lamp amber. The opening clock starts at sixteen seconds, which puts one packet mid array and the second just entering from the right. The panel is 3:1, centred on the near black ground. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Frame one shows exactly the intended state: one amber packet in full strength around the middle of the array, its rows visibly staggered into the diagonal, and the second packet entering at the right edge, brighter in the upper rows where its lag leads. Dots are flat squares with hard edges, the off dots a quiet grey, no halo anywhere. On the phone frame the 3:1 panel goes full width and both packets still read clearly at 390 wide. No adjustments were needed after the first render.
