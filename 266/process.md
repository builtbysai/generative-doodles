# No.266 What Melts Does Not Stay

## Concept
Stanzin Tsepel's Fremantle thesis in one frame: a glacial blue field at the top, releasing a slow stream of luminous particles that travel the full height of the piece and settle into white salt rings on a sand floor at the bottom. What melts in the Himalayas doesn't stay there. A band of nambu stripes divides the frame horizontally. The flow runs on a 35 second cycle, seamless. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, cover-fitted so the three fields meet the frame edge to edge. The glacier is a blue mass with a seeded jagged calving edge, its history held as thirteen pale streaks that drift a few pixels on the 35 second cycle. The nambu band is a wool field with three bold black stripes and a faint woven tick texture. The sand floor carries 650 seeded grain flecks and a scatter of salt specks thickening toward the landing zone. The stream is 90 particles riding a quadratic bezier from the glacier's edge, through the band, to the sand, each with its own offset, lateral sway and size, drawn as a soft halo over a bright core and fading in and out at the ends of the run. Salt rings are born on a fixed cadence matched to the arrivals: twelve seeded rings per cycle expand as flat ellipses from the landing zone and fade over five and a half seconds, over a faint static crust of older rings. The opening clock starts at 13 seconds, the stream in full flight and two rings already open on the sand. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no interaction handlers, no audio, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported resize listeners only. One fix: the wool band first ended ten pixels above the sand, leaving a stripe of glacier blue showing between band and floor; the sand now starts directly under the band, its shadow line intact. Frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: blue field, striped band, the bright stream curving down through both to the rings opening on the sand. On the phone the cover crop keeps the band, the stream and the landing zone in frame. No further fixes were needed.
