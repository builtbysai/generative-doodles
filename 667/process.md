# No.667 "Hace 7.000 Años"

EXPANSION #81 jose-perez-de-arce, prompt block 3. Build and QA by fly's builder; not pushed by the builder.

## Concept

Pérez de Arce's reconstruction practice as a generator. A single parametric figure, caught mid-action in deep time, drawn in graphite pencil-hatch on paper under a low horizon: a Chinchorro kneeling over a harpoon in progress, an Aconcagua hunter waiting with a bow, a Diaguita bent over a digging stick in a furrowed field, a Mapochoe raising the antara to his lips. Bottom-left, the caption engine writes in his own caption habit: line one says how long ago, line two adds one dry remark. Each tap is a new excavation; double-tap reseeds the dig. The figure is never a museum statue: every rig is mid-gesture at every phase, including frame one.

## Construction

- Single self-contained `index.html`, canvas 2D, no external assets. Composition space 1.6 x 1.0 units letterboxed onto the viewport; canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both `visualViewport` and `window`; canvas CSS size pinned in `draw()` every frame; phone detection via `window.screen` (min dimension < 700px) with the aspect fallback.
- Strict palette: paper #ECE5D3, graphite #2A2A2E, sea accent #3E6B6B (water marks only), ochre #C08A3E (exactly one prop per scene: harpoon shaft, bow, digging stick, antara tubes).
- Figure rigs: one joint solver per era returns composition-space joints from the scene-local time. Limbs are round-capped polylines with a fainter offset twin (pencil grain); heads are 20-gon outlines with a neck stroke, hair cap and nose tick; seeded hatch ticks ride the torso and thigh segments. Gestures: Chinchorro working hand wraps an ellipse around the harpoon binding (2.6 s / 1.3 s layered sines, phase-offset so t=0 is mid-wrap) while graphite shavings fall from the binding point; hunter's bow arm sways on a 6.5 s scan with a slow head turn and dust at the bow hand; Diaguita digs on a 3.4 s press cycle, soil specks bursting from the stick tip in the first 42% of each cycle; Mapochoe sways about the hips on a 7.5 s cycle, breath motes drifting from the antara tube ends.
- Backdrops per era, all seeded (mulberry32): Chinchorro, 30 sea-teal wavelet arcs above the horizon, gently lifting; Aconcagua, two abs-sine hill ridges; Diaguita, 11 furrows converging on a vanishing point at the horizon plus three curved cross rows; Mapochoe, three wavy teal river bands in the ground. Every scene also gets a hand-wobbled horizon line at 2/3, sparse seeded ground hatch and contact ticks under the figure.
- Era sequence: seeded (base seed 667001) shuffled bags of the four eras with no back-to-back repeat; index 0 is always the Chinchorro launch scene. Scenes crossfade over 1.2 s, the outgoing scene's gesture continuing on its own clock during the fade.
- Taps: recorded on the anchored clock (performance.now minus load anchor) and decided inside `draw()`, so state is a deterministic function of the clock plus the recorded tap times. A lone tap takes effect 0.35 s after landing (time to hear a second tap); two taps within 0.32 s reseed instead: new sequence from a new seed round, new caption picks. Never click-to-reload.
- Caption (the prompt's own specified text, the only visible text in the piece): line 1 per era: "hace unos 7.000 años." / "hace unos 500 años." / "hace unos 600 años." / "hace unos 300 años." Line 2 list: "Diferente a hoy, YYYY." (launch line), "Sin prisa, todavía.", "El oficio era el mismo.", "Nadie miraba el reloj.", "Trabajo lento, bien hecho.", "La herramienta alcanzaba." Later scenes pick from the list by seed.
- Runtime-year exception (per the prompt): the YYYY in the launch second line is the current year read once from the system date at load, because the line speaks of "hoy". It is the only date-derived element in the piece; no geometry, timing or sequencing derives from the date. Frame one is the synchronous `draw(0)` of the launch scene at loop t=0, never a live-clock reading.
- Determinism: all randomness is mulberry32 from fixed seeds; all motion is a phase function of the anchored clock; the animation loop is rAF-guarded with a `typeof` check; the QA renderer has no rAF, no AudioContext, no `document.createElement`, no offscreen canvas and no `ctx.filter`, and none are used. A `?seed=tNN` location hook freezes the clock for QA renders only.
