# No.234 Slow Mathematics

## Concept
After Kluber's 2026 Slow Mathematics and the 2012 untitled blue works: the quietest piece in the set. Thin black and light blue stripes on paper, the pattern divided into two unequal sections, two fifths at the left and three fifths at the right, each section with its own stripe sequence, and one solid black band cutting across the bottom third and disrupting the pattern. A single red line redraws itself continuously across the composition, slowly tracing the stripe boundaries, one edge at a time, stepping from edge to edge as it crosses the panel, a full pass every seventy five seconds. The line is the slow mathematics of the title made visible: a proof being worked at reading pace. A tap reseeds the construction and the line starts a new proof, crossfading over one second; the page never reloads.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. A state is one seeded layout plus one path: two stripe sequences generated per section from a mulberry32 seed (heights as panel fractions, colours strictly alternating black and light blue, both clipped at the two thirds line), and a path built from the sections' own edge lists, starting on a left edge, running right in horizontal runs along edges with vertical steps between them, and crossing the black band's top edge once on the way. Each frame the state is drawn flat (stripes clipped per section, the black band over the bottom third), then the red line is stroked up to the fraction of the path's length that the seventy five second pass clock has reached, the partial segment interpolated exactly. Frame one opens with the clock pre-run by twenty five seconds, so a third of the composition is already traced. A tap (short, near stationary pointer press) advances the seed, generates a new state, and crossfades from the old drawing to the new over one second. No grid, no measurements, no numbers anywhere in the piece. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The two sections read instantly, their stripe rhythms visibly different across the seam, the black band is exactly the bottom third, and the red line sits precisely on stripe edges, stepping between them, caught a third of the way through its pass in every frame size. On the phone frame the panel holds its 2:1 shape on the paper ground and the line is still findable at a glance. The tap reseed cannot be exercised by the headless renderer; its path (pointer press timing check, seed advance, one second crossfade) is simple state handling reviewed in the code. No adjustments were needed after the first render.
