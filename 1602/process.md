# No.1602 "A Single Scale Cannot Show It" (AW3, aluan-wang #211, PP849)

Driver: the verbatim AW3 prompt is the spec; one ink-wash work re-framed by keys 1, 2, 3 into rectangle, square, and circle over a 3s morph, with the ink content persisting across shapes.

What was built: a self-contained index.html on paper #F2EDE4. A seeded particle-wash system (52 soft grey wash blobs, breathing slowly) sits under 9 seeded black ink gesture paths, 7 fine gold thread-lines, and one coral stroke rising from lower left to upper right. A faint procedural cloud texture (seeded flattened ellipses at 8% opacity, Turner-echoing) hides inside the wash layer. The mask is a rounded-rect path whose width, height, and corner radius interpolate between rectangle (1.28 x 0.84), square (1 x 1), and circle (radius 0.5) over 3s with smoothstep easing; keydown 1/2/3 retargets the morph from the current interpolated shape, so the ink never resets. Default mask at load is the rectangle. Title, formula date 2006 · 06 · 01, and hint "Press 1, 2, 3 to reshape." render on the page in ink on paper. House rules kept: Visual Viewport sizing with resize on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned in draw() via style width/height in px, phone detection via window.screen with aspect fallback, deterministic first frame (mulberry32 seed 1602003, fixed phase 20.0, no Math.random or Date.now), one synchronous frame at load, requestAnimationFrame guarded by typeof checks.

Fix rounds: 1. First render pass showed the rectangle mask overflowing the viewport on phone (390px) and touching the thumb edges, its width 1.28 x s exceeding the frame. Capped the work size so the widest shape fits with margins (s = min(0.8 x min dimension, 0.92 x VW / 1.28, 0.95 x VH / 0.84)) and re-rendered. 2. Fixed a reshape() bug before rendering: it divided already-factored mask params by the work size; now it freezes the current interpolated factors directly as the morph origin.

QA: rendered desktop 1280x900, mobile 390x844, and thumb 720x720 from the actual script and read all three; composition, coral stroke, gold threads, faint clouds, and captions legible on all sizes, phone margins correct. Thumb rendered twice, md5 identical (deterministic first frame confirmed). Grep found no Math.random, Date.now, gradients, or shadowBlur.

Deviations from the prompt: (1) the mask morph interpolates rounded-rect width, height, and corner radius rather than a literal SDF interpolation; the visual result is the same legible 3s re-framing. (2) The hidden cloud texture is procedural seeded ellipses at 8% opacity, not a sampled bitmap, since the piece must be self-contained with no external assets. (3) On phones the work keeps its centered shape with paper margins rather than going full-bleed, per the house rule for centered geometric pieces.
