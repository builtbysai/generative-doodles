# No.231 Burning Year

## Concept
After Kluber's 2025 Burning Year, a 3D printed PLA relief driven by custom software and video. Here the relief is rebuilt in flat canvas: a square field of twenty four by twenty four cells, each cell split into four triangles meeting at its centre, 2304 triangles exactly. Colour runs cool to warm along the diagonal in hard stepped bands, neon green through yellow and orange to deep red, never a smooth gradient. Cells alternate peak and valley, so the field genuinely reads as a faceted relief of raised triangular cells. The relief never changes shape. Only the light moves: a slow sun orbits above it on a ninety second period, shading every facet, catching specular glints on the facets that face it, and dragging one warm highlight across the field like weather.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page, no 3D library. Everything about the relief is precomputed once from a fixed seed: each cell's colour is picked from a fourteen step ramp built by quantising the four palette stops, indexed by the cell's diagonal position with a small seeded jitter; each triangle's normal is precomputed from the cell's peak or valley orientation plus a slight seeded tilt, and normalised. Per frame the work is arithmetic: the light direction orbits, each triangle is shaded by the dot of its normal with the light (clamped, so shadowed facets fall dark but never black), and a specular term from the halfway vector adds warm glints at the tenth power. One rotated gradient band in rgba(255,214,150) at 0.07 alpha, drawn with 'lighter' and offset along the sun's azimuth, is the only gradient in the piece and the only soft thing in it; every triangle edge stays razor sharp, with no bloom and no glow filters anywhere. Frame one opens at twenty one seconds, the sun well off-axis, facets unevenly lit and glints standing on the green flank. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, the square centred on the near-black ground at every size, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The first render already had the intended read: a woven diamond relief, stepped colour bands running cleanly from green at the top left to deep red at the bottom right, individual facets catching and losing the light, no softness anywhere. Cell count verified by construction: 24 by 24 cells, four triangles each. The phone frame enlarges the square to ninety eight percent of the short side and the facet pattern stays crisp. No adjustments were needed.
