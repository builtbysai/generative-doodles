# №9083 · Strides

Impossible geometry after Joseff (jn3008.art): a Penrose triangle drawn as three
beveled beams in warm bone on near-black, with the over/under order cycled so no
consistent depth reading exists (s1 over s3 at the apex, s2 over s1 at right,
s3 over s2 at left).

Motion: a 12-second seamless loop. An amber pulse strides the impossible
perimeter forever; a raking light orbits the triangle so the bevel highlights
slide across the beams; the whole figure breathes gently. Dragging steers the
light toward the pointer; release eases it back so the loop stays seamless.

Faint dotted grid behind, code-native. No text in the artwork.
