# No.255 Hard Surface Mech

## Concept
After the Day 7 hard surface study (2021) by tanzinchospel.art: a chunky low-poly robot with large yellow rounded leg armour, green knee pads and pink-orange piston feet, standing on a flat green ground plane under a dark green sky, lit like a film still. It idles, armour plates shifting a few pixels, its emissive accents breathing. Drag to orbit it: the yaw carries inertia and settles. It is an explorable object, not a spinning gimmick.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, no WebGL (the QA harness has none, and the piece is a flat construction by design). The mech is drawn parametrically from a single yaw angle. Turning squeezes the silhouette (width follows the absolute cosine), converges the legs, slides the head and eye slit sideways, fades the front details (chest plate, vents, emissive strip) as the front turns away, and opens a flat side face on the chest with a piston detail riding it. Draw order keeps the orbit honest: far leg and far arm first, darker; then pelvis and chest; then near shoulder and near leg over the top. Each leg is thigh armour, a green knee pad, a shin, and a three-part piston foot. The idle is small and mechanical: a three pixel bob on the body, the chest plate drifting two and a half pixels, the shoulder pads out of phase, the eye slit and chest strip breathing in opacity. Three-point lighting is suggested with flat tints only, a green key wash clipped to the chest and head, a warm orange rim stroke on the near shoulder pad. Dragging maps horizontal movement to yaw velocity; on release the yaw continues and decays exponentially to rest. The still is contain-fitted, centred, the dark sky filling the rest of the screen. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no text, no UI.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Frame one is the opening three-quarter view (yaw 0.65), idle mid-shift. Two fixes. First pass: the key light was a free translucent polygon that floated in the sky behind the mech like a stray slab; it is now clipped to the chest and head shapes so the light lives on the body. The warm rim stroke also sat a few pixels off the silhouette; it now runs down the near shoulder pad's edge. The orbit and inertia cannot be exercised by the headless renderer, which fires no pointer events; the drag path was reviewed in code, damping set so a hard flick turns the mech about a full revolution before settling.
