# No.175 Plastic Spiral Moth

## Concept
After Fathauer's Witchy Convergence: a night black field covered in interlocking spirals built on the plastic number, and at the centre of the largest spiral a moth, a Black Witch in spirit, its wings made of the same spiral geometry, pale dust against the dark, dissolving into the field at its edges. The moth appears; it does not animate. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page, no interaction, no hint. Every spiral arm is a logarithmic spiral whose radius multiplies by the plastic number rho = 1.324717957... every quarter turn, drawn as a polyline with its arc length table precomputed so the draw on is a true length reveal. Three arms form the main family at the moth's centre; six satellite centres carry two arms each; exactly one satellite arm is picked out in copper #B87333, everything else #3E5C8A on #08080A. The moth is procedural: two forewings and two hindwings as flattened bezier outlines (right side defined, left mirrored), filled with wing shadow #8A7F63, then stippled with about 1500 dust dots per wing in #D8CFAF, rejection sampled with density falling from each wing's core, plus a sparse halo of dots just outside the outline that thins with distance so the wing edge dissolves into the field. Two small plastic spirals per wing are stroked in shadow tone over the dust as tracery, so the wings carry the field's own geometry. The body and antennae are simple bezier forms in shadow tone. The 36 second loop draws the spirals outward (10s), fades the moth in wing first (4s), holds a shimmer phase where each dust dot's alpha oscillates on its own phase (10s), then dims everything to true black (4s) and rests dark until the loop wraps seamlessly. First frame is offset to 17 seconds: spirals complete, moth present, dust shimmering. The moth is positioned on the main spiral centre by construction on every screen shape. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, full bleed field.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. The first forewing shape read as a rounded butterfly wing; the top edge was flattened and the tip sharpened so the silhouette reads as a moth. The first tracery pass used shadow coloured dots on a shadow coloured fill and was invisible; tracery is now stroked spiral lines over the dust, subtle but legible on the forewings. A placement bug put the moth below the main spiral centre on portrait phones (unit square mapping versus screen fraction); the moth now takes its position from the same mapped centre as the spirals. Final renders: moth centred on its spiral at all three sizes, copper accent spiral present exactly once, dust halo visibly dissolving at the wing edges.
