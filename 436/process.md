# No.436 Dive Repeat

EXPANSION #47, angelina_bambina_dsgn prompt ANG4 ("Dive Repeat"),
after her thepatternchallengesummer26 "DIVE" piece. Built per
BATCH-433-437-SPEC.md.

## Concept

Her seamless body-positive pattern engine, shown the way she shows
it: as a product. A half-drop repeat of diverse women swimming on
teal fills the upper two thirds of the frame as a pattern field;
below a cream horizon line, the same cloth appears folded over a
product mockup with a cream edge and a folded corner. Four swimmer
variants (yellow floral, red-white stripes, teal polka dots, pink)
differ in skin tone, hair colour, suit and body type. The figures
are flat stylised silhouettes, joyful and dignified, never
sexualised posing (her rule, stated in the prompt). The loop is
watch-only motion: the swimmers stroke in place, seaweed sways,
bubble streams rise. Dragging scrolls the pattern endlessly;
double-tap cycles the product (beach towel, pillowcase, bath mat).

## Construction

One canvas, flat colour only, no offscreen canvas, no gradients,
no shadowBlur, no text. The tile is motif DATA drawn at wrapped
lattice offsets each frame (the No.383 / No.397 precedent).

Tile geometry, so seamlessness is checkable:

- Tile TW x TH = 320 x 150. Column pitch 320, row pitch 150.
  Alternate (odd) world columns are dropped by TH/2 = 75.
  The pattern is the lattice generated by the column pitch and
  the per-column vertical period TH; every motif is drawn at
  all lattice translates covering the clip region, and is
  clipped only by the region, never per tile, so motifs spill
  across tile borders and are completed by the neighbouring
  instance. There are no per-tile seams to hide.
- Exactly 4 swimmer figures per tile, asserted in code
  (FIGURES.length === 4): two fully inside at (85, 42) and
  (240, 108), and two half-drop partners centred on the bottom
  edge at (120, 150) and (255, 150), whose lower halves are
  drawn by the instance itself spilling into the row below and
  whose upper halves sit at the tile's top edge in the row
  above, wrapping exactly within their column.
- No motif crosses a vertical tile edge, the one place a
  half-drop would break a figure: all figures' horizontal
  extents stay inside [12, 308] of the 320-wide tile.
- Also per tile: 2 coral clusters, 1 seaweed tuft anchored in
  the free bottom-left pocket, 2 floating seaweed sprigs in the
  open water between figures (the bottom edge is occupied by
  the partner figures, so the remaining seaweed floats as
  sprigs rather than overlapping them).

Swimmer rig: torso ellipse, suit clipped onto it (floral spots,
diagonal stripes, polka dots, or a cream waistband, per variant),
skin shoulder/chest ellipse, two-segment arms sweeping a full
front-crawl circle, two legs in a gentle near-parallel flutter
(kept close so they read as legs, not a tail fin), head with a
hair crescent and a trailing hair mass, a bun on two variants.
Arms run on 3 whole cycles per 24s loop, legs on 6, body bob on
2, seaweed sway on 2: every cycle count is whole, so the loop
wraps exactly. Each figure trails one bubble stream of 5 bubbles
that rises its full 88px once per loop, alpha stepping down as
it rises.

Products: exactly 3, asserted. Each is a cream shape with a
cream plate peeking 9px below (its thickness); the pattern is
drawn inside the inner window at the SAME scroll offsets as the
field, so the product reads as the same cloth. A folded corner
(cream flap over a darker-teal underside triangle) sits
top-right of each. The towel adds fringe ticks along its bottom
edge; the pillowcase adds a cream flap band near its open end;
the bath mat is a rounded rect. The pattern field (upper 2/3)
and the product window share one drawPattern routine with
different clip rects.

Interaction: DRAG scrolls a world offset (scrollX, scrollY);
drawing wraps it by the lattice, so scrolling is endless in
both axes and lands seamless at any offset. DOUBLE-TAP (two
taps under 280ms, under 14 design px of travel, within 400ms)
advances the product index. Pointer coordinates convert through
the stored cover-fit transform (S, OX, OY from frame()).

Determinism: frame one is drawn synchronously at the constant
phase T = 7.0s into the 24s loop (mid-stroke, bubbles spread
through their rise); the animation clock is anchored there and
advances in fixed 1/60 ticks from the rAF timestamp delta,
never from Date.now() minus an offset. QA probes via ?seed=
(scroll = start scrolled to (137, 61), p1 / p2 = start on the
pillowcase / bath mat) follow the No.432 probe precedent and
do not affect normal viewing.

Palette: the prompt's closed set only. Hair colours resolve
inside it: blonde = suit yellow #F2C230, white hair = product
cream #F5F0E6, dark brown = deep skin tone #8A4B2E, auburn =
coral #F27059. The cream also serves as the white of the
red-white striped suit. Two of the four variants share a skin
tone (#E8A87C) because the palette holds exactly three skins;
hair, suit and body type keep them distinct. The lower-third
surround takes the suit teal #2E8B8B.
