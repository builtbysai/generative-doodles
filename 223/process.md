# No.223 Generator Fugue

## Concept
McTavish describes her swarm as a loosely-coupled, reactive web of small generators, filters, transformers and renderers, a digital cyber-orchestration, the art of the fugue. This piece makes the pipeline literal and visible, because the rigging is part of the work. A cyan particle stream flows left to right across deep indigo-black through four stations, each with its tiny monospace label. GENERATE emits. FILTER culls about forty-five percent of what reaches it and leaves the culled as magenta residue marks at the station. TRANSFORM turns the survivors: their paths bend, their sizes scale and breathe, and a slow-turning glyph at the station shows the operation happening. RENDER stamps warm yellow marks from the transformed stream, and the marks accumulate into a drawing on the right third of the frame, fading over ninety seconds. Drag across the stream injects a burst of particles wherever the hand crosses, disturbing the system at that stage.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Particles are plain JS objects with stage tags, spawned at the GENERATE station about twenty-two a second and capped for discipline; crossings are detected against the four station x positions. The cull is a probability roll at the FILTER line, the transform a velocity and scale change with a sinusoidal path after it, the render a deposit stamp every fifty-five milliseconds while a particle crosses the right zone, into a JS deposit list with ages. Residue lives fifty seconds, deposits ninety, both drawn directly each frame; no offscreen canvas. Frame one is pre-run twelve seconds so the page opens with the stream in full flow, a residue line already standing at the filter, and a partial deposit drawing, dotted contours laid along the transformed paths, accumulated on the right. Drag bursts inherit the stage of the x they are injected at, so a burst past the transformer arrives transformed. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded. The only text is the four station labels, which are the artwork's own functional markings.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The pipeline reads at every size: labelled station posts, the cyan stream thinning visibly after the filter, the magenta residue standing in a line at FILTER, the turning diamond at TRANSFORM, and the yellow deposits building wavy dotted contours on the right. One adjustment at the gate: on the phone render the RENDER label sat on top of the brightest deposit row and lost its last letter, so each label now knocks out a small ground-coloured patch behind itself before drawing. The particles stay small, flat and disciplined throughout; nothing explodes.
