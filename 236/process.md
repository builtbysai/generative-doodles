# No.236 Cocktail Party

## Concept
After Woodfill's What If?, 1995, his junk electronics assemblage: five bare mechanisms sharing one dark room, drawn schematic in side view with all the hardware left exposed, bolts, brackets, cords, struts and feet, because the rig is the composition. A pendulum bar swings from the ceiling rail, a bare bulb bounces on its cord, a small wheel rolls around the inside of a rust circular track, a fan vane whirls inside its guard ring, and a metronome arm ticks over its trapezoid base. Each mechanism runs on its own period, seven, eleven, thirteen, seventeen and twenty three seconds, with its own phase, so the five almost never agree. Every few minutes the incommensurate clocks bring them near alignment, everything swinging together for one beat, and then they drift apart again. His reviewer's phrase for the original was a cocktail party for electronic nerds, who sometimes choose to acknowledge one another and sometimes not. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page, everything drawn fresh each frame from five small parametric rigs laid across five equal zones, under a shared mounting rail with a bolt over each zone and over a dark floor with a flat shadow pool under each rig. Pendulum: angle a 0.62 radian sine on the 7s clock, bar and bob in steel. Bulb: cord length breathing plus a slight sway on the 11s clock, the cord drawn as a quadratic curve with a drifting control point, socket and warm white bulb. Wheel: centre orbiting inside the rust ring on the 13s clock, three spokes counter rotating at the true rolling ratio. Fan: four petal vanes rotating continuously, one revolution per 17s, inside a thin guard ring on a post and trapezoid foot. Metronome: arm a 0.5 radian sine on the 23s clock with its sliding weight block riding at sixty two percent. One deviation, deliberate: the fan's angle grows linearly rather than following the oscillation formula, because a vane whirls rather than swings; its period is still the assigned 17s. Rig sizes key off zone width, and the pendulum's length is additionally capped by its distance from the room's edge so the swing can never leave the frame. The opening clock starts at 3.7 seconds with five scattered phases, nothing aligned. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. First round fixes: the hanging rigs were sized from zone width alone and looked lost in the tall room, so the pendulum bar and bulb cord were lengthened from the stage height, the fan enlarged and the metronome arm lengthened; and on the phone frame the longer pendulum swung its bob off the left edge of the canvas, so its length is now capped by the pivot's distance from the edge and the full swing stays in the room at both sizes. Final renders show all five rigs legible and at scattered phases, pendulum swung well left, wheel caught at the lower left of its track with spokes showing, fan vanes mid whirl, metronome tilted with its weight block clear. On the phone frame the rigs are small but each is still identifiable, hardware included.
