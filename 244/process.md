# No.244 Cocktail at Midnight

## Concept
After Bawa's Cocktail #ff0080, made for the dark storefront windows of Philomath, Oregon in 2013: a single hot pink scaffold frame, three bays wide and four tall, erected in a shop window at midnight. The pink is exact, the pink of her title. Nothing glows and nothing is written anywhere; the colour is flat, the glass below throws back faint streaks of the lower frame. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1000 by 1000 scene on near-black. The scaffold is a node and edge system built once: four standards, five ledger rows, horizontal and vertical tubes, and one diagonal per bay alternating direction, with a coupler disc at every node. Each edge carries the brightness of its midpoint: a sine wave travelling across the bay columns on a fifteen second period, lerped flat between the dim pink and the exact hot pink, so full and dim chase each other across the frame with no glow falloff anywhere. The storefront is only implied: a pane outline and two mullions stroked at five percent in the glass colour, cropped by the frame edges. Reflections are projected line work, not filters: edges low enough in the frame are mirrored across the glass line at the scaffold's base and redrawn in the glass colour at about twelve percent total, three stacked passes offset downward for the vertical smear, each pass shimmering on its own slow phase, plus a few faint vertical streaks under the standards. The opening clock starts at 3.75 seconds so frame one catches the wave at the left: left bays at full pink, right bays dimming, streaks already below. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Frame one reads exactly as specified: the left two bays burn at full #FF0080, the right bay falls off toward the dim pink, couplers dot every node, the pane outline is barely there, and the mirrored lower bay ghosts faintly under the base line with its stacked smear. Flat colour throughout, no bloom. The phone frame keeps the square centred on the black ground and the wave split still reads at 390 wide. No adjustments were needed after the first render.
