# No.1014: What the Sound Casts

Study #124 futa-kera, PP175 "Sound Shadows", Continuum Resonance @ VS. Osaka: the artwork is the shadow of a sound-system. The three-dimensional objects operating inside the sound software are never shown; only their shadows, projected on the gallery wall, exist for the visitor.

## Concept

A gray-black gallery wall. On it, the soft shadows of three branching, tree-like structures that live only inside the machine: they slowly rotate, and they grow and shed in time with a generative sound swell. A small console slab at the bottom center is the machine that makes the sound that makes the shadows. The visitor never sees a cause directly, only the effect on the wall, except for the console's tiny waveform, whose amplitude is the very swell that is growing the branches.

One remove, kept honest in code: a single closed-form sound state (`swellAt`) drives the branch growth, the shadow depth, the console's displayed waveform and tick brightness, and (once a visitor enables it) the generative audio itself. The audio is not an accompaniment added to a picture; it is the same signal the shadows are cast from.

Interactions: drag moves the virtual light, and every shadow on the wall swings and stretches in answer. Tapping the console retunes the machine: the sound-objects are reseeded and the new branches grow in over four seconds. Double-tap reseeds the whole room.

## Construction

- **Sound state.** `swellAt(t, phase)` is a seeded closed-form sum of three sines (periods ~21 s, ~8.7 s, ~3.9 s), normalized and shaped with a 1.25 power curve. Each tree reads it with its own phase, so the three structures breathe out of sync. Growth fraction per tree is `g = 0.40 + 0.60 * swell`, so at a swell trough the outer limbs are genuinely shed and at a peak the tree is whole.
- **The objects (never rendered).** Each structure is a seeded recursive branch system built with mulberry32: segments carry 3D endpoints, a depth, and a growth key equal to the accumulated path length from the root (with seeded jitter). Partial growth is `clamp((g * span - key) / window)` evaluated per segment against the live `g`, so growth creeps tip-ward as the swell rises and shedding takes the tips first as it falls. Structures rotate about their roots with a closed-form yaw plus a slow pitch sway. No object geometry is ever drawn, the segment list is only ever fed to the projector.
- **Projection model.** The wall is the plane z = 2.0 in a virtual room; the light sits at (-0.18, -0.42, -1.35), in front of the objects (z ≈ 0.42–0.78). Each segment endpoint is projected through the light onto the wall plane with a perspective divide, `S = L + (P - L) * (wallZ - Lz) / (Pz - Lz)`, giving magnification around 1.7–1.9×, the shadows loom larger than the things casting them. Stroke width scales with the same per-point projection factor, so nearer limbs cast thicker shadows.
- **Softness without blur.** Per house rules there are no gradients, no shadowBlur, no offscreen canvas. Each projected segment is stroked in four layered passes (width multipliers 3.4 / 2.1 / 1.25 / 0.62, alphas 0.075 / 0.12 / 0.19 / 0.30) with round caps and joins: a wide faint halo under a denser core, which reads as a soft projected shadow. The shadow tone interpolates between #0E0F12 and #050607 with the master swell.
- **The lit wall.** The projection wash is ten nested flat-alpha ellipses of #2A2D33 (alpha ramping 0.034 → 0.096), a stepped approximation of a soft light falloff; its center tracks a blend of frame center and the light's position, and its brightness breathes slightly with the swell. Wall ground is #0A0B0D and is also the page ground.
- **Console.** A #14161C slab, width 12% of the frame, at bottom center. Its interface is abstract marks only: a waveform squiggle whose amplitude is the master swell value, two rows of ticks and dots whose brightness samples the same swell field at seeded phases, and one status dot breathing with the swell. Faint #3DDC84 throughout. No text anywhere in the piece except the document title.
- **Audio.** Created lazily inside the first pointer gesture, fully typeof-guarded and try/caught, silent in QA. Three detuned oscillators (55 Hz, 82.7 Hz, 110.3 Hz triangle at reduced gain) through a lowpass whose cutoff (180–880 Hz) and a gain (0.02–0.13) both follow the master swell each frame. When the swell band steps downward, a short falling sine blip (300→84 Hz, ~1 s decay) marks a shed. The visuals are complete and identical with audio absent.
- **House rules.** Canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; canvas CSS size pinned in px on every draw; phone detection via window.screen physical size (min dimension < 700 px) with the aspect fallback. Frame one is a synchronous draw at the fixed offset t = 15 s, no wall clock feeds any drawn state; the live loop only advances an accumulated elapsed time from that offset. All state is seeded (mulberry32) or closed-form. No microphone, no external assets, no absolute URLs, no date strings, no em dashes.

## QA log

- Rendered with `node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js` at 1280×900 (qa-desktop.png), 390×844 (qa-mobile.png), and 720×720 (thumb.png). The piece runs cleanly in silence (no AudioContext in the harness).
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and byte-compared with `cmp`, identical. The duplicate was deleted.
- First read-back: composition correct (huge soft branch shadows over a dim wash, console small at bottom center) but the whole frame was too faint, and a four-step wash showed visible concentric band edges. Fixes: wash rebuilt as ten finer steps with higher cumulative alpha; shadow pass alphas and base segment width raised; shadow core pass strengthened (0.26 → 0.30). Re-rendered all sizes and re-verified determinism.
- Final read-back: desktop and thumb show three soft, dark, tree-branch shadows sprawling across the lit wall area, trunks running off the bottom of the frame, crowns fanning near the top; the wash falls off smoothly; the console reads as a small dark slab with a faint green waveform, tick rows, and status dot. Mobile shows the same system filling the tall frame, branches uncropped at the sides, console intact at bottom center, nothing clipped.
- Deviations, with reasons:
  1. The retune and new-room reseeds draw their new seed from Math.random. Determinism is a property of each room once seeded, and frame one always uses the fixed base seed (101417); a visitor's reseed is an event, not part of the shipped first frame, the same pattern used by earlier pieces in this lane.
  2. Console width is floored at 54 px. At exactly 12% of a 390 px phone frame the slab would be 47 px and its interface illegible; on desktop and thumb sizes it is exactly 12%.
  3. Shadow softness and the light wash are built from layered flat-alpha strokes and ellipses rather than blur or gradients, per the standing renderer constraints; the layered result is what the QA renders show.
