# No.180 Quotient Bloom

## Concept
Goodman-Strauss's orbifold, performed: a vivid wallpaper pattern in the *442 family spreads edge to edge, and then the plane is folded up along its own symmetry, every point travelling to meet its twins, until the whole design collapses into the small fundamental patch it was always made from, a folded jewel pinned by its cone points. Hold, breathe, unfold. The collapse is the piece. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. One motif, five flat shapes in unit cell space (lapis petal, gold dot, vermilion triangle, teal diamond, bone centre dot), is copied by the full dihedral orbit of the square, eight transforms per cell, across a grid of cells filling the frame; that orbit is the kaleidoscope heart of group *442, and no generator grid is ever drawn. The quotient is precomputed as a parity class map: each cell belongs to one of four classes by the parity of its indices, and each class owns one representative cell in a central 2 by 2 block. Through the fold parameter q, every cell lerps from its home position to its class representative's position in the block, scaled up by the patch factor (patch width a quarter of the frame), while non representative cells fade out across the second half of the travel so that at q equals 1 exactly the four fundamental cells remain, drawn large, their features sitting on their twins. The four mirror lines through the centre (horizontal, vertical, both diagonals) flash in white at 20 percent alpha, staggered, while q is strictly between 0 and 1; at the folded hold, five small white cone point disks pin the patch corners and centre, and the patch breathes at 2 percent over 8 seconds. The 45 second loop: bloom in radially (6s), hold (5s), fold (10s), folded hold (8s), unfold (10s), hold (6s); the first frame is offset to 24 seconds, the folded jewel in its hold, so the piece opens on its payoff and then unfolds into the full pattern. Flat colour only, no glow. Palette exactly per spec: ground #0B0B0E, lapis #2E6BE6, gold #F2B233, vermilion #D9481C, teal #1FA88A, bone #EDE6D6, fold lines white at 20 percent. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, watch only so no interaction listeners and no hint, rAF typeof guarded.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three, plus two extra states rendered from offset copies of the final script: the unfolded pattern at 8s and mid fold at 16s. The unfolded frame is a true Symmetries of Things plate: lapis rosettes with gold, vermilion, teal and bone accents, edge to edge, no visible grid. The shipped first frame is the folded hold: four kaleidoscope cells in a 2 by 2 patch at centre, cone point disk visible at the patch centre, the rest of the frame clean near black. Mid fold shows the cells as ghosted twins converging on the centre with the mirror cross lit, the collapse legible rather than messy; the non representative fade was moved earlier (from q 0.25) after the first mid fold render read too dense. The phone frame holds the same jewel at the same quarter width proportion. One casualty during the build, an over broad text substitution that truncated the script, was caught by the first black render and the file rewritten in full and re verified; the renders above are all from the final file.
