# No.863 "Silence Remembers"

Study #105 hsin-chien-huang, PP24 "Semantic Deluge".

## Concept

The Ekholux end-state in Huang's own words: "A world unraveled by language. A space where sound takes shape, and silence remembers." A wireframe megastructure stands in blackness, held together by a hum the visitor makes simply by moving. Attention is the structure. While the pointer moves, a sustain value rises toward 1 and the tower holds, its edges warming toward amber. After about two seconds of stillness the sustain decays with an eight-second time constant, and the vertices let go one lag at a time, drifting apart on seeded vectors into a slow, dim point-cloud, snow rather than shrapnel. One vertex is crimson: the keystone, last to drift and first to return. A tap pulses it, and for a moment it gathers its neighbours back before releasing them again. There is no repair button, no meter, no score. The state of the tower reads only in its geometry. The deluge is not failure; it is the piece.

Frame one is the tower whole: initial sustain is set to 1 at load (the load counts as an activity window at t = 0 with the standard two-second grace), because the piece needs the visitor to meet the structure before it starts, gently, to fall apart in front of them.

## Construction

- Tower: procedural wireframe from a seeded mulberry32 stream. 18 stacked rings of 15 vertices (270 vertices), each ring a jittered circle with a seeded radius profile (taper plus a slow bulge and twist), drawn in a shallow 2.5D projection (depth squashes the vertical slightly and modulates alpha). Edges connect ring neighbours, verticals, and one diagonal per quad (about 780 edges). One vertex on the second-highest ring, chosen for frontness, is the crimson keystone.
- Sustain model (closed form): pointer movement is coalesced into activity windows, each carrying a strength from the movement speed and a matching rise time constant. Sustain S(t) is evaluated in closed form from the recorded windows: inside a window, and for a 2 s grace after it ends, S rises toward 1; outside, S decays toward 0 with tau 8 s. No frame state is needed to know the sustain at any moment.
- Drift (light fixed-timestep integration, recorded per the house allowance): each vertex has a seeded drift vector (magnitude 0.03 to 0.14 of the smaller viewport dimension, slight downward bias) and a drift amount that follows the dissolution target (1 - S) through a first-order lag integrated at 30 Hz substeps. Time constants are asymmetric: 5 to 11 s to drift away, 2.5 to 5 s to return. The keystone is the exception both ways, 22 s out and 0.7 s back, so it drifts last and returns first. Frame one is exact regardless: at t = 0 every drift amount is 0 and sustain is 1.
- Keystone pulse: a tap records a pulse time; for about 1.5 s a decaying envelope (tau 0.6 s) scales down the dissolution target of vertices near the keystone, weighted by a squared falloff of home distance, so the neighbourhood visibly re-gathers and lets go. A double-tap reseeds the tower from a fresh stream and resets sustain, windows, and drift.
- Rendering: edges are coloured by mixing the wire tone toward the sustain glow by S and toward the drift tone by the endpoints' mean drift amount; alpha falls as drift grows. Vertices mix toward the drift tone and dim the same way. The keystone is drawn last, crimson, with a fading ring while a pulse envelope lasts. Strict six-tone palette throughout; no gradients, no shadowBlur, no filter, no offscreen canvas.
- Layout: canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; canvas style width and height are pinned in px inside draw(). Phone detection via window.screen (min dimension under 700 px), aspect fallback h > w * 1.15; on phones the tower takes a wider share of the frame so nothing crowds the edges. The tower occupies the middle 60 percent of the height, centred, over black space.
- Audio: created lazily inside the first pointerdown, all use typeof-guarded. Three detuned triangle oscillators (54 Hz, 54.4 Hz, 81.3 Hz) through a lowpass whose cutoff tracks movement speed (the hum), gained by sustain. A looped noise buffer through a bandpass carries the dissolution: its gain peaks mid-decay, (1 - S) weighted by a factor that returns to zero as S reaches 0, so the drone decays into filtered noise and then into silence. The QA renderer has no AudioContext; the piece renders and runs silently there.

## QA log

- Rendered with the house renderer at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png), and 720x720 (thumb.png). All clean, silent, no errors; listeners registered for visualViewport resize, window resize, and pointer events.
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and byte-compared with cmp: identical. The duplicate render was deleted.
- Desktop and thumb read-back: a tall angular wireframe of pale vertex dots and amber-warmed edges on black, centred, occupying the middle band of the frame; exactly one crimson vertex near the top (the keystone); nothing else in the frame, no text, no HUD.
- Mobile read-back: the same tower scaled to the tall frame, whole and uncrowded, keystone visible, nothing clipped.
- Dissolution check: rendered a throwaway at t = 26 s through the renderer's seed parameter (the script's QA hook boots the simulation at the given second; inert in normal viewing). Sustain by then is about 0.05; the tower has come apart into a dim grey point-cloud, edges gone drift-coloured and faint, vertices scattered on their vectors, still coherent enough to remember the tower, and the keystone still crimson near its home. Graceful, not glitchy. The throwaway was deleted.
- Deviations: none from the prompt's substance. Two implementation choices recorded: initial sustain is 1 at load (see Concept), and vertex drift uses a light fixed-timestep lag integration rather than a per-vertex closed form over the sustain history (frame one remains an exact synchronous draw at t = 0, per the gate).
