# No.1867 "Deep Listening Circle" (FN4, PP1114)

Source prompt: TASKS.md, francesca-naibo block, PP1114. Study #244 francesca-naibo.

Driver: a scored listening exercise. A text instruction arrives at the center of a dark hall, and the rings of seated listeners answer it in turn. The pattern of response IS the piece: one ring swells a tone, another answers a breath later, the outer ring stays silent longest, or a wave of sound crosses the rings, or all rings hold one note until it dissolves. The felt phenomenon is a dark hall where attention itself moves in waves.

Palette (hand-picked from the prompt's named colors): hall black ground #0A0908 with a slightly lighter center disc #0E0C0A, ring outlines in soft gold #C9A86A, text in warm white #F2EAD8, sound blooms in pale blue #A8C4D8, dark plate #171310 with a gold hairline.

Layout: centered geometric composition, a square kept whole on every screen with the page ground set to the piece's own hall black. Four concentric rings of seated listeners (8, 14, 20, 26 listeners) centered in frame, each listener a small warm dot on a thin gold ring circle. The text score floats at the center like a title card inside the dark disc, with five small gold dots under it marking which score is playing. Title and date plate top left, "Watch only. The circle listens on its own slow clock." hint bottom left, both drawn on the canvas.

Motion: watch only, no interaction. Five authored text scores cycle on a slow clock, each holding 75 seconds, 375 seconds per cycle. Each score has its own ring-response rule: score 1 call and answer (rings swell in turn with delays of 0, 6, 12 and 24 seconds, the outer ring latest); score 2 traveling wave (a wavefront makes two laps around the room and each listener blooms just after it passes); score 3 held unison (all rings rise together, hold, then dissolve over the last 20 seconds); score 4 neighbor chain (a ripple chases itself around each ring, one listener at a time); score 5 silence (only a few seeded listeners flicker faintly, the room mostly still). The text score fades in over the first 5 seconds of its hold and out over the last 5. Sound blooms are pale blue halos that grow around a sounding listener and fade as the tone passes.

The five text scores: "listen for the lowest sound in the room", "let one note travel around the room", "hold one note until it dissolves", "answer only when you hear your neighbor", "listen for the silence between sounds".

Fixed-phase frame one: t = 40.0 s, score 1 at local 40 s, ring 1 at full swell, ring 2 rising, ring 3 nearly quiet, ring 4 in soft decay, the first text score fully faded in at the center. All randomness is a seeded mulberry32 (listener angle and radius jitter, silence flicker seeds), never wall-clock, Date, or unseeded Math.random. One synchronous frame is drawn at load; requestAnimationFrame is guarded with a typeof check so node-canvas QA works. Visual Viewport sizing with resize listened to on both visualViewport and window; canvas element pinned in draw() via canvas.style.width/height in px; phone detection via window.screen (min dimension under 700px) with aspect fallback h > w*1.15; page ground is the piece background color.

Deviations from the prompt: the prompt specifies each listener as a sine or voice with real WebAudio; per the build brief this piece is visual-only, so there is no WebAudio at all (it cannot autoplay and the piece is watch only), and no sound of any kind. The response rules, delays, held unison and traveling wave are all built as visible bloom behavior instead. Everything else follows the prompt: dark hall seen from above, concentric rings, text score at the center, very slow and very quiet, no shimmer, no chakra colors, no mandala decoration.

Gallery note: "Concentric rings of listeners in a dark hall, seen from above, answering each text score in turn, one ring swelling, another answering a breath later. Watch the circle listen."
