# No.283 Salt Worn

## Concept
After Sophia Golovanevskaya's salt-eroded porcelain: a single cream vessel centred on a dark ground, its lower half eaten to porous matte bone, its upper half still holding a smooth teal glaze. Between the two zones a ragged shoreline marks where the glaze tide is receding, and barnacle clusters cling along that line. On a 30 second seamless loop the tide line breathes, rising and falling a few percent of the vessel height, the erosion pits deepen and refill, and a slow specular sweep crosses the glaze. Watch-only; nothing reloads and there is no interaction.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, centred fit so the shaped vessel keeps its proportions, with the dark ground as the page ground. The palette is held to the six specced colours only: dark ground, bone white, pitted shadow, teal tide, deep teal pool and barnacle. The vessel is a lathe form: its half width is a Catmull-Rom spline through twelve control radii, giving a flared rim, a narrow neck, a swelling shoulder and belly and a tucked flat foot, 675px tall, 75 percent of the frame height. The mouth is drawn as a shallow ellipse tipped toward the viewer for the gentle three-quarter view, pooled deep teal inside. Material zones come from height plus seeded data: the shoreline height at any x is a base level plus two-octave value-noise fbm, and two shoreline seeds are crossfaded over the loop so the ragged edge morphs and the loop seam is exact. Everything above the shoreline is filled teal, modelled only with flat translucent deep teal side bands; the specular sweep is a low alpha bone band clipped to the glaze zone, fading in and out as it crosses once per loop. The matte zone gets no gradients at all: its texture is seeded cellular data held purely as marks, about 310 craters (some in tight eaten clusters) plus 900 fine pores, all drawn as flat bone and pitted shadow ellipses clipped to the silhouette and masked to below the current shoreline, so pits appear and vanish as the tide breathes. Each crater deepens and refills on the loop through its radius and the alpha of its shadow bowl, on its own phase over a global deepen cycle. Barnacles are 34 seeded clusters of small ellipses with shadowed centres, anchored along the shoreline with density falloff toward the vessel edges, and they ride the breathing line each frame. A faint contact shadow under the foot is layered translucent pitted shadow only. Frame one opens at loop time 8 seconds, so the tide is already partway down, the pitted zone is developed and the sweep is already on the glaze. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds, no text, no audio, no offscreen canvas, no filters or shadowBlur.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; a grep confirms exactly the six specced palette colours and no em dashes. Three fixes during QA. First, the profile interpolation stepped at its control points and the foot came to a point, so the profile was rebuilt as a Catmull-Rom spline with a shallower foot curve. Second, the pits and barnacles read too faint and small, so crater count, size and shadow alpha were raised, eaten clusters were added, and the barnacle ellipses were enlarged. Third, the glaze shading ellipses and the matte shadow crescents bled below the shoreline as grey leaf shapes at the vessel edges, and a silhouette stroke crossed the mouth as a chord line; the shading was shortened to stay inside its zone and the edge is now stroked as two separate side profiles. Final frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: a smooth teal-glazed vessel above a ragged barnacled shoreline, porous pitted bone below, centred on the dark ground with no text anywhere. On the phone the centred fit keeps the whole vessel in frame and the shoreline and pitting stay legible.
