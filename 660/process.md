# No.660 "Río Bajo el Río"

EXPANSION #80 angela-leible, prompt block 4 "Río Bajo el Río" (verified verbatim from TASKS.md). Her 5x3 m painting EL RIO BAJO EL RIO as a living watershed: water descends from a cordillera ridge, picks up mineral color crossing the rock strata, deposits sediment in a basin, and flows on underneath as a ghostly gold river.

## Concept

Near-black ground. A dark mountain ridge fills the top 15% of the frame, crossed by horizontal strata lines. From its irregular lower edge, dozens of water ribbons pour down the frame as thin streams of overlapping flat dashes. The descent crosses three rock strata bands (marked by strata lines): iron rust, copper green, granite gray. Each ribbon that crosses a band carries that band's mineral away in its water, the stain deepening just after the crossing and fading partly back until the next band re-stains it. Where ribbons land at the surface line (y = 0.80), they deposit a grain of sediment in the basin, colored by the mineral they carried; the record builds grain by grain and fades only at 1%/s. Below the surface line, every ribbon's path is mirrored in pale gold at 40% alpha: the river under the river. Dragging stirs eddies and blooms mineral plumes; a tap sends a surge of new ribbons from the ridge. No text, no gradients, no cartoon water.

Strict palette, seven tones: ground #0A0A0B, mountain mass #3A3A3D, strata lines #6B6B6E, water #7FB3C8 at alpha, minerals rust #8A4A2B / copper #3E7A6B / granite gray #9A9A9C, under-river gold #D9A441. Mineral tints are per-dash blends of the flat water tone toward the flat mineral tone, never a gradient wash.

## Construction

- **Layout in fractions.** Everything (ridge edge, band ranges 0.300-0.375 / 0.465-0.540 / 0.615-0.690, surface line 0.80, particle and grain positions) lives in frame fractions, so any viewport refits the same watershed and resizes never disturb the simulation.
- **Ribbons.** 150 standing ribbons. Each is a particle advected at fixed dt = 1/60 on the anchored clock: fall speed 0.30 * speed (speed seeded per ribbon, 0.72-1.32) plus a seeded meander, vx = 0.055 * drift * sin(y*21 + phase + 1.7*sin(x*9 + 2*phase)). Velocity relaxes toward the field at 6/s. Spawns come 60% from five seeded spring columns under the ridge edge (triangular spread 0.16 wide), 40% uniform across the frame. Each ribbon keeps its last 30 positions as a trail, drawn segment by segment as overlapping flat dashes: alpha ramps 0.30 at the tail to 0.92 at the head, boosted toward 0.97 while the water is still above the first strata band (the spring glow, so the upper descent shines where the springs pour); line width is frame-scaled, about 3.1 px at reference scale times a per-ribbon factor of 0.85-1.20, tapering from 0.72 at the tail to 1.12 at the head, so each trail reads as one continuous thread of water, not a hairline and not a particle spray.
- **Strata lines.** Seeded wavy paths (fixed wave per line, identical every frame), never ruler-straight: three inside the ridge mass, and at each of the three band boundaries a main line on the band's top edge plus a lighter echo just below it. No other horizontal lines exist in the frame except the single surface line at the basin.
- **Tint model.** Per-ribbon state: current mineral index and strength s. Entering a strata band (edge trigger on the band range) sets the mineral and resets the crossing clock. For 0.7 s after a crossing, s rises toward 0.82 at rate 3.5/s (the stain deepens as the water loads); after that, s decays toward a 0.30 floor at rate 0.35/s (fades partly back, never fully, until the next band). Dash color = mix(water, mineral, s), computed per ribbon per frame, so the color is carried in the water itself and the whole trail wears it.
- **Sediment deposit model.** On landing at the surface line, a ribbon deposits one grain at its x (seeded jitter 0.012) and a basin depth skewed toward the floor, y = 0.806 + 0.184 * (1 - rng()^2.2). Grain color = mix(water, carried mineral, max(s, 0.65)); a ribbon that somehow lands untinted deposits mix(water, granite gray, 0.7). The buffer is a plain JS array drawn every frame (no offscreen canvas). Grain alpha = 0.9 * 0.99^age, exactly the specced 1%/s fade; grains are pruned below 4% visibility and the buffer is capped at 2600 (oldest dropped) for performance. Frame one holds 228 grains from the pre-run.
- **Under-river.** Each ribbon's trail is drawn a second time mirrored across the surface line, y' = 0.80 + (0.80 - y) * 0.6, in gold with alpha 0.40 * (0.45 + 0.55 * trail fraction), segments landing below 0.998 skipped. The ghosts of the lower descent fill the basin with slow gold curves that meet the visible ribbons at the surface line where the sediment drops.
- **Interaction.** Drag: a stir point follows the pointer; ribbons within 0.085 (aspect-corrected) are deflected radially plus a 0.7 swirl component and 0.25 of the pointer's own velocity, and their stain is boosted toward 0.95 (a plume: an unstained ribbon caught in a stir takes the mineral of the next band below it, since a plume needs a source). Tap (press under 0.4 s, under 8 px of travel): 30 surge ribbons spawn over 1.5 s, flagged temporary; they deposit sediment like the rest but are removed on landing instead of recycling. Everything else is watch-only; there is no reset and no click-to-reload.
- **Determinism.** All randomness flows from mulberry32 streams seeded off 660001 (ridge 660012, springs 660024, spawns/deposits 660048). The sim advances only in whole fixed steps toward (pre-run + anchored elapsed), so the state at any elapsed time is independent of frame rate. Frame one is the synchronous draw after the 600-step pre-run (10 s of flow): ribbons mid-descent at every depth, tinted below their crossings, 228 sediment grains laid. No Date.now, no Math.random anywhere.
- **House rules.** Sizing from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; canvas style pinned in px inside every draw; phone detection via window.screen minimum dimension under 700 px (aspect h > w*1.15 only as fallback when screen is absent), phone ribbons drawn 1.15x wider; rAF typeof-guarded; no offscreen canvas, no filters, no audio.

## Deviations

- The under-river mirror is vertically compressed (factor 0.6) where the prompt says only "mirrored at 40% alpha below the surface line." An uncompressed mirror throws every ghost of the upper two-thirds of the descent off the bottom of the frame; the compression keeps the whole river's echo inside the basin while preserving the mirror meeting at the surface line.
- The sediment fade is implemented as alpha decay at exactly 1%/s with pruning under 4% visibility, plus the 2600-grain cap; the prompt's "fades at 1%/s" and "never fully clears" are honored in rate, and the cap only ever drops grains that are already effectively invisible or oldest under heavy play.
- A stir plume on a not-yet-stained ribbon assigns it the mineral of the next band below its position. The prompt has plumes "bloom" near the fingertip without naming a source color; borrowing the upcoming band's mineral keeps the plume inside the strata logic instead of inventing a color.
- No other deviations. No blue-gradient water, no waterfall noise, minerals carried in the dashes themselves, no text.
