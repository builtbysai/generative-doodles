# №128 · Free Mint - process notes

## Concept
From lammetje's 1,352 free works and the fxhash free-mint ethos: generosity as a medium. The piece is a quiet field of faint drifting strokes, and every touch gifts the visitor a variant: where the finger lifts, a small unique composition blooms, signs itself with a tiny edition number, then detaches and drifts to the edge of the frame to join a slow orbit of the session's editions. The frame becomes a collection wall.

## Construction
- Single self-contained page, no libraries. 2D canvas. Field: ground #0E0D0B with faint drifting strokes in #3A352C, spawning, wandering and expiring continuously, always calm. Editions: seeded mini-compositions of 200 to 400 strokes inside a 200px square, generated at mint time by one of five micro-generators (nested arcs, flow burst, broken concentric rings, cross-hatch square, string knot) chosen from the seed, each in its own two-ink duo drawn from lammetje-like pairs (bronze/cream, indigo/bone, rust/paper and three variants). The edition number (#1, #2, ...) is drawn tiny in dim grey at the edition's lower corner; it is part of the piece per the prompt.
- Orbit: on mint the edition blooms at the lift point over about two thirds of a second, holds, then eases over the following seconds to its slot on a perimeter orbit (slots evenly spaced around an inset rectangle, recomputed for the whole collection at each mint) and settles at 62 percent scale with a slight rotation. The collection is capped at 24 visible editions; a 25th mint retires the oldest, which fades.
- Interaction: press, drag a little, lift: the edition blooms where the finger lifted. A soft ring marks the touching point while pressed. Double-tap clears the collection after a short heartbeat pause. No wallet or crypto UI anywhere; the gift is the interaction. Never click-to-reload.
- The opening frame is staged with three pre-minted editions already settled on the wall (one string knot, one hatch square, one arcs rosette, in three different duos) so the piece opens as a young collection rather than an empty room; the visitor's first mint is #4. Visual Viewport sizing with resize listened to on visualViewport and window; canvas pinned by style width and height in px; phone detection via window.screen with aspect fallback; full-bleed at every size. A synchronous first frame is drawn at load and requestAnimationFrame is guarded so node-canvas QA works.
