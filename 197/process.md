# No.197 Bike-Chain Fur

## Concept
Amanda Willshire's Sassy the Sasquatch stands eleven feet tall in a fur coat that is not fur: recycled bike chains from four different states, fabricated link by link over her body mass, with blue eyes that light up at night. The piece renders her exactly that way, a towering dark silhouette combed over with thousands of tiny interlocking chain links, nothing soft or hairy about her. Every forty five seconds the scene darkens, and at full dark her eyes bloom blue. Drag a hand across her and the chainmail parts and ripples, then settles back with the heavy lag of real metal.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The silhouette is a union of ten ellipses in figure space (head, shoulders, torso, arms, hips, legs, feet). A grid is walked over the figure and every cell inside the silhouette becomes a link: roughly four thousand on desktop, a little under that on phones. Each link is a single stroked loop, and neighbouring links alternate orientation by checkerboard parity, the way chain links interlock, one lying along the comb and the next across it. The comb direction is a precomputed field, hanging downward, sweeping out from the spine, radiating off the skull, with a slow sine wave laid through it so the combing reads hand-done. Lit links carry a partial rim arc in #4A5050 over the base #1F2421. There is no per-link physics: a drag injects velocity into links near the pointer path, radially outward and carried along the drag, and each link runs a stiff, loosely damped spring home, which is what gives the parting its metallic settle. The whole field breathes one to two pixels on a six second cycle. Darkness is a global overlay ramped by the forty five second cycle, and the eyes are radial gradient glows whose intensity follows the darkness, faint in daylight, full #38BDF8 at the bottom of the cycle. The clock starts mid light phase, so frame one is the combed figure with eyes faintly lit. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen, rAF typeof guarded, no text anywhere in the piece.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three, through three rounds. Round one fault: the glyph was two small ellipses per link, which at full size collapsed into a perforated dot texture, more rivet plate than chain. Round two rebuilt the glyph as one elongated loop with alternating parity orientation and separated the feet, which had merged into a single platform bar; a full resolution crop then read unmistakably as interlocking chainmail with the comb waves showing. Round three raised the link density (spacing from a 108th to a 116th of figure height) to bring the desktop count to roughly four thousand. Final frames: the hulking figure fills eighty five percent of the height, chain loops flowing down the body, two faint blue eyes in the head, generous black at the sides, the phone frame carrying the same figure full height.
