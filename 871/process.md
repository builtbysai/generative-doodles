# No.871 Paper Through the Machine

Study #106 lim-giong, PP32 "A Cursed Paper Man" (from The Assassin score: traditional paper talismans in a period world, heard through electronics).

## Concept

Three flat paper-cut figures, a person, a horse and a bird, drift slowly in blackness. They are folk-paper silhouettes: near-black bodies, thin parchment edges, and pattern holes punched clean through them (circles, diamonds, one star) with even-odd fill. At irregular intervals an electronic scan band sweeps the frame. Inside the band a figure turns translucent, its edges fray into short static strokes, and its cut-out holes bloom gold. Then the band passes and the paper restores itself. The scan never destroys anything; tradition and machine take turns occupying the same body. The tone is ritual, not fear. Watch-only by default; dragging makes the visitor the machine, tap births new paper figures, double-tap reseeds the drift. Sound starts only on the first gesture: a paper-rustle bed, a rising scan tone tied to band position, a faint gong when a bloom peaks.

## Construction

- Single self-contained index.html, canvas 2D only, no external assets. Strict palette: paper black #0C0A08, paper edge #D8CFAE, scan light #7FD8C8, bloom gold #E8A020, static fray #5A6A72. No gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas. No visible text except the title.
- Canvas size comes from the Visual Viewport API, with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; draw() pins canvas.style.width and height in px every frame. Phone detection uses window.screen (min dimension under 700px), aspect h > w * 1.15 as fallback only. Figures scale down slightly on phones and stay small in the void.
- Determinism: frame one is the synchronous draw at t = 0. All visual state is a closed-form function of seeded data, recorded pointer events, and t. No Date.now anywhere. Seeded RNG is mulberry32 throughout.
- Figure model: each figure is one non-overlapping outline polygon (hand-authored folk profiles) plus 4 to 5 hole shapes, filled with the even-odd rule so the holes punch through. Holes are also rim-stroked in parchment at low alpha, and while scanned each hole gets a gold fill (radius grown by up to 30 percent) over a faint gold halo disc. Drift is closed-form: base position plus two slow sine wander terms and a gentle sway rotation, all phases seeded per figure. Depth is carried by scale: person 1.00 (near), horse 0.78, bird 0.55.
- Scan model: the autonomous schedule is a seeded list of sweep starts per epoch, first sweep anchored at t = -1.2 s so frame one catches it mid-sweep on the person, later sweeps every 9 to 16 s (gap after each 3 s sweep). Band centre travels from -5 percent to 105 percent of width over 3 s. A figure's scan amount is a pulse over time relative to the moment the band centre crosses the figure's base x: attack smoothstep from -0.60 to -0.10 s, release smoothstep from +0.15 to +0.60 s, so the paper is whole again shortly after the band clears it. While the pointer is down, recorded drag samples override the schedule and the band centres on the pointer x; the scan amount then comes from direct band/figure overlap, and on release each figure's held amount is recorded and decays exponentially (tau 0.38 s). Fray strokes are regenerated every frame from a mulberry32 stream seeded by figure seed plus sweep index, so the jitter is stable for a given sweep and different on the next; strokes point outward along the outline normals, length and alpha scale with scan amount.
- Interaction: tap (deferred 0.30 s so a double-tap can cancel it) births a new figure at the tap point with a 0.8 s unfold (x-scale eases from a sliver, alpha ramps), cycling person, horse, bird; the roster caps at 10 and drops the oldest tap-born figure. Double-tap reseeds: new epoch seed, three fresh figures, new sweep schedule. Drag births nothing; it only holds the band.
- Audio: created lazily inside the first pointerdown, typeof-guarded and wrapped in try/catch. A looped noise buffer through a 420 Hz lowpass is the rustle bed; a sine oscillator is the scan tone, frequency 220 to 860 Hz by band x, gain following band presence and scan amount; when any figure's scan amount crosses 0.6 upward, a two-partial sine gong (196 Hz and 294.5 Hz) decays over 1.6 s. The piece renders and runs silently where AudioContext is absent.

## Deviations

- Restore timing: the prompt asks for restoration over about 1 s after the band passes. The full visible envelope here spans about 1.2 s from first contact to whole again, but the release tail after centre crossing is about 0.6 s rather than a full second. Reason: frame one is specced with the other two figures whole, and the bird's centre crossing falls at about t = -0.65 s; a 1 s tail would leave it visibly frayed in the opening frame. The cycle still reads as scan, hold, restore.
- Figure presence: the palette makes the figure fill and the void the same paper black, so unscanned figures read by their parchment edges and hole rims rather than by tonal contrast. This follows the palette literally (flat paper, flat light) and keeps the scan's translucency meaningful: inside the teal band the dropped body alpha lets the band tint show through the paper.
- Tap births are deferred 0.30 s for double-tap disambiguation (the house pattern from earlier pieces), so a new figure unfolds a beat after the tap rather than instantly.
