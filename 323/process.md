# No.323 Celestial Gingko

After Debbie Sharman's Celestial Gingko texture, this piece lays scalloped rows of gingko fans across a near black teal ground, the fans drawn in thin gold line with tiny cream flowers at their bases, like a texture mat made for the night sky. The rows are five in number, and they hold six, five, six, five and six fans from top to bottom, for exactly twenty eight fans in all. Each fan is a short stem with veins radiating up into a scalloped rim, a low teal arc tucked inside it, and exactly one tiny cream flower sits at the base of every fan, five petals around a gold heart, so the flowers are also twenty eight, counted and asserted in the code alongside the fans and the rows.

The idle motion is a twenty four second seamless loop. A slow shimmer passes along each row in sequence, travelling from left to right as a per fan brightness phase with the rows offset from one another, so a soft brightening moves through the gold line row by row. The tiny flowers pulse gently on staggered phases, three pulses inside the loop, each flower slightly out of step with its neighbours. Because every phase completes a whole number of turns inside the loop, the frame at twenty four seconds matches the frame at zero, and the first frame already opens with the shimmer mid row and the flowers at their staggered phases.

Tapping a fan starts its own small cycle and leaves the rest of the field alone. The fan sheds a drift of gold spores, short seeded filaments that float out from the rim and fade over exactly four seconds, while the fan's own strokes fade with them. The spores come from the tapped fan's fixed seed table, so the drift is the same authored drift every time that fan is tapped and nothing is chosen at random in the moment. Straight after the spores, the fan redraws itself in a two second stroke reveal, stem first, then the rim, then the veins growing one by one, and it is whole again at six seconds. A tap on a fan that is already mid cycle is ignored for that fan only, and the row shimmer never restarts. All marks are drawn directly on one canvas each frame from the seeded data.
