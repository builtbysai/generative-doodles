# The Mangled Chamber (No. 1764)

The felt driver is relief after noise: formal speech enters orderly, gets chewed apart by the machine, and the pieces settle into a quiet field that feels larger and calmer than the room they came from.

## Spec

TASKS.md prompt block PP1011, study #231 simon-scott (SS5), mechanism 9: modular-mangled live streams; Index/Kainos, Houses of Parliament recordings through modular synth into caustic noise sculpture resolving into spacious relief.

## Mechanism

Marks are particles with a four-state machine. A blue speech mark spawns at a bench every 2.4 seconds and rises with a slow sway (state: rise). Near the top of its rise it is pulled into the nearest gear of the vertical band and held there for 1.5 seconds, shrinking as it is ground down (state: capture). It then bursts into 4 to 6 jagged shards, hot rust or white, ejected rightward with chaotic velocity (state: shard). Shard speed and spread are gated by a slow sine mangling wave, so the violence swells and releases in waves. Shards damp exponentially; once slow, each becomes a pale amber mote that drifts gently and fades over 14 to 22 seconds (state: drift). The calm field is also stocked with ambient motes so the right side never empties. Three gunmetal gears rotate slowly at different rates and directions, with five modular blocks stacked in the band and a thin spine running its full height. Benches are three rows of five oak debate benches in the lower left.

## Palette

Institutional oak brown and parliamentary green ground, speech marks in formal ink blue, gears in gunmetal, shards in hot rust and white, the final calm field in pale amber.

## Periods

Mangling wave period 36 seconds. Speech spawn every 2.4 seconds. Rise about 13 seconds, capture 1.5 seconds, shard flight up to 9 seconds, mote drift 14 to 22 seconds. Gear rotation 0.16 to 0.28 radians per second. No flicker: nothing oscillates faster than the 36 second wave.

## Frame one

Fixed phase t = 60 seconds. The simulation is pre-run from t = 0 to t = 60 in fixed 1/30 second steps with seeded RNG only, so frame one is populated end to end: marks rising at left, shards mid-flight at center, motes drifting at right. First frame rendered identically at 1280x900 desktop, 390x844 mobile, and 720x720 thumb; all three inspected. Desktop uses a centered square field; phones use the full bleed. Title plate sits top left, clear of the gear band; hint plate sits bottom left.

## Interaction

Watch-only. No input. The pipeline runs continuously.

## Deviations from the prompt

None in substance. Two judgment calls: the capture hold is a fixed 1.5 seconds so a mark is visibly ground in the gears before it bursts, and the calm field carries a faint amber wash plus ambient motes so the right side reads as a field rather than empty ground. No words or letters appear as visual content anywhere in the piece; the speech marks are abstract bubbles with a tail and an inner dot.
