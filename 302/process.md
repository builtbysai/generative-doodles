# No.302 Panther Tatt-Tour

After Ali Fisher's vintage panther: a cream ceramic panther in a prowling pose, moving left to right across a flat warm grey studio ground, its whole body treated as a tattoo sleeve painted in underglaze cobalt. The head sits low at the right with a single ear, a dark eye and a small nose, the tail curves up behind the rear, and the four legs are caught mid stride.

Nine motif groups cover the body, each drawn three times with half pixel offsets in three depths of cobalt so the lines read as painted again and again for saturation. A filled heart on the chest carries the word MUM in hand drawn stroke letters. A barbed wire collar crosses the neck. A cherry pair and a pair of dice sit on the tail, swallows cross the upper flank, a mermaid and a dragon share the flank, two skulls sit at the shoulder, and tiger stripes are laid across the forward hind leg. The lettering throughout is drawn as stroke paths only, with no loaded fonts.

Below the panther stand its two companions, smaller and flanking it. The white dog at the left carries the word LOVE lettered across its flank above the front leg. The white cat at the right sits upright with a small heart on its chest holding the word MUM. Both companions are cream with black outlines, like the panther.

Motion is a slow tracking shot. Dragging horizontally dollies the camera along the panther's body, and the companions drift at a slower rate than the panther, giving a gentle parallax between the two rows. Each motif pulses once as it crosses the centre of the frame, as if the ink there were fresh. Tapping a motif redraws it in three animated passes, line, shade and saturation, at the same small offsets, and then it settles back into the sleeve. The panther never rotates and nothing ever reloads.

The palette is only the cream of the ceramic, three depths of underglaze cobalt, ink black for the outlines, and the flat warm grey of the studio ground. Frame one rests with the camera on the chest, so the MUM heart is the first motif that reads, with both companions in view below.
