# No.228 Split Infinitives

## Concept
After Kluber's 2021 Split Infinitives works: three densities of orange on bright yellow in one panel. Sixty four tight dark orange lines fill the left quarter, twenty eight looser orange stripes the centre half, sixty four tight lines the right quarter, and a deep orange vertical band, one eighth of the panel wide, bisects the centre and divides the density zones. The counts are exact, not scatter. The painting never moves. What moves is light: a single additive wash of white gold drifts left and right across the paint, its position driven by a forty seven second clock and its opacity by a seventy one second clock. Two incommensurable periods mean the composite of paint and light never repeats within a viewing session, which is Kluber's encoded-variables idea in its purest form.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The painted layer is static layout data drawn flat every frame: three sections of exact-count horizontal lines (64, 28, 64), the side sections in dark orange at forty six percent slot fill, the centre in orange at fifty five percent, the deep orange interrupt band drawn over the centre. No gradients exist anywhere in the paint. The wash is the only gradient in the piece: a soft-edged vertical bar about a sixth of the panel wide, drawn with globalCompositeOperation 'lighter' at a peak alpha of 0.25, its centre following a sine on the 47s period (the sine gives the long ease at each reversal) and its alpha modulated on the 71s period. The panel is 2:1, centred, and the page ground takes the painting's own yellow. Frame one opens with the wash partway across, visibly lightening the centre-right stripes. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The three densities read instantly, the interrupt band lands dead centre, and the wash is present but quiet, a brightening over the right half of the centre section rather than a special effect, which is the balance the work wants. On the phone frame the panel holds its 2:1 shape on the yellow ground and the line densities stay distinct even at 390 wide. No adjustments were needed after the first render.
