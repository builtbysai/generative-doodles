# No.1040 "Light, Ferried"

Study #128 kyohei-mouri, PP201 "The Moving Track" (Coded Field 2019: omni-wheel cart robots carrying lighting equipment, fully autonomous, routed around a courtyard temple).

## Concept

The show as logistics: light that arrives. A dark venue in top-down plan. Twelve carts, dark square bases with glowing domes (half warm, half cool), each driving its own seeded waypoint circuit around a small temple drawn in dark lines at the center. The carts are vehicles, not characters: no faces, no rotation, just translation. Each leaves a fading trail, and the trails are the piece's real subject, a glowing route-map of the venue woven by the fleet's dispatch. The temple is routed around with respect; nothing crosses it.

## Construction

- **Circuits.** Four concentric rectangular rings around the temple (seeded half-extents near 150x115, 250x195, 350x275, 450x360 logical units in a 1000x1000 plan), three carts per ring. Each ring is an 8-waypoint axis-aligned circuit (corners plus edge midpoints). A cart travels every segment at one constant speed, stops hard for a seeded dwell (0.5 to 1.1 s) at each waypoint, and never rotates: omni-wheel translation only, hard direction changes at waypoints.
- **Closed-form time.** Each ring's timeline (8 travel legs plus 8 dwells) is normalized to exactly 60.000000000 s, so cart position is a pure function of loop phase. Trails are sampled from the same function at past phases (a position buffer of 17 samples over 6 s, per-segment alpha fading from 0.25), never a canvas fade. Frame one is the synchronous draw at the fixed phase t = 20 s; the live loop is anchored to that phase plus elapsed time from the rAF timestamp. Nothing is derived from a wall clock.
- **Fleet math isolation.** The circuit builder and position function live in a delimited pure block in the script (no DOM references), so the QA probe below evaluated the shipped code itself rather than a copy.
- **Interaction, as recorded events.** Drag lays roadblock samples: carts within 170 units are pushed off their routes with a strength that decays over 2.2 s, then rejoin their circuits; because the offset is a function of the recorded event list, trails detour coherently too. Tap a cart (within 60 units) flashes its dome for about a second, with a small white core at the peak. Double-tap reseeds the fleet (LCG-advanced seed): new ring extents, directions, dwells and phase offsets. No click-to-reload anywhere.
- **Sound.** Lazy, typeof-guarded WebAudio created only inside the first pointer gesture: filtered-noise night air at low gain, two sine hums (warm group near 82 Hz, cool near 124 Hz) pitched and gained by each group's mean cart speed, and gentle two-partial chimes when a pair of carts newly enters 120 units of each other (throttled to one per 0.25 s). Fully silent in the QA renderer.
- **House rules.** Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; canvas style width and height pinned in px inside draw() every frame; phone detection via window.screen minimum dimension under 700 px with the aspect ratio as fallback only; on phones the cart glyphs scale up 1.3x while the plan itself stays whole and centered. Strict specced palette only. Dome glow is layered flat-alpha discs: no gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas. Zero visible text besides the title.

## QA log

- **Non-collision verification (numeric, on the shipped fleet math, seed 1040201).** Ring timelines each total exactly 60 s; maximum loop-closure error over the cycle is 9.7e-13 units (float noise). Sampling all 66 cart pairs at dt = 0.02 s across the full 60 s loop (3,000 instants), the minimum pairwise center distance is **71.27 logical units** (carts 4 and 7, rings 1 and 2, at t = 1.94 s). Two 30-unit square bases could only touch at 42.4 units corner-to-corner, so the fleet keeps about 29 units of true clearance at its worst instant. Same-ring carts share one timeline at 20 s phase offsets, so their separation is structural; cross-ring separation comes from the ring spacing (at least 60 units between adjacent lanes after jitter). Innermost ring clears the temple by 60.6 units or more; outermost reach is 458.3 of the 500-unit half-plan.
- **Determinism.** thumb.png rendered twice with render-piece.js and byte-compared with cmp: identical. The duplicate render was deleted.
- **Renders read back.** Desktop (1280x900): dark plan, temple legible at center as nested rectangles with a roof cross, 12 carts spread across all four rings (6 warm domes, 6 cool), axis-aligned warm and cool trails trailing each cart, faint waypoint dots on the circuits. Thumb (720x720): same composition, bases reading as subtle dark squares under the dome glows. Mobile (390x844, phone path via screen size): whole plan fits with margins, all 12 carts and trails legible, nothing clipped.
- **Static checks.** Grep-verified clean: no Date.now, no document.createElement, no canvas gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas, no getUserMedia, no external URLs, no non-ASCII characters (no em dashes). AudioContext is touched only inside the gesture path and the guarded update loop.

## Deviations

- **Trails start one step behind the cart.** The newest trail sample is taken at age 0.35 s rather than age 0, so the trail reads as a wake behind the base instead of a line drawn through the dome. The 6 s span and 0.25 peak alpha are as specced.
- **Tap flash adds a small white core.** At full flash the dome gains a tiny #FFFFFF center (the palette's waypoint white) so the flash reads at thumbnail size; the dome color itself only grows and brightens within its own hue.
- **Direction is seeded per ring, not per cart.** All three carts on a ring run the same direction on the same timeline. Opposite-direction carts on one ring would pass head-on in the same lane; the prompt's queueing and crossing happens across rings instead, which is also what keeps the non-collision proof simple and exact.
