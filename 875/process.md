# No.875 ROOTS

Study #107 xu-jared, PP36 "The Coupling" (the ROOTS duo with Vir Torres: butoh and flamenco meeting a no-input mixing board). Two fields share one square and neither leads: the BODY (warm, grounded, driven by the visitor's drag) and the MACHINE (cool, autonomous, never silent). The piece is the coupling between them, not either side.

## Concept

- BODY field: 300 mass-spring particles in ember, home-anchored as a heavy mass in the lower two-thirds. Slow drags are butoh: the mass leans, lags, and drifts with deep inertia. Fast drags are flamenco: they fire sharp red STRIKES where they pass, percussive flashes that shove the mass outward.
- MACHINE field: 120 phosphor-green filaments streaming over the whole frame on a divergence-free flow field, depositing energy into a coarse 26x26 grid as they pass and re-reading that energy for their own speed and brightness. The field literally feeds on itself, the visual form of the no-input board. In its quiet state it dims to deep green; it never stops, even in the body's stillness.
- THE COUPLING (automatic): the machine's smoothed amplitude (mLevel, from grid activity plus strike energy) scales the body's brightness, so the body sharpens when the machine flares. The body's strike envelope (strikeEnv) sets the machine's gain (gM), so hard flamenco strikes make the machine denser, faster, and brighter. Machine filaments also bend tangentially around the body's live centroid, parting around the mass.
- Interactions: DRAG is the body (speed-classified slow/fast). TAP is a flamenco heel-drop: a strike plus a green ripple the machine recirculates twice at decaying amplitude, with a local energy bloom at the tap point. DOUBLE-TAP releases the body to corpse-state (heavy damping, strong home pull, ember dimmed to 0.42) while the machine plays on alone; any real drag wakes the body. Taps are deferred 0.30 s in sim time so a double-tap can cancel the heel-drop before it fires.

## Construction

- Single self-contained index.html, canvas 2D, no external assets. Both fields run in one deterministic fixed-timestep simulation (DT = 1/120 s, normalized coordinates) with all randomness drawn at init from a seeded mulberry32; no RNG calls occur inside sim steps.
- Frame one: the sim is pre-rolled 10 s of fixed steps at load, so the first synchronous draw shows the developed coupling: filaments flowing frame-wide, body settled, machine at mid energy. One strike is scheduled at PRE_T - 0.9 s (e = 0.9) inside the mass; its impulse lands during the pre-roll and frame one carries the faint red afterglow ring plus the soft dent it left in the mass. Frame one is never derived from Date.now(); the live loop only advances the sim by elapsed real time from the pre-rolled state.
- Flow field: headings come from a three-mode stream function (velocity = perpendicular gradient of psi), which is divergence-free by construction. An earlier sin-sum heading field had sources and sinks: filaments braided into a few accumulation streams on the left and evacuated the right half. The stream function fixed the coverage; a 0.12 rad per-filament phase whisper keeps neighbours out of lockstep. Filament speed scales with the energy it reads from its grid cell and with gM; deposits are 0.34 x gM per second with a 5.5 s cell decay.
- Body: home springs (k = 2.2, 5.0 in corpse-state), two zero-rest-length neighbour links per particle (a cohesion web), analytic ambient drift (butoh breathing), drag force with speed-dependent damping (2.6 slow, 0.9 fast, 1.6 idle, 3.4 corpse). Strikes apply a radial impulse (r = 0.12, strength 0.55 x e) and feed strikeEnv (tau 1.1 s decay).
- Audio (lazy, first pointer gesture only, typeof-guarded, all best-effort): one AudioContext and one graph. Machine chain: looped noise buffer through a bandpass into a 0.27 s delay with a feedback gain (the self-feeding loop), wet gain to a compressor-capped master. Body chain: 54 Hz sine + 81.4 Hz triangle through a lowpass into a low drone gain. A coupling bus at 10 Hz reads the same sim values the visuals use: mLevel drives the body filter cutoff (140 + 760 x mLevel Hz), strikeEnv drives the delay feedback (0.34 + 0.24 x strikeEnv) and wet gain, and corpse-state drops the drone gain. Strikes fire a short bandpassed noise burst (150 + 320 x e Hz). Visuals never read any audio node; there is no AnalyserNode dependency.
- Palette strict, five tones only: black #0A0A0A, ember #B33A1E, strike red #FF2200, machine green #33FF66, machine quiet #0E3A1E. All blending is alpha over black; no gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas.
- House rules honoured: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; canvas pinned in px inside draw() every frame; phone detection via window.screen physical size (min dimension < 700) with aspect h > w*1.15 as fallback only; rAF typeof-guarded; no microphone; no visible text except the title "ROOTS".

## QA log

- Renderer: node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js (node-canvas; no rAF, no AudioContext, so the piece rendered complete and silent from its synchronous frame one).
- Renders: qa-desktop.png (1280x900), qa-mobile.png (390x844, phone path), thumb.png (720x720). All read back: green filaments distributed across the whole frame and parting around the body, dim ember mass grounded in the lower two-thirds, one faint red strike ring fading inside the mass, a bright machine flare where filaments bend at the mass's edge. Mobile holds both zones with nothing clipped.
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and cmp byte-compared identical, re-verified after every fix round; the duplicate render was deleted each time.
- Fix rounds during QA: (1) first read-back showed the strike afterglow as a large dark filled disc reading like a planet; the filled halo was removed, the ring shrunk and thinned. (2) Filaments braided into thick streams on the left (sin-sum field sinks) and the strike blew a crater in the mass; deposit and impulse were softened, a per-filament field phase added, and the body homes tightened. (3) The heading field was replaced with the divergence-free stream function and the seeded strike moved earlier (PRE_T - 0.9 s) so frame one shows a settled dent and a faint ring rather than a mid-explosion crater; a second concentric afterglow ring was dropped so the mark stays a single fading ring.

## Deviations

- Flare shimmer: the brief's "white shimmer tips" are rendered as enlarged full-alpha phosphor-green head dots when gM > 1.5. The five-tone palette is strict and contains no white; adding one would break the palette rule every sibling piece follows. The swell still reads as the tip flaring.
- The coupling bus updates audio parameters on a 100 ms interval from the sim state rather than per-sample: the values it carries (machine level, strike envelope) are slow envelopes, and the visuals and audio read the identical state variables, so the two never disagree about the coupling.
- Audio strike bursts use a fixed playback rate (0.6): no Math.random anywhere in the file, keeping the whole piece reproducible even in its sound path.
