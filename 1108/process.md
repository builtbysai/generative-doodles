# №1108 — Machine Dreams

Daniel Franke study block DF5 (GAN CHIMERA, 2021–2023). Dark mineral masses webbed with bioluminescent crystal: the machine dreams a new hybrid every ~30 seconds, and the artist's only hand is the curator's — three specimen frames along the bottom hold the machine's recent dreams, and tapping one promotes it to the main view. Title per the block's Title suggestion ("Machine Dreams", over the block heading "The Chimera Dream").

## How it is built

- **One generator, every view:** a single parameterized `drawChimera(spec, rect, t)` renders the main dream and each specimen from a seed-derived spec, so a specimen is a real seeded render of the same family — never a thumbnail of nothing, and promotion is just a seed swap. No offscreen canvas anywhere; specimens are drawn live each frame and clipped to their frames.
- **The chimera:** 2–3 domain-warped blob masses per dream. Each mass contour is a harmonic-perturbed radius (3 seeded sine harmonics with slow phase drift, plus an angular domain warp and a gentle breathing term), drawn as three layered flat shapes in the two stone tones (#2A241E / #3E362C) — painted, not photoreal. Filament veins are short bright segments riding the contours at 93% radius, teal #1E7A6E with occasional cyan #4DD8C8. Crystal clusters (6–9 per dream) sit on the masses: honest polygon shards (3–6 per cluster) with inner facet lines, a soft halo disc and a bright core, all in the cyan/teal pair with alpha driven by a slow per-cluster pulse sine — the breathing.
- **Dream cycle:** dream seeds come from an integer hash sequence (`seedAt(k)`), so the machine's dreams are inexhaustible and reproducible. Every 30 s of piece time the current dream dissolves (3.5 s smoothstep crossfade of the two specs at complementary alpha) into the next; the outgoing dream rotates into the specimen strip as the newest specimen, dropping the oldest. Frame one is at fixed phase t = 12 s: dream №0 fully formed mid-breath, specimens holding dreams −1, −2, −3.
- **Curation:** tap a specimen frame to promote it — the same dissolve hands the main view over (2.5 s), the old main dream takes the vacated specimen slot, and the 30 s dream clock restarts so the promoted dream gets its full showing. Double-tap the main dream and the machine dreams three brand-new specimens (fresh hash branch, 1 s fade-in each).
- **Stirring:** drag across the main chimera records stir points; crystal clusters near a fresh stir point have their shard angles and positions offset by a seeded per-cluster vector scaled by a Gaussian proximity × exponential freshness weight (12 s memory), so the growth visibly re-seeds where the hand passed and settles back. The machine still generates — the visitor only stirs.
- **Sound (specced, house-gated):** a deep mineral drone (54 Hz sine + 81.3 Hz triangle through a 240 Hz lowpass, low gain) plus glassy sine pings whose rate follows the current dream's crystal coverage. The WebAudio layer starts only lazily inside the first pointer gesture, is fully typeof/try-guarded, never autoplays, and the visuals are complete without it.
- **House rules:** canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; canvas CSS size pinned in draw(); phone detection via `window.screen` min dimension < 700 with the aspect fallback; square stage centered, full-bleed deep shadow #0B0906 including the page ground. Determinism: frame one is drawn synchronously at the fixed phase; the typeof-guarded rAF loop is anchored phase + elapsed, so the first frame is reproducible. No canvas gradients, no ctx.filter, no shadowBlur, no createElement, no microphone. No visible text anywhere except the `<title>` — the specimen frames are lines only, per the prompt.
