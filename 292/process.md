# No.292 Kawaii Vessels Grid

## Concept
After Ruby Waters' Kawaii Vessels print: a 4x3 flash sheet of creature-vessels, planters, pots, a cat-lamp and an octopus-pot, blinking and swaying in slow cascade, each cell alive on its own phase. Watch-only. Exactly 12 cells in 4 columns by 3 rows, asserted in code. Bold black outlines and flat fills on cream paper, thin vermilion rules between cells, generous margins. No text in the piece.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, centred fit so the sheet keeps its shape, page ground in the piece cream. Palette held to the specced six colours only: cream paper #F7EFE0, vermilion #D23A2A, cobalt #2B5EA7, black #1A1A1A, mustard #E0A437, mint #7FD1B9. The twelve curated vessels are, in reading order: daruma-planter with green fronds growing from its head, tiger-pot with striped body, blue cat-vessel holding three paintbrushes, striped cat-lamp with a glowing candle inside, flower vase with vermilion blooms, spotted dog, gourd-face lady, chrysanthemum pot, teapot with scarf, candle-shrine, octopus-pot, and cat-mug. Each vessel is a painter function taking centre and cell index, using a shared face system with only three vocabularies: googly eyes, closed moon eyes, and hannya-slit eyes, plus vermilion blush and a small mouth. Motion is a 24 second watch-only loop, staggered per cell: plant fronds and blooms sway on a sine phase per cell, candle flames in the cat-lamp and candle-shrine flicker as two to three stacked translucent ellipses with seeded sine offsets and no shadowBlur or filter, eyes blink in a slow cascade where eye height is scaled by a smoothstep around each cell's own phase, and the octopus tentacle fronds drift on their own sines. Frame one opens at a 7.4 second loop offset so several cells are already mid-blink and mid-sway and the sheet reads inhabited at once. Each cell is clipped to its rectangle when drawn, so sway never crosses the vermilion rules. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen min dimension under 700 with aspect fallback, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds where noise is used, no offscreen canvas, no shadowBlur, no gradients, no text, no em dashes.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported both resize listeners, and a grep confirms exactly the six specced palette colours, no em dashes, and no shadowBlur, offscreen canvas or text drawing. First pass fixes: the chrysanthemum bloom was enlarged and lowered onto its pot, the tiger muzzle block was shrunk, the dog ears were moved onto the head, and the cat-lamp flame was scaled up, then all three sizes were re-rendered and re-inspected. Final frame one reads as specced: a clean 4x3 flash sheet on cream with thin vermilion rules, all twelve vessels distinct and legible (daruma-planter, tiger-pot, brush cat, striped cat-lamp with its mustard candle window, bloom vase, spotted dog, gourd lady, chrysanthemum pot, scarf teapot, candle-shrine, octopus-pot, cat-mug), faces in the three specced vocabularies, several cells already mid-blink or mid-sway from the loop offset. Nothing clips at any size; on the phone the centred fit sets the whole sheet in the middle of the tall frame on the cream ground and every cell stays legible.
