# No.246 The Collapse

## Concept
After Bawa's A Pink Scaffold: The Collapse, 2020, the storm ruined scaffold she retitled a different piece altogether, with resilience and resurrection in its bones. A pink scaffold lies fallen in a white salt desert, poles splayed, one corner still upright. Over the loop it puts itself back together, stands whole for a breath, then collapses again and rests. No debris, no drama. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1200 by 800 scene: pale sky, a flat haze band straddling the horizon at forty percent, white salt below. The scaffold is four bays by three levels of articulated segments, five columns of three poles each, hinged at their base nodes; node positions are computed cumulatively along each column from its segment angles, and a fallen pole that would dip below the salt is rested on it. Fallen angles are a seeded table: the left bay barely falls, keeping one corner upright even in ruin, while the right columns splay progressively, the top segment of the fourth column folding back against the rest. A state machine runs the forty five second loop: fallen for eight seconds, building for twenty, standing for five, collapsing for three, rest for nine. Building is staggered column by column and bottom segment first, each pole easing to vertical through an easeOutBack curve whose overshoot is the wobble. Collapse runs the other way on a gravity ease-in with a per pole delay stagger. Colour follows standingness: each pole lerps from the pale fallen pink to the full standing pink by how upright it is, so the structure visibly deepens as it rises. No particles, no debris, nothing but poles, rails, diagonals and coupler dots. The opening clock starts at twenty three seconds, late in the rebuild, most bays standing and full pink while the last bay is still down and rising. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, shaped scene centred on the pale sky ground, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. First pass sprawled too far: the fallen right column reached the very edge of the frame and clipped. The fallen angle table was eased, the bays narrowed slightly and the whole structure shifted left; re-rendered, the sprawl now stays inside the salt with margin. Frame one reads as the resurrection mid act: three bays standing in the full pink with the top rail just above the horizon line, the fourth bay still trailing on the ground in the pale fallen pink, its last poles partway up. The phone frame centres the scene on the sky ground and the split between standing and fallen stays legible at 390 wide.
