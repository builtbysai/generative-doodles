# No.297 Okame Mask Field

## Concept
After Ruby Waters' okame masks: a field of eleven round moon faces drifting at three depths over faint giant chrysanthemums. Each mask is a dinner plate sized fortune maiden face, cream against a black bob cut, with high vermilion blush on both cheeks and a tiny vermilion smiling mouth. The ground is deep and warm, the chrysanthemum petals behind the masks are a low contrast vermilion, and red carries the piece. Nothing else enters the frame. The piece is watch-only: the masks bob and drift, near masks tilt slightly, the background turns a little and returns, and every eight seconds one mask closes both eyes in a slow blink.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, centred fit with the page ground in the piece ground colour. The palette is held to exactly five colours: deep warm ground, face cream, hair black, blush and lip vermilion, and faint chrysanthemum vermilion. Exactly 11 masks are built and asserted in code, split 4 near, 4 mid and 3 far, and the layer counts are asserted as well. Base positions are curated so the field reads scattered at load; per mask phase, drift amplitude and bob cycle come from a fixed seed. Depth sets scale, alpha and drift speed: far masks are smaller, softer and slower, near masks are larger, fully opaque and quicker. All bob and drift cycles are whole numbers per 32 second loop, so the loop is seamless. The chrysanthemum ground is three giant petal rings drawn as teardrop petal paths in the faint colour, rotated as one group by three degrees and back on a sine across the loop. Each mask is drawn by one reusable function: a black bob silhouette with side panels, a cream face circle, a black fringe cap, vermilion blush ellipses, small brow arcs, black oval eyes, and a small vermilion lip shape cut by a cream highlight. Blinking is a deterministic seeded rotation: a seeded shuffle fixes a blink order once, the four eight second beats in each loop blink the first four masks in that order in turn, and a blink lasts 300ms shaped as a sine pulse that morphs the eye height down to a thin line and back. No runtime randomness, so every play is identical. Frame one opens at T equals 23.86 seconds, so the masks are already mid-drift at varied phases and the next blink is 0.14 seconds away. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds, no text, no UI chrome, no audio, no offscreen canvas, no shadowBlur or filters, no em dashes.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The renderer reported both resize listeners at every size, and a grep confirms exactly the five specced palette colours, no em dashes, and no shadowBlur or offscreen canvas. One fix round: the first pass fringe cap rendered as a grey dome with a malformed edge, the side hair panels read as detached ovals, and the flower centres showed spiky gaps between petal bases. The fringe was rebuilt as a black ellipse clipped to the face circle, the side locks moved out onto the bob edge, and the petals widened with more petals per ring and a larger centre disc. Final frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: eleven cream moon faces with solid black bobs, high vermilion blush and tiny mouths, in three clear depth layers (small soft far masks at the top, mid masks across the middle, four large near masks low in the frame, one partly tucked behind its neighbour), over full faint chrysanthemum rings on the deep warm ground. On the phone the centred fit holds the whole field in frame with the ground colour filling the rest of the page, and every mask stays legible.
