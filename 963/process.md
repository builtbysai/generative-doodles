# No.963 LAUREN

Study #118 lauren-lee-mccarthy, PP124. The LAUREN mechanism: role-reversal embodiment, a human performing the smart home. Built and QA-rendered only; not pushed.

## Concept

A top-down floor-plan schematic on near-black. An attention-eye tours the plan room by room, and the house anticipates her: before she enters a room, its devices have already pre-empted. Lights warm to amber, door arcs swing open, speaker dots pulse magenta. A fading mint trail records where attention has been, the house's memory of being watched. The eye never reacts late and the devices never react late either; being early is the whole mechanism. The only warmth in the piece is anticipation: standby devices are cold blue-gray, and color exists only where the eye is, where she is about to be, or where she just was.

## Construction

- Plan: seeded BSP partition of a centered square (dark margin) into 6 to 8 rooms, walls stroked in #3A4050 at 2 px. Every shared wall segment gets a door gap, so the plan is fully connected; door positions are seeded along each shared segment. Page ground and pupil are #0B0C10.
- Devices: 3 to 4 per room, always one light (small rect) and one speaker (small dot), one door arc device where the room has a door (always), plus a seeded extra light or speaker. Standby tone #2A2E3A.
- The eye: mint #7DF9C6 circle with an outer ring and a dark pupil offset toward its heading. It moves by steering (max turn rate, speed eased near waypoints) at 55 px/s at gallery scale through a waypoint route: door midpoints along a BFS path through the room adjacency graph, ending at the destination room's center. Destinations prefer unvisited rooms, nearest by BFS distance with seeded jitter; the tour is perpetual.
- Anticipation model: each room carries a warmth value in [0,1]. In every fixed step, the warm targets are recomputed as: the room the eye is in; the NEXT room on her current route (path step after the current room), so its devices begin warming the moment the room ahead becomes the destination after next on her queue, well before she crosses its door; any room she left less than 4 s ago (the cooling tail); and any tapped room (extra lead time). Warmth rises or falls at a fixed rate, a full swing in 0.8 s. Lights lerp standby to #FFC44F with a flat alpha halo rect behind them at warmth; door leaves rotate open by up to about 75 degrees with their swing arc shown while warm; speakers lerp to #FF4FD8 and pulse (radius and ring driven by simulation time) only while warm. Frame one checks out against this model exactly: at t = 25 s the eye's room is warm, the destination room below her is fully warm with light, door arc and two pulsing speakers, and the room she left 3 s earlier is still warm inside its cooling window.
- Trail and heat: eye positions recorded at 60 Hz into a trail polyline whose alpha fades over 60 s. The same positions accumulate into a coarse attention grid (cells about 28 px at gallery size). Holding a press fades in a mint heat-map overlay from that grid ("see through walls"); releasing fades it out.
- House cycle: every 300 s of simulation time the house reseeds (new plan, devices and trail cleared) behind a 2 s dim-out.
- Simulation: fixed timestep at 120 Hz. Frame one is the synchronous draw after exactly 3000 pre-roll steps (25 s in, mid-tour, trail developed). All state derives from the seed (mulberry32) and fixed steps; no wall clock touches the drawing. The animation loop advances the same fixed steps from rAF elapsed time, capped per frame; rAF is typeof-guarded.
- Interaction: tap a room and the eye detours there immediately, that room becomes the destination and warms at once, and a doorbell two-tone answers. A quick double-tap reseeds a new home. Hold-drag shows the heat overlay. Tap detection uses distance and hold duration, not click-to-reload; nothing reloads.
- Sound: entirely lazy and guarded. AudioContext is created on the first pointer press only, inside try/catch, and every sound call no-ops without it. Room tone is a looped noise buffer through a lowpass filter at very low gain. Each pre-emption (a room's warmth leaving zero) plays a relay click plus a warm 110 Hz hum. A tap request plays the doorbell. QA renders in silence.
- House rules: single self-contained file, canvas 2D only, no external assets, no absolute URLs, no date strings, no em dashes. Only text is the title element. Canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; style width and height pinned in px on every draw. Phone detection via window.screen minimum dimension under 700 px with the aspect fallback; the plan scales to the frame and the page ground stays the ground tone on phones. No gradients, no shadowBlur, no canvas filter, no offscreen canvas, no microphone.

## Deviations

- Anticipation trigger: the brief phrases the trigger both as "about 1 s of travel away" and as "when the NEXT room in its queue becomes current-minus-one." I implemented the queue version: the next room starts warming as soon as it becomes the room after the current one on the route, which at room scale gives roughly 2 to 5 s of lead rather than exactly 1 s. Reason: it is the brief's own defining parenthetical, and it guarantees the frame-one composition (a fully warm room ahead of the eye) at any point in the tour, not only in the last second before a crossing. Being early is the mechanism; this errs on the side of earlier.
- Door arcs: the brief describes door devices as an arc segment at a door gap. I draw the door as a hinged leaf line that rotates open with warmth, plus its swing arc while warm, rather than a static arc glyph. Reason: the swing is what reads as anticipation in motion, and the arc trace of the swing is still drawn.
- Every shared wall has a door gap (rather than a seeded subset). Reason: it guarantees the BSP plan is fully connected for BFS routing with no unreachable rooms, and schematic plans with a door per shared wall still read correctly with clear gaps.
- Speaker pulse radius floors at about 1.2 px when drawn, so standby and warm dots stay visible at phone scale. Rendering floor only; the palette and pulse behavior are as specced.
