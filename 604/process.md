# No.604 "Un Jardín"

EXPANSION #73 paz-tamburrini, prompt block 4 "Los Hugos". Her 2020 creature world rebuilt as a living papercut ecosystem: a layered cut-paper garden on a plain paper ground, the black elephant and its pale-blue companion standing as anchors while small Hugos creatures wander the layers in stepped stopmotion.

## Concept

Three paper layers (back, mid, front) of cut ferns, bulbous perforated seed pods and curling stems in sage, peach and gray rise from the bottom of a fibrous paper sheet. Center-left, the anchor pair: a big ink-black elephant and a smaller pale-blue companion, one cut shape at two scales, with flat translucent contact shadows under everything. Five Hugos creatures (blob bodies, dot eyes, a line mouth; variants with an orange beak or a single antenna) wander seeded waypoint loops in the low cluster. Nothing in the piece moves smoothly: every animated value is a function of an integer step count, so the garden lives in held frames with a slight positional jitter, her native stopmotion grammar. Tapping empty paper cuts a new creature, which pops in at the sheet's edge and wanders in; the garden only grows (cap 12). Dragging rustles the nearest plant layer, which shivers in stepped frames and settles.

## Construction

- Single self-contained canvas piece, 1000x700 stage letterboxed on the paper ground (#EFE9DC); strict eight-color palette: paper #EFE9DC, sage #A8B89A, peach #E8C4A8, gray #B8B4AA, ink #1A1A1A, pale blue #A8C4D4, navy #2B4A6F, soft orange #E09A5A (pod dots and one creature beak type only).
- Stepped-motion implementation: STEP = 0.24 s. `step = floor(t / STEP)` and everything derives from that integer. Plant sway reads a triangle table TRI = [0, .5, 1, .5, 0, -.5, -1, -.5] indexed by (floor(step/hold) + phase) % 8, with per-plant hold (1 or 2 steps per tick) and phase restricted to {0, 4}; since TRI[0] = TRI[4] = 0, every plant sits at rest angle 0 on frame one while the garden still sways out of sync. Creature position is a pure function of step: distance = age x hop along a closed waypoint loop, precomputed segment lengths, so a creature only changes position on step boundaries; a two-step bob and a six-step jitter table (JIT[0] = 0) add the stopmotion tremble. Pop-in scale for a tapped-in creature quantizes 0.35 -> 0.7 -> 1 over its first two steps. No tweening or easing anywhere; drag rustle adds a fixed decaying degree table (RUST) indexed by steps since the rustle.
- Elephant rule: creatures wander in three zones whose waypoint loops and straight hops can never cross the elephant zone (x 195-565, y 400-605): A left strip (x 40-175), B right field (x 590-955), C front strip (y 620-652, below the elephants' feet). Zone y-bands start at 530-545 so creatures stay grounded among the plants instead of floating at elephant height.
- Geometry: all shapes are precomputed at load from mulberry32 streams (build seed 604073; a separate interaction stream, 604999, advances only on taps, so frame one never depends on it). Ferns are cubic-sampled stems with paired leaflets; pods are jittered blob bulbs on curved stems, perforations punched as paper-colored holes; curls are spiral polylines with a blob tip. Contact shadows are flat ink ellipses at 5-9% alpha. Paper grain is 320 seeded speckles plus 70 short fiber strokes drawn directly (no offscreen canvas, no filters).
- Interaction: tap (short press, under 7 units of travel) on empty paper — not on the elephants, a creature, or a plant base — cuts a new creature in the tapped region's zone, entering from the nearest sheet edge; after 12 creatures taps do nothing. Drag movement rustles the nearest plant within range (retrigger guarded per plant). Never click-to-reload; creatures never leave and are never erased.
- House rules: canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; canvas.style.width/height pinned in px inside draw(); phone detection via window.screen (min dimension < 700px) with the h > w*1.15 aspect fallback (stage letterboxes on phones, cluster keeps clear of the edges, ground stays the paper tone); a synchronous fully composed first frame at PHASE0 = 0 (elephants anchored, five creatures at their loop starts, plants at rest angles); rAF typeof-guarded and anchored as PHASE0 + elapsed-since-load; stepped time always quantizes the anchored elapsed via floor, never a wall-clock phase; no Date.now() anywhere; no audio, no microphone.

- Interaction verified at code level (the QA renderer fires no pointer events): tap-to-cut spawning with edge entry and the 12-creature cap, zone assignment by tap region, and drag rustle targeting are state-driven from the anchored step count.

## Deviations

- None from the prompt block. Construction choices recorded: the waypoint-zone system is how "no creature ever fully occludes the elephant pair" is guaranteed structurally rather than checked per frame; spawn entry points are the left/right sheet edges at ground level (the prompt says creatures "wander in from the edge" without fixing which edge, so the edge follows the tap's region); creature cap is the prompt's ~12, made exact.
