# No.889 "Chasing" (study #109 klsr.av, PP50 "Cross-Flare")

## Concept

Klsr's "Chasing" (2026-09-27) and "See you soon" (2026-08-22): a cross-shaped lens flare floating on near-black, a horizontal light blade crossed by a vertical beam, meeting at a hot center that breathes. The piece is the flare alone, with no particles, no stars, and no scenery: soft falloffs, a slight chromatic bleed at the blade edges, and a slow brightness breath, so it reads as a photographed lens artifact rather than a drawn logo cross. Two variants exist, GOLD (white-gold blade, violet-blue beam, after "Chasing") and BLUE (both blades blue, after "See you soon"), and only one is ever shown at a time. The tiny variant word at the bottom edge (GOLD / BLUE) is the prompt-specced readout and the only visible text besides the title.

Interaction: drag steers the flare center through the dark and drag speed stretches or contracts the blades; release lets it drift home to center. Tap strikes the center into a brief white flare. Double-tap switches the variant. Sound, started only by the first gesture, is a low airy drone whose low-band gain is the same center-brightness value the drawing uses, plus a soft high ping on each strike.

## Construction

- Single self-contained index.html, canvas 2D only, no external assets. Size comes from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; draw() pins canvas.style.width/height in px every frame. Phone detection uses window.screen physical size (min dimension under 700px), with the aspect ratio h > w*1.15 as fallback only.
- Flare model: each blade is drawn as 42 stacked half-ellipse steps from a wide faint skirt (alpha about 0.0035) to a narrow near-solid core (alpha about 0.065 per step, accumulating toward saturation), with length and thickness following smooth power curves of the step parameter. This approximates a gaussian falloff with flat alpha shapes only: no gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas. The center is the same idea in discs: 20 halo-tinted steps and 36 white steps whose alpha reaches near zero at the outer radius, under a small solid core, so no ring line forms. A four-step nested halo ellipse sits behind the whole cross.
- Chromatic bleed: eight mid-profile steps of each blade are redrawn offset 2.2px to both sides in the other blade's color at alpha 0.016, a faint fringe rather than a fill.
- Seeded asymmetry: mulberry32 (seed 889050) fixes a small left/right imbalance for the blade (about 3.5 percent) and top/bottom for the beam (about 2.5 percent), drawn as paired half-ellipses, so the flare feels photographed rather than mirrored.
- Breath: brightness B(t) = 0.82 + 0.18 sin(2 pi t / 8.5) and a coupled size factor 1 + 0.045 sin(2 pi t / 8.5 - 0.8). At t = 0 the breath sits at 0.82 and rising, mid-breath as specced. Blade brightness is 0.55 + 0.45 B, so the blades never fully vanish at the breath's low point.
- Closed-form state (determinism): center position and blade-length factor are "tracks": constants plus anchor lists {t0, from, to, tau}, evaluated analytically as exponential approaches. Drag moves push anchors with short taus (0.07 to 0.16 s, throttled to one per 50 ms); release pushes anchors back to center and neutral length with tau about 1 s (home in roughly 3 s). Strikes and variant toggles are timestamped event lists; the flash envelope is a sum of exp(-5.5 dt) decays and the variant at time t is the parity of toggles up to t. Frame one is the synchronous draw(0): no events, tracks at their constants, breath at phase zero. Date.now is never used anywhere; the live clock is performance.now and only advances the loop, never the first frame.
- Interaction details: a press that moves past a small threshold (or is held 0.15 s) becomes a drag; drag speed is measured in frame-widths per second and maps to a length target of 0.30 (still, contracted toward a point) to 1.20 (fast, stretched wide). A short still tap is deferred 0.30 s so a second tap inside 0.38 s converts it into a variant toggle and cancels the strike; a confirmed strike fires its flash and its ping at the deferred time so picture and sound stay in step.
- Audio: lazily created on the first pointerdown, typeof-guarded throughout. Two detuned sines (52 Hz, 52 x 1.007) and a quiet 104.5 Hz triangle feed a lowpass into a gain node under a compressor. Each frame the drone gain and filter cutoff are set from lastCenterBrightness, the exact value the draw pass computed (breath plus flash): what blooms is what sounds. The strike ping is a 1480 Hz sine with a 0.5 s exponential decay. In the silent QA harness no AudioContext exists and the piece renders complete without it.

## QA log

- Rendered with node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png), and 720x720 (thumb.png). The piece rendered cleanly in total silence at all three sizes.
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and cmp byte-compared identical, repeated after every fix round including the final file. Duplicate renders deleted.
- Read-backs:
  - Desktop and thumb (frame one, GOLD, mid-breath): near-black field, one cross flare centered, violet-blue vertical beam reaching most of the frame height, horizontal blade reaching most of the frame width with a warm cream-gold core that fades smoothly to nothing at the tips, hot white center point inside a soft glow, a faint halo wash behind the cross, and "GOLD" small and dim at the bottom center. No other marks.
  - Mobile (phone path): same composition in the tall frame; the beam stays inside the frame, the blade spans the width, the readout is legible, nothing clipped.
- Fix rounds: (1) the first build used 6 coarse blade layers and 7 hard center discs; the read-back showed contour banding along the blades and target-like rings around the center, plus a visible hard edge on the background halo disc. (2) A second pass with 9 computed layers and 12 stepped discs softened the halo but the steps still read as rings and the glow overwhelmed the blade core. (3) Final build: 42 fine steps per blade, 36 white plus 20 halo steps at the center, and a four-step nested halo; the read-back is a smooth photographic falloff with no banding. All rounds re-rendered and byte-compared.
- Grep checks on the final file: zero Date.now, zero em dashes, zero gradient / shadowBlur / ctx.filter / offscreen-canvas usage. Palette is exactly the six specced tones.

## Deviations

- None from the prompt's substance. Two recorded choices: the tap strike is deferred 0.30 s for double-tap disambiguation (house pattern), with the flash born at the deferred moment so picture and sound stay in step; and the audio clock runs on performance.now while all drawn state stays a closed-form function of the recorded events and t, which is what keeps frame one byte-reproducible.
