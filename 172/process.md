# No.172 Spiral Bloom Tessellation

## Concept
Robert Fathauer's spiral tessellations: kite shaped tiles grouped into self similar spirals, strictly two coloured in lapis and bone so no two neighbours ever match. Then the decoration devours its own structure: knot lines trace over the tile edges, the plain outlines fade to exactly zero, and what remains is an infinite interwoven spiral design with no visible scaffolding. A tap reseeds a new arrangement.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The tiling is a twisted polar grid: an even number of spiral boundary curves (8, 10 or 12 per seed) and geometrically growing rings divide the panel into kite quads, with a fan of triangles at the centre. In unwrapped (spiral index, ring index) coordinates the cells form a plain cylinder grid, so the adjacency graph is bipartite; the two colouring is computed honestly by BFS over the actual edge graph and assigned lapis #2E6BE6 and bone #E8E4D8 on the #101014 ground. The knot overlay is the same edge graph retraced as strands: the spiral curves, the ring circles and the centre spokes. At every vertex a parity rule picks the over strand, and the under strand is trimmed by a gap computed from the local cell size, so the weave is calculated from the geometry, not faked with a texture. The 21 second cycle assembles tiles from the centre (2s), holds (6s), draws the knot strands (3s), fades the tile outlines to exactly zero alpha (2s), then holds the woven design (8s). A tap on the canvas reseeds with a new arm count, twist, growth and handedness. The hint is a short lowercase line, allowed for this piece. First frame is offset 16 seconds into the cycle, at the woven hold. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, square panel centred at 92 percent of the viewport (97 percent of width on phones), page ground equal to the piece ground.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. The first pass used a slow ring growth and a full cell of twist per ring, which produced a tight vortex of ribbon thin tiles; ring growth was raised and the twist halved, and the tiles became the chunky kites of the reference. The weave gaps were also cut back (from 42 percent to 17 percent of the local cell arc) after the first render showed the strands chopped into dashes. Final renders: strict two colouring holds everywhere by inspection, knot strands read as continuous woven lines with small alternating gaps, spirals bleed off all four panel edges, and the centre fan resolves cleanly at phone size.
