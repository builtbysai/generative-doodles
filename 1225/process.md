# №1225 — Heavy Metal Parking Lot (Paper Cutout)

Leslie Supnet study block LS2, after her *The Animated Heavy Metal Parking Lot* (2008): the tailgate scene rebuilt as stop-motion paper cutouts on a tilted diorama board.

## Construction

- **Cutouts as data.** Every car, figure, cooler, the sun and the flag is a polygon shape list, never a clean vector. Each outline is generated by `tornRect` / `tornCircle`, which walk the perimeter and jitter every vertex with a fixed per-cutout seed hash — the tear is stable from frame to frame; only a ±1px per-frame "boil" jitter (hashed from frame + cutout id) makes the shapes tremble the way hand-cut paper does under a stop-motion camera.
- **Paper backing.** Each cutout is drawn twice: first as a 1.07× scaled copy offset (1.5, 2) in bone `#EFE3C2`, then the coloured shape on top, so a pale paper edge shows around every torn silhouette. Small translucent `#FFF8E0` rectangles at shape corners read as glue tabs.
- **The cast.** Ten cars in three rows (tailgate reds `#A63A2E`/`#7A2E22`, faded denim `#3E5A78`, aged paper `#D8C49A`), each with ink wheels, windshield bands, a torn roof panel and headlights; fourteen figures between the rows with bone heads, ink dot eyes and a line mouth; four cooler boxes; a red flag on a pole rising from the back-row car into the sky band.
- **Scene.** Flat painted sky `#C8B088` along the back edge with a big low torn sun `#E8A33D`, a darker paper horizon strip, then the asphalt field `#2E2A26` with faded bone parking stripes drawn as dashed, slightly wandering segments. The whole board is rotated −3° and sits on a table-dark `#241F1B` surround, so the diorama tilt reads.
- **Stepped motion.** Everything runs off the step index `f = floor(t × 6)`. Figures bob through a 4-step cycle, shuffle sideways every 24 frames, two car doors flap through a 3-stage stepped swing on 8-frame cycles, and the flag cycles four pre-torn wave shapes, two frames each. Paper grain is two layers of hashed specks (bone and ink, alpha 0.05/0.035) re-rolled every step so the whole frame boils at 6 fps. No easing, no tweens, no gradients anywhere.
- **Interaction.** Dragging pans the diorama like sliding a lightbox (clamped to ±15%). Figures within 90 design px of the pointer record a scatter disturbance: a stepped push away that decays in three flat steps over 18 frames — disturbance, not control; the tailgate carries on.

## Determinism

The first frame is drawn synchronously at the fixed phase T0 = 3.35 s (frame 20 at 6 fps: figures at mixed bob steps, the denim car's door fully open at swing stage 2, the flag at wave step 2), and the rAF loop (typeof-guarded) is anchored as T0 + elapsed-since-load. All noise derives from a seeded mulberry32 and an integer hash; no `Math.random`, no offscreen canvas, no audio. The thumb was rendered twice and byte-compared identical.
