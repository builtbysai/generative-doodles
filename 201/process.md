# No.201 Panther Prowl

## Concept
Willshire posts her builds in progress, plates numbered, the animal arriving as a kit of steel. Panther Prowl catches a Florida panther in that Workbench Thursday state and loops it: forty numbered plates hang scattered in a loose cloud, then fly in one by one, each landing with a white-blue weld flash at its seam, until the panther stands complete in a mid-prowl pose, seams showing, heat-gold accents on the flanks. It holds for ten seconds, shifting its weight in a slow two-step, then takes itself apart again. While it stands, dragging turns its head to track your finger, because even a kit panther is curious. It is assembled, never grown: the finished animal keeps every plate edge.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The panther is exactly forty plate polygons defined in a two-unit prowl space: five tapering tail segments, twelve leg plates in four three-plate legs posed mid-stride, ten body slabs along a gently arched spine, four belly plates layered over the underside, hip and shoulder octagons, two neck plates and five head plates. Two body slabs and the hip plate carry the gold, an overlay in #C9A227 that fades in as a plate seats. Each plate owns a scatter transform from a seeded generator (offset up to half a unit, rotation up to a radian) and interpolates home with an ease-in-out on its own stagger. The loop is forty seconds: eight scattered, twelve assembling, ten holding, ten disassembling in reverse order. A plate whose interpolation completes during assembly spawns a weld flash at its centroid, an additive radial burst with a cross spike that dies in under half a second. Stencil numerals in #C9CDD4 at sixty percent alpha are drawn only while a plate is in transit, never at rest. The head group rotates about a neck pivot toward the pointer during the hold, clamped and spring eased; idle, it drifts on a slow sine. The clock starts at twenty four seconds, so frame one is the completed panther in its prowling hold, gold on, seams showing, no numerals. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen, panther centred at sixty percent height and about three quarters of the width at every size, rAF typeof guarded, no text beyond the transit numerals.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three, plus two extra phase renders made from throwaway copies with the clock moved (the shipped file was not altered for these). Round one fault: the belly course hung below the body as four detached trapezoids, reading as a skirt of blocks rather than plating. Round two moved the belly plates up into the body's lower half and drew them after the body slabs, so they read as a second course of armour along the underside. The phase checks showed the scatter state as a loose cloud around centre with plates at rest and unnumbered, and the mid-assembly frame with the tail and first slabs landed, the gold slab seated, and numerals 15, 16 and 18 visible on plates still in transit. Final hold frames: the panther complete and prowling right, gold hip and flank slabs lit, ear up, tail curled, every seam drawn.
