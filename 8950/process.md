# №8950 · Tritave Rose - process notes

- **Title:** Tritave Rose
- **Seed:** 255 (The 3:1 Frame - Pierce/Mathews thirteenth-root-of-three)
- **Concept:** Structure repeats at 3x instead of 2x. Thirteen rings at
  radii R*3^(n/13), thirteen spokes. Marks at the intersections are
  sized by how close each ring's pitch sits to a simple odd ratio (the
  consonance curve); the outer tritave ring glows warm amber as the
  "home" interval, labeled 3:1 against the 1:1 center. One unhurried
  revolution takes four minutes. Click reseeds the marks.
- **Technique:** Vanilla canvas. Consonance computed from cents distance
  to the nearest odd partial; seeded PRNG for mark placement; slow
  constant rotation of the whole rose.
