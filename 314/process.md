# No.314 Gum Blossom Engine

After Coral Cockatoo's Eucalyptus Blossoms screen: a parametric engine for the gum blossom, built only from its real architecture. Each blossom is a woody gum-nut cup holding a cream receptacle, a dusty rose operculum cap, and a radial burst of gold stamens. There are no petals anywhere. The cap is the lid of the bud, and the stamens are the flower.

Exactly six blossoms stand on the near-black ground at varied scales, asserted in code, on short woody stems with narrow sage lanceolate leaves and a small half-open gum-nut bud on a side twig. Each blossom carries an authored seeded count of stamens inside forty to seventy, asserted per blossom, drawn as quadratic curve filaments radiating from the cup mouth, each with its own seeded length, angle and bend, tipped with a small cream anther.

The idle motion is a twenty four second seamless loop. Blossoms open on staggered phases: the operculum lifts, slides and tips away, the filaments unfurl outward as their curl parameter runs from curled to straight, then everything refurls and the cap settles back so the twenty fourth second matches the first exactly. Frame one opens mid-loop with the blossoms already at staggered phases and one mid-unfurl, so the engine reads at once.

Tapping a blossom releases it alone. Its stamens scatter as short drifting filaments on authored drift vectors and fade, then the flower regrows from bud over six seconds in total and blends back into its loop phase. Taps anywhere else do nothing, and nothing reloads. The rendering is flat screen-print fills on a single canvas with no gradients, drawn as plain path data each frame.
