# №443 Echo Chamber — EXPANSION #51 (maite-leon), prompt 3

## Concept
A large hand reaches in from the right toward its own reflection inside a floating gilt oval mirror on the left, tilted 8 degrees, on a deep blue ground #1E2A8A. Between the real fingertips and their twins, 120 tiny star-particles drift toward the touch point and dissolve. The reflection is wrong in exactly one gentle way: its fingers are a little longer (x1.16), and it holds one small caught object in its palm that the real hand does not. Tender, never uncanny.

Palette (from the prompt, verbatim): ground #1E2A8A; hand #2E3D6E with glow edge #8FA3E8; mirror glass #0E1440; gilt frame #C89B3C with highlight #F5D78E; particles #FFE9C9; the caught star #FFFFFF.

## Construction
- Single self-contained index.html, canvas 2D only. Canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API (visualViewport width/height, resize listeners on visualViewport and window); canvas.style.width/height pinned in px on every draw. Phone detection via window.screen min dimension < 700px, aspect h > w*1.15 as fallback only. Centered-subject composition: the mirror and hand keep their shapes at phone widths and the page ground is the piece's own #1E2A8A.
- Mirror: gilt ellipse (left 40% of frame) built from a linear gilt gradient, a darker bevel ring, and a beaded inner ring drawn exactly as the prompt's technical note specifies: zero-length line dashes with round caps on an ellipse path, bead count 72, spacing from the Ramanujan perimeter approximation. Glass is a radial gradient over #0E1440; a clipped diagonal sheen is laid over the reflection so it reads as glass.
- Hands: one articulated bezier hand path drawn twice. Each finger is a quadratic stroke through a joint mid-point with a slow per-finger curl, plus a thumb, a palm blob with radial sheen and two knuckle creases, and a glow-edge pass in #8FA3E8. The real hand is placed so its index tip lands a hair from the touch point on the mirror's right edge; the reflection is the same path x-flipped, clipped to the glass, at 0.92 scale with fingers lengthened x1.16.
- The lag: hand offsets are pushed into a position history buffer every frame; the reflection is drawn from the sample 1.5 s old (and counter-shifts slightly in x, as a mirror twin does). Its palm carries the caught object from a fixed authored table, index 0 at load: star -> key -> seed, drawn small and in-palette (white sparkle; gilt key with #F5D78E shaft; cream seed with a gilt sprout line).
- Particles: 120, pure data (seeded sin-hash per particle), drawn directly each frame as tiny four-point sparkles or dots. Each rides a 20 s cycle: radius shrinks toward the touch point with a slight spiral while alpha follows a sine envelope, so particles gather at the fingertips and dissolve. Density is biased toward the touch point by a pow curve on the radius.
- Interaction: DRAG moves the hand (target clamped, position eased); the reflection follows 1.5 s behind through the history buffer; particles swirl around the pointer path (positions within 0.20u of the pointer are rotated by a distance-falloff angle). DOUBLE-TAP (two taps within 380 ms and 48 px) cycles the caught object. No hint text or UI in the piece.
- Determinism: the synchronous first frame is drawn at the constant loop phase OFFSET = 4.0 s; the rAF loop is anchored as OFFSET + elapsed-since-load, and rAF is guarded by a typeof check. No offscreen canvases, no document.createElement, no sprites; no Date.now() anywhere in the draw path.
