Driver: a single tone line is the whole practice room. Twelve semitone blocks split into quarters, then into hairlines, while a practice cursor tries to land on a target the viewer proposes by tapping. The miss is the event, so the first two attempts drift past and the third lands.

Mechanism: a subdivision engine steps the line through 12, 48, 192 and 768 divisions as the session clock passes 3, 6 and 9 seconds, with the hairlines shimmering slowly at about 0.35 Hz. A chalk triangle cursor rides a guide thread above the line. Each attempt lasts 2.6 seconds: it slides toward the target on a fixed drift while a wobble decays, so the amplitude narrows attempt by attempt (0.050, 0.030, 0.012, then finer). The attempt table is fixed: drift plus 0.042 (miss), minus 0.020 (miss), plus 0.003 (landed), then refinements under 0.002. Completed attempts leave marks below the line, chalk ticks for misses and a pale gold square for a landing, fading over 14 seconds. After a miss the line brightens briefly as it redraws and tries again. A small readout in the bottom right shows the live division count. No note names, no score, no tuner needle.

Frame one: fixed phase t = 10 seconds, target 0.618, hairline depth (768 divisions). The fourth attempt is mid-wobble near the target, with two chalk miss ticks and one gold landing square in the history below the line.

Interaction: tap to propose a target only. The tap snaps to the nearest division at the current depth, marks it with a red chalk tick above the line, and restarts the attempts from the tap with a fresh wide wobble. No drag, no zoom, never click to reload. A tap also plays a lazy sine tone at a pitch mapped from the target, with a detuned second voice so a miss beats; audio is created inside the tap gesture only, never autoplay, and is a silent no-op where AudioContext is missing.

Palette: ground #0A0A0A, chalk #F2EFE6, signal red #E03131, pale gold #E8C86A. Hand-picked from the named colors in the prompt, since studies/olga-siemienczuk.md does not exist in the repo. Nothing else.

Deviations from the prompt: none in the mechanism. The tap target snaps to the current division depth, which the prompt implies by calling the target microtonal.
