# No.328 Moonbeams

After Debbie Sharman's Dark Academia moonlight design, this piece fills the whole frame with scallop tiles on an ink black ground, eight across and six down, for exactly forty eight tiles. The tiles reuse the scallop geometry of Scallop Field: a scalloped disc in deep green with an ink edge and a thin candle amber ring inside the rim. Every tile holds exactly one moon, a cream circle with crater arcs clustered to one side, and eight radiating beams drawn as tapered triangles in beam gold and candle amber. One beam is longer and wider than the rest, so the facing of each moon reads at a glance. She debated keeping the moons all facing one way or alternating them, so the piece keeps both: the orientations alternate across the field by default.

The motion is a twenty four second seamless loop. The beams sway slowly within their tiles, out of phase tile to tile like candlelight, and their length flickers gently on three whole cycles per loop, so the frame at twenty four seconds matches the frame at zero. The first frame opens with the beams already at staggered phases across the field.

Tapping a tile flips it between aligned and alternating orientation with a soft settle. Single taps resolve only after a three hundred and fifty millisecond window closes. A second tap inside that window is read as a double tap instead: the pending flip is discarded and the whole field advances to the next of four pre-authored orientation fields, alternating, all aligned, column stripes and row stripes, in that fixed order. Nothing is reseeded at random, nothing reloads, and all forty eight tiles, their single moons and the four fields are counted and asserted in the code. All marks are drawn directly on one canvas each frame from seeded data.
