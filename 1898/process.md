Driver: two voice agents trade atonal phrases across a bright empty room. Each phrase draws itself as a loose ink ribbon, and the answer contradicts rather than imitates. After eight exchanges both ribbons curl into a shared embrace, the amber afterglow rises, and the game reseeds under a new hidden no-rule.

Mechanism: one game is dealt from a seed (mulberry32, seed 1898 plus tap count and game index). The hidden rule is one of four: contradict, shadow, interrupt, lag. Eight phrases alternate speakers, ink on the left and rust on the right. Each statement takes its own lane down the room, and each answer contradicts the phrase it answers: wrong register (a lane 0.26 higher or lower), wrong length (shifted by 0.12 to 0.26 of the field), wrong direction (drawn from the opposite side). Ribbons draw themselves progressively over 1.8 seconds each, as filled tapering bands with a darker spine and a round speaking tip. The two players stand at the edges, breathing slowly, with an open mouth arc while speaking. At 14.4 seconds the exchange ends and both ribbons curl into one shared embrace: two spirals winding into a single loop over 3 seconds while a soft amber radial glow rises behind them, then fades as the next game is dealt at 18 seconds. No metronome, no score, no customization.

Frame one: fixed phase t = 12 seconds, game 0. Exchange 7 of 8 is 67 percent drawn, six ribbons already hang in their lanes, ink from the left and rust from the right, before the embrace.

Interaction: tap to reseed only. A tap deals a fresh game with the same two players and a new hidden no-rule, and plays a short phrase in two contrasting generated timbres, low sine for ink and higher triangle for rust. Audio is created lazily inside the tap gesture only, never on load, and the visual game stands alone without it. No drag, no zoom, never click to reload.

Palette: room #F7F0E3, floor #E4D7BE, shadow #D8CBB2, ink #1A1712, rust #B3402A, amber glow #F2A93B. Hand picked from the named colors in the prompt, since studies/olga-siemienczuk.md does not exist in the repo. Nothing else.

Deviations from the prompt: none in the mechanism. The tap also sounds the two voice timbres, as the spec prefers, in addition to reseeding the game.

Both fixed, and a debug render at t = 16 seconds now shows the embrace cleanly. Second pass caught the low lanes tangling into a knot and the wobble reading as lightning; fixed by giving each statement its own lane, cutting the end drift and wobble, and keeping older ribbons near full ink.
