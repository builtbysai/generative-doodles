# №8952 · Clang - process notes

- **Title:** Clang
- **Seed:** 253 (Available Pitch Process - Clang ritual)
- **Concept:** Sixteen agents draw from a fixed menu of seven ink
  marks. Each mark swells in from silence, holds, fades out, then rests
  at least as long as it sang. Overall density follows the ritual arc:
  accumulative for two thirds of the 54-second cycle, dissolutive for
  the last third. Three percussive clangs punctuate the cycle (shockwave
  rings + page flash), the middle one at the golden section; a thin
  timeline marks the three punctuations.
- **Technique:** Vanilla canvas. Attack/sustain/release envelope per
  mark; density function drives respawn probability and rest length;
  wraparound and golden-section crossings trigger the clangs.
