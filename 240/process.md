# No.240 Training Models

## Concept
After Woodfill's Code Practice wall models of 2019 and the stop-motion animations of Certain Objects and Certain Systems: a workshop shelf of boxy wood tone forms, birch, white and grey with exactly one green accent box, two of them open frames, one carrying a clamp. They are alive in stop motion: every half second each form snaps to a neighbouring grid cell or turns by fifteen degrees, with a two pixel handmade jitter on every step. There is no tweening and no easing anywhere; the animation is quantized, the way his films are. A drag may nudge a form off its cell, and on release it snaps to the nearest cell and re-enters the walk.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Seven forms live on a 12 by 8 grid of 100 unit cells in a 3:2 panel. A seeded walk advances on a half second step clock: each step, each form has a seeded chance to step to a free neighbouring cell (moves that would overlap another form are refused), or to turn by fifteen degrees, turns capped at 30 degrees for boxes and 15 for frames so the shelf stays shelf-like. The per-step jitter is a seeded hash of step and form index, plus or minus 3 units (about two pixels at desktop scale). The walk is pre-rolled fourteen steps so frame one is mid walk with the forms scattered. Dragging draws the held form at the pointer with no smoothing; release snaps it to the nearest free cell. Flat fills only, thin grey edges, no gradients. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Two fixes at the gate: the first build let forms turn up to 45 degrees and tumble, reading as debris rather than models, so turns were capped and the pre-roll shortened; the second pass found a real bug on the phone frame, the form bodies drawn in grid units while their positions were in pixels, making them three times too large at 390 wide (invisible on desktop where the scale is near one). After the fix, frame one at all sizes shows the seven forms spread across the shelf at modest tips, frames open, clamp on the birch box, the one green box among them.
