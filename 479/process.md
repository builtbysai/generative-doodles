# Loupe Snow (No.479)

EXPANSION #57 julandthefox / Giulia Neri, prompt GN7.

## Concept

A large round magnifier's loupe rests on a grey-blue field (#93A3AD). Through its pale glass (#E8F0F4 at 0.5 alpha, rim #3B2A24) the snowflakes beneath it are seen enlarged. From the lower-left edge of the lens a dense stream of flakes pours across the ground toward the lower left like sand. The woman from the study is tiny at the left edge, about 12 percent of the frame height: closed-eye profile in skin #EBC9A8 with dark hair #3B2A24 and a mustard dress #D9A03F, blowing gently toward the loupe, with one small breath curl. No Christmas cues, no glitter, no text except the single lowercase hint word "drag" in the lower-right corner in the rim color at low alpha.

## Construction

- Single self-contained index.html, 2D canvas only, no external assets, no audio, no microphone. Title tag is the piece title.
- Seeded data, drawn fresh every frame (mulberry32, initial seed 479071): 150 field flakes, 95 stream flakes, and 850 grain dots. Grain is a precomputed seeded dot array (dark #5E707C or light #C7D2D9 specks at 0.03 to 0.08 alpha) drawn identically every frame.
- Every flake is a six-arm snowflake drawn with strokes: a main arm plus two small branches near each tip. Field flakes carry a seeded base position, radius (2.6 to 6.2), rotation, drift phase, sway amplitude, and a slow fall rate. Position is a pure deterministic function of loop time: y wraps from the seeded fraction plus t times the fall rate, x adds a small sine sway. Nothing accumulates.
- Magnification: each frame every field flake's position is tested against the lens circle. Flakes outside are drawn once at base size. Flakes inside are skipped outside and drawn again inside a circular clip, over the pale glass disc, at 2.2x scale, plus a faint white sheen ellipse on the glass.
- Stream: each stream flake advances a seeded parameter s at a slow seeded speed and wraps. Its start point is the lens rim on the trail side; the trail direction is (-0.816, 0.578) on wide frames and steeper (-0.55, 0.835) on phones. Trail length is capped at the first frame edge it would cross, so the whole band stays on the ground and spreads along the visible field. Perpendicular offset widens with distance, alpha fades slightly, and flakes under the lens are skipped so the band appears to pour out from the rim. The stream follows the lens automatically because its start point is derived from the loupe position each frame.
- Loupe: dark rim stroke, a thin white highlight arc on the upper-left rim, and a short handle stub at the lower right drawn as a thick round-capped line starting just outside the glass (0.78R along the diagonal) so it sits flat on the ground plane and does not bleed under the glass.
- Figure: drawn from simple shapes at the left edge: mustard triangle dress, dark hair mass and bun behind a skin face circle, a tiny nose step on the profile edge, one curved closed-eye line, a small round mouth, and one low-alpha breath arc.
- Composition and layout: canvas size comes from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; draw() pins canvas.style.width and height in px every frame. Phone detection uses window.screen (minimum dimension under 700px) with the aspect fallback (height greater than width times 1.15) only when the screen test fails. On phones the loupe shrinks to 0.32 of frame width, moves to 60 percent width and 33 percent height, the stream steepens, and the figure's baseline rises to 44 percent of height so she stands clear of the stream; the page ground is the grey-blue everywhere.
- Interaction: drag (pointerdown near the loupe, pointermove, pointerup) sets a target and the loupe follows with a 0.20 lerp; flake velocities never change with drag speed. On release the loupe eases at 0.03 toward a slow idle sway around its home position. A double tap (two pointerdowns within 0.35s of loop time) or a dblclick reseeds the snowfall by advancing the seed and rebuilding all seeded arrays. Never click-to-reload.
- Determinism: the first frame is drawn synchronously at load with loop time fixed at t = 9s, the loupe exactly at its home position, and no clock-derived values anywhere in frame one. The rAF loop is typeof-guarded and anchored as the fixed offset 9 plus elapsed time from the first rAF timestamp. No offscreen canvas, no createElement, no AudioContext anywhere, so the QA renderer draws the identical first frame.

## QA log

- Rendered with node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png), and 720x720 (thumb.png).
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and compared with cmp; byte-identical. The second render was deleted.
- Desktop render read-back: loupe reads instantly as a dark-rimmed magnifier with pale glass at center-right, flakes inside clearly larger than the small flakes outside, snow stream trailing from the lower-left rim down to the lower-left ground, tiny woman in profile with mustard dress and closed eye at the left edge, "drag" hint in the lower-right corner. Nothing clipped.
- Thumb render read-back: same composition holds in square format; stream reaches the lower-left edge, handle stub clear of the frame edge.
- Mobile render read-back (after fix, see below): loupe fits the width with its handle inside the frame, magnified flakes large and clear, stream runs from the rim down to the lower-left edge clear of the figure, who stands fully visible at the left edge; hint readable in the corner.
- Fix during QA: the first mobile render had the stream exiting the left edge almost immediately (trail too long for the narrow frame, most flakes offscreen) and passing through the figure, and the handle's round cap showed as a pale blob under the glass. Fixed by steepening the phone trail direction, capping trail length at the first crossed edge, raising the phone loupe and the figure's baseline, and starting the handle stub outside the glass. Re-rendered all three sizes and re-verified determinism after the fix.
- Deviations: the loupe home sits at 56 percent of frame width on wide frames (60 percent on phones) rather than 45 percent, so the rim and handle stay fully in frame with generous ground around them while still reading center-right. The stream direction and figure baseline adapt on phones only, to keep the composition intact in portrait; all other elements are identical across sizes. No other deviations from the brief.
