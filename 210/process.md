# No.210 In Need of Love

## Concept
The most unsettling of Asia Ward's re-fleshed creatures are, in her words, still in need of love and attention. This one sits small and hunched in a large dark field: patchwork worn cloth and old-coat wool, one limb too long, glass eyes slightly mismatched, and it flinches at nothing. The piece is a care mechanic with no instructions and no meter: a hidden trust value is read only in the body. Fast drags and hard taps startle it, trust drops, it recoils and hunches tighter. Slow gentle drags across its back soothe it, trust rises, a warm blush blooms through the fabric, it uncurls and leans into the touch. At full trust it performs its one trick, a soft full-body jiggle of contentment, then settles. Double-tap deals another unsettling creature. It never becomes a pretty animal; it just becomes less afraid.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The creature is drawn fresh each frame from a variant record (which side grows the long limb, which eye is bigger, a slight whole-body tilt, a hunch bias, swapped cloths, all reseeded on double-tap): a hunched back blob in cloth #57504A or #3E4A55, an old-coat wool panel #4E3E35 over one shoulder, a second cloth panel low on the body, the too-long wool limb drooping to the ground, a tucked short arm, a tucked head carrying two bead eyes of deliberately different sizes in #C8D4DE and #9FB8C4, dashed seams throughout. Spine curl, head height and fabric warmth are continuous functions of trust: the fabrics are mixed toward the blush #C87E5E as trust rises, with an added soft blush pool on the back above a quarter trust. Pointer velocity classifies every contact: above a screen-scaled threshold it is a startle (recoil impulse away from the hand, trust down 0.15, rate limited), below it while in contact over the body it soothes (trust up 0.1 per second) and sets the lean target toward the hand. Untouched, trust decays slowly and, while it is low, the creature flinches on a loose nine to fifteen second timer. Frame one opens at low trust, hunched, breathing, blush absent. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, creature held at about a third of viewport height with the emptiness around it kept, rAF typeof guarded, no text anywhere, and nothing of the trust value displayed.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The creature reads at once as a sewn thing: patchwork panels with dashed seams, two mismatched bead eyes on the tucked head, and the too-long limb trailing to the ground at its right, with the field around it properly empty and no trace of blush in the opening frame, as designed. Mobile holds it at a similar share of the frame height with the same emptiness. The soothe and startle paths cannot be exercised by the still renderer; they are verified in code, with the thresholds scaled to the screen so a stroke that is gentle on desktop stays gentle on a phone.
