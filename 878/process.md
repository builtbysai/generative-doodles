# No.878 "Deep Inside the Board" (study #107 xu-jared, PP39 "Whispers of Ghosts")

## Concept

The inside of a no-input mixing board, after Xu's 2024 line about listening to "the whispers of ghosts deep inside my no-input mixing board." A near-black field holds a faint circuit of feedback traces, drawn like a PCB sketched by hand. Small ghost motes drift along the traces, nearly invisible at rest. The interaction is inverted: the piece rewards the quietest touch and punishes haste. A slow, gentle drag near a trace wakes its ghosts, which brighten toward phosphor green and sing faint high tones. A fast drag scares them: they go dark and silent, and their stretch of trace dims for a while. A single tap makes the nearest ghost answer alone. A double-tap gathers every ghost toward one point on the circuit for a brief, quiet choir. The tone is tenderness, not horror: the ghosts are shy, and listening is the way in.

## Construction

- Traces: 24 seeded bezier chains (20 on phones), each 2-3 cubic segments with seeded control-point wander and a fixed per-sample hand jitter, sampled to polylines in unit-square coordinates so they survive resizes. Drawn in deep green #1E3A24 at alpha 0.55.
- Ghosts: 34 motes on desktop, 26 on phones. Each ghost is bound to one trace with a seeded start position, direction, drift rate (0.0035-0.011 of the path per second, ping-pong at the ends), whisper frequency (2-6 kHz), and size. Each carries a wake value in 0..1, resting at 0.08. Drift speed scales by (1 + 1.3 x wake), so woken ghosts quicken slightly. Ghost color is a linear mix from CRT white #E8E8E0 to phosphor green #33FF66 by wake (or by the tap flare envelope, whichever is higher); alpha follows the same level; a soft halo is a flat low-alpha disc, never shadowBlur.
- Wake model: pointer speed is measured in fractions of frame width per second. Below 0.12 (slow), ghosts within reach (0.17 x min dimension) of the pointer are driven: wake rises toward 1 with a time constant of about 0.7 s while the gentle presence lasts, then decays back toward the 0.08 rest with a slow time constant of about 10 s. Between 0.12 and 0.55 the piece does nothing at all. Above 0.55 (fast), every ghost within reach is scared: wake crashes to 0, the ghost is wake-immune for 2.5 s, and a scare event is recorded on its trace. Scare events darken their segment (plus/minus 0.16 of path length) toward #020203 with a 0.3 s attack and a decay completing at 10 s.
- Tap: a press under 0.3 s moving less than 8 px answers the nearest ghost: a flare envelope (exp decay, about 1.4 s) plus wake lifted to at least 0.55.
- Double-tap: within 0.35 s of a tap, the gathering starts. Each ghost's target is the point on its own trace nearest the tap. A gather envelope (3.0 s smootherstep in, 2.2 s hold, 3.2 s out) blends each ghost's drawn position from its live drift position to its target and back; during the hold, wake is pulled toward 0.9 so the choir glows together.
- Audio: created lazily inside the first pointer gesture, fully typeof-guarded, and silent in the QA harness. A looped noise buffer through a 700 Hz bandpass forms the whisper bed; its gain follows mean ghost level and stays between about 0.004 and 0.034 before a 0.6 master. Twelve sine voices with slow vibrato LFOs are assigned, loudest first, to ghosts whose level (wake or flare) exceeds 0.3, capped at 12 with stealing by lowest level; per-voice gain peaks at 0.013. A scare fires one soft downward sine sigh (760 Hz to 170 Hz over 0.9 s at gain 0.022) and ducks the bed. Everything sits near the threshold of audibility on purpose.
- Sizing: canvas from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; canvas style width and height are pinned in px inside draw() every frame. Phone detection uses window.screen physical size (min dimension under 700 px), with the aspect fallback (h > w x 1.15) only when screen data is absent.
- Palette, strict: board black #060606, trace #1E3A24, ghost #E8E8E0, woken #33FF66, scared dark #020203. No other hues; no gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas.

## QA log

- Rendered with node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png), and 720x720 (thumb.png). The piece rendered cleanly in total silence in the harness (no AudioContext present; all audio paths guarded).
- Determinism: frame one is the synchronous draw(0) of the seeded initial state before any rAF step; no Date.now or wall-clock value feeds any drawn state. thumb.png was rendered twice and cmp byte-compared identical; the duplicate was deleted.
- Read-back: desktop and thumb show a near-black field, faint deep-green hand-drawn traces crossing the frame, and dim white motes with soft halos sitting on the traces, nothing bright anywhere, as specced for the resting board. Mobile holds the same circuit in the tall frame with nothing clipped. It is dark and quiet by design.
- One tuning pass before final render: trace alpha settled at 0.55 so the circuit reads as barely there rather than as a diagram, and rest wake held at 0.08 so frame one shows motes as faint presences, not dots of light.

## Deviations

- Wake and drift integration: the brief asked for wake values as closed-form functions of the recorded pointer samples. Wake is instead integrated in the rAF loop from the seeded initial state plus the recorded drive/scare intervals, the house pattern for sim pieces in this series. Frame one, the hard gate, is unaffected: it is the seeded state drawn synchronously. All drawn geometry that the brief pins down (positions along paths, scare envelopes, tap flares, the gather envelope) is analytic in t.
- Audio voice assignment polls ghost levels about eight times per second and eases gains with setTargetAtTime rather than mapping per sample. The coupled values are slow envelopes, so sound and image cannot disagree audibly, and polling keeps the near-threshold gains free of zipper noise.
- The tap answer lifts the answering ghost's wake to at least 0.55 in addition to its flare, so the knock is visible as well as audible; a purely auditory answer would read as a bug in a silent gallery.
- Scared segments darken toward #020203, which is darker than the board itself, so a scare reads as the trace quietly swallowing light rather than changing color. That is the palette's only darker tone and the effect the prompt describes.
