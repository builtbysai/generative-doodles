# No.245 Stoically Itself

## Concept
After Bawa's #FFFFFF at Ditch Projects: a glossy white scaffold in a white gallery room, barely distinguishable from the room itself. The structure is not the subject. The subject is its shadows, long interlacing geometric webs cast across the floor and the back wall by a single moving stage light, growing more visible and present while the structure's weight dissolves. It stands there, stoically itself, and does nothing. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in an 1100 by 850 room. The scaffold is a five bay, three level node and edge grid standing on a floor line at mid height, and it never moves. The stage light orbits on a thirty second loop, kept high, travelling from one side of the room to the other. Shadows are projected line work, drawn per edge from projected copies of the whole grid: onto the back wall with a modest throw in the pale shadow grey, and onto the floor with a height mapped projection in the darker grey, where a node's shadow lands lower and further sideways the higher the node sits, leaning away from the light, so the floor webs interlace, stretch and swing as the light travels. Contact shadows in near-black anchor each pole foot. The dissolve trick is wired to the geometry: a shadow length score from the current stretch and skew drives the structure's alpha down as the webs lengthen. The structure is stroked white at that alpha over a whisper of an edge pass in the pale shadow grey at twenty percent, the lightest touch that lets it register against the room at all; it is never darker than its shadows. The light phase is baked in so frame one opens with the light off to the left and the webs already long. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text, no colour anywhere.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The first projection model threw radial shadows from the light point and exploded: webs filled the whole frame in giant diamonds and the piece read as abstract line art. The model was rebuilt as a height mapped floor projection plus a modest wall throw and re-rendered: now the white grid stands faint at centre with its pale ghost on the wall behind, the dark floor web interlaces across the lower frame from the contact points, and the balance is right, shadows dominant, structure dissolving. The phone frame keeps the room proportions and the web play stays legible at 390 wide. One deviation, recorded: the faint edge pass under the white strokes, without which the structure is literally invisible in a still render; it is drawn in the palette's own pale shadow grey at low alpha, lighter than every shadow in the piece.
