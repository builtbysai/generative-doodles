# No.179 Hierarchical Draw

## Concept
Goodman-Strauss's trilobite and cross, the aperiodic pair whose markings force hierarchy: fill the field with the two tiles, mark one side indigo and the other rust, and a lattice of squares assembles itself inside the pattern, then another twice as large, then another, as far as the eye can follow. The piece draws the doublings in one at a time, holds the whole nested structure, and restarts one level deeper; a tap reseeds the tiling.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Two tile outlines in unit space: the trilobite, a square with a quarter scale square notch cut from one side, and the cross that fills its gaps. Tiles lay on a jittered grid bleeding off all edges; type and rotation come from a seeded integer hash, with trilobite rotations based on a checkerboard so notches face tip to tip, varied on a seeded minority so the field never settles into a plain repeat. Markings are layered per tile: two indigo arc bands on the notch side, one rust band on the flat side (crosses carry one small arc in each of two opposite arms), plus an ink diamond at the centre. Every stroke vertex is displaced by a 1 to 2px wobble hashed from the shared lattice coordinates, so adjacent tiles wobble together and still meet, the hand drawn feel of his wall sized tilings. The hierarchy is drawn, not implied: overlay lattices of squares at cell scales s, 2s and 4s, aligned to the tile grid, ink outlines with the left edge indigo and the right edge rust, revealed square by square in growth order during seconds 4 to 13 of the 23 second loop after the tiles lay down radially over the first 4 seconds; hold with all three doublings visible, soft reset, repeat. Tap (short, under 14px of travel) reseeds. Palette exactly per spec: ground #EFE9DC, ink #1A1A1A, indigo #2E3A9B, rust #C4451F. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, tiles full bleed including portrait phones, rAF typeof guarded, hint lowercase and short.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. The desktop frame (loop offset 19s) shows the completed state: trilobites and crosses edge to edge, indigo and rust bands pairing across tile boundaries into bowtie figures, and all three lattice scales legible at once, fine squares everywhere, the 2s lattice in mid weight lines, the 4s squares spanning the frame with indigo left edges and rust right edges. The phone frame keeps tiles large enough to read individually, nine across, with the hierarchy still visible at two scales. Honest trait: the seeded rotation minority means a few neighbourhoods align into short periodic runs before the pattern breaks again; that near miss quality is in the source drawings too, and the overlay makes the forced hierarchy, not the tile repeat, the subject.
