# No.270 Interlock

## Concept
After Shupiwe Chongwe's interlocking raku sculpture: two matte ceramic forms holding each other on a plain warm ground. The left form is raku black with tan crackle veining, the right form warm stoneware with sparse darker veins, and the unit is the couple, never the single hero object. Dragging horizontally pulls the pair apart along their embrace; releasing lets them settle back together slowly, with no bounce, and the black form's veining reads denser where the two meet. At rest the pair breathes faintly. Drag to separate.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, centred fit with the page ground in the piece's own warm ground colour, so the shaped pair keeps its proportions at every size. Each form is a smooth closed blob built from seeded polar points joined by midpoint quadratic curves, tapered narrower at the shoulder and wider at the base so it reads as a vessel rather than an egg, with a gentle bulge on the contact side and a slight lean toward its partner. Veining is seeded wandering polylines clipped to each silhouette: twenty seven tan crackle veins on the black form, ten sparse darker veins on the stoneware. Surface shading is layered translucency clipped to the same path, a faint lightening high and a soft darkening low, with no filters and no shadow blur. The interlock parameter q runs from 1 nested to 0 separated; each form offsets by plus or minus (1 minus q) times 150 design pixels. While dragging, q follows the horizontal drag distance; on release a critically damped spring (stiffness 1, damping 2) eases q back to 1 with no overshoot. Vein alpha near the contact edge is scaled by q, so the crackle gathers as the forms rejoin. The contact shadow is three layered ellipses in shadow grey at low alpha under the pair, widening as they separate. Breathing is a one percent scale sine on an 8.5 second cycle applied to each form, and the opening clock starts at 1.4 seconds so frame one is nested with the breathing mid-cycle. Palette held to the five specced colours only. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, pointer coordinates converted through the stored centred fit transform, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds, no text, no audio, never click to reload.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported the two resize listeners and the pointer handlers. Two fixes. First pass: the forms read as plain eggs leaning apart, and the clipped shading ellipses left hard visible arcs on both surfaces; the blobs were retapered into vessel shapes with a contact side bulge, the leans were reversed so the tops incline toward each other, and the shading was softened and pushed further off centre. Frame one after fixes reads as specced at all three sizes: two embracing ceramic forms centre frame on the warm ground, tan veining clear on the black form, the stoneware form quieter behind it, a soft shadow pool beneath, generous negative space on every side and nothing clipped. On the phone the centred fit keeps the whole pair and its shadow in frame. No further fixes were needed.
