No.310 Tower of Chance takes Xanthe Caesia's wizard tower as a playable instrument. The tower is built from exactly seven stacked trapezoid stone courses, each shrinking as it rises, with seeded per course jitter, rough speckle marks and a mortar course line on every level. Slit windows glow amber through layered translucent halos, a carved arched door sits at the base, and a small chimney stands on the roof cap beside the dark roof mouth. Backflow smoke threads from the chimney over the dark ground, and blue grey mist breathes and pools at the base, seeping faintly at the course seams when the backflow is pulsed.

Exactly three bone white dice rest at the foot at frame one, two lower left and one mid rest at the door, their faces read as pip and facet marks only, with no numerals anywhere. Dragging a die into the roof mouth sends it down one hidden spiral path inside the tower, drawn as a flat tumbling polygon with physics eased bounces against the path, before it rolls out of the base door and settles. Its settle face is taken in order from an authored seeded roll sequence written into the code, never from runtime randomness, and the smoke thickens briefly with each roll. A die dropped short of the mouth eases back to its resting place at the foot. Tapping the chimney fires a single backflow pulse: the smoke wicks down the chimney side and mist seeps from the seams for that pulse, then the normal rise resumes.

The first frame is already developed, with smoke threading, windows lit, mist pooled and the door die in place, so the game reads at once. The palette is held to stone, mortar shadow, window amber, smoke, mist blue grey, dice bone and the dark ground. Everything is drawn from fixed data on a single canvas each frame.
