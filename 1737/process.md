# The Keeper Test, No.1737

TD2, Taylor Deupree study, PP984. Gallery date 2006 · 10 · 14.

## Mechanism

Six soft graphite marks loop on a faint oval guide at the center of a paper
white ground. Each mark drifts on a tiny slow wobble, a sum of two sines
with a seeded phase, so the motif is alive but never busy. A stillness
metric runs every frame: the largest frame to frame displacement across
the six marks. While that displacement stays under threshold, the muted
signal red zone bar at the right margin fills slowly, one full bar per
45 seconds of stillness. On a fixed deterministic schedule a single mark
jumps, the displacement breaches threshold, the bar empties, and the
motif reseeds into a new stillness: the marks ease to fresh seeded
positions over 1.4 seconds. The reset is calm, no flash, like erasing
a take.

## Interaction

Watch only. There is nothing to touch; the loop auditions itself for
stillness and only earns its continuation.

## Frame one

Fixed phase, seeded PRNG (mulberry32, seed 1737). The zone bar opens
42 percent filled so the mechanism reads in the thumbnail.

## Deviations

None from the prompt. The oval guide is drawn faintly so the motif's
loop reads; the bar carries a small dot at its fill line and a ZONE
caption, both quiet.
