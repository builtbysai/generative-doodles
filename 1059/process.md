# No.1059 "The Suit Remembers"

Study #130 hideaki-tai, HT4 / PP220, the Synesthesia Suit line (2015, X1-2.44 with evala, 2019). The wearable as instrument, rendered as the thing it is: a field of haptic-light cells holding a fading map of where an unseen body has been. The wearer is never drawn. No audio: the prompt specs none.

## Concept

150 cells lie in a loose organic scatter on a near-black field, a patch of the suit rather than a whole world. An unseen wearer drifts across the frame on a seeded waypoint path, pausing at every second waypoint. Cells heat by proximity and cool slowly, so the field remembers: a white-hot head at the body, warm cream behind it, ember red cooling to dark over the following seconds. Drag and you are the wearer; your finger becomes the body, cells flaring under it and cooling behind it. Double-tap relocates the wearer to a freshly seeded path; the cells keep their memory.

## Construction

- Sim: fixed step DT = 1/60 s, seeded mulberry32. Frame one is the synchronous draw after a 26 s pre-roll (1560 steps). No clock-derived state anywhere; the rAF loop (typeof-guarded) advances the same fixed steps from accumulated elapsed time, capped per frame.
- Cells: 150 points from a seeded mix (62% clustered around six seeded cluster centers via Box-Muller, 38% uniform), then three relaxation passes enforcing a 0.045 minimum separation, so the scatter is organic but never clumped into a grid. Per-cell radius 0.014 to 0.025 world units. Edge fade: a smoothstep on distance from center (full strength inside 0.40, falling to 0.28 at 0.68) scales the whole disc stack, so outer cells sit dimmer and the patch fades to black.
- Wearer: one waypoint per zone of a 3x3 stratification, visited in seeded shuffled order, joined by a closed Catmull-Rom loop. Segment lengths are sampled (16 subdivisions), durations are length / SPEED (0.085 world units per second), and a 3 s pause follows every second segment. The schedule (segments with cumulative start times) makes wearerPos(t) a pure function of the schedule clock; the full cycle for the shipped seed runs about 63 s. A pause is where the bloom forms: cells near the still wearer saturate.
- Temperature model: per cell, per step. Proximity influence uses a cosine falloff over FALLOFF = 0.16 world units (about 90 px on a 720 stage): infl = 0.5 + 0.5*cos(pi*d/FALLOFF) for d < FALLOFF, else 0. If infl exceeds the cell's temperature, temperature rises toward infl at rate DT*(2.5 + 6.5*infl), so the core saturates in well under a second while the fringe warms gently. Otherwise temperature cools linearly toward infl at DECAY = 0.05 per second: a saturated cell takes about 13 s to fall through the ember band (t 0.75 to 0.1), which is the lingering memory. Cells never snap off; the only way down is the slow linear cool.
- Ramp table (piecewise linear in RGB): t 0.00 = #15161A (idle), t 0.25 = #2A1512 (cooling ember, dark), t 0.50 = #B23A2E (ember), t 0.75 = #FFE9C4 (proximity warm), t 1.00 = #FFFFFF (active flare). Nothing cool anywhere; the ramp passes through orange tones between the ember and warm stops by RGB mixing.
- Drawing: each cell is three layered-alpha discs of its ramp color (radii 1.9r, 1.3r, r; alphas (0.06+0.14t), (0.10+0.30t), (0.38+0.62t), all scaled by the edge factor). No gradients, no shadowBlur, no strokes. The wearer has no representation at all.
- Interaction: pointer drag overrides the wearer position with the pointer (in world coordinates) for as long as the drag lasts; on release the scheduled wearer resumes from its own clock, which kept running. A double-tap (two quick, near-stationary taps within 0.35 s) advances the seed by an LCG step, rebuilds the schedule, and restarts the wearer clock; cell temperatures are untouched, so the old body's map cools out under the new one.
- Viewport: canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; canvas style width/height pinned in px inside draw(). Phone detection via window.screen (min dimension < 700 px) with the h > w*1.15 aspect fallback. The world is a unit square, contain-fit and centered, so the scatter scales to fit at 390 wide with nothing cropped.

## Deviations

- Falloff radius 0.16 world units rather than the prompt's ~90 px: at the 720 reference stage 90 px is 0.125, and with 150 cells the wearer path can pass whole stretches without a cell inside the hot radius; 0.16 (about 115 px at 720) guarantees the head and bloom always have cells to live in. The falloff shape is a cosine rather than a plain linear ramp, for the same reason: a usable warm fringe around a saturating core.
- Decay is 0.05 per second rather than the prompt's 0.06: with the wider falloff the field holds slightly more total heat, and 0.05 keeps the visible ember lifetime near the specced ~10 s (about 13 s through the ember band from saturation).
- Pauses fall at every second waypoint on a fixed pattern rather than at seeded random waypoints: the pattern guarantees blooms recur steadily (roughly every 11 to 14 s of travel) instead of clustering by chance.
