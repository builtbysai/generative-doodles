# No.249 Mineral Spirits

## Concept
After Bawa's Mineral Spirits, her 2016 Disjecta biennial installation in the vacant Astor Hotel in Astoria, Oregon: a golden scaffold standing in a dark abandoned hotel lobby, tall bays rising into blackness, dust hanging in the light shafts, the gold picking up warm highlights. The shafts drift on a thirty five second loop, the gold breathes subtly, and forty dust motes wander. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, full bleed. The lobby is near black, with two columns hinted at the frame edges in a dark dust tone with simple capital and base bands, and a faint floor line. Three light shafts, flat translucent quads in the shaft white at low alpha, slant down from the upper right and land on the floor; their slant and sideways drift ride the thirty five second loop with individual phases. The scaffold is four bays wide, drawn as a line system of poles, rails, alternating diagonals and coupler dots, its levels running from the floor up past the top edge of the frame. Each edge is coloured by its own height, a linear falloff from full gold at the lit top down to dim gold near the floor, with a little extra brightness toward the upper right where the shafts enter, and the whole structure breathes by easing a few percent toward the dim gold on a nine and a half second cycle. Forty seeded dust motes wander slowly around their homes, twinkling gently. The opening clock starts at eleven seconds, shafts already angled through the bays and motes already in the air. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text, no gradients, no god-ray effects: the shafts stay flat quads.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The first pass failed outright: the per-edge gold was computed by mixing an already mixed colour string, which produced invalid colour values the canvas silently ignored, so the scaffold rendered in the leftover faint dust stroke and was effectively invisible, leaving only shafts and motes. The colour math was rebuilt in raw channels. The shafts were also narrowed and stopped at the floor line instead of running past it, and their alpha dropped a touch. Frame one now reads as specced: the gold grid standing full height in the dark, shafts raking down behind it from the upper right, motes scattered through the light. On the phone frame the scaffold turns into a tall golden lattice filling the portrait, shafts and columns intact at 390 wide.
