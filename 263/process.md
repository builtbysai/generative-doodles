# No.263 Chadar Balance

## Concept
After Stanzin Tsepel's 2023 chadar-trek scrap piece: a laden porter assembled from rusted scrap, glowing headlamp, chain icicles dangling from the torso, a weathered jerrycan pack, balanced high atop tilted diamond plates of thin formed ice. The whole group sways a degree and a half on a 25 second loop, the chains swinging gently out of phase: a held breath, never a fall. Watch only, no interaction; a fall would be melodrama.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a vertical 880 by 1240 design space, contain-fitted and centred so the composition keeps its shape on any screen, the page ground the same deep blue as the backdrop. The porter is a rigid group of flat scrap parts, jerrycan, legs, rusted torso, arms, headwrap and head, pivoting at the feet on the apex plate and rotating plus/minus 1.5 degrees on a slow sine. The headlamp is layered translucent red over a solid lamp with a faint beam thrown forward. Four chains hang from the torso as linked segments along quadratic curves, each swinging on the sway's frequency with its own phase lag behind the body, tipped with small ice points. Below the standing plate, five more tilted translucent ice diamonds cascade down the frame, each with a specular streak. The backdrop is deep blue darkened toward the corners by wide patina layers whose edges stay off-frame, with a cold pale halo of open air around the figure. The opening clock starts at 3.5 seconds, mid sway, the chains lagging to one side and the lamp lit. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no interaction handlers, no audio, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported resize listeners only. One fix: the first backdrop pass drew its vignette as four tight ellipses whose edges landed inside the frame, reading as dark discs floating behind the figure; the ellipses were widened, pushed outward and layered at lower alpha until the darkening reads as depth rather than shapes, and the central halo was enlarged and softened to match. Frame one now reads as specced at all three sizes: the small porter standing high on the first plate, red lamp lit, chains hanging between and beside the legs, the plate stack falling away beneath into blue negative space. On the phone the portrait design fills the screen almost exactly, the strongest of the batch's mobile frames.
