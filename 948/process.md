# No.948 "A Reef That Never Stops Growing" (study #116 refikanadol, PP109 "Coral Dreams")

## Concept

A reef that is mid-growth at every frame and never resets. Seeded coral colonies root along the bottom of a black seabed and grow upward by walker tips that extend, fork, and die; every length of branch is a recorded segment that slowly fades over the following minute or so, so the colony is a time-lapse of itself: bright new growth at the tips, older growth dimming toward the roots, black water above. On a seeded schedule a bleach wave sweeps down one colony, paling its skeleton and killing its tips; the colony immediately re-seeds at its root in the next palette hue and grows up through its own pale remains. Death is always fade, birth is always growth. No cuts, no clears, no text.

## Construction

- Single self-contained `index.html`, canvas 2D only, no external assets, no visible text (zero fillText; the only words are the `<title>`).
- Simulation: fixed timestep at 120 Hz (`STEP = 1/120`), driven by a seeded mulberry32 stream (seed 948109). At load the sim runs exactly 4200 pre-roll steps (35 s) synchronously, so frame one is a grown, still-growing reef and is byte-deterministic. In the browser a `typeof`-guarded rAF loop advances the same sim by elapsed time in fixed steps (capped at 30 steps per frame); pointer input is recorded as step-stamped events and consumed by the sim in step order, so drawn state never derives from `Date.now()` or wall-clock state.
- Colonies: 6 on desktop, 5 on phones (phone = `window.screen` min dimension < 700 px, aspect `h > w*1.15` fallback only). Each colony has a seeded root along the bottom (y at 0.80-0.96 of height), a seeded hue from the five-coral palette, and a seeded growth ceiling (0.30-0.48 of height) that keeps generous black water above.
- Walker tips: `{x, y, angle, depth, hue, noisePhase, speed, born, die}`. Each step a tip extends 0.13-0.19 px, steered by a 2D value-noise field sampled at its own per-tip phase (children inherit the parent phase with a small jitter), plus a spring pull toward straight up. Tips die at their seeded lifespan (2.2-4.2 s), at max depth 9, at their colony ceiling, or off the sides. Live tips are capped at 150.
- Branching: after 0.5 s of age a tip can fork, probability 0.0042 per step scaled down by depth; the parent is replaced by two children at depth+1 with a +/- (0.45-0.95) rad spread, a small perpendicular position offset, and jittered noise phases. Colonies also seed fresh root tips every 2.2-4.5 s, so growth never stops.
- Segments: when a tip has travelled 12 px since its last joint, a segment `{x1, y1, x2, y2, hue, born, fade, colony, depth, bleached}` is recorded. Segments draw as 2.6 px lines (1.8 px at depth 6+) with alpha `1 - age/fade`, fade seeded per segment at 60-90 s; dead segments are pruned. Segment cap 14,000: past the cap the oldest unbleached segments have their fade shortened (gone within about 5 s) rather than being cut, per the no-hard-reset rule. At frame one the reef sits at roughly 7,000 segments, under the cap.
- Rendering batches segments into buckets keyed by hue (or bleach), alpha band (8 bands), and width class, so a frame is a few dozen strokes. Live tips draw as a white-hot 2 px core over a 5 px halo in the colony hue. No gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas.
- Bleach waves: a global scheduler fires every 50-70 s of sim time at a seeded non-bleaching colony. The front is a horizontal pale band that sweeps from the colony ceiling down to its root over 6 s; segments it passes are flagged bleached (drawn in #F5F0E6 at alpha up to 0.5, fading over 150 s as skeletons) and tips it passes die. On completion the colony hue rotates to the next palette color and three fresh root tips seed. The first wave is scheduled at 55-70 s, after the pre-roll, so frame one is unbleached.
- Interaction: TAP blooms the nearest live tip (a radial flower of 6-8 petal rings in its hue, growing in 0.4 s, holding to 5 s, gone by 6 s); taps are disambiguated from double-taps with a 320 ms window. DRAG is the current: drag events set a field bias (direction and strength from the drag vector) that bends all tips; on release the bias decays exponentially with about a 10 s relax. DOUBLE-TAP seeds three fresh root tips per colony, growing up through the old fade.
- Sound: all WebAudio is lazy and typeof-guarded behind the first pointer gesture, so the piece renders complete in silence. A looped brown-noise swell through a lowpass filter (LFO on the cutoff) is the water; blooms play a soft glass ping (pitch derived from the sim step, never Math.random); a bleach wave plays a low sine sweep down. No microphone.
- Canvas size comes from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; a size change re-seeds and re-pre-rolls the sim deterministically. `draw()` pins `canvas.style.width/height` in px every frame. Page ground is the seabed color #0B0E17 at all sizes.

## QA log

- Renderer: `node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js` at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png), 720x720 (thumb.png). All render cleanly with no audio and no rAF.
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and `cmp` byte-compared identical after the final tuning round; the duplicate render was deleted.
- Fix rounds: (1) the first tuning grew long laminar ropes flowing sideways along the bottom, because every tip in a colony shared one noise field and followed the same streamline; (2) shorter, faster-forking branches helped but neighbouring ropes still braided together. Final fix: per-tip noise phases (children inherit with jitter) plus a stronger upward spring and weaker noise steering, which made forking, not streamlines, the dominant structure. Re-rendered at all three sizes after each round.
- Final frame one reads as specced: six branching colonies on desktop (five on mobile) in four of the palette hues, rooted along the bottom, white-hot tips at the branch ends, black water filling the top half or more, nothing clipped on mobile.
- Source greps clean: no Date.now, no Math.random, no shadowBlur, no canvas gradients, no fillText, no em dashes, no offscreen canvas.

## Deviations

- Colony hue is drawn per colony from the seeded stream rather than assigned in palette order, so two colonies can share a hue (frame one shows coral and lime twice each, four distinct hues). The brief allows 5-7 colonies over a five-hue palette, and frame one still lands in its "3-4 hues" reading.
- Bleach fronts sweep vertically (a horizontal band travelling from ceiling to root) rather than horizontally across the colony; the brief calls for "a pale horizontal front", and a horizontal front sweeping down the water column is the reading chosen.
- Each colony has a seeded growth ceiling so the reef keeps "generous black water above" at any aspect ratio; without it the fork-driven frontier eventually reaches the surface on tall phone frames.
- Root-cue dimming at frame one is mild: with 60-90 s fades, the oldest (35 s) segments sit near half alpha at frame one and the fade deepens over the first minute of viewing, as specced by the fade model.
