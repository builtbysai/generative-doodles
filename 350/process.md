# No.350 Helmet Cats

## Concept

After Emi Bee's MCM kitty cats, the ones "with and without space helmets": a parade of flat geometric cats marching across a paper frame. Circle heads, triangle ears, dome bodies, one paper eye each, tails up at the rear. Three of the six wear space helmets, drawn as a stroked arc dome over the head with a single highlight tick. Nothing else is in the frame: no ground line, no scenery, just the parade band centred on paper. Exactly three flat colours ever, paper #F4EFE3, ink #1C1A17 and teal #2E8B8B. Watch only: there is nothing to tap or drag, just the march.

## Construction

One canvas, everything as JS data, no offscreen layer, no gradients, no shadowBlur, no filters. The roster is a fixed table of exactly 6 cats (asserted), each row fixing its colour (alternating ink and teal), its helmet flag, its tail phase and its helmet bob phase. The helmet subset is exactly 3 (asserted). Cats sit at an even pitch of 256 design px, and the roster's total width is 1536, the 1280 design width plus exactly one pitch (asserted), so positions wrap modulo 1536 and the parade tiles seamlessly: at the standard speed of 64 design px per second the loop is 24 seconds, and the frame at 24s is pixel-identical to frame 0. Each cat is composed from primitives in local units: two legs swung in anti-phase off the travel distance (a full leg cycle every 128 px of travel, 12 per loop), a tail stroke swaying on 6 whole cycles per loop, a flat dome body, a circle head seated into the dome, triangle ears (tucked smaller on helmet cats so the tips stay inside the dome), one paper eye dot, and for the helmet cats a stroked dome arc in the contrasting colour with a paper highlight tick, bobbing on 3 whole cycles per loop. Every sine completes whole cycles inside the 24s loop, so no motion can break the seam. House rules throughout: Visual Viewport sizing with resize on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned every frame via style width and height in px, phone detection via window.screen min dimension under 700px with aspect fallback, centred fit at 1280x900 design space with the page ground in the paper colour, a constant-phase offset clock (7.3s into the loop) so the first synchronous frame is already mid-march and the rAF loop stays anchored to that constant plus elapsed time, typeof-guarded rAF, fixed table instead of runtime randomness, no text or UI in the piece.

## QA log

Rendered with node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png) and 720x720 (thumb.png), and all three inspected. The thumb was rendered twice and the two files are byte-identical, so the thumbnail reproduces exactly from the shipped script. The first draft failed the gate on readability: the head circle floated above the body dome with a band of paper between them, so each cat read as two separate shapes. The head was reseated lower and into the dome, the ears and helmet arc re-centred on it, and the legs lengthened slightly so the feet show under the dome; the shipped renders read as one cat per roster slot. Final renders confirmed: six cats at even spacing across the band (with the wrap, parts of the entering and exiting cats show at the frame edges), helmets on exactly three, highlight tick visible inside each dome, tails curved at the rear, generous paper margins above and below the band. Mobile keeps the centred band whole on continuous paper. Palette audit: only paper #F4EFE3, ink #1C1A17 and teal #2E8B8B appear in the file. Loop seam verified by construction: travel, leg, tail and bob cycles are all whole numbers over the 24s loop. No em dashes in the file. Not pushed; staged for fly's gate.
