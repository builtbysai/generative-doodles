# №1101 — Meandering River

onformative study block OF6 (Meandering River, 2018). A data river over satellite-dark terrain: three braided channels of cyan-teal particle flow in the upper half merge into one meandering main stem below, with gold sediment picked up at the bends, soft orange plume blooms on a slow pulse, and one oxbow that pinches off and fades each loop. No map furniture — the only marks besides the river are small functional tick marks along the right edge, onformative's functional-label habit reduced to marks.

## How it is built

- **Sim pattern (sanctioned):** fixed-timestep simulation, dt = 1/30 s, all randomness from one seeded mulberry32 stream. Frame one is the state after a **fixed synchronous pre-roll of 24 simulated seconds** (720 steps), so the thumbnail is reproducible; the browser then continues the same sim from that state via a typeof-guarded rAF accumulator. No `Date.now()`, no `Math.random()`.
- **Flow field:** three channel centerlines are analytic curves whose braid offsets are traveling sine waves (120° apart, one full temporal cycle per 60 s loop) inside an envelope that tapers to zero at the merge (s ≈ 0.5); below the merge a single stem carries a meander whose amplitude breathes on the same 60 s period, so the field itself loops seamlessly. A three-term analytic curl-noise potential (integer temporal frequencies) adds drift. Each particle is assigned a strand plus a small lateral home offset; velocity = downstream tangent flow + capped spring attraction to its strand point + curl + optional drag vortex.
- **Particles (~420):** 300 water particles drawn as short prev→pos segments (no canvas-fade), colored by speed, cyan #3FA8C9 at low speed lerping to teal #2FE0B0 when fast; 90 sediment particles spawned at five high-curvature bend stations, drawn gold #E8C77D and slower; a 30-particle plume pool released in 16-particle bursts every 10 s at alternating stations (merge, lower stem), orange #F5A66B at 0.5 alpha, layered soft dots, 6.5 s life. Because of the burst schedule and the 24 s pre-roll, the t = 20 s burst is mid-bloom in frame one.
- **Oxbow:** an envelope on the 60 s loop (rise 34–44 s, hold, fade 52–59.5 s) simultaneously narrows the s ≈ 0.70 meander and fades in a crescent of still teal/gold dots just outside the crest — the pinched-off lake.
- **Banks and body:** the channel glow is drawn, not accumulated: wide #16242B bank strokes under a low-alpha river-tint underlay along the same centerlines, with the particle density on top doing the rest. Terrain is flat #0D1418 with 24 seeded soft alpha patches in neighbouring tones.
- **Interaction:** drag injects a vortex into the sim step at the pointer (rotational velocity with a Gaussian falloff plus a weak inward pull); vortex energy ramps in while the pointer is down and decays over ~2.5 s after release, so the river bends and re-carves around the drag path and then settles. Watch-only otherwise.
- **House rules:** canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; canvas CSS size pinned in draw(); phone detection via `window.screen` min dimension < 700 with the aspect fallback; square stage centered and full-bleed terrain on phones. No offscreen canvas, no gradients, no ctx.filter, no shadowBlur, no createElement, no audio (none specced — silent). No visible text anywhere except the `<title>`; the edge ticks are marks, not text.
