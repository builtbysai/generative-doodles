# No.248 Secretariat Study

## Concept
After Bawa's Secretariat series and her Greyongrey prints: a hard edge geometric abstraction of Le Corbusier's Chandigarh Secretariat, stacked horizontal slabs, a brise-soleil grid and deep reveals, drawn in graphite on paper. The lines are admirably straight but hand drawn, wobbling one to two percent off true, the slight imperfection that paradoxically adds beauty, and the whole elevation is embossed a second time, offset by a breath, like a relief print. Strictly monochrome. A still drawing: tap reseeds a new abstraction of the same building grammar.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, full bleed paper. A seeded grammar builds the elevation: six to ten stacked slabs of varied heights fill most of the frame and crop off the right edge, leaving a generous paper margin on the left. Each slab carries a brise-soleil rhythm of vertical fins with a seeded skip probability; some floors are recessed and sparse, some get a mid rail, some a deep reveal line doubled under the slab top, and about one fin in eight is drawn heavy in oil-stick black as a deep reveal. A long roof slab runs past the left end wall, carrying a small rooftop block and a thin fin, and one diagonal ramp crosses the lower slabs. Every stroke is generated as a segmented polyline pulled toward its straight target with low amplitude seeded noise, one to two percent of its length, endpoints true, so nothing is machine perfect. The emboss layer is the identical geometry drawn first at a small offset in the pale ghost tone; the graphite pass follows on top. Tap or double tap advances the seed and rebuilds the grammar; there is no drag and no other motion. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no text, no colour anywhere.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; it passed on the first pass. Frame one reads as a graphite elevation study: nine slabs, the wobble visible in the long horizontals if you look closely, the ghost emboss ghosting a hair to the upper right of the heavier lines, the rooftop block and the ramp in place. On the phone frame the elevation turns portrait with it, the left margin and the cropping intact, fins still fine at 390 wide. The reseed handler is registered as pointerdown only, matching the spec: tap for a new abstraction of the same grammar, nothing else.
