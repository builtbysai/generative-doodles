# 8924 One Tenth — process

Seed 442, from the Goya Black Paintings study: one vast composed ground,
a single small figure pressed against the bottom edge, the ratio doing
all the work.

First version failed the seed's own test: covering the figure left a blank
beige page, not a composed field, and the figure read as a pebble. Fixed
both: the ground now carries a visible soft diagonal band plus a breath
of light off-center, and the figure became a head with cranium, lifted
snout, and one eye, gazing upward. Still about a tenth of the frame tall.

It stays very quiet. That is the discipline, not a lack of finish. Zero
console errors, holds at 390px.
