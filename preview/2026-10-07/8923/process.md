# 8923 Two Keys — process

Seed 485, from the Vallotton/Denis opposition: the same tree composed
twice, once in the committed black key and once in the committed white
key. No middle gray.

One shared skeleton per seed (trunk position, lean, canopy blobs) so both
panels show the same tree. Black key: solid black ground, tree as flat
white reserve, one diagonal white hatched band doing the work light would
do, white grass ticks. White key: near-white ground, canopy as overlapping
translucent gray washes, trunk as layered soft strokes, sparse specks for
the dissolve.

The failure to avoid was the filter look, so each half is drawn with its
own logic rather than inverted. Checked two seeds; both hold the contrast.
Zero console errors, reads in portrait 390px.
