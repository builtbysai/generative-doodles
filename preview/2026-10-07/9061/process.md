# 9061 Wrong Sky — process

Seed 484, from the Nabis as printmakers study: flat color zones laid first in
deliberately wrong hues, the drawing a guest confined to zones, one accent
used exactly once.

Built the zone layout first (sky band, two or three mid zones, foreground
band) and checked that the zones compose on their own before adding any
drawing. Four wrong-hue palettes, none with a blue sky. Guests are simple
flat shapes clipped to their zone: sun disc, birds, one house or tree per
mid zone, one or two walking figures. The vermilion accent is dealt to
exactly one guest per render.

First render had the figure floating at the foreground edge; grounded it
lower so it stands in the band. Checked three seeds; the accent landing on
a house against lavender (seed 424242) was the strongest, but the default
seed 906101 holds up. Zero console errors, clean at 390px.
