# No.220 Stitched Maps

## Concept
Kathy McTavish's count. map. pulse. breathe. programmed a robotic quilting machine to stitch maps across fabric, with looser counterpoint stitched by hand over the machine's precision; the textiles are the archive of a running machine. This piece runs that division of labour on muslin. The machine layer is precise: contour lines in indigo following a noise field, topographic stitching laid down as dashed running stitch from left to right. The hand layer follows in madder, visibly irregular: varying stitch lengths, jitter, short perpendicular drop-stitches, and small knot clusters where the hand worries a crossing. When a map completes it holds, then crossfades into the next map; the machine never stops. Watch only: there is no touch layer, because the record is the point.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Each map is precomputed as stitch segment lists in an abstract 1200 by 800 space, scaled to fit the frame and drawn directly; no offscreen canvas. The machine layer traces five contour levels of a three-octave seeded value-noise field by marching squares on a 48 by 32 lattice, chains the segments into polylines by shared endpoints, and resamples each polyline into uniform 11-unit stitches with 6-unit gaps, sorted left to right so the draw-on reads as the longarm travelling. The hand layer samples six jittered bezier sweeps into stitches of random 6 to 15 unit lengths with random gaps, adds drop-stitch ticks at about one stitch in eight, and plants four knot clusters of tiny overlapping segments along the curves. The loop runs thirty-eight seconds: draw-on over twenty-four (machine fraction ahead, hand fraction following), a hold, then a four second crossfade during which the next map is already starting under the needle. Frame one opens fifteen seconds in: the machine layer complete, the hand layer about half across with its knots showing. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback (on phones the scroll keeps its landscape shape, centred, the page ground the same muslin), rAF typeof guarded, no text anywhere.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. One adjustment at the gate: the first build opened thirteen seconds into the cycle, which left the hand layer only a third drawn and the frame reading as machine work alone; the opening clock moved to fifteen seconds so the madder counterpoint is properly under way with knots visible. Final renders show the stitched survey at every size: dashed indigo contours flowing as continuous topographic lines, the madder hand sweeps crossing them with irregular stitches and drop ticks, and the whole map flat on the muslin with no fabric texture faked.
