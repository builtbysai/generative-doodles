# No.285 Crater and Gloop

## Concept
After Sophia Golovanevskaya's crater and gloop glazes: a glossy black vessel skin seen so close that no vessel outline remains, only the surface itself, pocked with golden-brown crater rings like coral or cells under a microscope and interlaced with silver-grey cracked streaks that melt downward. The field is a slow-motion loop: the gold rings breathe and the silver streaks creep down. Tapping replants a new crater and gloop constellation with the same grammar; double-tapping regrows the silver streaks only. Nothing ever reloads.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, cover-fitted so the black skin fills the frame edge to edge, with the black as the page ground. The palette is held to the five specced colours only: black ground, crater gold, crater deep, silver streak and streak shadow. Crater centres come from a seeded scatter with a relaxed minimum distance, so the layout packs like a Voronoi field: each cell claims the ground around its centre and its rim radius is set from the distance to its nearest neighbour, and about ninety smaller satellite pocks fill the gaps. Each crater is drawn from data in layers: a faint deep halo where the glaze thickens, a thin deep wash at the floor with the black ground laid over it in proportion to that cell's own centre depth, one small dark pit offset from the centre, and the raised rim itself as a smooth closed oval whose outline wobbles on three seeded sine harmonics rather than faceted segments, squashed and turned per cell. Rim width varies widely from cell to cell, from under three pixels to over sixteen, and centre depth varies just as widely, which is what keeps the field reading as glaze rather than leopard print: no two craters carry the same ring weight or the same dark heart, and the black ground stays dominant between them. Gloop is layered translucent ellipses only, pooled toward the upper left of each crater floor in silver and gold at low alpha, plus nine broad faint sheen pools lying on the black skin itself. Silver streaks are vertical crack polylines held as data, a jagged spine stepping downward with short crack branches and a melt displacement per node, drawn as a shadow copy offset behind a bright core, running behind the craters so the two systems read as interlaced and then drawn once more at half alpha so some tips melt visibly over the black. On the loop each streak creeps downward on a sixty-four pixel cycle and its nodes sway further sideways as the creep grows, while each crater breathes on its own phase and rate. Interaction runs from a single pointerdown listener: a tap is held for a beat to see whether a second follows, a single tap then replants the whole constellation from the next seed, and a double tap regrows only the streaks from a fresh streak seed, leaving the craters in place. Frame one opens developed, with the clock offset so the rings are mid-breath and the streak birth time set back so the streaks are already fully grown. Pointer handling converts client coordinates to design coordinates through the stored cover-fit transform (S, OX, OY) set by frame(). House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds, no text, no audio, no offscreen canvas, no filters or shadowBlur, all glow and sheen as layered translucency.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported both resize listeners and the canvas pointerdown listener, and a grep confirms exactly the five specced palette colours and no em dashes. Two fixes during QA. First, the initial build threw on the satellite pocks, which carry no gloop highlights; they now carry an empty gloop list and the field renders. Second, the first successful render read wrong: the rims were faceted polygons, the centres filled mid-brown, and a concentric inner ring made the cells read as wood slices rather than crater glaze. The rim outline was rebuilt as smooth harmonic wobble, the centre was reworked so a thin deep wash sits under a heavy black overlay keyed to each cell's depth, the inner ring was replaced by a single offset dark pit, rim widths were spread wider, and the streak spines were straightened so they melt rather than strike like lightning. Final frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: a full-bleed glossy black skin pocked with smooth golden crater rings of widely varying weight around near-black centres, faint gloop sheen pooled inside the rings, and thin silver-grey streaks running down between and behind them, with craters running off every edge and no outline or text anywhere. On the phone the cover crop centres on the middle of the field and the craters and streaks stay legible.
