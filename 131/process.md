# №131 · Gather - process notes

## Concept
From Bug's planning-review prompts (seed 335, after j2rgb): a slime-mold agent field on warm paper. Thousands of Physarum agents weave angular trails into a reticulated network, and the only gesture is press-and-hold: the swarm gathers to the pointer, and on release a flat disc is stamped there and stays as an obstacle. The composition becomes a history of gathers, the network visibly rebuilding around each stamped disc.

## Construction
- Single self-contained page, no libraries. 2D canvas. One ink family, three values: ground warm paper #F2EDE3, trails mid ink #6B5D4F, stamped discs deep ink #2A2118. No gradients, no glow, no black background, no text or readouts on the canvas.
- Jones-model agents: 6,200 agents (5,200 on phones), seeded with mulberry32 seed 335. The trail map is a Float32Array grid at 0.5x render scale, fitted inside a 416x312 cap, held in plain JS and drawn on the main canvas only (no second canvas, no ImageData, which the node-canvas QA shim does not provide). Sensor angle 22 degrees, rotation angle 20 degrees, sensor offset 9 cells, step 1.7 cells, deposit 0.85 capped at 1.6. Each step an agent samples ahead-left, ahead and ahead-right, turns toward the strongest trail, moves and deposits; the map diffuses and decays every fourth step (centre-weighted 5-neighbour kernel, decay 0.90 per pass). Headings start on the 20 degree lattice the turns move in, so paths stay polygonal and angular rather than smooth. Positions wrap at the edges.
- Rendering is thresholded and crisp: the trail grid is drawn as batched fillRect runs per row in three bands (mid ink at two strengths, deep ink only where the trail saturates), with no smoothing, so diffusion never appears as a blur in the final look.
- Interaction: press-and-hold grows a gather radius from 26px to about 120px over roughly a second; agents inside it are pulled to the pointer. Release stamps a flat disc of that radius into a Float32Array obstacle mask on the same grid: agents sense through the mask, turn away on contact and deposit no trail on it. Stamped discs fade very slowly over minutes (disc alpha eases toward a 0.25 floor over about four minutes, the mask decays alongside). No disc exists in the initial state; discs are the viewer's marks only. Never click-to-reload.
- Pre-warm: 400 agent steps run before the first frame so the piece opens on a formed reticulated network, not dots on blank paper. Visual Viewport sizing with resize listened to on visualViewport and window; canvas pinned by style width and height in px in draw; phone detection via window.screen with aspect fallback; full-bleed at every size. A synchronous first frame is drawn at load and requestAnimationFrame is guarded so node-canvas QA works.

## QA log
- Frames rendered from the actual page script (node-canvas) at 1280x900 desktop, 390x844 mobile and 720x720 thumbnail size and inspected before shipping. Each render completes in about a second: the capped grid and every-fourth-step diffusion keep the 400-step pre-warm fast in Node.
- First pass of this build formed a dense network but read soft: low trail values rendered as a wide faint band, giving veins a blurred halo, and paths curved smoothly. Fixed by dropping the lowest render band and raising the thresholds, weighting the diffusion kernel toward the centre cell, and starting agent headings on the 20 degree turn lattice. Second pass shows a formed angular reticulated network in mid ink on warm paper at all three sizes: straight vein segments, polygonal cells, crisp edges, full-frame coverage, no disc in the initial state, nothing clipped. An earlier delegated build found in this folder was superseded by this build and its renders; recorded so the swap is not silent. Hans's real-device check is the final gate.
