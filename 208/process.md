# No.208 Message Wall

## Concept
For Messengers, Asia Ward built three tardigrade time capsules to hold thousands of visitor messages, with a wall where every contribution could be read. Here the contributions are light, not language: three plump amber water-bear vessels rest on a low horizon, each glowing faintly in the hue of what it holds, and behind them a reef-wall of small glowing marks accumulates into strata. Tap anywhere and a mark blooms at your touch, drifts up into the wall and settles; the nearest vessel pulses and its glow shifts a little toward your mark's position-hue. The wall remembers the whole session. Double-tap clears it back to three fresh empty vessels.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The wall is a column grid over the upper field: each column keeps a fill count, and a settled mark is a small #E8D8A8 quad with its own alpha and twinkle phase, the oldest rows drawn flattened and dimmed where they are absorbed into the base. Contributions spawn as flying particles that ease from the tap point to their grid cell with a bloom swell, then join the wall; the list is capped at 800, oldest shed first. Vessels are parametric tardigrade glyphs, five overlapping body segments in #C8A24B and #A8833B, four stub legs each and a snout, breathing on a slow scale pulse, with a radial glow behind each tinted by a hue that blends circularly toward every received mark's position-hue. A tap is committed after a short delay so a second tap inside the window reads as the double-tap reseed instead. Frame one opens with the wall pre-seeded into uneven strata and the vessels already holding contributions, so the piece starts as a wall in progress. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no text anywhere.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Round one fault: the seeded strata heights carried too much per-column randomness and read as a city skyline rather than sediment; the random term was cut and a second slower sine added, so the wall now rises in layered swells. Round one also had the vessel legs nearly hidden under the body, so the bodies sit higher and the legs hang longer, and the three vessels now read as creatures resting on the horizon, each inside its own tinted glow, teal, pink and green. The thumbnail keeps the strata, the horizon and all three glowing vessels legible.
