# No.226 Resonance Field

## Concept
McTavish's chance sound work is a networked orchestration, a sound mobile of small buffers of found sound, with a generated hum of sine waves hovering near four tonal centres and a minimalist soundscape for out-of-phase multiple channels. This piece is that system drawn instead of heard. Four ripple centres drift slowly on near-black ground, each throwing concentric rings that fade with distance; where the ring systems cross they moire. Small warm glyphs, broken-circle marks the colour of a chordal wash, orbit each centre at differing radii and rates: the found-sound buffers made visible. The piece stays silent. Her original mixes the buffers through the Web Audio API, but this gallery drawing of the system omits the sound itself, which suits her low-bandwidth, anti-spectacle discipline; the hum is present as pattern, not audio. It is a watch-only piece with no interaction at all, not even a reseed: the centres are never synced, and the out-of-phase drift is the whole composition.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Everything is a function of one clock. Each centre has a base position, a slow Lissajous drift (periods between fifty-seven and eighty-nine seconds, different per axis and per centre), its own ring spacing and expansion speed, and a phase offset, so the four ring systems never align. Rings are drawn as stroked circles whose alpha falls with the square of the remaining radius fraction, interference suggested by the layering of the transparent systems rather than by wave mathematics, which is cheap and looks right. Three glyphs per centre orbit on elliptical paths, drawn as partial arcs with a centre dot in the warm accent. The clock opens at forty-one seconds, so frame one is already mid-interference, rings from all four centres crossing and the glyphs mid-orbit. There are deliberately no pointer handlers: the piece does not take input. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, the field full-bleed on phones, rAF typeof guarded. No text anywhere.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. No adjustments were needed at the gate: the first renders already showed the meditative field the prompt asks for, four cyan ring systems interfering across the black, their centres visibly off-centre and out of phase, the warm glyphs small and sparse against the cool rings. The phone frame holds up as a full-bleed field with the same crossing systems. Nothing pulses to a beat and nothing asks to be touched.
