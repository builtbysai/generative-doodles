# No.1016 "The City, Edited"

Study #125 yuta-asai, PP177 "Ad-Null City" (Squarepusher "Terminal Slam" diminished reality).

## Concept

A night street, drawn rather than photographed, whose advertising surfaces refuse to stay advertisements. On a slow horizontal pan, designated ad slots on the buildings cycle between counterfeit generative posters (pure shapes: neon marks, halftone fields, black slabs of form, no letters, no brands) and total erasure: a flat white slab. Every edit is violent, not tidy. Each transition fires a datamosh burst of cyan and red macroblocks around the slot, with displaced smear bars inside it, decaying over 0.8 seconds. The city is quiet; the edit is loud.

## Construction

- Single self-contained `index.html`, canvas 2D, no external assets. Canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; `canvas.style.width/height` pinned in px on every draw. Phone detection via `window.screen` physical size (min dimension < 700px), aspect `h > w*1.15` as fallback only. Page ground is the night tone.
- City: two seeded building rows (far row at 0.45 pan factor, alpha 0.55; near row full) generated by mulberry32 from the city seed over a strip 3 viewport-widths wide, wrapping. Windows are a per-building grid whose lit/dark state and lit alpha come from an integer hash of (building, column, row): mostly dark blue, sparse lit yellow. Streetlights are hashed dots along the ground line.
- Edit-schedule model: slot state is a closed-form function of piece time. Six slots sit at fixed strip positions near the golden points, anchored to host buildings. Each slot runs a 12 s cycle (poster for 7 s, erased for 5 s) with a per-slot design phase; the poster variant index increments every cycle, so each pass composites a different forgery. Slot states are pure functions of t, so the whole edit history is deterministic per seed. A transition burst is active whenever the time since the slot's last cycle boundary is under 0.8 s; burst blocks are positioned and sized by an integer hash of (slot, cycle, boundary), so the burst is also closed-form.
- Frame one is the synchronous draw at fixed T0 = 11 s (mid-pan): phases were chosen so that at t = 11 two visible slots are erased slabs, one carries a live poster, and the third slot crossed into erasure at t = 10.6, leaving its burst mid-flare at age 0.4 s. The animation clock afterwards is T0 plus elapsed rAF time; frame one is never derived from Date.now().
- Interaction: drag across a poster erases it (the erase commits at press and on drag entry, so a stroke crossing a poster always catches it) with the mandatory burst and a digital crunch; tap a white slab composites a fresh counterfeit poster with a small burst and a synth mark; double-tap reseeds the city (buildings and slots regenerated from the next seed, edits cleared). User edits are recorded as per-slot override events {mode, variant, t0} and override the schedule until the next reseed.
- Sound: all WebAudio is lazily created inside the first pointer gesture and typeof-guarded. A low traffic hum starts with the first gesture; erasures play a descending band-passed noise crunch, counterfeits a bright triangle synth mark plus a short noise swell, bursts a noise swell. Scheduled (non-user) transitions trigger the same sounds once audio exists. The piece is fully visual and silent in headless QA.
- Palette is strict: night #05070D, buildings #0E1220, windows #1E2A4A / lit #FFD93B, slabs #FFFFFF, bursts #00E5FF / #FF2E4D, poster inks #FF2EA6 / #8A8D84 / #000000. No gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas.

## QA log

- Rendered with `node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js` at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png), and 720x720 (thumb.png). All render cleanly in silence (no rAF, no AudioContext in the harness).
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and compared with `cmp`: byte-identical. The duplicate render was deleted.
- Desktop read-back: dark drawn street of blocky buildings with sparse lit windows; two flat white slabs read first; one counterfeit poster live (halftone dot field with a magenta diagonal bar, pure shapes, no letterforms); cyan/red macroblocks and smear bars mid-flare around the freshly erased right-hand slab.
- Mobile read-back: same composition compressed; slabs stay prominent, poster and burst legible, nothing clipped.
- Deviations: (1) The burst's in-slot smear bars draw over a freshly erased slab for its 0.8 s decay, so the slab is only "flat, total" once the burst dies: the burst is specced as mandatory at the moment of erasure, and this is the overlap of the two requirements. (2) Slot rectangles are centered on their strip target and can overhang a host building edge slightly, like a mounted billboard; vertical placement and size derive from the host. (3) A tap on a poster also erases it, because erase commits at pointer press to make drag-erase reliable; the specced tap target (a slab) is unaffected.
