# No.243 Blue Punch

## Concept
After Bawa's Blue Scaffolds in Zbayul, Ladakh 2026: a deep blue scaffold structure compressed into the floor of a V shaped mountain gorge, packed so dense it burps, poles interpenetrating, the top mass bulging as if about to burst. One saturated hue against brown and grey rock, a thin pale slit of sky at the top. In her own phrase, a blue punch that pops out of the landscape. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1200 by 850 scene. The gorge is flat polygons: brown walls converging at bottom centre, grey facet planes, near-black shadow wedges down the inner edges and under the mass, and a sky band mixed flat from the palette's pale blue and rock grey. The scaffold is a parametric grid, six bays by six levels, drawn as line work: verticals extended a few units past each node so the poles interpenetrate, horizontals per level, and diagonals whose endpoints shift sideways with the compression parameter so the brace angles skew as the mass packs tighter. Width per level is a lerp from the floor width to a narrower top, plus a sine bulge across the middle levels. The fill rule is recursive in spirit and drawn literally: inside every bay cell, clusters of three short horizontal logs, each cluster shrinking by the 0.82 ratio within itself and again as the clusters climb. Six seeded burst poles poke out of the top. The breath is a twenty second sine: on the inhale the mass tightens (bulge and level height ease down) and brightens toward the pale blue; on the exhale it relaxes, widens and dims back to the deep blue. Twenty six seeded dust motes drift on slow sines. Frame one opens at ten seconds, mid exhale, the mass relaxed and bulging. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, shaped scene centred on the rock grey ground, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Frame one shows the blue mass wedged into the gorge floor, dense with diagonal bracing and the triple log fills reading as small pale dashes inside the cells, burst poles poking above the top rail, the walls converging behind it and the sky slit pale at the top. The phone frame letterboxes the scene on the rock grey ground and the mass stays legible at 390 wide. No adjustments were needed after the first render.
