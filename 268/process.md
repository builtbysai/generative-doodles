# No.268 Nduku

## Concept
After Shupiwe Chongwe's Bedforms wall sculpture: a wide horizontal rhythm of fragmented ceramic shards, matte black spheres and ovoids, and curved brown panels on a stark white gallery wall, the silhouettes abstracted from Ngoni weaponry. One shield shaped fragment carries tan raku veining beside a smooth black ovoid. The forms hang in near stillness from invisible pivots. Dragging through them sets them swinging, shadows swinging with them, until they settle slowly back to rest.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, centred fit on the gallery white ground with generous white above and below the band. Eleven forms sit across the middle third at varied scales and heights, none dead centre: angular shard polygons, flat matte spheres and ovoids with a faint grey crescent, curved brown panels drawn as bent bezier plates with a quiet inner curve, and one brown shield whose five seeded tan vein polylines are clipped to the shield path, placed beside a smooth black ovoid. Each form hangs from a shared invisible pivot line above the band, its centre found from its angle and length each frame. Motion is per form pendulum integration: gravity restoring proportional to the sine of the angle with a per form rate, velocity damped at 0.995 per frame scaled by elapsed time so a disturbance settles over about twenty seconds. Two forms open with small angles of 0.07 and minus 0.06 radians so frame one already shows displaced shadows. Shadows are drawn first for all forms as the same silhouette offset down and to the right plus a swing offset, filled in shadow grey at low alpha, with no blur, no filters and no offscreen canvas. Drag uses pointer down, move and up on the canvas: pointer positions convert to design coordinates through the stored centred fit transform, pointer velocity in design coordinates adds angular velocity to forms within reach with a squared falloff, and nothing ever reloads on click. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, a synchronous first frame, and rAF typeof guarded.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported the four canvas pointer listeners plus both resize listeners. Two fixes after the first pass: the shield first read as a teardrop with its veins tangled at the centre, so it was redrawn with a flatter top and shoulders tapering to the point and its five veins spread across its width; and the seeded shard polygons came out small and squashed and the panels read as barrels, so the shards became larger hand set angular fragments, the panels lost their bulge for a slight bow, and the sphere sheen was widened and softened. Frame one now reads as specced at all three sizes: eleven forms in a wide band across the middle third on gallery white, shadows offset and visible under every form, the two swinging forms showing displaced shadows, the veined shield clear beside the smooth black ovoid to its right, nothing clipped. On the phone the centred fit keeps the whole band in frame, small but complete.
