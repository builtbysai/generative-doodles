# No.251 Lost Glacier

## Concept
After A Sky for Henasku by tanzinchospel.art, Stanzin Chospel's digital abstraction made with Avantika Bawa, a meditation on a lost glacier. A digital horizon in stacked horizontal bands: pale sky, a white glacier band sitting at the horizon, dark rock below, deep shadow in the foreground. Over a slow thirty second loop the glacier thins and recedes upward, then restores. The sky band drifts almost imperceptibly. No objects, no text, no chrome. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, full bleed flat bands. The horizon sits at forty percent of the height; the glacier band's top edge is fixed just above it and a single melt parameter, driven by a sine ease over thirty seconds, scales the band's thickness down to forty percent of full at the worst of the melt, so the lower edge recedes upward and the rock shows through where the ice was. Band edges are softened with stacked low alpha strips of the band's own colour bleeding over each boundary: no gradient fills, no blur. Two faint pale streaks drift slowly across the sky, drawn in horizontal slices with a feathered alpha profile so they have no hard ends, wrapping at the edges. The ice carries three faint striation lines and four hundred and thirty seeded grain specks in the sky blue and shadow tones; a speck is drawn only while the thinning band still covers it, so the grain itself melts away and returns. The opening clock starts at seven and a half seconds, mid melt, the glacier already thinned to seventy percent and the loss visible against the rock. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. One fix: the sky drift streaks were first drawn as plain translucent rectangles and their hard vertical ends showed as faint blocks in the sky; they are now drawn in slices with a feathered alpha profile and read as slow, edgeless drift. Frame one reads as the photograph-like horizon the source suggests: pale sky, the white band with its grain and striations, brown rock, the near black foreground, nothing else. The phone frame is the same horizon full bleed, band proportions intact at 390 wide.
