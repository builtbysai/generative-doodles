# No.688 — Trescientos Sesenta

EXPANSION #83 lachimuchina, prompt block 8 "Trescientos Sesenta" (verified verbatim from TASKS.md).

## Concept

La Chimuchina's 360-degree performance format, the one used at the "Al rio Maipo" inauguration: the performers stand in a circle around the audience and the ensemble plays the space itself. Here the visitor sits at the center of a ring of 24 glyph figures on a dark field. Each figure periodically emits a resonance ring that travels inward toward the center; where rings converge near the middle their light adds up into a soft interference bloom. The center itself stays dark and quiet: it is the listening point, and it belongs to the visitor. There is no front, no stage, and no figure is ever singled out: anti-protagonism is structural, not decorative.

## Construction

- **Scene.** Ground #0E0C0A. A circular field is centered in the viewport with diameter equal to the smaller viewport dimension (field radius FR = 0.47 x min(W,H)). A faint warm boundary circle marks the field and a fainter circle marks the emitter radius. 24 figures sit at 0.8 FR, seeded glyphs in figure dark #2A211D with a thin ring-warm outline at low alpha so they read as barely lit marks: four glyph kinds (notch, bar, dot cluster, ring-dot) with seeded rotation and size jitter from mulberry32 (seed 688001). No two figures are lit longer or brighter than another; when a figure emits it shows a small ember #B0532A point that fades within 0.9 s.
- **Inward-ring model.** A ring is a concentric circle centered on the field center. It is born at the figure radius and its radius shrinks as FIGR x (1-u)^1.12, where u is age/travel, so it eases as it converges. Alpha follows a sin^0.75 envelope over the journey and the stroke color shades from ring warm #E8B96A toward bloom #F5D9A8 in the inner half. Rings are stroked with the canvas 'lighter' composite so overlapping light accumulates, and while a ring is inside the inner third (u > 0.62) it also lays down a faint filled disc of its current radius; successive discs pile warmth at the center as the interference bloom. Rings fade out completely before reaching radius zero, so the exact center is never drawn on.
- **Idle schedule.** A slow activation wave makes one revolution in 24 s. Figure i emits at phase i x (24/24) s within every period, with a fixed seeded strength jitter (0.85 to 1.15) that repeats identically each revolution. Idle ring travel time is 5.0 s, so about five rings are in flight at any moment and frame one already shows rings mid-journey with the wave at angle 0 (figure 0 at the top, just emitted). The whole idle system is a pure function of elapsed time on the anchored clock: no integration state, no wall clock.
- **Conduct mechanic.** Pointer events are timestamped with the loop's elapsed time and stored as emission events {figure, t, kind}. A tap near a figure (within max(26px, 0.05 x S)) cues it: a brighter (strength 1.55), faster ring (3.4 s travel, slightly wider stroke) plus a soft tone. Dragging in a circle conducts: whenever the drag's angle comes within 0.1 rad of a figure's angle (and that figure has not been conducted in the last 0.3 s), the figure emits a strength-1.15 ring at 4.2 s travel with a quieter tone, so sweeping around the center plays the ring as a round in sequence. Drags starting within 0.25 FR of the center do not conduct: the listening point stays quiet.
- **Audio.** WebAudio is created lazily inside the first pointerdown only, typeof-guarded and try/caught, never autoplayed. Tones are a sine fundamental with a quiet octave partial, 1.8 s decay envelope; pitch steps around the ring, 98 Hz rising two octaves across the 24 figures. Peak gain 0.08 for a tap cue, 0.045 for conducted emissions. Where no AudioContext exists (including the headless QA renderer) the piece is simply silent.
- **House rules.** Single self-contained file, canvas 2D only, no external assets, no absolute URLs, no date strings, no em dashes, no visible text except the title. Sizing comes from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; canvas style width/height are pinned in px inside draw() every frame. Phone detection via window.screen (min dimension < 700px) with the h > w x 1.15 aspect fallback; the circle refits a phone frame centered with diameter = min dimension and the page ground takes the piece's ground tone. rAF is typeof-guarded and the loop is anchored as elapsed-since-load; frame one is the synchronous draw at t=0. No microphone input.

## Deviations

- The prompt describes rings emitted by individual figures traveling inward; physically distinct per-figure circles collapsing toward the center would either converge on their own figure (wrong direction) or drift. The piece models each emission as a concentric circle born at the figure radius and shrinking to the center, which is the reading that keeps every figure equivalent, keeps the interference at the shared center, and keeps the listening point exact. Recorded here per house practice.
- The interference bloom is helped by faint filled convergence discs in the inner third (layered alpha, the house substitute for glow), because one-pixel strokes alone converge too sparsely to read as a bloom at thumbnail size. The discs use only the ring/bloom palette tones at low alpha.
