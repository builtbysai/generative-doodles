# No.278 Smoke Resist

## Concept
After Njalikwa Chongwe's wax-resist raku firing: a cream vessel stands centred on a near-black ground while dark smoke streams across it and lays black soot everywhere on the vessel except where wax resist has been painted. The resist pattern stands cream against the soot. Dragging paints wax-resist strokes onto the vessel and the resist is permanent within the piece: there is no eraser and no undo. A double-tap clears the soot and picks a new smoke direction, and the resist itself stays in place through the re-fire. Frame one opens already fired: soot is deposited over part of the vessel and one seeded resist band stands cream across the belly, so the mechanism reads before the first drag.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, centred fit so the vessel keeps its shape at any aspect, with the near-black ground as the page ground. The palette is held to the four specced colours only: the near-black ground, vessel cream, soot black and the thin resist edge ochre. The vessel stands 674 design pixels tall, 75 percent of the frame height, centred at x 640 with its rim at y 113 and its foot at y 787. Its silhouette is a piecewise lathe width function, fourteen knots of height fraction against half width, smoothstep interpolated and sampled at 72 steps per side, giving a narrow neck, a swelling shoulder, a wide belly and a settled foot. Everything is data, with no offscreen canvas anywhere: the resist mask is a list of stroke polylines flattened into segments, each segment indexed into a coarse 48 pixel grid bucket, and a deposit point counts as masked when its distance to a nearby segment is within that segment half width plus a small margin. The soot is a stored list of deposit points, each one tested against the vessel silhouette, drawn each frame as a small soot square clipped to the profile path. Painting a new stroke also purges the deposit points beneath it, so fresh wax stands cream at once over old soot. The seeded band is a gently sagging 38 pixel stroke across the belly, laid down before the opening soot is seeded, and the opening soot is some 5200 seeded points spread through the silhouette, denser on the left where the first left to right smoke arrives, and never inside the band. The live smoke is 800 particles riding a seeded curl-ish flow along the current smoke direction at 130 design pixels per second, with a perpendicular sine curl; the particles themselves are never drawn, and a particle inside the vessel and outside the mask has a steady chance each frame of leaving one deposit point, up to a 9000 point cap, so no smoke ever shows outside the silhouette. Each resist stroke is finished with a thin 2 pixel edge line in the ochre so the painted wax reads against both cream and soot. A double-tap, two taps within 400 milliseconds and 60 design pixels, empties the deposit list, picks a new direction from a fixed set of angles that never repeats the current one, and respawns the particle stream across the field; single taps and drags only paint. Pointer coordinates convert from client space to design space through the stored centred fit transform. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds, no text, no audio, no offscreen canvas and no filters or shadowBlur.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported the four pointer listeners plus both resize listeners, and a grep confirms exactly the four specced palette colours in the script, no offscreen canvas element, no gradients and no em dashes. Two fixes. First, the seeded band runs before the soot in build order and its purge step touched the deposit list before that list existed; the list is now declared ahead of the mask section and the run renders clean. Second, the first render's soot read as thin speckle, too sparse for laid soot: the seed was raised from 3200 to 5200 points and the deposit square from 6.8 to 7.4 pixels, and the vessel now reads properly darkened, near solid at the neck, thinning toward the right where the smoke has had less time. Final frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: a cream bottle vessel at 75 percent of frame height on the near-black ground, soot laid across it, and the seeded resist band standing clean cream across the belly with its thin ochre edge line. On the phone the centred fit keeps the whole vessel in frame with the band still legible and nothing clipped. No further fixes were needed.
