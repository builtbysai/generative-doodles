# No.1046 "A Thousand Devices, One Score"

Study #128 kyohei-mouri, PP207 "Coded Field" (Coded Field 2019: 1,000 balloon-shaped handheld devices with GNSS/LED/speakers whose light and sound change with location, omni-wheel autonomous light carts, the audience's movement synchronizing light and sound).

## Concept

The venue as a shared instrument: 1,000 players who do not know they are playing. A dark temple courtyard seen from above. Across it, a thousand tiny lights drift in slow currents, and each light takes the color of the zone it currently stands in, because the score is the map: five territories, five colors, five pentatonic voices. The crowd is not simulated as people; the devices are lights first, and the piece is the luminous field they make together. Through the crowd, three autonomous light carts drive real waypoint routes around the temple, threading between the people and the anchor, and two drones cross overhead with short streaks. The temple sits small and dark at the center: everything routes around it with respect.

The visitor can join the crowd: drag, and devices stream toward your finger's path, a current of your own. Tap a zone and its tone solos for five seconds while its lights brighten. Double-tap and the night reseeds: a new crowd, a new map.

## Construction

- **World**: logical 1000x1000 courtyard, contain-fit and centered (the No.1040 plan model), page ground is the courtyard tone #070809. Temple drawn in dark lines #1C1E24 at the center; dots are physically kept out of the temple rectangle in the sim.
- **Zones**: five Voronoi territories from seeded sites (four quadrant-ish, one near the heart, each jittered by the seed). Zone colors by site: warm #FFE9B8, rose #FF9D9D, green #9DFFB3, cool #7FD4FF, warm #FFE9B8 (the two warm territories are never adjacent). A dot's color is looked up from its position at draw time, so crossing a boundary changes its color on the exact frame it crosses. Zone boundaries are cached at build time on a 20-unit grid (cells whose right or down neighbor belongs to another zone) and drawn as faint points, #FFFFFF at 0.05.
- **Crowd flow**: fixed-step sim at 30 Hz. Each dot advects through a layered sine flow field (three terms per axis, spatial periods of 1000/2, 1000/1.7, 1000/3 and 1000/1.3, slow time phases) scaled by a per-dot speed factor (0.6 to 1.4), plus a gentle radial breathing term on the 65 s loop period so the field slowly gathers and releases. Dots wrap toroidally at the courtyard edges and are pushed out of the temple rectangle along the shallowest exit. A small closed-form wander offset (3.2 units, per-dot phase) is added at draw time so the field shimmers without disturbing the sim. Frame one is the synchronous draw after exactly 780 pre-roll steps (t = 26 s), by which time the uniform seed has organized into visible density streams and clumps along the flow cells.
- **Loop closure, honestly**: the carts and drones are exact (below). The crowd is a continuous flow, not a loop: its state at t + 65 s resembles but does not equal its state at t, because advection through a time-varying field is not periodic dot-for-dot even though every ingredient (flow phases excepted at incommensurate rates, breathing term) is near-periodic. The piece is specced as a seamless 65 s loop for the machines; the crowd's refusal to repeat exactly is the living part of the field, and it is recorded here rather than papered over.
- **Carts**: the No.1040 route model at LOOP = 65: three concentric rectangular rings around the temple (half-extents about 160x122, 295x228, 428x332, seed-jittered), one cart per ring, eight axis-aligned waypoints per ring, constant velocity, hard dwell (1.0 to 1.5 s) at each waypoint, timeline normalized to exactly 65 s, omni translation only (bases never rotate). Drawn as dark bases with layered-alpha white domes, brighter than any crowd dot.
- **Drones**: two closed-form crossings. Drone A crosses from (-70, 175) to (1070, 645) over 65 s; drone B from (1070, 830) to (-70, 295) over 32.5 s with a phase offset, so both are mid-courtyard at frame one. Each is a warm #FFE9B8 light at 0.9 with a four-segment fading streak behind it along its velocity.
- **Drawing performance**: dots are filled rects only, two passes (halo at 0.15, core at 0.7), batched by zone color with a single fillStyle per zone per pass; no gradients, no shadowBlur, no per-dot paths. Zone populations are counted during the core pass and feed the audio.
- **Interaction**: drag records pointer points with sim-time stamps; in the step, dots within 150 units are pulled toward recent points with strength falling off over the points' 1.1 s life (the visitor becomes a current). Tap soloes the tapped zone for 5 s (its dots brighten to 0.95, others dim to 0.45, its voice comes forward). Double-tap reseeds the world (sites, crowd, cart rings, boundaries) and re-runs the pre-roll, so the new night opens equally developed. A phone-class resize rebuilds at the phone dot count and re-pre-rolls.
- **Sound** (lazy AudioContext inside the first gesture, typeof-guarded, silent in QA): five sine voices at A minor pentatonic frequencies (220, 261.63, 329.63, 293.66, 392 Hz), one per zone, gain following the zone's live population share under a slow generative swell; solo brings the tapped zone to the front. A triangle cart glide near 96 Hz tracks how many carts are moving; a faint 1240 Hz drone whir sits under everything; sparse, very faint ticks at doubled zone frequencies stand in for individual devices. No microphone anywhere.
- **House rules**: canvas size from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; canvas style width/height pinned in px inside draw(); phone detection via window.screen min dimension < 700 with aspect fallback only; deterministic first frame (no Date.now anywhere; the only Math.random-free hash in the audio tick picker is a sine hash of sim time); rAF typeof-guarded with a fixed-step accumulator capped at 8 steps per frame; zero visible text except the title.

## Deviations

- Phone dot count is 600 instead of 1000 (draw and sim cost on small devices), as the brief allows; desktop and thumb keep the full 1,000. Recorded here and in the code path (phoneNow branch in buildWorld).
- The courtyard uses contain fit (whole plan visible, courtyard tone filling the rest of the frame) rather than a cropped cover fit, following the No.1040 plan presentation: at 390 wide a cover fit would crop more than half the courtyard horizontally and could take the carts out of frame. The field still fills the short dimension edge to edge.
- Two of the five zones share the warm color (the palette offers four zone colors for five territories); the two warm sites are placed non-adjacently so the territories still read as distinct regions, and each keeps its own pentatonic voice.
