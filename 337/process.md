# No.337 Cutter Stack

## Concept

After MyClayCo's Animal/Bird stack cutters: their scale-stack merchandising read as art. One hand-authored bird profile, a plump forward-leaning bird with a notched beak and a flicked tail wedge, is drawn as exactly seven concentric outlines at a one-and-a-half size progression, like a set of nested cutters laid out on paper. Nothing is filled; the stack is pure line. The whole set breathes on a twenty-second loop, handles swaying gently, so the stack reads like a cut being pressed and released. Watch only: there is nothing to tap, drag or fix, just the press and release.

## Construction

One canvas, everything as JS data, no offscreen layer, no gradients, no shadowBlur, no filters. The profile is seventeen hand-placed anchor points, deliberately a little irregular so the wobble is authored in rather than smoothed away. Each frame the anchors take a small elliptical offset from a fixed seed table held as data (static jitter plus a sine wobble per point), handles are derived Catmull-Rom style with per-anchor tension, low at the beak notch and the tail tip so those points stay crisp, and then take their own seeded offset. Every sine in the piece completes whole cycles inside the 20s loop (the table's cycle counts are integers 1 to 3, and the global breath is one cycle per loop), so frame 20s is pixel-identical to frame 0 and the seam cannot show. The wobbled pose is computed once per frame in local units and stroked at seven asserted scales, ratio exactly 1.5 between neighbours, all sharing the profile's bounding-box centre, so the outlines nest and never cross. The fourth (middle) outline is Sculpey teal #2E8B8B; the other six are ink #1C1A17 on paper #F4EFE3, and nothing else appears. Stroke width grows gently with outline size. House rules throughout: Visual Viewport sizing with resize on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned every frame via style width and height in px, phone detection via window.screen min dimension under 700px with aspect fallback, centred fit at 1280x900 design space with the page ground in the paper colour, an offset clock so the first synchronous frame is already mid-breath, typeof-guarded rAF, fixed seeds, no text or UI in the piece.

## QA log

Rendered with node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png) and 720x720 (thumb.png), and all three inspected. The first three profile drafts failed the gate on readability: the first two read as a leaf or seed pod (body too elongated, tail a long thin spike dominating the left side), and the third, a cocked wren tail, overshot in the tail cluster and threw a long thin blade across the stack. The shipped profile fixes all three: a plumper body, a higher and more convex back, a shorter and thicker tail wedge, and lowered handle tension across the tail anchors. Final renders confirmed: exactly 7 outlines, cleanly nested with no crossings, beak notch visible on the right of the larger outlines, tail flick at the back left, teal on the fourth outline only, generous paper margins on every side with the largest outline inside the frame at the rendered mid-breath pose. Palette audit: only paper #F4EFE3, ink #1C1A17 and teal #2E8B8B appear in the file. Loop seam verified by construction and by inspection of the seed table: all cycle counts are integers over the 20s loop. No em dashes in the file. Not pushed; staged for fly's gate.
