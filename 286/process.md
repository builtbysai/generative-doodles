# No.286 Belly Crack

## Concept
After Sophia Golovanevskaya's sodium-silicate crackle: a pale slip-white vessel in side profile on a dark ground, its skin split into a geometric network of straight fracture segments, like cracked dry mud on a pot. The belly slowly swells and the crack network grows with it, propagating outward from the belly; when the belly relaxes, the cracks hold. Tapping re-seeds a new fracture constellation and replays the swelling; double-tapping holds the belly swollen. Nothing ever reloads.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, centred fit with the dark ground as the page ground. The palette is held to the five specced colours only: slip white for the vessel, crack line and crack deep for the fractures, the dark ground, and the warm rim light used for one subtle rim stroke. The vessel is a lathe profile: half widths are control points against height fraction, sampled with Catmull-Rom so the curve stays smooth, and the vessel stands 70 percent of the frame height, centred. The swell is a gaussian weight centred on the belly that widens the profile by up to a fifth at full swell and nowhere else. The crack network is a polygonal fracture graph held purely as edge data: nodes sit on a jittered grid in vessel coordinates, position across the local profile width against height fraction, so the whole network stretches as the belly swells. Edges join grid neighbours, most cells left quadrilateral like dried mud plates with a seeded diagonal in a few, and every segment is straight, so the network reads geometric rather than organic. Each edge carries a propagation threshold based on its distance from the belly centre, so cracks appear at the belly first and spread outward in proportion to the swell; a partly grown edge is drawn partway along its length. Cracks are drawn clipped to the silhouette in three batched passes: a wide under-stroke in the crack line colour, a thin slip line offset to one side so each crack keeps a pale edge, then the dark core in crack deep. The warm rim light is two translucent strokes down the right profile edge, a wide faint one under a narrow stronger one. The swell runs on a 14 second cycle on a fixed tick: the belly rises, holds at full swell, then relaxes partway and rests, while the crack reveal tracks the highest swell reached in the first cycle and then holds for good, so relaxing never un-grows the network. The clock starts 3.4 seconds in, so frame one opens with the belly partway swollen and the network already partly grown. A tap waits out a 380 millisecond double-tap window, then re-seeds the graph with a fresh fixed seed and restarts the cycle; a double-tap toggles the hold, easing swell and reveal to full and keeping them there until the next double-tap. Quiet construction rings at the neck and foot and a dark mouth ellipse with a slip rim finish the form. Pointer handlers convert client coordinates to design coordinates through the stored centred-fit transform (S, OX, OY) set by frame(). House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds, no text, no audio, no offscreen canvas, no filters or shadowBlur, glow and shading only as layered translucency.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported both resize listeners and the three canvas pointer listeners, and a grep confirms exactly the five specced palette colours and no em dashes. Two fixes after the first render: the profile had a pointed belly and a kinked shoulder, so the control points were densified around the belly and shoulder; and the first network read as a narrow triangulated mesh band with stray part-grown stubs floating in empty slip, so the grid was widened to the full surface, most cell diagonals were dropped in favour of quadrilateral plates, and the propagation thresholds were compressed so the frontier stays connected. Final frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: a smooth slip-white vessel on the dark ground, belly partway swollen, a straight-segment geometric crackle of mud-plate cells grown across most of the skin and still short of the edges, the warm rim light faint on the right profile, no extra vessels and no text. On the phone the centred fit keeps the whole vessel in frame and the crackle stays legible. No further fixes were needed.
