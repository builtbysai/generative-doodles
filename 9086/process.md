# №9086 · Shear

A hard-edge geometric composition of diagonals, in the concrete art tradition after Lucien Groen. Companion to №9080 "Concrete" and №9081 "Orbit" — the diagonal voice of the family.

## Concept

Diagonal bars, triangular wedges, and thin lines clustered along a diagonal energy band, with deliberate negative space elsewhere. Asymmetric and dynamic — the composition leans. Ivory ground (#E6E1D3), restrained inks (black, warm greys, one ochre), a single deep teal accent (#1F5A5A). No orthogonal grid anywhere: this is the anti-9080.

## Motion

Tension in movement:
- Bars glide along their own long axis on slow sine drift
- Triangles snap-rotate 90° on staggered cycles (eased)
- Tap flips the nearest triangle or re-angles the nearest bar, with a soft pop

## Palette

Ivory ground #E6E1D3; ink #1F1E1A, umber #3A382F, warm grey #7A766A, ochre #C08A2D; single deep teal accent #1F5A5A.
