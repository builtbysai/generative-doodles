# No.1030 — The Flock, Wired

Study #126 tomoaki-yanagisawa, PP191 "Ball Swarm" (24 Drone Flight).

## Concept

A flock of 24 skeletal drones flies in a flat, total paper-white void. Each drone is drawn minimal — a thin dark quad outline with four faint rotor discs — and each carries a glowing white ball on top: the light rides in a ball, per Yanagisawa's recurring module. The flock wheels as one mind (boids), and on a timer it gathers into formations — ring, wave, column — holds each for a breath, then dissolves back into the wheel. Ball brightness obeys the flight: climbing drones glow toward pure white, diving drones dim. It is birds, not billboards: no LED patterns, no sky text, no product-shot drones, and the void is flat #F6F6F4 with no sky, clouds, horizon, or gradient.

## Construction

- **Sim.** Fixed-step boids at 120 Hz (dt = 1/120) in a fixed 240x240-unit virtual world, mapped to the canvas with a fit scale (min dimension x 0.96) so the formation always completes inside the frame, phone included. Forces: separation (radius 9), alignment and cohesion (radius 20), a tangential swirl around the flock centroid (the wheeling), per-drone seeded sinusoidal wander, and soft containment steering beyond radius 96 from center. Acceleration clamped to 150 u/s^2; speed clamped 12-30 u/s in free flight.
- **Formations.** An integer-step scheduler runs an 11 s cycle: free flight 2.5 s, gather 2.5 s, hold 4 s, release 2 s, cycling ring → wave → column. During gather/hold/release a formation weight w (smoothstep ramps) drives a spring to each drone's slot (k = 6.0) with slot-velocity feed-forward computed by finite difference of the slot function, while boid weights scale down by (1 - 0.85w) and the minimum-speed clamp relaxes toward 2 u/s so drones can settle onto moving slots instead of orbiting them. Slots: ring radius 58 rotating slowly; wave = a travelling sine line spanning x 30-210; column = a vertical file spanning y 34-206 with a gentle sway. All slot margins keep formations complete in frame.
- **Balls and climb.** Each drone's climb value is a smoothed function of its vertical velocity (-vy). Ball rendering is layered flat-alpha circles only (no gradients, no shadowBlur): three #E4EEFF halo discs plus a #FFFFFF core, offset above the frame, with alpha/radius scaled by climb brightness (0.45 floor so diving drones stay present, up to full white on a hard climb). While the visitor drags through the flock, an alarm envelope dims all balls by up to 45 percent.
- **Frame one.** The piece pre-rolls exactly 2400 fixed steps (20 s) and draws synchronously. With the scheduler above, step 2400 lands exactly at the start of the wave formation's release (w = 1): the render shows the full wave held, drones settled on their slots, balls at varied climb brightness. No wall clock, no Date.now, no Math.random anywhere in the sim; all randomness is mulberry32 seeded (base seed 1030191, overridable via ?seed= for testing).
- **Interaction.** Drag through the flock: a repulsion field (radius 34 units) veers drones around the pointer while the alarm dim runs; the flock re-forms behind the finger. Tap: advance immediately to the next formation's gather. Double-tap: reseed a new flock (size 22-26, new swirl character) with a fresh 2400-step pre-roll. Pointer state is event-driven; frame one is always the untouched pre-roll.
- **Audio.** Lazy and typeof-guarded, created only inside the first pointer gesture; the piece is silent in QA. A looped seeded-noise buffer through a bandpass forms the airy wash (frequency/gain follow smoothed flock speed, with a flutter term while dragging). Entering a formation hold starts a soft three-sine resolving chord (196 / 261.63 / 329.63 Hz) that releases when the hold ends; ending a drag plays a settling tone (320 → 170 Hz).
- **House rules.** Single self-contained index.html, canvas 2D, no external assets or URLs, no visible text except the title. Canvas size from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; canvas.style.width/height pinned in px inside draw() every frame. Phone detection via window.screen (min dimension < 700 px) with the h > w*1.15 aspect fallback only; page ground is the void white; the world-to-screen fit keeps the flock centered with room for a complete ring at 390 px wide. requestAnimationFrame is typeof-guarded and steps the fixed-step sim from an accumulator (capped at 10 steps per frame).

## QA log

- Rendered with node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png), 720x720 (thumb.png). Renders cleanly in silence; only resize/pointer listeners register.
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and byte-compared with cmp — identical. Duplicate deleted.
- First read-back: the flock read as a loose ring and slightly sparse — frame one landed at the first step of a hold before the springs had settled, and the 12 u/s minimum-speed clamp kept drones orbiting their slots. Fixed by retiming the scheduler (cycle shortened to 11 s so step 2400 lands at a release start with w = 1 after a full 4 s hold), relaxing the minimum speed toward slot speed under formation weight, stiffening the slot spring (k 6.0, damping 2.4), reducing boid weight under formation to 15 percent, and enlarging the drone drawing (frame half-size 3.1 units, ball core 2.2 units).
- Final read-back: desktop and thumb show all 24 drones settled on the wave formation across the middle of the flat paper-white void, each a dark skeletal quad with faint rotor discs and a glowing white ball above it, brightness varying with climb; nothing clipped. Mobile shows the complete wave inside the 390 px width, flock whole and centered.

## Deviations

- Formation cadence is an 11 s cycle rather than the prompt's approximate 14 s timer, chosen so the fixed 2400-step pre-roll ends exactly on a fully held formation (the prompt's frame-one requirement) while keeping the specced 4 s hold. The timer is step-based rather than separately seeded; the flock itself, the formation order, and per-flock style remain seeded.
- Ball glow is built from layered flat-alpha discs instead of a gradient sprite, per the house no-gradient rule; the halo color #E4EEFF is the specced glow color.
- Drone count is exactly 24 for the shipped flock; the specced double-tap reseed varies it (22-26) as "new flock size."
