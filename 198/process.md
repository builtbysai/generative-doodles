# No.198 Brake-Light Wings

## Concept
Willshire's Monarch is a butterfly whose colourful wing parts are not paint but salvage: brake lights and turn signals, domed translucent lenses in red and amber set into a dark steel frame. The piece builds her at that level of honesty. The wings are a mosaic of lens panels clipped inside a steel vein frame, each panel carrying a soft domed highlight, and the mosaic itself is the only pattern she gets. On a thirty second loop she beats her wings slowly, holds while light drifts across the domes, takes the hail strike she once documented, three panels cracking and dimming, and is then repaired panel by panel, re-glossed as if re-epoxied. Drag a wing tip and she tilts, the panels on the lit side flaring.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Each wing is two smooth closed outlines (forewing and hindwing) drawn through hand-set points with quadratic smoothing, filled first in frame steel #23262B. The panel grid is walked in wing unit space and a cell becomes a panel when its centre falls inside either outline; panels are drawn clipped to the outlines so the mosaic fits the wing exactly, each panel inset to leave the steel showing as veins. Panel colour is assigned by radial banding from the body with seeded jitter, brake red #C81E1E and amber #F59E0B alternating, so the pattern is fabrication, not illustration. The dome is faked with a radial highlight clipped inside each panel, swelling when the specular sweep passes or when tilt lights that side. The loop runs thirty seconds: two slow beats in eight seconds (wings folding to sixty degrees by horizontal compression about the body axis), a ten second hold with the sweep crossing, cracks blooming on three chosen panels across two seconds (branching polylines in #0A0A0A over fills mixed toward grey), a three second hold, then repairs staggered two seconds apart until every panel is glossed again. The clock starts at twelve and a half seconds, mid hold with the sweep crossing, so frame one is the spread butterfly lit. Tilt is drag driven with an eased return to level. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen, the butterfly keeps its shape on phones with the page ground at the piece's own near black, rAF typeof guarded, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three, through three rounds. Round one fault: panels were drawn unclipped, so square cells overhung the wing outline and read as pixel art with stray squares floating at the tips. Round two clipped the panels to the wing outlines and shortened the body, which had stretched into a candle below the wings, but the fore wings rendered as empty steel blobs: the clip path was built by two path calls and the second beginPath wiped the first, so only the hindwing survived the clip. Round three made the path builder append subpaths for the clip, and the final frames show the full mosaic seated in smooth steel frames on both wings, dome highlights riding the panels, the sweep brightening a diagonal band across the right wing at frame one.
