# No.839 — Not Bloody Likely

EXPANSION #102 john-cale, PP8 "Wrong Way Up". Title shipped is the prompt's title suggestion, "Not Bloody Likely".

## Concept

The *Wrong Way Up* cover (Eno/Cale, 1990) rebuilt as a layout system. On the olive ground, a border of repeated "ENO/CALE" text runs around all four edges in orange. Inside it, two flat portrait busts lie head-to-toe: the upper in Cale-dark tones, the lower in Eno-light tones as its exact 180-degree counterpart, their noses nearly touching at the center seam, where a row of nine small steel daggers stands. Purple lightning zigzags sit in the top-left and bottom-right corners. The busts are profile forms, shapes rather than likenesses: a silhouette, a hair cap, a face plane, a collar band, and one eye slit each. On a 132-second cycle the pair ease toward each other for a minute, hold at a minimum gap, then ease apart — the friction of those sessions as choreography. They never touch, never align, never resolve. Dragging across a portrait flips it 180 degrees; double-tap reseeds the bust forms. There is no sound: like the cover, it is a silent layout piece.

## Construction

- **Bust forms.** Each bust is generated in a local profile space (facing +x, crown at −y, classical truncated base at +y) from a seeded parameter set: head length, nose protrusion, crown height, neck inset, shoulder and chest reach, hair sweep (mulberry32; base seed 839208, per-bust offset). The outline is a cut-paper polygon of 17 vertices — forehead, brow, nose tip, mouth notch, chin, throat, chest, base, shoulder, back of neck and head, crown — drawn as a polygon whose fill is stroked with itself at round joins, softening corners without rounding the vertices away. Over the silhouette sit three further flat shapes: a hair cap following the crown edge, a face plane hugging the profile front, and a collar band across the truncation, plus a single eye-slit dash. Double-tap starts a new form epoch; because the parameters are a pure function of the epoch seed, the forms at any time t are closed-form in the recorded reseed list.
- **Tones.** Strict palette: olive #8A8A3A, Cale dark #1A1A1A, Eno light #D8D4C0, border orange #D96C1A, zigzag purple #6C2A8C, dagger steel #9AA0A6. Each bust's tone steps are mixes of its base colour toward the olive ground, at fixed recorded factors: silhouette 0.20, hair and collar pure base, face plane 0.36, eye slit 0.62. No colour outside the palette and these mixes is drawn.
- **Arrangement and approach.** The upper bust's base orientation is +90 degrees, rotating its profile face to point down at the seam, crown to the right, shoulders to the left; the lower bust sits at −90 degrees, the 180-degree counterpart, face up, crown left. A flip adds PI to a bust's angle, eased over 1.5 s with a smootherstep, recorded as {t0, a0, a1} events, so `angleAt` is a closed-form replay of the flip list. The approach is a closed-form cycle: phase = (t + 26) mod 132; close over 60 s, hold 12 s, apart over 60 s, all smootherstep-eased. Centre distance runs between a fit limit (stage half-height minus 1.22·s, so neither bust can leave the stage) and a minimum set by the longer nose plus half the minimum gap (0.06·min(W,H)); at closest approach the nose tips stop that gap apart, and the dagger row, 0.046·min(W,H) tall and centred on the seam, always fits between them.
- **Border.** Per the brief's constraint, there is no offscreen canvas: the "ENO/CALE" repeat is drawn directly every frame from live `measureText` metrics of the unit string, so spacing is identical across frames and sizes. Top and bottom rows read left to right; the left column reads bottom to top and the right column top to bottom, rotated; rows and columns are trimmed to keep the four corners clear. Font size clamps between 12 and 30 px from the min dimension.
- **Daggers and zigzags.** Each dagger is one steel silhouette path — round pommel, grip, wide guard, tapering blade — all nine pointing down along the seam. The lightning bolts are fixed flat polygons in the two opposite stage corners (layout furniture, independent of the bust seeds).
- **Interaction.** Pointer down records a hit test per bust (the point rotated into bust-local space against the form's bounds, so flips work at any angle). A drag of more than 44 px from a bust fires one flip for that bust. A double-tap within 0.35 s reseeds both bust forms. No audio anywhere; no microphone.
- **House rules.** Canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both `visualViewport` and `window`; canvas CSS size pinned in px inside `draw()`; phone detection via `window.screen` min dimension < 700px with the `h > w*1.15` aspect fallback only when screen is unavailable. The clock is `performance.now()` elapsed — `Date.now()` is never read — and frame one is drawn synchronously at t = 0, phased 26 s into the closing half of the cycle so the pair sits mid-approach. rAF is `typeof`-guarded; no gradients, shadowBlur, filters, or offscreen canvas; the only visible text is the specced "ENO/CALE" border.

## QA log

- Rendered with `render-piece.js` (no rAF, no AudioContext in the harness): qa-desktop.png 1280×900, qa-mobile.png 390×844, thumb.png 720×720.
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice at each build state, `cmp` byte-identical every time; the duplicate was deleted.
- Frame-one read-back (all three sizes): olive field, orange ENO/CALE border on all four edges with clear corners, dark Cale bust above in profile with its nose pointing down at the seam, light inverted Eno bust below with its nose pointing up, nine steel daggers in a row at the seam, purple zigzags in the top-left and bottom-right corners. Mobile keeps the whole layout inside the tall frame; nothing is clipped at any size.
- Fix rounds during QA: (1) the first silhouette used a midpoint-quadratic smoothed path, which melted the nose, brow and chin into a blob; rebuilt as a vertex-true cut-paper polygon with same-colour round-join stroking, after which the profile, hair cap and face plane read as distinct flat shapes. (2) The first dagger construction (stacked triangle, guard bar and pommel dot) read as division signs; rebuilt as a single-silhouette dagger, pommel to blade point. (3) The border's rows and columns crowded the corners, worst at phone size; both are now trimmed to keep the corner squares clear.
- Deviations: none from the brief. Two recorded choices: the tone steps inside each bust are mixes of the palette base colour toward the palette ground (factors recorded under Construction), since the prompt asks for 3–4 flat tone shapes per bust within a six-colour palette; and the fit limit in the approach cycle uses a 1.22·s allowance (the form's true half-extent including the shoulder reach) so the busts stay inside the stage at maximum separation on every screen shape.
