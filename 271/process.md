# No.271 The Clouds That Kept Us Grounded

## Concept
After Shupiwe Chongwe's Leh sky forms: heavy burnished stoneware clouds rest low over a dark Ladakhi ground, from days of staring at the sky in Leh and hoping the clouds don't clear. Six soft, heavy, low-slung forms occupy the upper two-thirds of the frame above a dark ground in the lower third. They drift almost imperceptibly while the low sun breathes across their burnished surfaces. This is the slowest piece in the set and it has no interaction at all: stillness is the point. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, cover-fitted so the dusk sky and the dark ground fill the frame edge to edge, with the Leh sky dusk as the page ground. The palette is held to the five specced colours only: burnished stoneware, cloud highlight, dark ground, Leh sky dusk and low-sun gold. Each of the six cloud-forms is a cluster of eight overlapping circles and ellipses, jittered per form from a fixed seed, filled over a solid underlay in flat stoneware so the cluster reads as a single heavy mass. The sky is flat dusk with two very faint horizontal tone bands in the palette's own colours, and the ground is flat dark with 760 seeded flecks in the palette colours at low alpha. Motion is two slow sines: a lateral drift of about 25 design pixels on a roughly 120 second period, phased per form so the forms never leave the frame, and a sun angle oscillating over about 60 seconds. The sun carries the sheen: a broad highlight wash and smaller offset ellipses in cloud highlight at low alpha, plus a thin low-sun gold arc whose centre sweeps across the top of each form. The opening clock starts at 18 seconds, so frame one opens with the sheen mid-travel on the largest form. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, no interaction handlers, no audio, no text, no offscreen canvas and no filters or shadowBlur.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported resize listeners only, and a grep confirms no interaction handlers, no shadowBlur and exactly the five specced palette colours in the script. Two fixes. First, the initial render read as flat pancakes merging into one mass, with a regular row of pale ovals like a plate pattern: the forms were rearranged into a three, one, two layout with only slight overlaps, the lobe table was rebuilt with varied domes over a flatter base, highlights were restricted to the dome lobes, and the single floating gold ellipse arc was replaced with short gold arcs hugging each dome. Second, the low shade ellipse read as a dark oval disc in each form, so its alpha was halved and its size reduced until it sat as a faint tonal band. Final frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: six heavy stoneware forms, not white and not cartoon clouds, with the sheen and gold rim light mid-travel, over a clear dark ground band with its fleck texture. On the phone the cover crop centres on the middle forms and the outer clouds run off the frame edges as sky does, with the ground band and sheen still legible; nothing is clipped badly. A third fix landed at fly's gate: the single-path silhouette fill left tiny sky-coloured slivers where lobes met (a thin slit inside one form, small bites at two form edges), so the silhouette now fills a solid underlay ellipse first and then each lobe as its own path; re-rendered and re-inspected at all three sizes, the forms are solid masses with no sky showing through. No further fixes were needed.
