# No.264 Winter Warrior

## Concept
After Stanzin Tsepel's 2022 bronze pair: a lone winter figure in heavy clothing and a big rounded fur hat, hands clasped, standing frontal on rough rocky ground, a dark bronze study with rough, expressive surfaces. Monumental and still. Almost nothing moves: a slow breathing rise and fall, and a drifting breath-fog wisp from time to time, over a 30 second loop. No rotation, no turntable, no pedestal. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, contain-fitted and centred, the page ground the piece's own sky grey. The figure is built in local space, feet at the ground line, from layered flat shapes: legs and boots, the great coat with a shadowed right half and one lit fold, arms running down to two clasped hand ellipses, a face in shadow under the fur hat's dome and brim band. Its bronze skin is 270 seeded flecks of highlight and rock tone scattered across coat and hat, plus a rim-light stroke down the right silhouette and a dusting of snow on hat and shoulders. Breathing scales the figure vertically by 1.2 percent on a 30 second sine, anchored at the feet. Breath fog is three staggered wisp systems on a ten second cycle each, so a wisp is always somewhere in its drift; each wisp is three soft snow-white circles that grow and fade as they leave the face. The ground is a seeded craggy ridge polygon in rock grey with a contact shadow under the figure and sixteen snow patches settled on the stone. The opening clock starts at 5 seconds, mid breath with one wisp just leaving the face. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no interaction handlers, no audio, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported resize listeners only. Frame one reads as specced: the figure frontal and monumental in the middle two-thirds, clasped hands and rim light legible, the wisp visible beside the face, snow patches on the rock. On the phone the contain fit sets the whole scene in a field of its own sky grey, the ground colour matching by design, so the figure reads small but complete and centred. No fixes were needed after the first render pass.
