# No.674 — Cáusticas

EXPANSION #82 clemente-mckay, prompt block 2 "Cáusticas" (verified verbatim from TASKS.md).

## Concept

Clemente McKay's reel "Cáusticas de luz sobre campana de agua": one large ceramic water bell, matte clay, half-submerged in deep water, with caustic light dancing across its body. "Los elementos jugando, tocándose, bailando y siendo espontáneos" — the elements playing, touching, dancing, being spontaneous. The caustics are the water's voice: there is no particle water simulation, no literal waves doing the work. The light pattern itself carries the water.

The piece is watch-only at rest: a seamless 30 s loop of caustics playing over the bell while the water gently agitates on a slow breathing cycle. A drag disturbs the surface — the caustics flare and scatter where the hand passes, the waterline ripples hard, and everything settles back over about five seconds. Nothing is clicked, nothing reloads.

## Construction

- **Bell.** A lathe profile in bell-local units (eleven points from crown to mouth rim; the mouth spans 1.32 bell heights), drawn as a flat clay silhouette in #A4552F. Burnish is rendered as flat tone steps clipped to the profile — a #C97B4A band across the shoulders, #241A12 bands low on the body and at the wet rim, one soft shadow step on the right flank. No gradients anywhere. A dark mouth ellipse (#241A12) sits exactly on the waterline, rimmed in burnish, so the bell reads as breaking the surface.
- **Caustic field.** The light is an interference field: four sine waves with mutually warped arguments (two cross-warped directional waves, one diagonal wave, one radial wave) summed over bell-local coordinates. Every phase speed is a whole number of cycles per 30 s loop, so the calm state loops seamlessly. 3,600 seeded sample points (mulberry32, seed 674001) are rejection-sampled inside the bell silhouette. Each frame, the field is evaluated at each sample; samples on a bright ridge of the field (above a threshold) draw a short stroke of caustic #F5E9C8 oriented along the ridge — perpendicular to the numerically evaluated field gradient — with length and alpha scaled by how far above threshold the field sits, plus a fainter echo stroke offset along the gradient (the double-line shimmer of real caustics). Brightness comes from line density and clustering, never from shadowBlur or glow; strokes are masked to the bell by construction, since no sample exists outside the silhouette.
- **Agitation.** A single parameter drives the water. At rest it breathes between about 0.1 and 0.6 on whole-cycle loop sines. It lowers the ridge threshold, raises the field's spatial frequency, and injects a fast scatter phase, so disturbed water tears the web into scattered bright fragments. A drag records timestamped path samples on the anchored clock; each contributes a local agitation bump (Gaussian in space, ~1.9 s exponential decay, pruned after 6.5 s), and the drag also lifts the global surface briefly. Settling takes roughly five seconds.
- **Water surface and reflection.** The waterline is drawn as short pale ticks along a two-sine displaced line at the bell's mouth height, amplitude scaled by global agitation and drawn brighter across the rim. Below it, the bell's reflection is the same profile mirrored across the waterline and squashed to 0.46, filled with clay at low alpha, then broken by 46 seeded horizontal water-colored slashes and a scatter of faint drowned caustic ticks that pulse on the loop. Occasional ripples radiate from the two rim contact points and the mouth center: seven seeded spawn times per loop, expanding flat ellipses that fade over ~2.5 s and swell with agitation.
- **Frame and determinism.** Canvas size comes from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; the canvas style width/height are pinned in px inside draw(). Phone detection uses window.screen (min dimension < 700 px) with the aspect ratio as fallback only; on phones the bell takes 92% of the frame width. Frame one is the synchronous draw at elapsed 0 — the bell at rest, caustics at their calm phase-0 web, waterline steady. No Date.now() anywhere; the live clock is anchored performance.now() minus its load value, and requestAnimationFrame use is typeof-guarded so the headless QA renderer (no rAF, no AudioContext, no document.createElement, no offscreen canvas, no ctx.filter) draws the identical first frame. No audio, no microphone. The only text is the document title.
- **QA hooks.** `?seed=tNN` pins the elapsed time for the headless renderer; `&ag=1` additionally pins agitation high so the disturbed state can be rendered and inspected headlessly.

## Deviations

None from the prompt. Two construction choices worth noting: the caustic "mask" to the bell is achieved by sampling only inside the silhouette rather than by canvas clipping per frame (same visual result, cheaper, and it keeps every stroke's endpoints inside the clay); and the occasional waterline ripples are scheduled deterministically from seeded loop times rather than triggered by a live simulation, which the determinism gate requires.
