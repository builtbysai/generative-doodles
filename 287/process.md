# No.287 Wobble Mug

## Concept
After Sophia Golovanevskaya's classic Hound wobble mug: a squat black-clay mug whose wall carries a deep radial wobble, the rim undulating like a thrown mistake kept on purpose. The outside is glossy black and rippled; the inside stays smooth satin, the functional constraint she insists on. No handle, no liquid, no steam. Dragging around the mug twists the wobble phase and depth live while the inside wall stays smooth; double-tapping re-throws the mug from a new wobble seed. Nothing ever reloads.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, centred fit with the dark ground as the page ground, the mug centred slightly above centre with a generous dark margin. The palette is held to the five specced colours only: black clay for the body, clay highlight for the light bands and the defining edge, satin for the interior, the near black ground, and the warm rim colour used only as two thin translucent rim strokes. The mug is built as two lathes sharing one Catmull-Rom profile, sampled as elliptical cross sections stepping down from the rim centre to the base. The outer lathe radius carries the wobble, A times sin(k theta plus phase) with a smaller seeded second harmonic, weighted by a belly function that is zero at the rim and at the base and full at mid wall, so base and rim stay anchored while the belly of the wall wobbles deepest; at the rim a gentler share of the wobble plus a small vertical undulation waves the rim outline in plan. The inner lathe is the same form scaled in and kept completely smooth at a quarter share in spirit: the opening is one smooth satin ellipse with a flat darker hollow and one quiet sheen arc, and nothing inside it ever wobbles. Highlight is flat bands only: the front wall is drawn as 72 strips running from the front rim arc down the profile to the base arc, clipped to the silhouette, with crest strips taking the clay highlight and trough strips taking a wash of ground, so the wall ripples in flat colour with no gradients, no filters and no shadowBlur. The top annulus is drawn in sectors between the wobbled outer rim and the smooth inner rim, crest sectors highlighted, and a highlight edge stroke holds the black silhouette against the near black ground. The wobble also drifts very slowly on the clock and breathes by a few percent, so frame one already shows the full depth with the phase turning almost imperceptibly. Dragging converts the pointer to design coordinates through the stored centred-fit transform, sweeps the phase by the angle travelled around the rim centre, and maps vertical travel to depth, clamped; a double-tap advances a deterministic seed cycle (287011 plus 7919 per throw) and rebuilds the wobble with fresh phase, harmonic and depth. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds, no text, no audio, no offscreen canvas.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported both resize listeners and the four canvas pointer listeners, and a grep confirms exactly the five specced palette colours and no em dashes. Two fixes during QA. First render: the front wall strips near the sides projected outside the silhouette envelope, leaving a black sliver and a diagonal fold at both lower corners; the strips are now clipped to the silhouette path, so the fluting wraps cleanly to the mug edge. Second fix: the lower profile pinched into a sack waist where the side wobble met the narrowing foot, so the foot control points were eased outward and the piece re-rendered. Final frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: a squat black mug centred with margin on the dark ground, the rim outline waving in plan, deep fluted wobble bands down the front wall, the satin interior a smooth unwobbled ellipse, the warm rim a faint line on both rim edges, no handle and no text. On the phone the centred fit keeps the whole mug in frame and the wobble stays legible. No further fixes were needed.
