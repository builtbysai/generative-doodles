# No.173 Infinity Strand

## Concept
Fathauer's iterated trefoils (Infinity, 2006; Five Iterations of a Trefoil Knot, 2015): one copper strand on black that draws itself into a trefoil, then smaller whole copies of the knot grow inside its loops, then smaller still, five generations deep, the loop count doubling each generation. The strand never breaks. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page, no interaction, no hint. The base motif is the (2,3) torus knot projected to the plane, x = (2 + 0.85 cos 3t) cos 2t, y = (2 + 0.85 cos 3t) sin 2t, with z = sin 3t carried along: the angle winds twice while the radius breathes three times, giving exactly three self crossings, found numerically by segment intersection and deduplicated, with over and under decided by the sign of z at each crossing. The over strand is redrawn as a short bright segment in #E8B06A and the under strand is gapped in the ground colour, so the weave is real. Each instance stamps two child instances at 0.30 scale inside two of its lobes (centres on the lobe axes), recursively to generation 5: 31 instances, each one a single continuous closed path, each placed against its parent so the whole figure is one connected strand drawing. The 45 second loop draws generation 1 on by arc length (8s), grows generations 2 to 5 in nested sequence (4s each, scaling up from their own centres), holds with a 0.6 percent breathing scale (16s), then fades from the innermost generation outward (5s). First frame is offset to 30 seconds, mid hold, fully formed. Strand widths step down per generation with a legibility floor so the deepest knots still read as knots rather than vanishing. Palette is exactly the spec: ground #050505, strand #B87333, highlight #E8B06A. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, knot centred spanning about 75 percent of the viewport.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. Two construction attempts were rejected on inspection: the hypotrochoid parametrisation (sin t + 2 sin 2t, cos t - 2 cos 2t) rendered as a plain rounded deltoid outline whose lobes were vast and empty, and the first crossing pass produced duplicate crossings with gaps chopped along the whole strand. The torus knot projection replaced it (three true crossings, three full lobes), crossings were deduplicated by proximity, and the under gaps were cut to a single sample either side. Child placement was also tuned (scale 0.34 to 0.30, centres moved out to the lobe pockets) so the nested generations sit inside the loops instead of fighting the parent strand. Final renders show a clean woven trefoil with two lobes of visibly iterated mini trefoils, correct at phone size.
