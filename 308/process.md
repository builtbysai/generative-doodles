No.308 Tiny Hut Row takes Xanthe Caesia's tiny ceramic incense cottages, Baba Yaga style, as a row of five huts under a night sky. Each hut is built from a slightly lopsided cream clay body with jittered corners, a gabled soot roof with a handmade ridge, a chimney on one roof slope, exactly one glowing amber window with a soot frame and cross mullion, and two stubby chicken feet with three toes each. The bases are blackened with layered soot strokes rising unevenly from the foot line, heavier at the centre, as incense smoke darkens slipcast clay over time. Every hut differs in height, width, roof pitch, tilt and window placement by its fixed seed data. Exactly one hut, the wide middle one, carries the moss planter treatment: a soot planter box at its front with green moss mounds and tiny sprigs, and moss creeping over its roof edge and body corner.

The idle motion is watch only and runs on a 75 second cycle. Incense smoke rises from all five chimneys at once, drawn from a pooled system of one hundred recycled particles whose ages wrap in place and whose sideways drift comes from a deterministic curl field of layered sines, so the pool never empties and the seam never shows. Exactly one hut per 15 second beat, in a seeded rotation through all five, does a little scratch and settle dance: its body bobs and rocks on an authored envelope while its right foot drags back, lifts and scratches, then both feet settle again for the rest of the beat. Tapping a hut is the only interaction. It flares that hut window alone, with a brighter halo and a pale core, and pours a thicker, faster plume from that hut chimney alone for three seconds before settling. No other hut reacts, and nothing reloads.

The first frame is already developed: the clock starts inside the first beat, so smoke is already curling above every chimney and the middle hut is mid dance with its scratch foot lifted. The palette is held to six colours only: cream clay, soot, window amber, moss, smoke grey and the night ground. Everything is drawn from fixed data on a single canvas each frame, with no randomness at runtime and no text.
