# No.184 Quarter Turn

## Concept
Goodman-Strauss's Double Triamond work rests on a restriction: a colouring rule so tight that only cyclic permutations of the colours are legal, ever. This piece is built so the restriction cannot be broken even in principle. A dark gyroid lattice turns slowly on black, its two sides shaded differently so the surface can never be flipped into its mirror, and the 42 nodes at its vertices carry red, yellow, green and blue under a single global counter: every node's colour is the palette read at its own fixed offset plus that counter. Advance the counter and the whole assignment rotates one step through the cycle. There is no code path, in the loop or behind the tap, that produces any other permutation.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The lattice is the gyroid band: a 5 by 5 by 5 grid of points in 3D, keeping the 85 points where |sin x cos y + sin y cos z + sin z cos x| falls under a band threshold, joined to their grid neighbours (144 struts). Each strut is drawn twice: once offset by 4px in near black #141416 at full width, once in place in matte charcoal #3A3A40, so the band reads as a surface with two differently shaded sides. The solid rotates about the vertical axis (one turn in 64 seconds) with a slow breathing tilt, orthographically projected and centred at about 85 percent of the smaller viewport dimension. Forty two of the band points, chosen by a fixed modular rule, carry nodes. Node colour comes from one float, S: the loop's scheduled steps plus a tap accumulator eased toward the tap count; each node's colour interpolates between palette[floor(base + S) mod 4] and the next entry, which means the only states the surface can ever show are the four cyclic assignments and blends between consecutive ones, exactly the crossfades the loop itself uses. The 48 second loop: 20 seconds of pure rotation, 8 seconds in which S advances through the full four step cycle, 6 seconds holding the final assignment, then 14 seconds fading all nodes to lattice charcoal and back to colour. A tap adds one to the tap count and the crank turns one more quarter. Palette exactly per spec on #060606. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, rAF typeof guarded, hint lowercase and short. The clock is offset 13 seconds so the first frame is mid rotation at full colour.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. The first render failed compositionally: at the initial projection scale the lattice overflowed the frame on all sides and read as stray diagonal lines rather than one surface. The scale was derived from the point cloud's true radius and set in two steps (0.19, then 0.125, settling at 0.135 of the smaller dimension), and the band threshold widened so the lattice carries 144 struts instead of 108 and reads as a woven surface. The final frame shows a centred diamond of dark lattice with the offset underlay making the two sides legible, nodes distributed over the vertices in the four cycle colours. Node count is 42, inside the prompt's "somewhere around forty". The phone frame keeps the whole solid in view with the hint clear at the bottom.
