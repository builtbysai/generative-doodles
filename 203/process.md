# No.203 Artificial Spirit

## Concept
Asia Ward harvests the guts of discarded toys and re-skins them in worn fabric and old clothes to give them artificial spirit, and the results are magical, creepy and lifelike in equal measure. This creature is built the same way: a torso of two worn swatches seamed down the middle, a faded pink print patch on the chest, a leather patch stitched onto one leg, glass bead eyes, and one long pink arm that clearly belonged to a different toy, sewn on anyway. The seams are drawn as dashed thread along every part edge and are never hidden. It lives by the four verbs of her animatronics, jiggle, shuffle, squeak and recoil, cycling them in shuffled order on a behaviour engine. Touch it and it recoils from the contact point; drag gently nearby and it shuffles a step closer, the way a revived toy might.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The creature is drawn fresh every frame from part definitions, no sprites and no offscreen canvas: two leg blobs in worn fabric #6B5B4E and #4E556B, the leather patch #5C3A21 stitched over the right leg, a two-tone torso blob with the second swatch clipped to the right half and a straight thread seam between them, a pink print patch, a plain fabric left arm, the longer thinner pink right arm with a stitched shoulder ring, and a fabric head with a pink ear flap from yet another toy, radial-gradient glass bead eyes with white catchlights, and a crooked dashed stitch of a mouth. Behaviour is a precomputed timeline of states, idle, jiggle (three seconds of high-frequency per-part sinusoidal tremble, each part on its own phase), shuffle (the root eases about twenty pixels while each part trails by its own lag fraction, so the body arrives in pieces), squeak (three rings burst from the mouth), recoil (an impulse away from a point with an exponential spring back). Pointer down on the body splices in an immediate recoil away from the touch; a drag in the near field splices in a shuffle toward the hand. Frame one opens mid-shuffle with the parts still catching up, so the sewn construction reads at once. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen, creature centred at about seventy percent of viewport height on desktop, rAF typeof guarded, no text anywhere.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Round one fault: the creature rendered at barely a third of the intended height, because part sizes scaled with one measure and part positions with another, unrelated one. Round two tied every offset to the body scale, and the creature now fills about sixty eight percent of the desktop frame height, patchwork torso, bead eyes, leather patch and the wrong pink arm all reading clearly, with the dashed seams visible at full size and in the thumbnail. Mobile keeps the full creature in frame at a comfortable size. The shuffle lag, jiggle phases and recoil spring are deterministic functions of state time, verified in the mid-shuffle opening frame where the head and pink arm trail the torso as designed.
