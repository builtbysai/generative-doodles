# No.269 Bedforms

## Concept
After Shupiwe Chongwe's Bedforms: a sediment field of ripples in warm clay browns, geology reading as skin, organised by one diagonal flow, with a few vessel silhouettes half buried at the edges and a sparse scatter of salt flecks. The field is a slow viscoelastic solid. Dragging presses dents into it like a hand into wet clay; the dents relax over about fifteen seconds but never quite vanish, and the whole field breathes on a forty second cycle. Drag to press the clay.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, cover-fitted so the field fills the frame edge to edge. The ground is moist clay brown with nine broad tonal patches in dry sediment and ripple shadow laid along the flow direction. Over it run about 110 diagonal sine ripple strokes, alternating dry sediment and ripple shadow, varied in spacing, amplitude, width and opacity, with seeded breaks in some crests so the field reads as sediment rather than stripes. Each stroke is drawn as short segments of about 26 pixels so dents can bend it. A dent is an event with a position, a radius and a starting depth; its current depth decays exponentially toward a memory floor of one fifth of the original, and the displacement at any point is the sum over dents of that depth times a tight gaussian falloff, applied mainly along the ripple normal with a smaller radial spread, scaled by the breathing sine. Each dent also lays a shallow depression shading under the ripples, two layered shadow discs and a dry offset disc, with alpha scaled by its current depth. Dents are added along the drag path, throttled to one every 36 design pixels, and the list is capped so the oldest touch is dropped while the seeded dent is kept. Frame one seeds a single older dent low centre, born nine seconds before the opening clock so its depth already sits near memory plus a partial remainder and its ripples are clearly displaced. Three dark vessel silhouettes sit half buried at the left, right and lower edges of the visible frame, anchored to the visible design bounds so they hold the edges at any aspect; each has a rim, a dry sheen and a sediment mound heaped at its base. The Zambezi green appears only as a thin moss line along the lower vessel's rim. Salt flecks are displaced with the field like everything else on the ground. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, pointer coordinates converted through the stored cover transform, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds, no text, no audio, no offscreen canvas, no filters or shadowBlur.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported both resize listeners and all four pointer listeners. The field reads as a clay sediment bed at every size: wavy diagonal ripples in dry sediment and ripple shadow over the moist brown ground, sparse salt flecks, and the three vessels half buried at the left, right and lower edges, the lower one carrying the green moss line on its rim. Two fixes. First, the seeded dent was too diffuse to read: its falloff is now a tighter gaussian, its starting depth is deeper, and a layered depression shading sits under the ripples, so the low centre dent visibly crowds and bends the crests in all three renders, clearest on the phone and the thumb. Second, the burial mounds first rendered as wide flat saucers under the side vessels; they are now narrower, flatter and lower in contrast, reading as heaped sediment. No further fixes were needed.
