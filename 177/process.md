# No.177 Groovy Hyperbolic Field

## Concept
Fathauer's negative-curvature ceramics translated into light: a dark relief field that grows exponentially outward from a slightly off-centre point, grooves carved along the growth direction like cactus ribs, lit from one side so every ridge sweeps through highlight and shadow as the light moves. Dragging is the instrument here; the light is what you play.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The relief is a heightfield on a 128 column grid of filled cells: height is an exponential radial envelope times a ridged angular groove term (abs(sin(13 theta)) sharpened, warped by a slow 3 theta wobble, calmed near the singular centre), times a radial ring term whose phase advances one ring per 20 seconds for the idle growth pulse, plus a saddle term (u squared minus v squared, radially damped) for the negative curvature. Normals come from finite differences of the height, shading is a plain Lambertian dot product against a draggable light vector, and colour is a three stop ramp through the spec palette only: shadow #1E1A2E, lit #5A4A7A, groove highlight #8A76B0 on ground #0E0E12. Dragging rotates the light azimuth and tilts its elevation (clamped); nothing else animates, so the field is a developed, fully lit relief from the first frame. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned in px every frame, the field full bleed at every size including portrait phones, requestAnimationFrame typeof guarded, hint lowercase and short.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. The desktop frame shows 26 ribs radiating from the off-centre growth point, the right hand ribs catching the highlight colour while the left falls into shadow, exactly the one sided ceramic light the prompt asks for. First pass had a noisy singular centre and a flatter radial falloff; the groove term is now damped toward its mean inside r 0.22 and the exponential envelope strengthened, and the centre reads as a calm hub. On the phone frame the ribs run full bleed top to bottom with the growth point above centre. Honest trait: at cell resolution the travelling ring pulse shows as a faint dotted texture along the ribs; at full size it reads as surface grain, not artifact.
