# No.334 Spring Convenor

## Concept

After Arunima Dazess Wangchuk's 2023 protest installation: an arched bamboo mirror stands on black fabric, its face covered in hand written paper strips with red backing, each strip carrying a single short line. The mirror implicates the viewer, and the strips lean almost imperceptibly toward whoever looks. The widest strip, set high on the mirror, carries the study's quoted line: "If only... Nature was our God."

The prompt's camera reflection takes the standing no-camera treatment for this gallery. The mirror records and depicts nobody. Its reflection is a soft abstract presence drawn as data: muted silver grey sheen shapes on the arch interior, drifting very slowly, implying someone standing before it without ever picturing them. There is no camera, no microphone, and no permission prompt of any kind; the viewer's part in the piece is pointer position only.

## Construction

One canvas, design space 1280 by 900, centred fit with the page ground in the fabric black of the piece. The arch is centred and fills the frame vertically: posts at x 340 and 940, spring line at y 370, apex at y 70, feet at y 868. The palette holds to five colours only: mirror silver grey, bamboo tan, strip red, fabric black, strip paper, with every tonal step made by layered translucency of those five.

The mirror body is the arch path filled with silver grey at 0.58 alpha over the black ground. Six authored sheen blobs live inside it as JS data, each drawn as three nested translucent fills of the same ellipse, wide and faint, then smaller and stronger, then a soft core, clipped to the arch path. Two are dark pools in the fabric black, one is a pale borrowing of the strip paper, three are the silver itself. Each blob drifts on its own slow sine path, periods between 67 and 113 seconds, so the reflection is never quite still and never repeats simply. Two soft dark strokes just inside the rim shade the edge so the mirror reads as a set surface. The bamboo frame is three arch strokes, a heavy outer rail, a thin groove line, an inner rail, with wrap and cross lashings at eleven authored nodes up the posts and around the arc, and two short foot strokes under the posts.

Exactly twelve strips are authored in a table in the code, and the count is asserted there along with exactly one quote strip. Each strip is a paper rect with a red backing rect offset down and right so the red peeks along two edges. All lettering is stroke paths, no fonts, no fillText. The quote line uses a small stick hand defined glyph by glyph as polylines in a 10 by 14 box, with the ellipsis drawn as three filled dots. The other eleven strips carry short authored script lines: each is generated from a fixed per strip word table into cursive hump chains with one tall loop, a dotted letter tick, and a flourish on the final word, so they read as handwriting without spelling anything. Four strips near the arc were nudged inward during QA so every strip sits fully on the mirror.

Interaction is proximity only. Strips near the pointer take a lean target toward it, up to about a quarter radian scaled by a squared distance falloff inside 280 design units, and a lift target of up to 9 units toward the pointer. Lean and lift run on per strip underdamped springs, stiffness 1.7 and 2.2 against damping 0.55 and 0.7, which puts the visible settle near ten seconds after the pointer passes. There is no tap action. The first frame opens developed, clock offset to 23.7 seconds, sheen already drifted, strips in place. House rules hold throughout: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window, the canvas pinned by style width and height every frame, phone detection from window.screen with the aspect fallback, fixed seeds, every requestAnimationFrame typeof-guarded, no offscreen or stacked canvases, no shadowBlur, no filters, no em dashes, no absolute URLs.

## QA log

Rendered with node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png) and 720x720 (thumb.png), and all three inspected. Desktop and thumb: the arch fills the frame vertically, the silver mirror reads muted with soft light and dark sheen pools, the bamboo rails, groove, lashings and feet are clean, all twelve strips sit on the mirror with red backing showing, the quote line is legible as hand lettering and reads exactly "If only... Nature was our God.", and the script lines read as short cursive lines with dots and end flourishes. First pass had four strips near the arc overlapping the inner rail by up to about 29 units; their centres and widths were adjusted and the re-render shows all twelve fully inside the mirror. Mobile: centred fit on the fabric black ground, the whole arch visible and undistorted, quote still legible at phone scale. Static checks: exactly five hex colours in the file, all from the spec palette; no fillText, no createElement, no shadowBlur, no media APIs; the 12 strip count and single quote strip are asserted in code and throw if violated.
