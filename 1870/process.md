# No.1870 "Left-Handed Mirror" (FN7, PP1117)

Source prompt: TASKS.md, francesca-naibo block, PP1117. Study #244 francesca-naibo. The study file studies/francesca-naibo.md does not exist in the muse-soul repo (404), so hexes were hand-picked from the prompt's named colors.

Driver: one melodic line with two handednesses; the felt phenomenon is a reflection that has its own ideas, and the seam is a balance you hold in your hand.

Palette: left side warm room tones, ground #241A12, field #2E2115, fretboard varnished wood #7A5233 with frets #C9A06A and strings #E8DCC8, warm skin #C89B7B, lamp amber #E8B96A; right side the same scene cooled and flipped, ground #12181F, field #18222C, fretboard #3C4E63 with frets #8FA8C0 and strings #C8D6E2, cool skin #A9BCCB, lamp steel blue #8FB4D4; seam in thin gold #D4A94E; plate #161310 with bone text #EDE3D2 and hint gray #B0A693.

Layout: field-like composition, full-bleed on phones and a centered square on desktop. A vertical gold seam divides the frame at the middle; each half shows the same fretboard scene in opposite handedness, with "normal" and "mirror" labels above the board. Title and date plate top left, "Drag the gold seam to rebalance the mirror." hint bottom left, both drawn on the canvas in dark plates. A small gold-rimmed chip under the board shows the balance as two percentages (50 / 50 at rest).

Motion: drag earned, never click-to-reload. One melodic line of 8 notes (semitones 0, 3, 5, 7, 10, 7, 5, 3 above A3, 4 s per note, 32 s loop) is rendered twice. The left hand walks its fingers along the frets toward the bridge; the mirror hand walks in reverse with its finger order swapped, and its strings bend at the sounding note instead of vibrating, the bend growing with the note index so the reflection plays differently. Dragging the seam left or right (pointer or touch, 28 px hit zone, clamped to 12 to 88 percent of the field) grows or shrinks the mirrored territory continuously and reversibly; the seam position crossfades the mix and pans each voice to its side. Audio is lazy inside the first drag gesture only: the AudioContext is created and resumed there, never on load, and the visuals are complete without it. The normal voice is a triangle pluck at the line pitch; the mirrored voice is a sine whose pitch is interval-inverted around A3 and bends up then down over each note, with reversed articulation.

Fixed-phase frame one: t = 10.0 s, note 3 of 8 sounding, finger mid walk, mirror bend half formed. All motion is a pure closed-form function of t; all randomness is seeded mulberry32 (room dust), never wall clock, Date, or unseeded Math.random. One synchronous frame is drawn at load; requestAnimationFrame is guarded with a typeof check so node-canvas QA works. Visual Viewport sizing with resize listened to on both visualViewport and window; canvas element pinned in draw() via canvas.style.width/height in px; phone detection via window.screen (min dimension under 700px) with aspect fallback h > w*1.15; page ground is the piece's own split background color. Reduced motion renders a still frame. Flicker is slow by construction: the only oscillation is a gentle string vibration at the note rate, well under 14 Hz.

Deviations from the prompt: none material. The prompt's WebAudio approach is followed, with audio deferred to the first drag gesture as the brief requires; the mirrored voice's interval inversion and reversed articulation are implemented as a pitch-inverted sine with a slow bend envelope, and the seam crossfade and panning follow the seam position. The balance chip is an addition to the prompt, making the rebalance visible.
