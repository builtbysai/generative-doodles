# No.258 Silhouette Signal

## Concept
After tanzinchospel.art's Day 10 hardsurface silhouette study: a chunky black mech on a dark teal ground, mirrored top and bottom across a waterline at half height, cyan lights dotted along its armour seams. The lights chase slowly along the seams; the reflection below runs the same chase a third of a second behind. No other colour exists here. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1200 by 900 design space, cover-fitted so the mirrored field fills any frame. The mech is 17 flat black plates (torso, chest inset, head, shoulder pads, arms, thighs, shins, feet, backpack) drawn twice: once upright, once flipped about the mirror line at 60 percent opacity. Thirteen armour seams are defined as polylines and sampled every 16 design pixels into a single ordered run of lights. Each light carries a resting ember brightness plus a chase pulse: a bright head travels the seam order over a 6 second loop from one master clock, and the reflection pass reads the same clock minus 0.3 seconds, so the lag is exact rather than approximate. The figure is zoomed 1.22 about an anchor on the mirror line so the reflection zooms identically and the feet stay on the waterline. Below the mirror the teal deepens with a flat translucent black band and a hairline of cyan marks the surface. The opening clock starts at 2.35 seconds, the chase mid run across the chest rim and waist, the reflection visibly a beat behind. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no interaction handlers, no audio, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Two fixes. The mech first sat small in the field; a zoom about the mirror line brought it forward. At 1.35 the zoom clipped the head dome under the desktop cover crop, so it settled at 1.22, where the full figure and its reflection survive every aspect. Frame one reads as specced: black mech centred on the waterline, the chase bright on the chest plate rim and waist seam, the mirrored chase below it dimmer and behind.
