# No.256 Yeti Hunter

## Concept
After the first ZBrush sculpt by tanzinchospel.art (Apr 2021): a portrait bust of an elderly yeti hunter, long white beard, heavily tattooed torso, dark robe, red sash and headwear, lit by one deep red spotlight on black. One light only. The spotlight slowly arcs across the bust, so the tattooed planes catch the light in sequence and the beard brightens side to side. Dust motes hang in the beam. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, the bust built from layered flat shapes in a 1000 by 1100 design space, contain-fitted and centred in the void. The robe is a dark mass cut from the near-black ground, its edges stroked with red only where the beam passes. Bare skin is drawn as flat planes (neck, shoulders, two chest panels, the head) in a dimmed warm tone, then relit by a second pass in a red-mixed skin tone whose alpha follows a light function of the arc position, so each plane brightens as the beam crosses it. The spotlight swings on a 44 second sine; its beam is three nested translucent red trapezoids from above the frame, and 46 seeded dust motes drift inside it, visible only where the light is. The beard is 330 seeded strands flowing from the jaw down the chest, each strand's alpha driven by the light at its own x position, so the bright side of the beard is the lit side. Tattoos are seeded dark strokes in four kinds (strokes, arcs, dots, chevron band rows) across the chest panels and shoulders; their alpha runs from near-invisible in shadow to full where the light sits, which is what makes the planes catch in sequence. Face detail is kept flat and quiet: brow shadows, closed eyes, a nose stroke, the mouth lost in the beard. The red cap and its knot, and the diagonal sash across the lower robe, take the same light modulation. The opening clock starts at 4.1 seconds, light mid arc with the right side lit. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no interaction handlers, no second light source.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Three fixes. The shoulder planes first stuck out past the robe silhouette like knobs; they are smaller and tucked inward. The red headwear first sat as a band with a sliver of bald scalp above it; it is now a cap drawn over the crown with the band below it. The chest panels were narrowed and shortened so they read as a bare chest inside the open robe rather than hanging shapes. Frame one reads as specced: beam arcing in from the upper right, right chest tattoos and the right of the beard lit, the left side of the figure still in shadow.
