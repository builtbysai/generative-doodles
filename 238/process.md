# No.238 Honest Signals

## Concept
After Woodfill's Contingent Flag Forms and the Semaphore pieces: a field of ninety six semaphore flags on thin steel poles, white and signal red, each holding one of four orientations, north, east, south or west. The field runs a message: a seeded pattern propagates left to right as a wave of flips. Then noise creeps in, spurious flips rising until the pattern is nearly unreadable, until a call and response sweep re-asserts the honest signal and the noise floor resets. The re-stabilization is the point; the noise is never permanent. The signal is abstract, no letters are spelled. A tap reseeds the message; nothing else responds.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The 12 by 8 grid is exact. The message is blocky by construction: flags in a 3 by 2 block share an orientation derived from the cycle seed, so the assembled field reads as a pattern rather than static, and each flag's colour (white or red) follows its orientation and block parity. Each flag's behaviour over the 84 second cycle is a deterministic event list computed from a seeded PRNG: one propagation flip (timed by column, sweeping left to right), up to two noise flips biased later in the noise phase as the floor ramps, and one sweep flip back to the honest orientation. Drawing walks the event list and animates any flip in progress over 200 milliseconds. Poles are drawn full length to a common base line with a mounting plate at the foot of each column. The opening clock sits 44 seconds into the cycle, so frame one shows the message assembled with the first spurious flips already showing. A tap replaces the seed and restarts the cycle. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Frame one shows the full gantry of poles with signal heads in a mixed but structured pattern, red and white distributed in visible blocks, a minority of heads turned against their block (the early noise), plates at every base. On the phone frame the 3:2 panel goes full width and all ninety six heads stay legible. No adjustments were needed after the first render.
