# No.298 Flash Drop

After Ruby Waters flash sheets. A 3 by 3 grid on cream paper holds nine motifs in reading order: puffer, daruma, cat, koi, chrysanthemum, hannya, Mt Fuji, horse, boar.

Watch-only, 48 second loop. Each cell takes four seconds: the outline draws itself with a stroke-dash reveal, flat colour floods in as a clip reveal centred on the motif and clipped to its silhouette, then a detail pass adds eyes, dots and small marks. Nine cells fill in 36 seconds, the finished sheet holds for 6 seconds, then it washes back to bare outlines over 6 seconds and starts again.

Frame one starts partway in, with the first two cells complete and the third mid-fill, so the sheet reads as work already under way.

Palette is paper, ink black, vermilion, cobalt, yellow and mint only. All marks are drawn from path data each frame. No offscreen canvas, no masks, no text in the piece.
