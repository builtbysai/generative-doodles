# No.274 Negative Space

## Concept
After Shupiwe Chongwe's line that it is often not the vessel itself which is valued, but the negative space it holds. Nine tall unfired vessels stand in a loose ring in a dark room, near-silhouettes, seen slightly from above so their mouth ellipses show. The composition is the light between them: glowing gaps, bright mouths and hollow interiors catching light. A single slow light orbits the ring over about 45 seconds, so each gap and hollow flares and dims in turn and the negative space breathes. The vessels never move, only the light travels. Watch only, no interaction.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, centred fit with the room dark as the page ground. The palette is held to the five specced colours only: vessel silhouette, glowing gap amber, interior light, room dark and straw fleck. The nine vessels are placed on a jittered elliptical ring from a fixed seed, back vessels slightly smaller and drawn first so front vessels overlap them. Each vessel is a dark lathe silhouette path with a mouth ellipse at its top: a room-dark hollow over a vessel-tone rim, filled with interior light whose alpha follows the angular distance between the vessel angle and the orbiting light angle. Gap glow sits behind the vessels: for each adjacent pair, a vertical lozenge at the midpoint built from layered translucent amber ellipses plus a pale interior core, with no filter or bloom, its alpha on the same light falloff. Faint straw flecks are seeded as short strokes on each vessel body. Dust motes drift only inside gaps whose light intensity is currently high. The opening clock starts at 10 seconds, about 10s into the 45s orbit, so frame one already has one gap flaring and its neighbouring mouths lit. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds, no interaction handlers, no audio, no text, no offscreen canvas and no filters or shadowBlur.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported resize listeners only, and a grep confirms no interaction handlers, no shadowBlur, no offscreen canvas and exactly the five specced palette colours in the script. Three fixes. First, the initial lit-flank washes were unclipped ellipses: one read as a round disc on each front vessel chest and the other spilled past the tapering lower body, so both were replaced with a single amber wash clipped to the silhouette path. Second, the contact shadows were wider than the vessel bases and read as floating black discs under the back vessels, so they were narrowed to the base width and lightened. Third, that clipped flank still read as a full oval inside the body, so it was shifted to the silhouette edge where the clip turns it into a thin rim-light crescent, and the gap-glow core alphas were lowered so the layered lozenges sit softer. Final frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: nine tall near-silhouette vessels in a loose ring on the room dark, mouth ellipses visible from slightly above, the front mouths bright, one central gap already flaring amber with dust motes inside it, straw flecks on the bodies and dark room edges. On the phone the centred fit holds the whole ring in the middle of the portrait frame with the flaring gap and lit mouths still legible and nothing clipped. No further fixes were needed.
