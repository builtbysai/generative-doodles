# №1747 Order Pole, Chaos Pole (PP994, expansion study #229 studio-zimoun)

Driver: the same swarm breathing between order and chaos. One hundred and eight identical ticking boxes hold still as a strict grid, then loosen into a floor scatter, then gather back into rows, and the morph itself is the subject: nothing about the units ever changes, only where they stand and how their whiskers point.

## Mechanism

Two layout functions share one swarm of 108 units (12 columns by 9 rows). The grid pole is a strict lattice; the scatter pole is a seeded poisson-ish rejection sample (minimum distance 0.078 of the field, mulberry32(1747 + 991), with a seeded jittered-lattice fallback so the count is always exactly 108). Each unit is the same in both grammars: a kraft tan box with a darker kraft edge, a dark grey pivot dot, and three dark grey whiskers fanning from its top edge. A unit's whisker angle is its phase: all three whiskers of a unit share one phase that drifts at a per-unit rate (0.04 to 0.14 rad/s) from a seeded offset, so the fan ticks with a slow sinusoidal sway of 0.45 rad while the layout morphs underneath it, fully independent of position.

The morph runs on a seamless 120 second cycle: grid dwell for the first 24 seconds, smooth morph to scatter over seconds 24 to 54, scatter dwell to second 66, smooth morph back to grid over seconds 66 to 96, grid dwell to the loop point. Per-unit interpolation is delayed: each unit has a seeded delay in [0, 1] and its own morph value is smoothstep(clamp(m * 1.4 - delay * 0.4, 0, 1)), so the change ripples across the swarm instead of snapping, and each unit also rotates toward a seeded scatter rotation (up to 0.45 rad either way) in proportion to its own morph value. Frame one is a fixed t = 0, the strict grid state, drawn synchronously at load; the animation clock starts there. Watch-only: no pointer listeners. The formation spans the frame in both states, inset only by a top strip (80 px desktop, 74 px phone) and a 50 px bottom strip so the title/date and hint plates never cover a unit. Seeded RNG only (mulberry32), no Date.now, Math.random, or performance.now anywhere; requestAnimationFrame guarded by a typeof check; reduced-motion visitors get the static frame one.

Palette, hand-picked from the prompt's named colors (studies/studio-zimoun.md does not exist locally, so no study file was consulted): white gallery ground #FAF9F6, kraft tan boxes #C2A878 with edge #A98F5F, dark grey whiskers and pivot #3A3A3A, white plates with dark grey ink #3A3A3A and hint ink #6B6B6B.

## Acceptance numbers and probes

Rendered the actual script with node render-piece.js at 1280x900 desktop, 390x844 mobile, and 720x720 thumb; read all three renders. Desktop shows a square stage on white ground with the strict grid and legible plates; mobile is full-bleed with plates legible and no clipping; thumb is the 720x720 render. Probe renders at t = 39 s (mid-morph) and t = 60 s (scatter rest) confirmed the morph visually: at mid-morph the swarm is visibly between grammars with units at different stages of travel, and at scatter rest it is a loose rotated floor scatter with no overlaps. Probe (window.__probe) measured at 1280x900: unit count 108 in both states (one fixed array, count identical by construction); morphAt(0) = 0.000, morphAt(24) = 0.000, morphAt(39) = 0.500, morphAt(60) = 1.000, morphAt(84) = 0.352, morphAt(120) = 0.000, so the cycle is seamless grid to scatter and back; mean unit displacement at rest is 0.000 px both at the grid pole (t = 0 to 6) and at the scatter pole (t = 60 to 66); mid-morph the swarm moves 42.902 px mean in one second (t = 38 to 39), 229.747 px mean from grid rest to mid-morph, and 429.527 px mean from grid rest to scatter rest, showing a ripple in progress rather than a snap; per-unit morph values at t = 39 spread from 0.226 to 0.772, confirming the delay ripple; whisker phases at fixed time t = 10 s span 0.067 to 5.957 rad (range 5.890, sd 1.754), so phases differ across units; all 216 unit positions (108 units at both poles) sit inside the frame. Determinism: seeded RNG only, fixed t = 0 frame one, thumb re-rendered independently and byte-identical.

## Deviations from the prompt

One fix during QA: the first build let the title and hint plates cover edge units (the top-left grid boxes and the first two bottom-row boxes on the square thumb); the field now carries a top strip and a 50 px bottom strip so no plate ever covers a unit, and all three sizes were re-rendered. During the morph, units briefly overlap as their straight-line paths cross; no collision avoidance was added, since the prompt asks for pure interpolation between the two layouts and the crossing is the visible ripple. No other deviations.
