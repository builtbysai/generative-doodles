# No.217 Antipode

## Concept
David Bowen's the other side takes satellite imagery of the point on earth exactly opposite the gallery, converts it to a relief model, and carves it in pink foam, inviting viewers to touch the opposite side of the earth. This piece keeps the whole conceit: a rectangular pink foam panel hangs in a near-black gallery field, its surface a miniature topography of clouds and ocean swells under raking light, with two older carvings leaning at the right like finished works. The relief is static, as a carving is. Every fifty seconds a new antipode is carved: the finished panel slides into the stack and a fresh topography scrapes in under a faint horizontal toolpath shimmer. Dragging across the panel is touching the foam; the finger presses a subtly deeper trail that persists on that carving until it is replaced. A double tap carves a new antipode now. Nothing names the place; which antipode it is stays a mystery.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The relief is four-octave value-noise fractal on a 150 by 100 grid, shaped for long swells and softened at the rim, rendered with hillshade lighting from the upper left: each cell's colour walks the foam ramp from shadow #C87898 through base #E8A8B8 to highlight #F8D8E0 by the dot of surface normal and light, with flat foam resting mid-ramp so slopes read on both sides. Grid, pressure map and per-cell colours are plain JS data redrawn directly each frame; no offscreen canvas is used, a substitution for the prompt's precompute-to-offscreen that changes nothing visible. Touch is a persistent pressure grid per carving: drag stamps deepen the effective height field, so the trail shades as a real groove, and it travels with the carving when it joins the stack. The carving cycle runs fifty seconds with a four second transition: stack panels shuffle down one slot while the new relief reveals top to bottom behind three or four sweeping shimmer lines over uncut flat foam. Frame one opens mid-cycle on a settled carving that already carries one faint touch curve, the two predecessors leaning beside it. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, phone layout keeps the panel's shape with the stack tucked lower right, rAF typeof guarded, no text anywhere.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The first two builds failed the gate on shading: the hillshade ramp saturated because a flat surface's dot product sat near the top of the ramp, washing the panel to nearly uniform pale pink with the topography barely visible. The fix recentered the ramp so flat foam sits at its midpoint and steepened the height gradients, after which the renders show real carved relief at every size: ridges and basins reading under the raking light, darker carved channels, the foam slab edge peeking below the panel, and both older carvings cropped at the right with their own distinct topographies.
