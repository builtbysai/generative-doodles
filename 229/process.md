# No.229 Never the Same Twice

## Concept
The fullest statement of Kluber's method in this batch. A painting of teal, dark teal and chartreuse stripes, grouped in three vertical zones with a solid dark teal interrupt band standing in the right third, after the 2021 Split Infinitives canvases, holds perfectly still. Over it, four independent light layers play, each on its own prime-number clock: a warm wash drifting horizontally (37s), a cool wash rising and sinking (53s), a narrow light rectangle travelling and reversing (61s), and a slow scanline flicker (89s). Because the periods are primes, the composite state space is effectively non-repeating; the painting you see in any given minute will not come round again. There are no controls at all. Kluber's works have none, and neither does this.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The painted stripes are precomputed per zone from fixed seeds (three mulberry32 streams) as band lists in a three hundred unit design space, drawn directly each frame; the seed is stored in the layout itself, so a resize re-derives nothing and the painting is identical on every load. The four light layers are drawn additively with 'lighter', clipped to the panel: the warm wash as a wide soft vertical gradient bar, the cool wash as a soft horizontal bar, the rectangle as a narrow gradient-edged bar, and the flicker as one-pixel light lines every four pixels whose alpha breathes between zero and 0.032 on the 89s clock, far too slow and faint to strobe. Layer phases are offset so frame one is already a genuine composite: rectangle mid-travel in the left zone, cool wash low, warm wash present. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, page ground in the painting's dark teal, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The first build drew the painted bands too tall, eight to twenty eight units, and the field read as chunky colour blocks rather than a stripe painting; band heights were cut to three to eleven units and the flicker and rectangle alphas trimmed, after which the second render showed the fine stripe field with the light layers playing over it as intended: the three zones distinct, the interrupt band standing clear in the right third, the travelling rectangle visible as a soft beam in the left zone. Phone and thumb frames hold the same read.
