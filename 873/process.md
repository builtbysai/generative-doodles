# No.873 Held Back

Study #107 xu-jared, PP34 "Berserk to Ghost". Her arc from deafening harsh-noise assault to whispers inside a no-input mixing board, held in one system: a mass capable of violence, played at whisper volume, that never releases.

## Concept

A black field holds one dense coiled mass at its center: 600 particles on tight three-arm spiral orbits, drawn as short filament strokes in feedback red. A single pressure value governs everything. At rest the coil idles as a dim ember. Dragging builds pressure: the filaments tighten, brighten toward full red, tremble, and pick up amber highlights near the top of the range. Releasing lets the pressure bleed off slowly while the mass exhales grey ghost shimmer that drifts outward and fades. The explosion never happens. The restraint is the composition.

## Construction

- Pressure model (closed form): pressure is a function of recorded event segments, never frame-integrated state. Segment kinds: const (hold), drag (exponential rise asymptotic to the cap), decay (exponential ease toward a target). Rest pressure is 0.25. Dragging pushes a drag segment whose build rate scales with pointer speed (0.25 to about 1.85 per second); pressure approaches the cap of 0.92 asymptotically and can never reach 1, so the howl is approached and never arrived at. Release pushes a decay toward rest with tau 3.0 s, so the exhale takes roughly 8 s to settle, slow enough to feel like relief. Double-tap pushes a fast bleed (tau 0.35 s) toward the floor of 0.05, followed by a re-coil decay back toward rest with tau 7.0 s, near rest again in about 20 s. The floor is 0.05, not 0: the coil always re-forms.
- Orbits: each particle's angle is base angle plus arm offset plus a spiral twist term plus an orbit phase shared by all particles with a per-particle rate (inner orbits faster). The shared phase is the analytic integral of orbit rate over the pressure history: orbit rate is 0.15 + 0.5 * p, and the integral of pressure over each segment kind has a closed form, so any frame is a pure function of the event list and t. Filament radius tightens by up to 42 percent as pressure rises; tremble is a deterministic high-frequency sine jitter whose amplitude scales with p cubed.
- Brightness: red alpha maps from pressure (dim ember at rest, near full at the cap). Amber appears only when pressure exceeds about 0.72, on the subset of particles seeded as amber candidates. Tap flares are recorded events that add a short local brightness boost to nearby filaments.
- Ghost shimmer: two families. Ambient motes (42, seeded) cycle continuously so frame one already has faint grey motes around the coil. Exhale motes are derived deterministically per decay segment (seeded by segment index), born across the first seconds of the exhale, drifting outward and fading on a sine envelope.
- Sound: created lazily inside the first pointerdown, typeof-guarded. A looped noise buffer through a lowpass filter plus a 36 Hz sine sub, both driven by the same pressure value the visuals read (pre-limiter drive is what you see). The chain runs through a tanh waveshaper ceiling and a hard compressor (threshold -16 dB, ratio 20, knee 0) into a master trim of 0.42, so output stays at a whisper-to-moderate level even at maximum pressure. The exhale drops the filter cutoff and gains with pressure, low-passing the mass into a soft grey bed. No microphone, no external samples.
- House rules: canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; canvas style width and height pinned in px inside draw(); phone detection via window.screen physical size with aspect fallback; frame one is the synchronous draw(0) at rest pressure; no Date.now in drawn state; seeded mulberry32 throughout; no gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas; rAF typeof-guarded. Palette is exactly the four specced tones: black #0A0A0A, red #FF2200 (dim states via alpha over black, reading as the ember #7A1408 family), amber #FFB000, grey #8A8A86.
