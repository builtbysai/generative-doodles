Generative Doodles

№8984 · Threshold Menagerie

Each plate grows a field, cuts it at a threshold, and stamps one glyph per grid cell: dots where it is dim, dashes, rings, triangles as it brightens. Cells below the cut stay empty. Click the plate for a new specimen, or change the field to meet another animal.
