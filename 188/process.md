# No.188 The Jiggle

## Concept
Sargent's 1 of N is forty inches of steel space filling curve weighing seven hundred pounds, and Goodman-Strauss's favourite fact about it is that it jiggles. This piece is about that mass and that wobble: a thick Hilbert curve tube fills a cube so completely it looks immovable, then an impulse runs down its length and takes seconds to settle, on springs tuned to feel like seven hundred pounds rather than seven grams. Between jiggles the curve quietly redraws itself as a different valid space filling path, the transitions masked by the motion.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page, pseudo 3D. The curve is a true order 2 Hilbert style path through a 4 by 4 by 4 lattice, 64 vertices, built recursively: an order 1 path through eight octants, each octant carrying a rotated or reflected copy of the order below chosen by a signed permutation search so consecutive octants join exactly, every vertex distinct, every step a unit step. The generator was verified numerically before use: 64 distinct points, zero bad adjacencies. Three variants, the base curve and two whole cube rotations of it, cycle on a 25 second block: five seconds of alpha crossfade between paths, ten seconds of hold, an impulse at fifteen seconds, then settling. Rendering projects the lattice through fixed yaw and pitch with mild perspective and draws each segment in depth order as three strokes laid down together: a wider shadow pass in #3A3D44 offset away from the light, the steel body in #7A7F88, and a thin gold rim stripe in #F2B233 riding the lit edge, with quiet seam marks at the vertices in #23242A. The passes are interleaved per segment precisely so a rear segment's rim can never paint over a front tube. The jiggle is one spring damper per vertex in screen space, period about 2.2 seconds, lightly damped, driven by the loop impulses and by drags. Dragging swings the sculpture on a second, slower spring (period 2.5 seconds, heavily damped) to at most a few degrees; releasing it fires an impulse scaled by the swing's velocity, so a hard swing earns a long wobble. Palette exactly per spec, on the #0A0A0C ground. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, the centred cube keeps its proportions on phones with the page ground matching the piece, rAF typeof guarded, hint lowercase and short. The clock offset starts the piece inside a hold, fully formed.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. The first pass failed on the rim light: drawn as one pass after the entire steel body, the gold stripes of rear segments painted across the front tubes and the sculpture read as a gold wireframe hovering over grey lumps, and at the first chosen scale the cube overflowed the frame. Fixes: the cube was scaled down to sit in generous black margins, the seam marks were shrunk and halved in alpha so they read as joints rather than holes, and the three strokes were restructured to draw per segment in depth order (shadow, body, rim together), which put the gold back on the lit edge of each individual tube where it belongs. After the fix the desktop frame reads as a single heavy woven mass with warm light along its upper edges and believable over under depth; the phone frame keeps the same mass centred with margins on all sides.
