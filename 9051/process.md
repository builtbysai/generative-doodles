# №9051 · Ruled Lines

## Study
Instagram generative-art search (2026-10-03): the plotter-art revival is
dominant — ballpoint pen on ruled notebook paper, process-as-content
timelapses, precise algorithmic linework. Jacob Joaquin's "Ballpoint
Rotations" (29K likes): rotating 3D wireframe fractals in blue ballpoint
on college-ruled paper. The current is raw material honesty: real ink,
real paper, the machine's patience as aesthetic.

## Concept
Dense hand-drawn horizontal rules in ballpoint blue on paper white, with
a red margin line. A hidden radial field lifts and waves the lines —
the ghost of a circular presence you feel in the linework before you see
it. The drawing is the tension between the lines, not the lines.

## Process
- Paper: warm white (#faf8f2) with faint grain flecks.
- 110+ horizontal rules, ballpoint blue (#2b4d9b), varying width/alpha
  per line for hand feel.
- 1-2 hidden gaussian "ghost" centers modulate line height (9x line gap)
  and wave amplitude.
- Hand wobble: two sine frequencies per line.
- Red margin line at 8% width, like a notebook.
- Click reseeds.

## QA
- Desktop 1280x800 inspected: wave form clear, ghost presence felt.
