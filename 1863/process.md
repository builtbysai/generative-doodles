# No.1863 "Twenty-Six Little Deaths" (CK8, PP1110)

Source prompt: TASKS.md, carla-kihlstedt block, PP1110. Completes #243 carla-kihlstedt / EXPANSION #243.

Driver: a kraft-paper world in black pen and ink where one black umbrella opens at the start of each loop and 26 tiny vignettes play in sequence, one per letter, each a small contained mechanism, the umbrella closing when Z is done and the paper world resetting for A again.

Palette (hand-picked from the prompt's named colors; studies/carla-kihlstedt.md does not exist): kraft brown ground #C8A06B with darker #B98F57 and lighter #D9B98A, ink black #1E1A16, deep black umbrella #141210, tiny red letter accents #A62B25, peach tone #D08A4E, cream plate #F3E9D2.

Layout: field-like composition, full-bleed on phones (5 columns by 6 rows) and a centered square on desktop (6 columns by 5 rows). The umbrella anchors the top center above a 26-tick march line; the vignettes tile the lower field in an A-to-Z grid that activates row by row. The active cell gets a red border and red letter glyph; played cells show their finished state in ink; waiting cells sit dim at 45 percent ink. Title and date plate top left, "Watch only. The alphabet marches on its own." hint bottom left, both drawn on the canvas.

Motion: watch only, no interaction. A sequencer walks A to Z, 2.4 s per letter, 62.4 s per loop. The umbrella opens over the first 1.6 s and closes over the last 1.6 s. Each vignette is a small state machine driven by its progress p (0 idle, 1 done); letters before the active one show the done state, letters after it sit idle.

Fixed-phase frame one: t = 30.0 s, letter M half played (moon half waned), umbrella fully open, letters A to L in their done states, N to Z idle. All randomness is a seeded mulberry32 (paper ticks, window frost), never wall-clock or unseeded Math.random at frame one. One synchronous frame is drawn at load; requestAnimationFrame is guarded with a typeof check so node-canvas QA works. Visual Viewport sizing with resize listened to on both visualViewport and window; canvas element pinned in draw() via canvas.style.width/height in px; phone detection via window.screen (min dimension under 700px) with aspect fallback h > w*1.15; page ground is the piece background color.

The 26 vignettes: A sleigh bell shaken (swings, then rests tilted); B peach eaten (four bites, pit left); C tower clouded over (cloud descends over the crenellations); D gin bottle emptied (liquid level falls, one drop left); E dripping tap (drop falls, puddle grows); F winding key (key turns, spring coil tightens); G candle burning down (candle shrinks, wax pool grows, flame dies); H spinning top slowing (spin lines slow, top tips over); I seed sprouting then wilting (stem grows, leaves, then droops); J match struck and burned (flame flares then dies, stick chars); K hourglass draining (top sand falls, bottom sand rises); L kettle steaming (steam curls grow); M moon waning (dark half grows across the disc); N book pages turning (right page lifts and flips); O balloon deflating (balloon shrinks and sinks, wrinkled skin left); P pendulum swinging and stilling (amplitude decays to vertical); Q clock hands spinning to midnight; R teacup emptying (liquid falls, steam fades); S snail crawling (snail crosses the cell, trail grows); T icicle melting (icicle shortens, drips, puddle grows); U kite falling (kite descends, tail flutters); V violin bow drawing (bow sweeps, string vibrates); W window frost melting (frost strokes recede, moon appears); X box crossed out (two red strokes draw in turn); Y yarn ball unraveling (ball shrinks, thread runs away); Z door closing (light slit narrows, door panel shuts).

Deviations from the prompt: the prompt specifies WebAudio with each vignette having its own voice; per the build brief this piece is visual-only, so no WebAudio, no sound toggle, and no sound of any kind. Everything else follows the prompt: pen-and-ink rendering on a kraft ground, umbrella opening and closing with the loop, A-to-Z sequencer, tiny red letter glyphs, dry mechanical tone, nothing macabre, nothing cute.

Re-rendered all three sizes after the fix; thumb.png is the 720x720 render of the final index.html. No em dashes in visible copy.
