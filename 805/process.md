# No.805 "Manifestation Five" — EXPANSION #98 olivier-mosset, PP6

## Concept

December 1967, 8 rue Montfaucon: BMPT's Manifestation 5, the motif-swap. Tonight the ring is painted by Toroni's hand, tomorrow by Buren's, the day after by Mosset himself. This piece rebuilds that swap as a generative engine: one square canvas, one ring motif, three hands in rotation on a 60-second loop. The motif never changes — a centred ring whose centreline diameter is 66% of the canvas side, the geometry of No.800. What changes is authorship: the same circle rendered as a circumference of brush imprints, as an annulus of stripes, as a flat administrative outline. The viewer watches the idea stay while the hand changes. Hard cuts only, because a swap is a swap; nothing morphs, nothing blends, and no take ever mixes two hands.

## Construction

- Single self-contained `index.html`, canvas 2D only, no external assets, no audio, no interaction. Watch-only.
- Canvas sizing comes from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both `visualViewport` and `window`; `draw()` pins `canvas.style.width/height` in px every frame. Phone detection uses `window.screen` physical size (min dimension < 700px) with the aspect fallback (`h > w*1.15`) only when screen data is absent.
- Scene: one centred square canvas (fits the frame's smaller dimension, 94% desktop, 92% phone) on a constant surround, #E8E5DD, the gallery wall of No.800, with the same 1px hairline at 10% black alpha so the square's edge stays perceptible whichever hand is up. The surround does not change at the cuts; only the canvas changes hands.
- Loop: 60 s, seamless, three takes of exactly 20 s. The take index is a pure function of loop time (`floor(t/20) mod 3`); nothing inside a take reads the intra-take phase, so each boundary lands in a single frame with no fade, morph, or blend.
- Take 1, Toroni's hand (ground #ECE8DE, dabs cobalt #2B5FA6): 63 imprints of the No. 50 brush set around the motif circle. The dab construction is the study's own, carried over from No.792 unchanged: a solid banded body, near-opaque through the upper two-thirds, cobalt bristle ridges and ground-coloured gap scores inside it, a dense load cluster at the top edge. Dab size is 3.6% of the canvas side, approximating the ring's 3.3% stroke width; spacing is about 0.91 dab-widths of arc, so the imprints touch and read as one ring of distinct stamps. Each dab is rotated so its width runs tangential and its dense load edge points outward; angular jitter is seeded at ±0.25 of the neighbour step, radial jitter ±0.12 of the dab size. All 63 imprint textures and jitters are precomputed once from one mulberry32 stream (seed 805001) and drawn directly each frame; no offscreen canvas.
- Take 2, Buren's hand (canvas #F6F3EC, vermilion #C8102E): the ring as an annulus one full stripe pair thick (outer radius centreline + 8.7% of side, inner radius centreline − 8.7%), filled with horizontal bands at the 8.7 cm module — one band is 8.7% of the 100-unit canvas side — anchored at the canvas's top edge and clipped to the annulus (outer circle, inner circle wound in reverse). The white bands are the canvas white itself, so the ring reads in vermilion fragments where the red bands cross the annulus, exactly as a Buren stripe field lets the ground do half the work; the module never adjusts to the motif, which is why a thin vermilion sliver of the topmost band grazes the annulus's top edge.
- Take 3, Mosset's hand (ground #F4F2EC, ring #141414): the canonical outline from No.800, complete from the cut (no draw-on here; the swap is the event). Stroke is exactly 3.3% of the canvas side, built from 160 arc segments with the same low-frequency wobble, amplitude 0.3% of side, harmonic and phase seeded once (seed 805003).
- The counter: the prompt for PP6 explicitly sanctions one tiny take counter, and it is the only visible text besides `<title>`. Bottom-right corner of the canvas, inset 2.2% of the side, neutral grotesk (Helvetica/Arial), size 1.6% of the side (minimum 10px), drawn in the current hand's dark tone at 0.55 alpha, reading 01/03, 02/03, 03/03 and changing only at the cuts. This follows the sanction recorded for No.798's counter.
- Determinism: frame one is the synchronous first draw at the constant phase t = 0 — take 1 complete, counter at 01/03. The rAF loop (typeof-guarded) continues as phase plus elapsed-since-load; no `Date.now()` or any wall clock shapes any frame. All variants are seeded precomputed data (mulberry32). No gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas anywhere in the piece.

## Deviations

- Surround tone #E8E5DD is a constant presentation tone outside the three per-take palettes, following No.800's recorded precedent: the canvas edge must stay perceptible against the page, and one unchanging wall is what makes the three grounds read as three canvases' worth of difference. Recorded here for the same reason it was recorded there.
- Material thickness differs by hand around the shared centreline: Mosset's stroke is the specced 3.3% of side and Toroni's dab approximates it (3.6%), but Buren's annulus is one stripe pair thick (17.4% of side). A 3.3%-thin annulus would contain less than half a band and no stripe could ever read; the pair is the smallest thickness in which the 8.7 cm module exists at all. The motif (centreline, diameter, centre) is identical in all three takes; only the hand's material differs, which is the piece's subject.
- The Buren bands are anchored at the canvas's top edge rather than centred on the ring, so the stripe phase is the fabric's, not the motif's. Centring the bands on the ring would be composition, and Buren's stripes do not compose.
