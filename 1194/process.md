# №1194 — Ghosttown, Never Twice

joncates study block JC3, grounded in *Ghosttown Spirit Simulator* (2021, with Evan Meaney): "you can never step into the same Ghosttown twice." A procedural ghost town at night — dark wood-and-adobe silhouettes against a deep desert sky, one small fixed moon, green spirit wisps rising from the windows — that exists for three minutes, dissolves into glitch-static, and is replaced by a different town. Forever. No town ever repeats.

## How it works

**The town generator.** Each era `e` seeds its own layout with mulberry32 (seed derived from the era index): 7–11 buildings drawn as flat silhouette polygons in two clusters flanking an empty main street. Five types — saloon (sign board, porch line), water tower (braced legs, tank, cone roof), church (steep roof, steeple, cross), store (parapet, awning), house (gable) — alternating the two shadow fills `#141019` / `#1E1420`. Every town is guaranteed at least one water tower and one church, and at least nine windows; windows are lit gold (`#D9A441`) or dark. Foreground fences (`#6B4F35` at 60%) and 46 seeded gold glints on the sand (`#0E0C14`) complete the era. The layout is a pure function of the era index, recomputed identically on every frame — there is no stored town to return to.

**Eras and the dissolve.** An era lives 180 s. Its last 2 s are a dissolve: horizontal static bands (hash tiles in 9-px rows, `#8A8A93` shades, per-row tear shift) ramp up in coverage while the old town fades, then ramp down as the next era's town fades in underneath. The era timeline is a pure function of piece time plus the recorded summon list, so era boundaries are computed, never accumulated.

**Wisps and the spirit count.** 26 wisps per era rise from the town's windows, lit windows first. Each wisp is closed-form: `age = (t − eraStart + i·7) mod life`, with a per-wisp seeded lifespan of 13–22 s, a sine sway, and a rise of up to ~0.36 of the frame height; alpha peaks at the specced 50% with fade-in and fade-out envelopes, drawn as paired arcs (soft halo + core — no gradients, per house renderer rules). The counter bottom-left, "spirits N", is also a pure function: the number of wisp rises completed since piece time zero, summed floor-by-floor across all elapsed eras — the town remembering its visitors.

**Interaction.** Two, both quiet. Dragging is wind: the pointer's recorded samples yield a velocity that bends wisps and dust motes sideways while the drag lasts and decays exponentially after release; buildings never move. Holding a touch on the moon for 2 seconds (a progress ring fills around it) summons an early dissolve — the summon is recorded as an event at the hold's completion moment and the era timeline re-derives from it. Nothing else is controllable; there is no map, no seed display, no HUD beyond the counter.

**House adaptations.** The whole scene breathes with a closed-form parallax (`0.02·W·sin(2πt/47)`) applied to the town layer only; the moon stays fixed. Canvas sizing uses the Visual Viewport API with dual resize listeners, CSS size pinned in the draw path, phone detection via `window.screen` with aspect fallback. The first frame is drawn synchronously at a fixed phase — T0 = 40 s into era 0, town complete, wisps mid-rise, counter reading 173 — and the animation loop is anchored as T0 + elapsed, typeof-guarded. Seeded hashes only; no Math.random, no gradients, no `ctx.filter`, no shadowBlur, no offscreen canvas, no audio. The town is silent.
