# No.331 Singhay Chosgyal

After Arunima Dazess Wangchuk's Singhay Chosgyal, this piece sets a precise white line wrathful deity on a black ground, drawn with exactly eight fold rotational symmetry, a count asserted in the code. The deity is fixed path data repeated around the ring sector by sector: crown arcs and a skull on the outer ring, a spiral eye on each sector bisector, cheek strokes and jewel dots, and two fang triangles per sector mirrored about the bisector, sixteen fangs in all. The palette outside the disc holds to two colours only, the black of the ground and the white of the line. Inside the disc the field colours take over: red, yellow, green and a field black, and nothing else is used anywhere in the piece.

The outer drawing never moves. It is drawn after the field every frame, from the same path data, in the same place. The inner field does all the moving: forty two pigment blobs travel seven authored closed streamlines inside the disc, six blobs to a streamline, clipped to the disc by a path clip on the single canvas. The streamlines themselves are drawn as faint closed bands along the same data, so the blobs read as pigment moving through a churn rather than dots crossing empty black.

The loop is thirty six seconds and seamless. Every streamline is completed a whole number of times in the loop, one, two or three circuits depending on the ring, and every wobble in a streamline closes on itself a whole number of times, so the frame at thirty six seconds matches the frame at zero while the deity has not shifted at all. The first frame is already mid roil, with the blobs staggered around their circuits. There is no interaction: the contrast between still geometry and moving feeling is the piece, and it is watch only.
