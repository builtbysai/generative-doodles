# No.221 Chance Cloud

## Concept
Kathy McTavish calls chance a frayed thread, a stochastic cloud, a pointillist field: time and space carve out convergence or dissolution, balance, extinction or chaos. This piece is that field, and nothing else. Hundreds of small ink dots cover warm gray paper, each holding a little energy. The rule is ecological, not random: every dot shares energy with its near neighbours, loses a little to decay, and drinks from slow pockets of energy that bloom and die across the field. Where energy concentrates, dots darken into clusters; where it drains, they fade below visibility and are gone. The few dots holding the most energy burn rust, the high-energy survivors. The whole field breathes on roughly a thirty second cycle of convergence and extinction. It is the batch's purest watch piece: no touch layer at all.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Dots sit on a jittered lattice fitted to the frame (roughly a thousand on desktop, fewer on phones), with neighbour lists taken from the lattice, which serves the spatial-hash role at this scale. The simulation steps at a fixed thirtieth of a second: each dot's energy moves ten percent toward its neighbours' average, decays slightly, and gains from five roaming pockets whose strength follows a sine envelope over a seven to thirteen second life; dead pockets respawn elsewhere, which is what keeps clusters forming and dissolving instead of settling. Rendering is direct from the dot list each frame: alpha follows energy, radius grows slightly with it, and dots above 0.8 energy draw in rust #B4552D against ink #14120E. Frame one is pre-run for 350 steps, about twelve seconds of ecology, so the field opens with clusters already formed and dissolving and survivors already marked. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, the field full-bleed at every size, rAF typeof guarded, no text anywhere.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. The first build failed the gate on energy balance: diffusion outran the pockets, energies pooled at low values, and the field rendered as a ghost of itself with no dot ever reaching the rust threshold. Injection was raised, the diffusion rate lowered so clusters hold together longer, a fifth pocket added, and the alpha gain increased. Final renders show the intended ecology at every size: dense dark clusters with rust survivors at their hearts, mid-energy ink scattered between, and whole regions of paper where the energy has drained away to nothing.
