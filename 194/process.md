# No.194 Push a Hundred Times

## Concept
Eugene Sargent's Tinkerfest lesson, made into an object: "They're going to push a button a hundred times before they even know what it does... it has to be intuitive." So here is the button, dinner-plate sized, steel and rubber, one NeoPixel ring, alone in the dark. No label says press me. No counter is displayed and no level is ever named. The escalation is discovered, not announced, and the only teacher is the attract loop: left alone for twenty seconds, the button presses itself once and shows you what a press does.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. The button is layered circles: a #23242A rubber housing, a #7A7F88 steel bezel carrying the NeoPixel ring as 28 arc segments in a single hard-switched hue (the hue jumps 60 degrees per press and steps 24 degrees every 0.9 seconds, cycling the full spectrum one colour at a time), and a radially shaded steel face that squashes on a 0.22s press curve. A hidden press counter maps to four response levels: presses 1 to 5 answer with a single white ripple ring; 6 to 15 with three staggered ripples; 16 to 40 the ring throws ballistic light arcs that fall under gravity and bounce off the viewport edges at 0.92 restitution, drawn in the ring's current hue with white heads; 41 and up is the full cascade, six arcs, double ripples, echo ripples spawned where arcs strike the edges, and a faint white field pulse that each new press re-energises. Rapid tapping is never throttled or punished; the emissions simply stack and the field blooms. The counter resets only after thirty seconds with no user press, fading down through the levels rather than snapping off. Self-presses run the current level's response without incrementing the counter. From level 2 up, a press may also sound a short sine blip whose pitch rises with the count, but the audio path is strictly optional: it is typeof guarded, wrapped in try/catch, created lazily inside the tap gesture, and the piece is complete in total silence (and in the QA harness, which has no AudioContext at all). Frame one shows the button at rest in its opening hue with the faint tail of a self-press ripple still expanding, and the attract clock is offset so the first demonstration press lands about a second after load. Palette exactly per spec. No text anywhere. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen, rAF typeof guarded.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. The first render was already the piece: dead centre, generous black around it, rubber housing reading as a dark halo, segmented ring crisp in its teal opening hue, face shading giving the button real dinner-plate mass, and the seeded ripple visible as a thin white circle at about 80 percent of its travel, faint as intended. The phone frame keeps the button at 40 percent of width and dead centre with the ripple ring still inside the frame. No changes were needed after inspection. The escalation ladder, arc integrator and counter fade are interaction driven and cannot appear in a still; they were verified by tracing the press() emission table against the spec bands (1-5, 6-15, 16-40, 41-100), the 30 second inactivity gate on the fade, and the 20 second attract timer, and by confirming the audio function returns silently when AudioContext is undefined.
