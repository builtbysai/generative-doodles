# No.265 Factory Summit

## Concept
After Stanzin Tsepel's 2023 industrialization piece: a pale blue-grey craggy mountain mass with multiple peaks, surmounted at the summit by a dense factory silhouette, tan blocks, vertical towers, chimney stacks, horizontal beams, dominating the natural form below. Over a 40 second loop the factory's smoke thickens and the structure subtly extends across the ridge, then recedes and the peak is clean again: the warning, not the catastrophe. The mountain endures. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, contain-fitted and centred against a stark pale sky, like a studio photograph. The mountain is two layered ridge lines, a far range mixed toward the sky and a front mass in full ice blue-grey, each ridge roughened with seeded intermediate crags; darker facet planes and small snow lies sit on the front slopes. The factory is a block assemblage on a grey platform straddling the highest peak: two halls, a tying beam, two towers and two standing chimneys, with lit window slits. A single dominance parameter, eased on a triangle over the 40 second loop, drives the encroachment: a third chimney rises off the main hall, then extension blocks creep out along the ridge to the left and right, the furthest shed last. Smoke is one seeded system of 30 soft particles shared by the stacks, rising, drifting and fading, its density and opacity scaled by the same dominance parameter; the third stack only smokes once it has risen. The opening clock starts at 11 seconds, mid encroachment, smoke already up and the first extension block out along the ridge. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no interaction handlers, no audio, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported resize listeners only. One fix during construction: the third chimney was first drawn growing downward from its cap; it now rises upward from the hall roof, its smoke origin matched to its full height. Frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: the factory dense on the summit with smoke climbing, the left extension visibly out along the ridge, the massif quiet beneath. On the phone the contain fit keeps the whole massif in frame on its pale ground. No further fixes were needed.
