# №9084 · Murmur

Particle loop after Joseff (jn3008.art): 380 luminous cyan particles on deep
black that drift in a harmonic noise field, coalesce into a perfect glowing
ring, hold it while it rotates, then dissolve back into drift — a 14-second
seamless loop (all motion uses integer harmonics of the loop frequency).

Additive blending with fade-fill trails; particles brighten and swell as the
ring forms. A faint guide ring fades in with the coalescence. Touch stirs the
field with transient, decaying offsets so the loop is never broken.

No text in the artwork.
