# No.191 Forty-Five Foot Worm

## Concept
The Wilson Park worm in Torrance, California: forty five feet of ferrocement, built to be climbed on, painted in weathered segments with the occasional red one, and loved for decades. This piece lays it across warm paper like a treadmill diorama of the park: the worm crawls in place on a lazy S that bleeds off both edges of the frame while benches and the park's little blob creatures drift past underneath. A peristalsis wave runs continuously from head to tail. Conduct it with a drag, and if you tap one of the red segments it tucks itself smaller, shy, then relaxes back. No face, no eyes: it is concrete, and that is the charm.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Forty four segments ride a sine spline sampled across the frame; each segment is a rounded rectangle rotated to the local tangent, filled in segments grey-green #9AA39B with a seam outline in #6E766E and seeded weathering speckles, drawn over a soft shadow band in #D8CFBB that follows the spline underneath. Every tenth segment (indices 4, 14, 24, 34) is painted signal red #C4451F with pale ferrocement chips flecked across it. The crawl is a travelling wave: thickness swells and lifts phase-shifted along the body, the phase advancing four full cycles per 45 second loop so the motion is seamless, and the whole park layer below (three slatted benches, three blob creatures in worm colours, one of them red) drifts slowly leftward on the treadmill, wrapping at the edges, while the worm itself stays anchored. Dragging along the body plants ripple pulses at the finger's position that propagate toward the tail with gaussian lift and a 1.8 second decay, and segments directly under a held finger lift and settle in sequence as it moves. A tap landing on a red segment runs its tuck: a smooth shrink to about sixty percent with a slight extra rotation and a pull toward the body line, held briefly, then relaxed over the following second. The clock starts twelve seconds in so frame one is mid crawl. Palette exactly per spec, on the warm paper ground, hint in seam green. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, full-bleed field on phones, rAF typeof guarded, hint lowercase and short.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. Desktop was right on the first pass: the S reads, the reds land every ten segments, the shadow band grounds the body, and the park strip sits small along the bottom as intended. The phone frame failed proportionally: segment thickness keyed to height alone stayed at desktop scale while the horizontal pitch compressed to a third, so the segments stacked into a dense overlapping thicket that read as a centipede. Fix: segment thickness is now the smaller of the height key and 2.6 times the actual segment pitch, and all lift amounts (crawl, conducted pulses, finger follow) are expressed in units of that thickness, so proportions hold at any aspect. After the fix the phone frame shows the same beaded worm following the same S at a smaller gauge, reds and benches legible, and the desktop and thumbnail frames are unchanged in character.
