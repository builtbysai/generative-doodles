# No.239 Ladder Ladder

## Concept
Woodfill's own account: he tried to hack a table into a sculpture and it ended up back as a table, which led to using things to build the same things, "like LADDER LADDER, where a ladder was turned into a ladder." So: a ladder built out of small ladders. Each rail is a stack of six tiny ladders, each rung is a tiny ladder laid across, and every joint is clamped and bolted, provisional and revisable, a few loose offcut ladders scattered at the base. Drag any mini ladder and it pulls out of the assembly; let go and it springs home, the whole rig wobbling with the disturbance before it settles back into itself. The piece always returns to itself; there is no reload gesture.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Eighteen draggable pieces (twelve rail segments, six rung ladders) are drawn by one mini ladder routine (two stiles, rungs, bolt heads at the stile ends), in birch and poplar alternating, each with a small baked rotation jitter so nothing is CAD clean. Steel clamp bands with bolt dots sit at the seven joints of each rail. The whole assembly leans two and a half degrees off vertical and floats on the paper ground in a 4:5 portrait panel, three offcut ladders lying at its foot. Each piece carries a spring offset (stiffness 90, damping 9); dragging displaces a piece against its spring, release propagates a velocity kick to its neighbours (rail segments touch their vertical neighbours and the rung at their level; rungs touch both rails) and excites a soft whole-rig wobble spring. Frame one is the assembly whole and settled. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Frame one shows the meta ladder complete: rail stacks and rung ladders legible as ladders at both scales, clamp bands at every joint, the lean visible, offcuts scattered at the base. On the phone frame the portrait panel centres and the whole rig fits with the offcuts in view at 390 wide. The headless renderer fires no pointer events, so the drag, spring home and wobble were set from the spring constants and reviewed in code; the settled state renders correctly at all sizes. No adjustments were needed after the first render.
