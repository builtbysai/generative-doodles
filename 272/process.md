# No.272 Rock That Was Once Rock

## Concept
After Shupiwe Chongwe's stoneware rock forms: vessel-forms inspired by rock formations, made in material that was once rock, clay being rock ground down and given back its shape. Four craggy, strata-banded vessels of varied heights stand in a loose, off-centre cluster on a bare studio ground, reading as both geology and pot, with long soft shadows and empty warm ground around them. A tap turns the rock over: the silhouettes stay fixed while the strata bands, iron seams and lichen patches re-form on another face in a slow crossfade. Watch only between taps.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, centred fit on the studio ground, with the studio ground colour as the page ground. The palette is held to the five specced colours only: strata grey, iron band, lichen, studio ground and deep crevice. Each of the four vessels is a fixed seeded craggy lathe silhouette, a side-profile path sampled at 16 levels from a piecewise width profile with angular jitter per side, built once and never regenerated. A dark mouth ellipse at the top of each vessel holds the pot reading and is also fixed. A strata set is pure data derived from a strata seed: horizontal bands covering the full height of each vessel, their tones mixed only between palette colours, iron seam polylines, dark crevice cracks and lichen patches with satellite spots. Drawing a set clips to the fixed silhouette and paints the data directly, with no offscreen canvas. A deliberate tap, a pointer press and release within a small movement, generates a new strata set and crossfades old to new over about three seconds by alpha; a tap mid-fade settles the running fade first. Long soft shadows are flattened, right-shifted silhouette copies in deep crevice at low alpha in two layers, plus a contact shadow under each foot, with no blur or filters. The studio ground is flat with a slightly darker back wall band, a floor line and seeded flecks in palette colours at low alpha. Frame one opens with the first arrangement fully formed and the shadows settled. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds, pointer coordinates convertible through the stored centred-fit transform, no text, no labels and no shadowBlur.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported canvas pointerdown and pointerup plus both resize listeners, and a grep confirms no shadowBlur, no offscreen canvas and exactly the five specced palette colours in the script. One round of fixes. First, the flattened silhouette shadows stood too tall and read as a translucent ghost vessel beside the cluster, so they were flattened further, stretched longer and lowered in alpha until they lay on the floor as long smears to the right. Second, the flank modelling was a single rect whose straight edge cut a hard vertical stripe down each vessel, so it was rebuilt as five stepped strips at low alpha that fade across the flank. Third, the lichen satellite spots were large enough to read as bubbles on the smallest vessel, so the patches were flattened and the spots shrunk and softened. Final frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: four craggy strata-banded rock-vessels of varied heights in a loose off-centre cluster on the bare studio ground, iron bands and seams and lichen readable, dark mouths holding the pot reading, long shadows settled, and generous empty ground around them. On the phone the centred fit keeps the whole cluster in frame with the strata still legible and nothing clipped. No further fixes were needed.
