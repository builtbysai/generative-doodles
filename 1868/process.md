# No.1868 "Weather Eye" (FN5, PP1115)

Source prompt: TASKS.md, francesca-naibo block, PP1115. Study #244 francesca-naibo.

Driver: negotiation between two voices, approach, listen, withdraw, never collide, with distance mapping to harmonic tension. The felt phenomenon is a dialogue of care between a restless small form and a slow large one.

Palette: near-black ground #0B0B0D with a slightly lighter field #121214, charcoal small form #2B2B30 edged #45454C, deep gray large form #1E1E22 edged #3A3A41, one faint warm edge #B98A5E on the large form, voice-threads and plate text in bone white #E8E2D4, hint gray #9A958A, dark plate #16161A.

Layout: field-like composition, full-bleed on phones and a centered square on desktop. Two forms sit off-center with breathing room around them; the ground is a field, not a stage. Title and date plate top left, "Watch only. Two voices negotiate." hint bottom left, both drawn on the canvas in dark plates with bone text.

Motion: watch only, no interaction. Two blob agents drift on closed-form paths, a restless small one on faster cycles and a slow large one on slower cycles, over a 64 s loop. Attraction and repulsion with a minimum-approach distance of 0.30 field units: if the raw drift would bring the forms closer than that, they are pushed apart along the line between them, so they never collide. Distance maps to harmonic tension, 1 at the minimum approach and 0 at 0.75 units. Two pale voice-threads weave between the forms, their brightness following the tension, entering when the forms are close and fading when they separate; small pale beads ride the threads. The small form carries a pale core, its listening eye, which brightens as tension rises. The large form carries a faint warm edge on the side facing the small form.

Fixed-phase frame one: t = 14.0 s, forms mid approach at about 0.42 field units apart, tension about 0.72, threads half lit. All motion is a pure closed-form function of t; all randomness is seeded mulberry32 (ground specks, thread beads), never wall clock, Date, or unseeded Math.random. One synchronous frame is drawn at load; requestAnimationFrame is guarded with a typeof check so node-canvas QA works. Visual Viewport sizing with resize listened to on both visualViewport and window; canvas element pinned in draw() via canvas.style.width/height in px; phone detection via window.screen (min dimension under 700px) with aspect fallback h > w*1.15; page ground is the piece background color.

Deviations from the prompt: the prompt gives each form a voice (plucked granular guitar, bowed cello pad) and shares one state between motion and audio; per the build brief this piece is visual-only, so no WebAudio at all, no sound toggle, and no sound of any kind. The distance-to-tension mapping is kept as the visual brightness of the voice-threads. Everything else follows the prompt: two dark rounded forms on a near-black ground, approach, listen, withdraw, never collide, pale voice-threads weaving between them, abstract and careful, no faces, no characters, no romantic narrative.
