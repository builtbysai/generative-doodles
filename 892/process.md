# No.892 — No Star Is Lost

Study #109 klsr.av, PP53 "Ink Inversion". The one light-ground piece in the study: Klsr's "No Star Is Lost" (2026-09-14) inverts his usual black-ground bloom — the light is the canvas, the color is the event.

## Concept

On a clean off-white page, an organic ink bloom grows from a central seed like a creature: petals of magenta and purple push outward, teal accents appear inside the color, and thin black smoke filaments thread upward through it. The bloom lives on a budget. Left alone it grows from SEED to full BLOOM, then begins to go SPENT — colors desaturating toward the page, emission stopping, a pale wash rising. Dragging through the ink feeds it: color is pushed outward along the hand's path and the budget refills. A tap drops a new seed with its own small micro-bloom; a double-tap washes the page clean and starts over with one fresh seed. The only text on the page is the small state word at the bottom — SEED, BLOOM, or SPENT — as specced.

## Construction

- **Particles and petals.** The main seed owns 400 particles (300 on the phone path); each tap seed owns 90. Particles are fixed at build time from a seeded mulberry32 stream: each belongs to one of 9 petals (6 for micro-blooms), carries a radial fraction f along its petal path, angular jitter, a curl that bends its angle as f grows, a per-petal reach, a size, and a color (about 53% magenta #C81E8F, 32% purple #6A1FA0, 15% teal #1E9E8A, with every third petal biased purple). A particle is drawn as three layered flat alpha discs; a slightly larger disc in the bloom-edge tone #F5F0EA is drawn first as the feathered rim. No gradients, no shadowBlur, no filter, no offscreen canvas.
- **Growth / budget model.** Each seed's growth g(t) is a closed-form function of its age and the recorded feed-event list: rise = 0.92·(1 − exp(−age/55)); a decline factor exp(−(age − D)/150) applies after D = 170 + 160·min(total feed, 1.2), so feeding literally postpones the spent phase; each feed event adds a boost amt·exp(−(t − t_event)/100). Spentness sp rises as (age − D)/110 once past D, scaled down by recent feed boost. Particle activation is clamp((g − 0.78f)/0.22): outer particles wake only as growth reaches them, and slide outward as it passes. Spentness mixes particle colors toward the ground color, fades alpha, and raises a pale wash overlay (alpha = sp·0.30). Emission stops on its own because declining g deactivates the outer particles first.
- **State word (precise rule).** Computed from the main seed: SPENT when sp ≥ 0.5; otherwise SEED when g < 0.25; otherwise BLOOM. Frame one has g = 0.54 and sp = 0, so it reads BLOOM.
- **Feeding.** Dragging records a feed event every ~26 px of travel, credited to the nearest seed: fixed amount 0.035 plus a reach term clamp(speed/(1.2·S), 0.08, 0.55) that decays with a 45 s constant and stretches petal reach (multiplier capped at 1.9) — fast drags throw long petals, slow drags dense core growth. Drags also record decaying push events that displace nearby particles outward, so the hand visibly pushes color along its path.
- **Filaments.** 18 dark filaments on the main seed (6 per micro-bloom), seeded wiggle polylines that rise on individual cycles through a band about one bloom-radius tall centered on the seed, alpha up to 0.26 in smoke black #1A1A1A.
- **Interaction summary.** Drag = feed and push; tap = new seed after a 0.33 s deferral so a double-tap can pre-empt it (cap 5 seeds total, main included); double-tap = wash: seeds, feed and push lists reset, one fresh central seed, a brief edge-tone flood flash. Audio starts only on the first pointer gesture: a looped noise wash through a lowpass plus two low sine tones, gains driven by the main seed's living growth (g·(1 − 0.85·sp)), thinning toward silence when spent. AudioContext is created lazily inside the gesture handler and every use is typeof-guarded and try-wrapped; the piece renders completely in silence in the QA harness, which has no AudioContext.
- **Determinism.** Frame one is the synchronous draw(0) at load. The main seed's birth is set to t = −48 s — a fixed analytic pre-growth segment, not a simulation — so frame one evaluates the same closed-form growth functions at age 48 s: g(0) = 0.92·(1 − exp(−48/55)) ≈ 0.54, the young BLOOM in the renders. Nothing in the drawn state reads Date.now() or any wall clock; the rAF loop clock is only an elapsed offset for t > 0 and rAF itself is typeof-guarded. Canvas size comes from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window, and the canvas style is pinned in px inside draw(). Phone detection uses window.screen's min dimension (< 700) with the aspect fallback. The page ground is #EDE8E2 at every size, per the light-ground rule.

## QA log

- Rendered with `node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js` at 1280×900 (qa-desktop.png), 390×844 (qa-mobile.png), 720×720 (thumb.png). Renders cleanly in silence; no console errors.
- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and byte-compared with `cmp` — identical, before and after the tuning round below. Duplicate render deleted.
- First read-back: two faults. (1) The smoke filaments read as gray rain across the middle of the page — their rise band was 1.8 bloom-radii tall with offsets spread wider than the ink. Fixed: rise band shortened to ~1.05 radii, offsets narrowed to the bloom's core, alpha lowered to 0.26; they now thread through the color. (2) The creature was too small (about a quarter of the frame) because newly activated particles sat at 35% of their path radius. Fixed: radius slide eased to 0.5 + 0.5·activation, max reach raised from 0.27·S to 0.30·S, petal reaches widened; the bloom now spans about a third of the frame.
- Final read-back: all three sizes show the clean off-white ground, a magenta-purple ink creature with teal accents inside and black filaments rising through it at center, and the small word BLOOM at the bottom. Mobile (phone path, 390 wide) keeps the bloom centered with nothing clipped. No text besides the title and the specced state word; palette is exactly the six specced tones; no em dashes in the file.

## Deviations

- **Frame-one growth is 0.54, not exactly 0.50.** The brief asked for a pre-grown bloom at growth ≈ 0.5 reading BLOOM. The analytic rise curve gives 0.54 at the chosen 48 s pre-growth; it sits squarely in the BLOOM band (0.25 ≤ g, sp < 0.5) and matches the specced visual, so the exact decimal was left where the curve puts it rather than adding a fudge constant.
- **Tap seeds are deferred 0.33 s.** A tap must wait out the double-tap window before its seed is committed, or every wash would first plant a seed. This is the established house pattern for tap/double-tap disambiguation; the seed appears a third of a second after the tap.
- **Filaments are short rising segments, not continuous trails.** Standing trails would need accumulated frame state, which the determinism gate forbids; each filament is instead a seeded wiggle polyline whose position is a closed-form function of t, redrawn every frame.
