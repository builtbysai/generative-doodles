# №9000 · Interruptions — process

Seed 19 from the 2026-09-23 Vera Molnar study ("Interruptions"): her
1968-69 series where a dense field of equal-length randomly-rotated lines
is interrupted by erased regions, and the voids become the figure.

Recipe, from the study notes: a jittered lattice of short ink dashes,
constant stroke weight per piece (her plotter line), warm paper. Then
erasure as a drawing tool: dashes whose midpoint falls inside a seeded
hidden shape are removed. The shape is one of three: a circle bloom, a
diagonal band, or a crescent (a circle with a second circle cutting back
into the erasure). The ragged boundary left by the dash midpoints is kept,
as in her originals, so the void is shaped by the lines around it.

Shown as the final state on load: the field and its voids together, no
growth animation. Click picks a new seed and a new hidden shape.

Seed 196819 is the default: a large circular void blooming off-center in
the weave, the closest to the DAM piece the study inspected.
