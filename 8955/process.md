# №8955 · Convergence - process notes

- **Title:** Convergence
- **Seed:** 250 (Containment Cone - Tenney Figure 6)
- **Concept:** A 5-limit tonnetz lattice (2^a 3^b 5^c) drawn as a tilted
  crystal on dark indigo. Every node has a road home: faint gold guide
  lines converge from all nodes onto the 1/1 root. Pulses launch from
  random nodes, accelerate along the guide lines, and bloom on arrival
  with an expanding ring.
- **Technique:** Vanilla canvas, seeded PRNG for node jitter. Additive
  glow via layered radial gradients; trails drawn as fading line
  segments. Hover maps pointer to nearest node and shows its reduced
  ratio; click sends a volley of pulses. Drifting dust and a vignette
  finish the scene.
