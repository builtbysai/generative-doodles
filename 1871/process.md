# No.1871 "So Much Time" (FN8, PP1118)

Source prompt: TASKS.md, francesca-naibo block, PP1118. Study #244 francesca-naibo, completing the study at No.1871.

Driver: three voices at incommensurate periods drifting in and out of phase; the felt phenomenon is waiting for the rare moment all three land together, and the held breath when they do.

Palette: booth black-red ground #14090A with a slightly lighter field #1C0C0D, child thread pale yellow #F2E6A2, adult thread deep blue #33507F, guitar thread warm copper #C07A3E, particle dust dim gray #8A8580, hint gray #A89E96, dark plate #1E0D0E.

Layout: field-like composition, full-bleed on phones and a centered square on desktop. Three horizontal bands, one per voice, each a looping ribbon across the frame; the booth walls are barely visible as dark strips at the field edges. Title and date plate top left, "Watch only. Three voices drift in and out of phase." hint bottom left, both drawn on the canvas in dark plates.

Motion: watch only, no interaction. Three voices loop at incommensurate periods, child 7 s, adult 11 s, guitar 17 s. Each band is a sine ribbon with a glowing head that travels the loop left to right and wraps; a brighter trail follows the ribbon for 0.42 of the loop behind the head, and seeded dim gray dust particles mark each thread's path, fading with distance behind the head. Phase convergence is computed, not faked: the mean resultant length of the three phase angles, raised to the fourth power, drives a soft pale glow that gathers where the heads meet and brightens all three ribbons as the phases cluster, then lets them separate again. Motion is slow by construction, well under 14 Hz, and prefers-reduced-motion renders a still frame.

Fixed-phase frame one: t = 23.0 s, heads mid drift and distinct, child phase 0.29, adult phase 0.09, guitar phase 0.35, no convergence yet. All motion is a pure closed-form function of t; all randomness is seeded mulberry32 (dust particles, wall specks), never wall clock, Date, or unseeded Math.random. One synchronous frame is drawn at load; requestAnimationFrame is guarded with a typeof check so node-canvas QA works. Visual Viewport sizing with resize listened to on both visualViewport and window; canvas element pinned in draw() via canvas.style.width/height in px; phone detection via window.screen (min dimension under 700px) with aspect fallback h > w*1.15; page ground is the piece background color.

Deviations from the prompt: the prompt specifies 2D canvas with WebAudio and three looped voices; per the build brief this piece is visual-only, so no WebAudio at all, no sound toggle, and no sound of any kind. The three periods and the computed phase coincidences are kept as the visual phasing of the ribbons and the convergence glow. The study file studies/francesca-naibo.md does not exist in the muse-soul repo (404), so the hexes were hand-picked from the prompt's named colors. No timeline scrubber, no progress bars, no "wait for the drop" energy; the convergences arrive unannounced. Everything else follows the prompt: a dark, still booth, three threads at different periods drifting in and out of phase, faint dust marking each path, watch only.
