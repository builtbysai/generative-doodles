# No.342 Flat Net

## Concept

After MyClayCo's bow cutter and the moment their flat net "looks kinda weird before you assemble it": the net of a bow laid out on paper as pure line. Two loop blanks left and right, a knot band in the middle, two notched tails splayed below, and a dashed terracotta score line on every fold. On a twenty-four-second loop the net folds itself up into the bow and unfolds back to the net, holds at both ends. The transformation is the piece: flat line drawing one moment, dusty-rose bow the next, and the dusty rose belongs to the assembled state only. Watch only: there is nothing to tap or drag, just the paper engineering doing its trick.

## Construction

One canvas, everything as JS data, no offscreen layer, no gradients, no shadowBlur, no filters, no 3D library. The loop blanks are lens outlines sampled from paired cubic arcs that meet at pointed tips, with a small authored jitter baked into the samples away from the tips so the line keeps a hand-drawn stance and the tips stay crisp. The knot is a rounded-rect band sampled as a polygon; the tails are authored ribbons whose bases meet exactly at the centre of the knot's bottom edge, each with a V notch at the far end. The loop runs 5s flat hold, 8s fold, 5s bow hold, 5s unfold, 1s flat, summing to 24s, and the fold amount is a pure function of the loop phase, so frame 24s is pixel-identical to frame 0. The fold is 2D hinge work computed as data: each loop compresses toward its hinge, swells taller and lifts, the far edge lifting most, which reads as the blank rotating up with a perspective skew; each tail swings about its base hinge and tucks up under the knot; the knot cinches about its own centre and gains two ridge lines as the fold completes. Fill weight tracks each piece's own fold amount, so mid-fold the pieces are translucent and the flat net is lines only. The dashed score lines are printed on the material: the loop creases travel with the compression and the tail scores ride their base edges as the tails swing. Draw order is tails, loops, score lines, knot last on top, so the knot covers the seams where the loops tuck in. Palette is paper #F4EFE3, ink #1C1A17, terracotta #C25E3C and dusty rose #D98E8E only. House rules throughout: Visual Viewport sizing with resize on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned every frame via style width and height in px, phone detection via window.screen min dimension under 700px with aspect fallback, centred fit at 1280x900 design space with the page ground in the paper colour, an offset clock (9s) so the first synchronous frame is already mid-fold with pieces part-raised, typeof-guarded rAF, fixed data, no text or UI in the piece.

## QA log

Rendered with node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png) and 720x720 (thumb.png), all three inspected, plus two extra state probes rendered from temporary copies with the clock offset moved: offset 0 for the flat net and offset 15 for the assembled bow. Two defects were caught and fixed during QA. First, the original tails were authored with overlapping bases under the knot, so their inner edges crossed below the knot and the translucent mid-fold fills showed a spurious X; the tails were re-authored with bases meeting at the centre line, and the same pass fixed a stray dashed segment at the right tail's notch, where the mirrored tail's base indices had been reversed so its score line was drawn at the far end. Second, at full fold the tail swing angle of 0.40 rad over-straightened the wide ribbons and their inner far corners crossed past the centre, one corner poking below the other tail as a sliver; the swing was eased to 0.30 rad, leaving a slight natural splay with no crossing. Final renders confirmed: the flat net is lines only with all four score lines visible; frame one is mid-fold with pieces part-raised and the fill translucent; the assembled bow is fully opaque rose with plump standing loops, a cinched knot with ridge lines layered on top, and tails hanging clean either side of the centre with their notches clear. Mobile keeps the centred fit on a continuous paper ground with nothing clipped. Palette audit: only the four specced hexes appear in the file. No em dashes. Not pushed; staged for fly's gate.
