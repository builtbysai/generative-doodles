Driver: AW4, aluan-wang study #211, PP850, built to the verbatim prompt as a tap-to-reseed parametric wreath.

What was built: №1603 "Phyllotaxy, Set in Motion". Sixteen plants arranged radially on a tan roundel (#EFE6D0) with a darker tan vignette margin outside it. Each plant is generated from a seeded parameter vector (phyllotaxy mode opposite/alternate/whorled, internode length with a phototropism bias toward the top, entire or serrated margins, branching angle, node density, sage or deep green leaves, mauve seed pods on some plants, ochre stems). Growth is animated by revealing stem segments node by node, the newest frond unfurls on a 20s cycle, seed pods swell slowly, and a warm-white butterfly-form at one plant tip opens once per 60s cycle and closes. Tap anywhere cycles through a fixed authored seed sequence (six seeds) and regrows the whole wreath over ~6s. One warm-white accent bloom sits beside the butterfly. Flat vector shapes with seeded paper grain, palette exactly as specified, no other colors except a darker tan shade of the roundel ground used for the page/vignette margin. Title, date "2006 · 06 · 02", and hint "Tap to regrow." rendered on the page. House rules followed: Visual Viewport sizing, canvas pinned in draw(), window.screen phone detection, deterministic fixed-phase first frame with mulberry32 seeds only, no Math.random, no Date.now, no shadowBlur, no gradients.

Fix rounds:
1. Caught before first render: plant points were stored in a per-plant frame but drawn as absolute offsets, which would have piled all 16 plants at one spot. Fixed the generator to store absolute coordinates and absolute heading.
2. First render: the wreath ring sat at 0.30-0.49 of the roundel radius, so the wreath covered only ~45% of the frame and the butterfly read as a closed white blob. Rescaled the ring to 0.58-0.88 of the roundel radius (wreath now ~85% of frame), lengthened leaves, moved the butterfly open window to 12-36s so it is fully open at the fixed first frame (t=20s), and offset the accent bloom clear of the wings. Re-rendered desktop, mobile, and thumb; all three read well.
3. Determinism check: thumb rendered twice, md5 identical (60fe1cf4ae56332f68b871adf60c9561).

Deviations from the prompt: none in the system. Two judgment calls: the vignette margin outside the roundel uses a darker shade of the same tan ground (#E2D5B4) rather than a new color, keeping the palette to the specified hues; the butterfly opens once per 60s cycle (the prompt says "opens once and closes" without a period, so one open per slow cycle was chosen).
