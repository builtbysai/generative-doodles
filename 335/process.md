# No.335 Raudra Roop

## Concept

After Arunima Dazess Wangchuk's 2025 ceramic mask: a wrathful deity mask in glazed ceramic fills the frame, crowned with skulls, spiral brows over bulging eyes, fangs bared and a tongue protruding over the chin. Wrath as devotion, the physical manifestation of inner rage. The mask itself never moves a fraction. Only the glaze lives: mottled turquoise, teal and blue, darkening where it pools in the crevices and thinning toward blue on the high points, flowing the way a kiln firing never quite ends. The palette holds to the five specced colours only: glaze turquoise, teal, glaze blue, the dark pool colour, and the clay body colour, which shows at the rim and nowhere else, over a near-black ground.

## Construction

One canvas, 2D, no offscreen or stacked layers, no filters, no shadowBlur. The mask is fixed symmetric path data drawn every frame in the same place: a smoothed silhouette sampled from a symmetric authored control polygon, a crown band of three arcs carrying exactly five skulls (the count is asserted in the code; the prompt gives no skull count, so five is fly's resolution, replaceable on Bug's word), spiral brows, a third eye, bulging eyes, cheek and side spirals, a nose, a toothy mouth with two great fangs, and the tongue. The crown rises just past the top edge of the design space and the chin just past the bottom, so the frame crops the mask slightly.

The glaze is a field of stepped colour cells held as JS data, drawn under a path clip of the inner silhouette, so no cell ever leaves the mask. Colour migrates across the surface on a travelling wave, but the cells themselves stay put, as glaze does. Pooling is precomputed, never calculated at runtime: an authored concavity table samples points along the brow furrow, the eye rings, the nose sides, the mouth line, the crown groove, the tongue groove and the chin crease, and any cell near a crevice point carries a dark bias, while cells on the forehead dome, the cheekbones and the nose bridge carry a thinning bias toward glaze blue. The one warm side light is suggested with layered translucent strokes of the palette's own turquoise and blue along the left edges.

The loop is sixty seconds and seamless. Both wave terms complete a whole number of cycles in sixty seconds (two cycles and one cycle, asserted as whole numbers in the code), so the colour field at sixty seconds is exactly the colour field at zero, while the mask has not shifted at all. The first frame opens mid-flow, the loop clock offset to 27.3 seconds, with pooling already in place. There is no interaction of any kind: watch only, per the prompt and Hans's RETHINK INTERACTION rule.

## QA log

Rendered from the actual script with the node-canvas renderer at 1280x900 (qa-desktop.png), 390x844 (qa-mobile.png) and 720x720 (thumb.png, in the piece folder), and all three inspected. First pass failed the gate twice over: the silhouette read as a plain narrow oval with too much dead ground at the sides, and the pooling bias was so strong that whole blocks of near-black cells swallowed the brow spirals and nose. Fixes: the silhouette control polygon was widened by about a fifth (crown arcs and skull positions moved to match, skull angles respread to -90, -65, -115, -40 and -140 degrees), the crevice bias was softened from -1.15 to -0.7 over a tighter 34px radius, the base pool frequency and wave amplitude were reduced, the cell grout was closed to a hairline, and the nose was rebuilt as one filled glaze-blue form with the nostrils pooled dark on it instead of three floating strokes. Final renders: desktop shows one large wrathful mask cropped slightly at crown and chin, the clay rim showing only at the edge, exactly five skulls on the crown band, spiral brows, third eye, bulging eyes, cheek and side spirals, a readable nose, and the fanged mouth with the tongue over the chin, all over a mottled turquoise, teal and blue stepped-cell glaze with darker pooling gathered at the mouth and crevices. Mobile (390x844) keeps the centred fit on the piece's own near-black ground with every feature still legible; the thumb reads the same at 720x720. The renderer registers only the two resize listeners, confirming watch-only with no interaction handlers. Palette audited in code: only the five specced colours are used. The 60s seamlessness rests on the asserted whole wave cycles (2 and 1) with every other element static, so frame 60s equals frame 0 by construction.
