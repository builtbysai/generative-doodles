# No.247 Scaffold Configuration No. 7

## Concept
After Bawa's 3D-printed Scaffold Configurations, Agenda Gallery 2020-21: a cluster of small nickel plated steel scaffold maquettes in a dark room, dramatically lit from one side, throwing webs of interlacing geometric shadow across the walls and floor. The grids are Tinkertoy-like and the viewer feels Gulliver sized looking down at them. The light drifts on a forty second loop and the shadow webs breathe apart and together; the maquettes themselves never move. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1200 by 800 scene: a black room, its back wall and floor lifted a breath from pure black in the steel blue-grey family so the pure black shadows can read against them, with two faint vertical wall seams. Six maquettes are generated from a fixed seed into spread cluster slots along the floor, each a parametric scaffold of two to four bays and two to three levels with a shallow depth offset for the three-quarter view: front grids in nickel, back grids and depth connectors in steel blue-grey, alternating diagonals, and a single warm rim edge, the top front rail of each maquette, drawn at twenty percent. The light is an off-frame point drifting on a forty second ellipse. Shadows are drawn two ways, always as projected line work, never filters: a radial magnification from the light point sprawls a faint far web over everything, and the near floor web displaces each node along the light's throw direction in proportion to the node's height above its own base, so feet cast at their feet and tops shear far across the floor. The opening clock starts at twenty nine seconds with the light well off to the left and the webs already spread. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, shaped scene centred on the room black ground, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text, no dust, no flare.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Two fixes before this passed. First, the maquettes were seeded anywhere across the floor and two landed on the same spot, tangling into one knot on the right; they are now placed in jittered cluster slots so neighbours touch but never coincide. Second, the floor shadows initially projected every node onto a single floor line, which collapsed the whole web into one black bar under the structures; the projection was rebuilt as a height-proportioned shear along the light's throw, with the wall and floor surfaces lifted slightly in the steel family, and the webs now shear long across the floor and interlace as the light drifts. Frame one reads as the cluster caught from above at an angle, six little grids in a row, their shadows already raking away to the right. The phone frame centres the scene on the black ground and the cluster stays legible at 390 wide.
