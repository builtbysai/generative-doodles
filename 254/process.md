# No.254 Chaos Poster

## Concept
After the 2024 TouchDesigner bomb posters by tanzinchospel.art, CHAOS*, LITTLE BOY and FAT MAN: a monochrome violet graphic poster in that template. One emblematic object at centre, a dome fragment; museum-label typography below it; the two-character chaos compound small at top right; one satirical caption line at the bottom. One hue family only, purple on purple. The typography is the artwork. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D. The poster is a fixed 4:5 rectangle (400 by 500 design units), centred on a black page, scaled to fit any screen. Behind the object, three layers of seeded smoke blobs (black, graphic mid violet, highlight) advect on slow sine drifts at different speeds and scales, clipped to the poster, so the churn is seamless and never repeats obviously. The dome fragment is a hemisphere of seven flat facet slices toned from the mid violet toward the highlight on the lit side, a jagged shard edge along its broken base, short seam ticks at the rim, two inner arc seams, one dark crater facet, and a hard highlight rim stroke down the right arc: flat graphic, no shading gradients anywhere. Type is set in a condensed grotesk stack with a tracked-text helper that measures and spaces each glyph: the title CHAOS*, a hairline rule, the three label lines (STUDY IN PURPLE, NO. 1 / REALTIME RENDER, SINGLE PASS / LADAKH / 2024), and the caption at the bottom, "rendered in real time, regretted at leisure." The chaos compound is drawn as text glyphs in a CJK font stack. The opening clock starts at thirteen seconds so the smoke is already developed behind the dome on frame one. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no interaction handlers.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. One fix: the first dome carried full radial seam lines from its centre and read as a rising sun fan; the seams are now short rim ticks plus two concentric arcs, and the shape reads as a faceted dome fragment. The chaos glyphs render correctly in the harness (Noto Sans CJK is installed) and on the phone frame the poster keeps its 4:5 with every line of type legible.
