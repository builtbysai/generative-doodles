# No.309 Arcane Inventory

After Xanthe Caesia's Arcanum catalog releases: a dark apothecary shelf in black-walnut holding three ceramic vessels at a time, drawn only from her five observed forms, a cauldron, a potion bottle, a pumpkin jar, a faceted gem box and a wizard tower, in flat glazes of rust, celadon, cobalt and cream with gold lustre rim lines.

Under each vessel sits a small parchment plate in engraved-look lettering: a numbered release line, one rarity line and one usage line, all from authored lists in her comic research register. Below the shelf, a narrow Field Applications panel holds exactly three one-line vignettes for the current item, for a Wizard, a Rogue and a Warlock.

Tapping a vessel issues it: the tapped vessel eases off to the left and a new release slides in from the right, taking the next form, glaze and enchantment from the authored rotation, with its plate number exactly one higher than the last issued. The panel then types out its three vignettes in order. At idle, dust motes drift across the shelf and a candle glow breathes at both ends in layered translucency.

Frame one shows three releases already shelved with their plates reading, and the first item's three vignettes already typed out in the panel below.
