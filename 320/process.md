# No.320 Cockatoo Press

After Coral Cockatoo's brand bird: a sulphur-crested cockatoo as a flat screen print, one bird only, perched on a diagonal eucalyptus branch that runs from the lower left to the upper right of the frame, with generous teal margins around it. The palette is only five colours: the cockatoo white, the sulphur crest, black for the beak and eye, sage for the branch and its leaves, and eucalyptus teal for the ground. Every shape is a flat fill, with no gradients and no shading, so the piece reads like a poster.

The crest is a fan of exactly eight feather paths, asserted in the code, rotating around one pivot at the top of the head. Each feather has a folded angle and a raised angle, and the loop interpolates between them, so the fan opens wide and then settles back down. The eye is a single black bead with one white catchlight. The tail hangs below the branch behind it, the feet grip the branch as two small black shapes, and the wing is a white shape on the white body edged with ground teal lines so it stays visible.

Idle, the piece runs a seamless sixteen second loop. The crest slowly raises and lowers, the tail tips gently, and there is one slow blink at nine seconds, done by scaling the eye bead shut and back with a smoothstep. Frame sixteen seconds is identical to frame zero, and frame one opens with the crest half-raised, mid-gesture, so the bird is already alive. A tap plays a head-bob dance on the head group only: exactly three quick bobs inside a keyframed burst of 1.2 seconds, counted and asserted in the code, after which the head settles and the crest and tail loop carries on underneath, unrestarted. Nothing reloads.
