# №444 — The Gilded Object

## Concept
A calm botanical tarot built from Maité Léon's frame-device: one full card in strict 2:3.4 format on a deep blue page ground (#1E3A6E), the card itself a small authored object. A single gilded object sits in an arched niche on a cream card (#E8E2D4), flanked by two symmetric climbing vines carrying exactly one pomegranate, a crescent moon above the arch, a thin outer border, and a deep blue title band across the bottom 10% with the roman numeral and name in tracked-out plain serif caps (cream #F2ECDC). No occult clichés — the mood is botanical and quiet. The deck is a fixed authored table dealt in order: I THE KEY, II THE VESSEL, III THE RING, IV THE MOTH, V THE APPLE, then it wraps.

## Construction
- Single self-contained index.html, canvas 2D only, no assets, no audio. Canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; draw() pins canvas.style.width/height in px every frame. Phone detection via window.screen min dimension < 700px (aspect fallback only); the card keeps its strict 2:3.4 shape at every size and the page ground stays #1E3A6E, so the card reads as an object.
- All five objects share one parametric grammar: gold linear-gradient body (pale #F5D78E through #C89B3C to deep gold), deep-gold #8A6A2A outline, exactly one burnt-orange #C75B2B accent (gem, band, wing spots, star), and one pale highlight. Key = ring bow + shaft + collar + two teeth; vessel = beziers amphora with arc handles and a clipped accent band; ring = stroked band with prongs and a kite-cut accent gem; moth = mirrored bezier wings drawn twice under scale(-1,1); apple = two-bezier body, stem, vine-green leaf, accent star.
- The niche arch is a path (rectangle with semicircular crown) filled with a horizontal gradient (#D9C9B4 centre, #C4AE8D edges) for the inner-shadow feel. Vines are mirrored sinusoidal stems with six leaves each (leaf = two quadratic curves plus a vein); the single pomegranate hangs on the left vine with a three-spike crown and one glint. The crescent moon is a single two-arc path in #F5F0DC over a soft radial glow. Paper speckle on the card comes from a fixed-seed mulberry32 table computed at load (260 specks, deterministic). The title lettering is canvas fillText in Georgia serif with manual per-character tracking — designed content per the prompt; no other text anywhere.
- Interaction: TAP (short press, under 10px movement) deals the next card — the current card slides up and out while the new one rises from below, 0.7s easeInOut; deck state advances through a dealt-history stack. Horizontal DRAG fans the last three dealt cards sideways with offsets following the drag; on release the fan eases back to the current card over 0.32s. Never click-to-reload.
- Ambient loop: vines sway 2px on an 8s sine; the moon glow breathes on a 6s sine.
- Determinism: the synchronous first frame is drawn at the constant loop phase PHASE0 = 2.4s; the rAF loop (typeof-guarded) is anchored as PHASE0 + elapsed-since-load. No Date.now() anywhere in the frame path. No offscreen canvases, no document.createElement, no sprites — everything is drawn directly per frame.
