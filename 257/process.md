# No.257 David from Ladakh

## Concept
After tanzinchospel.art's clay-sculpt joke, kept straight: a grey clay bust of David, curly haired, wearing a tall slightly worn top hat, on black. The bust turns slowly on a turntable, one full turn every 24 seconds, a warm rim light on the clay. Tapping reseeds only the hat's tilt and the curl variation; the joke never changes.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, the bust built from layered flat shapes in a 1000 by 1150 design space, contain-fitted and centred on the black. Shoulders and chest form a broad clay trapezoid; the head is drawn under a horizontal squeeze driven by the turntable phase, so the silhouette narrows toward profile while brow, eyes, nose and mouth drift sideways with the turn. A bowed shadow plane covers the turning-away cheek, and the chest shading is clipped to the torso silhouette so it can never spill past the clay. The curls are 62 seeded arc clusters in a band over the crown and down both sides, riding the head's squeeze. The hat is a charcoal cylinder with a seeded tilt, yaw and brim pitch, a worn dent and scuff kept in the charcoal family, its brim carrying a warm rim kiss on the lit side. Clay texture is 1100 seeded speckle points masked to the sculpture. A pointerdown advances the seed and rebuilds only the curls and hat pose. The opening clock starts at 3.4 seconds, a three-quarter turn with the rim lit. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no audio, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Three fixes. The chest shadow plane first protruded past the right shoulder as a dark slab; it is now clipped to the torso path. The face shadow first split the head with a straight vertical edge and the brow was a grey band across the face; the shadow's inner edge is now a bowed curve, the band replaced by two short brow strokes, and the curls were enlarged and re-centred on the head so the locks spill visibly under the brim instead of hiding behind it. The head's rim-light stroke hung past the jaw into the black; it now ends at the cheek. Frame one reads as specced: three-quarter turn, tilted hat, curls at the temples, warm rim on the lit cheek.
