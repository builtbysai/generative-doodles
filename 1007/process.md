# No.1007 - System, Not Picture

Study #123 yuya-hanai, PP168 "The Credit Line": his signature made visible - the system, shown instead of the image. The image is deliberately subordinate, the blocks are a machine rather than a flowchart, and the flow is never hidden: packets travel the lines where everyone can see them.

## Concept

A dark room in three columns. Left: four live input feeds - CAMERA (drifting soft blobs), TRACK (dots moving on a faint grid), DATA (a scrolling tick stream), MIC (a waveform; simulated data, never a microphone). Center: THE SYSTEM, a tall stack of four chunky processing blocks, SENSE, DETECT, RENDER, OUTPUT, each with a darker cap strip, ports, vents, a status LED and a small glyph of its trade. Right: THE IMAGE, a framed render of a figure moving through light - beautiful, but shorter than the system column beside it, because the hierarchy is the point. Thin flow lines carry mint packets from the feeds, through the blocks in order, and out to the image, constantly. The figure's phrase advances only when packets arrive at OUTPUT: arrivals step the pose, which then settles, so the image is visibly fed by the machine. Along the bottom, the only sentence in the piece: "SYSTEM: HANAI. IMAGE: SOMEONE ELSE."

## Construction

Single self-contained canvas 2D page, no external assets. Everything drawn is a closed-form function of piece time plus a recorded list of visitor events; nothing is frame-integrated.

- Feeds: seeded generators. CAMERA is five layered flat-alpha blobs on seeded orbits, its currently largest blob supplying the image figure's accent color. TRACK is four lissajous dots with short trails; dot zero's position drives the figure's lean and sway. DATA is a hash-driven tick stream scrolling at a fixed rate. MIC is a three-component sine waveform with a slow breathing envelope.
- Packets: each feed spawns packets on its own seeded cadence (periods 2.9-4.1 s, tuned phases). A packet's path is fixed at spawn by the feed's entry block at that moment: feed edge, across to the entry block's center, down through the remaining block centers, out of OUTPUT, and into the image frame. Paths are polylines with cumulative lengths; a packet's position is its age times a constant speed, and a block's glow is the strongest proximity envelope of any packet to that block's center distance along its path. Every glow has a packet behind it.
- The image: the figure is a forward-kinematics glyph (pelvis, chest, head, jointed arms and legs) whose pose comes from TRACK, whose accent comes from CAMERA, and whose phrase phase is driven by OUTPUT arrivals: phase = (arrivals - 1) x 0.85 + 0.85 x (1 - exp(-2 x time since last arrival)), a step-and-settle per arrival, counted in closed form over the spawn schedules grouped by reroute segments. DATA sets the echo detail (two ghost echoes of the figure at full pipeline, one when DATA enters at DETECT, none later; DATA straight to OUTPUT rings the figure with raw mint ticks). A layered flat-alpha pool (no gradient) grounds the figure.
- Interaction, all recorded as timestamped events: tap a block and it opens for 10 s (through the series-standard 0.35 s pending-tap window so a double-tap can preempt it): SENSE shows the four feeds enlarged, DETECT shows the track field with detection boxes (one target briefly lost, its box red), RENDER shows the figure's geometry with joint boxes and construction rings, OUTPUT shows the image's layers separated into pool, figure, and composite panels, with a remaining-time line along the bottom. Drag a feed onto a different block to reroute it for the rest of the session: its flow line re-plumbs, a mint pip marks the feed, and the image changes character (see Deviations). Double-tap rebuilds (new epoch: reseeded feeds, entries reset, opens cleared).
- Sound, lazily created on the first pointer gesture and fully typeof-guarded: CAMERA is a band-passed static bed, MIC is a low-passed breath whose gain follows the waveform's amplitude, TRACK/DETECT entries pluck a triangle tone as packets reach the DETECT center, DATA spawns tick a short square blip, OUTPUT arrivals land a soft sine thock, and one master lowpass stands in for the blocks' filter character, its cutoff rising with the deepest lit block. The QA renderer runs silent and the visual piece is complete without audio.
- Determinism: frame one is the synchronous draw at fixed offset T0 = 13 s - feeds live, packets mid-flow on every path, SENSE and DETECT lit, six OUTPUT arrivals already counted, the figure mid-phrase. The animation loop is anchored as T0 plus elapsed time since load; the start timestamp never feeds the drawn state of the first frame. Canvas sizing uses the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; draw() pins canvas style width/height in px every frame; phone detection uses window.screen's smaller dimension (< 700 px) with the height > width x 1.15 aspect test as fallback only. No gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas, no microphone.

## Deviations

- Reroute simplification: a reroute applies to packets spawned after the drop; packets already in flight keep the path they were born with. The image's response to a reroute is a discrete stage-skip mapping rather than continuous re-processing: CAMERA entering past SENSE leaves the figure uncolored (gray accent, no head core); MIC entering at RENDER or OUTPUT, or TRACK entering at OUTPUT, quantizes the figure's phrase to raw steps; DATA's entry sets the echo count and, at OUTPUT, adds the raw tick ring. The mapping is recorded here and in the header comment; the plumbing, the packets, and the arrival-driven phrase are exact.
- Audio simplification: the four blocks' individual filter characters are represented by one master lowpass whose cutoff follows the deepest lit block, and the four input voices are two continuous beds (CAMERA static, MIC breath) plus event tones for TRACK, DATA, and arrivals. The mechanism-carrying sounds (detect pluck, arrival thock, breath) are exact.
- Phone layout: compression was chosen over stacking, per the brief's option - the three columns narrow, the system stays taller than the image, and the figure's width-capped scale keeps it whole.
- Frame-one geometry note: the lit pair at T0 (SENSE and DETECT) is exact on the desktop and thumb geometries; on the taller phone geometry the DETECT packet is mid-link at that same instant (DETECT's LED is lit, its edge just under the tag threshold). The mechanism is identical; only the sampled instant differs, because path lengths are layout geometry.
