# No.301 Babushka Stack

After Ali Fisher's babushka dolls: a three zone doll drawn as
flash on a black tee, ink black field behind, one doll centred
and tall with generous margins.

## Zones

Exactly three zones, asserted in code, stacked on one spine:

1. Head: an animal face framed by a babushka scarf that ties in
   a knot under the chin.
2. Torso: a peony style flower, an outer petal ring fixed to the
   torso around a phyllotaxis spiral of teardrop petals.
3. Base: a striped pot, navy bands with mustard stripes and thin
   paper pinstripes, finished with a mustard rim.

Stitch dots mark the two seams between zones.

## Heads

Exactly four head variants, asserted in code, cycled by tapping
the head. Tapping is a costume change only: it never resets the
motion and never reloads.

- Grumpy cat (frame one): heavy lidded eyes, flat straight
  mouth, whiskers, ears poking through the scarf. Scarf pattern:
  white polka dots on vermilion.
- Chihuahua: big upright ears, big round eyes, worried brows, a
  long little snout. Scarf pattern: white diagonal stripes.
- Croc: eye bumps riding on top of the head, a long snout with
  scale arcs and nostrils, big jagged teeth on the jaw line.
  Scarf pattern: white barbed wire zigzag bands.
- Mermaid: navy wavy hair, a mustard shell clip, scale dots on
  the forehead, calm lashed eyes. Scarf pattern: white waves.

## Motion

- The petal spiral rotates on a 30 second loop.
- The pot base sways 2 degrees on a 6 second cycle, pivoting at
  its rim so the seam with the torso holds.
- Frame one opens on the grumpy cat with the spiral mid turn
  and the pot mid sway.

## Palette

Ink black #141414, paper #F3E9DC, scarf vermilion #C73E1D with
white dots and patterns, peony pink #E8A0A8, pot navy #1B2A4A
and mustard #E0A437. Nothing else. Flat colour only: no
gradients, no shadows, no text.
