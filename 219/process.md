# No.219 Machine Quartet

## Concept
Kathy McTavish's chance installs circles of machine quartets that investigate chance, emergence, friction, resonance and change: a loosely coupled web of small generators, choreographed by code, the art of the fugue. Here four machines draw on warm paper in counterpoint, four thin continuous lines whose pens never lift: indigo runs a jittered random walk, madder traces a Lissajous figure around a roaming centre, ochre drifts on curl noise, slate rides a slow decaying orbit that resets to a fresh centre as it winds down. Every twenty seconds or so a convergence event pulls all four toward one shared, invisible attractor point for five seconds, the lines braiding together, then releases them back to their own rules; the unison events are the music. Old ink fades over about a minute so the braid never clutters. Dragging near a thread makes the nearest machine abandon its rule and follow the hand for three seconds before rejoining.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Each thread is a small state machine with its own rule function and seeded random stream; positions integrate velocities with soft margin steering at the paper's edges. Histories are JS point lists stamped with their birth time, stroked in age runs so no single path mixes two fades, at a flat 2px with round joins; no glow, no gradients. The convergence scheduler works on a twenty-two second cycle with a five second event window, an eased strength envelope, and a gaussian pull weight by distance to the attractor, each thread aiming at a slightly offset point on a small ring so the braid crosses instead of collapsing to a dot. The curl thread reads a two-scale value-noise field. Drag override targets the nearest thread head, follows the pointer while held, and decays over three seconds after release. Frame one is developed honestly: after layout, the simulation pre-runs fifty-five seconds at a thirtieth of a second per step, so the paper opens with four distinct wanderings, two convergence braids already in the fading tails. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, the field full-bleed on phones, rAF typeof guarded, no text anywhere.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; this piece passed on the first build. The desktop and thumb frames show all four rules legible at once: the slate orbit's nested ellipses, the madder Lissajous loops, the ochre curl's long sweeps, the indigo walk's jittered climb, with older ink visibly paler and a braid gathered at the centre where the last convergence took them. The mobile frame keeps the same counterpoint in the tall field, threads respecting the soft margin. Frame one is a developed drawing at every size, not a blank page with four dots.
