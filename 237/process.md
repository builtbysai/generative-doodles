# No.237 Shadows Variable

## Concept
After Woodfill's Crossing Signals works of 2022, whose catalog captions literally read "(shadows variable)": a cluster of flat geometric panels, bars, triangles and one disc in birch, grey and white with a single safety orange accent, drifting and turning slowly on a pale wall. Two fixed warm lamps off frame throw long hard shadows down and to the right. The shadows lag the panels by a beat and a half and stretch as a panel nears its lamp, so the shadow layer is a second artwork trailing the first. The lamps never move; the world does. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Nine panels move in an 800 by 1000 unit panel space (4:5), each on independent slow sine drifts and rotations with periods between 40 and 90 seconds. Shadows are drawn first: for each panel and each of the two lamps (fixed points up and to the left, off frame), a copy of the panel shape is drawn in shadow colour at 55 percent opacity, offset along the lamp vector with magnitude inversely proportional to the panel's distance from the lamp, and dilated slightly by the same measure. The shadow pose is not the panel pose: each shadow runs a critically damped spring follow (omega 3.4) of its panel, seeded a second and a half in the past, which produces the lag honestly. Panels are then drawn flat on top, no shading, no gradients, hard shadow edges throughout. The opening clock starts at 23 seconds so frame one is mid drift with the shadows already thrown and trailing. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. Frame one shows the cluster mid wall with every panel carrying two overlapping hard shadows to the lower right, the doubled shadows visibly offset from their panels, the white disc's twin shadow discs pooled beneath it, and the orange bar reading as the single accent. On the phone frame the 4:5 panel centres on the wall colour ground and the shadow play stays legible at 390 wide. No adjustments were needed after the first render.
