# No.242 Deluxe Unit

## Concept
After Woodfill's Deluxe Unit from Crossing Signals 2022, whose materials list reads like a hardware store receipt: custom speakers, sign rotator, Bluetooth stereo amp, tablet, plywood, casters, hardware. A plywood tower on casters with a sign panel up top flipping through geometric glyphs, speaker cones below, an amp band with tiny green and red status LEDs. It rolls a slow small circle on a dark floor, playful and exposed, a rig rather than a robot. Silent despite the speakers. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1000 by 1000 unit square, drawn in simple 2.5D: front face flat, right side and top faces offset, shaded with flat translucent overlays from the palette itself. The glyph clock runs on an eight second cycle: in the first 400ms of each cycle the sign panel flips around its vertical axis (a cosine width squeeze, glyph swapping at the edge-on midpoint) through a seeded order, triangle, X, circle, bar. The flip time within the cycle also drives the speaker cones: two cabinets with tweeter rings and concentric woofer rings scaled by an exponential pulse with a 300ms decay, plus one expanding kick ring per kick. The roll is a 48 second circuit on a shallow ellipse; the unit scales a touch as it nears the viewer, tilts a hair, and all four casters swivel to the tangent heading, the back pair dimmed by the side offset. LEDs: green steady, red lit on alternate glyph cycles. A blank tablet sits below the amp band, no logo. The opening clock starts at 0.3 seconds so frame one catches the panel mid flip into the triangle with the cones just kicked. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no handlers, no text, no audio.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. First pass had the unit riding high in the frame with too much empty floor beneath it; the roll circle was lowered and the renders re-checked: the unit now sits mid frame, sign panel up top with the triangle readable at flip width, both woofer cones carrying the fading kick ring, green and red LEDs lit, tablet blank, casters visible at the base with the floor shadow pooled under the rig. On the phone frame the square holds its shape on the dark ground and every element stays legible at 390 wide.
