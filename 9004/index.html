№9004 · Tangency

A circle-packing study. Discs are placed largest first, each grown until it just touches a neighbor, so every circle in the piece is tangent to something. A handful of big discs set the composition; the smaller ones pack the gaps like grout. Rings and arc segments in rust, ochre, and slate on warm paper. Click the piece for a new variation.
