# No.187 Gravity Light

## Concept
Eugene Sargent's Po Glo Sticks and Rainbow Springs, translated literally: light that does not pretend to bounce, but actually does. A wall of twenty four vertical acrylic tubes fills the frame, and inside them pulses of light fall under gravity, accelerate, strike the tube bottoms and bounce with decaying height until their energy is spent. Nothing is tweened; the motion is a physics simulation rendered as light. The one instrument is the gravity vector itself: drag to tilt it, tap to drop in a new ball.

## Construction
Canvas 2D, single self contained page. Twenty four lanes span the viewport edge to edge, drawn as dark glass columns in #1A1A20 on the #050505 ground, bleeding past the top and bottom of the frame. Each ball is a point mass in a single lane with position, velocity, gravity scaled to the frame height, restitution 0.82 and slight air drag, integrated on a fixed 120 Hz step accumulated from frame time, so the motion is identical at any refresh rate. Bounce energy is tracked from the apex of each bounce: fresh balls burn hot white at the core with cyan or magenta trails, and as the apex decays the light cools through its lane colour to amber, finally pooling as a dim amber glow on the tube floor before fading out after eight settled seconds. Balls in adjacent lanes whose heights cross exchange a fraction of their velocity difference, the collision kick. Dragging sets a target tilt: sideways drag leans the balls against the tube walls, downward drag strengthens gravity up to nearly double; a short tap instead injects one new ball at the top of the tapped lane. Untouched, the wall feeds itself, spawning a new ball on a slow irregular rhythm whenever the population runs low. At load the simulation is pre-run for several seconds with staggered spawns, so the first frame is already a living wall of mixed ages. Palette exactly per spec. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, full-bleed field on phones, rAF typeof guarded, hint lowercase and short.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 and inspected all three. The first pre-run was too honest: six simulated seconds let the whole opening population settle and fade, leaving a near empty wall with a few dim floor pools. Fixes: spawns during the pre-run are staggered in three waves of decreasing age so balls are caught at every stage of their bounce life, settled balls now linger eight seconds with the fade confined to the last three, the self feeding rhythm was quickened, and a final pair of fresh drops was added at the end of the pre-run so frame one always includes falls beginning in the upper half. After the fix the desktop frame shows white cores in flight across the full height, cyan and magenta gradient pulses trailing them, and amber pools gathering along the floor; the phone frame keeps all twenty four lanes readable with the same spread of activity. The gradient pulse direction follows the velocity sign, so rising balls trail below and falling balls trail above, which reads correctly in the stills.
