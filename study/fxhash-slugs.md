## What is known from the platform context

fxhash is the open generative-token platform on Tezos (later Ethereum
via fxhash 2.0), launched November 2021 by ciphrd. The long-form
constraint applies to every token on it: one algorithm must produce a
whole edition of unique, interesting outputs. See the main
galleries-fxhash-openprocessing.md note for the platform study method
(design the parameter space, not the image) and the avoid-list.

The five slugs are a technique-survey pick: the names alone suggest
the range (a clock, a rotating system, wisps, landscapes, a stroll),
which is exactly the cross-section of generative tropes worth
studying. Studying them is still worthwhile: five small projects, five
different answers to "what does one algorithm explore."
