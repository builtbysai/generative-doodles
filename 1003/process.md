# No.1003 - A Light That Does Not Exist

Study #123 yuya-hanai, PP164 "Virtual Shadow" (Perfume "Reframe", 2019): a virtual illuminant casting real shadows. The light does not exist; the shadow does. No lamp is ever drawn, the shadow never faithfully matches the body, and the strangeness stays mechanical, never horror.

## Concept

A dark stage. Center-left, one white articulated figure performs a slow seeded choreography (reach, sway, fold; 12 s phrases). It is lit by nothing. On the back wall filling the right two-thirds, its shadow is projected anyway: the same pose from 0.5 s ago, roughly twice the figure's size, stretched vertically, soft-edged, thrown from an invisible source point that wanders slowly on its own. On a seeded schedule the shadow detaches for 3-5 s at a time, drifting off on its own path while the figure keeps dancing, then rejoins. Every ~45 s a second virtual light flickers on for ~10 s and a second shadow appears from a different angle, crossing the first: two shadows, one body, zero lamps. The only trace of the light itself is a pale pool with a faint cyan rim sliding along the floor as the invisible source moves. Drag to hold the lamp that is not there; tap the shadow to pin it as a sculpture for 10 s while the body dances on; double-tap to reseed the phrase and the wander.

## Construction

Single self-contained canvas 2D page, no external assets, no visible text (title tag only). Everything drawn is a closed-form function of piece time plus a recorded list of visitor events; nothing is frame-integrated.

- Choreography: 7 seeded keyframe poses per 12 s phrase (mulberry32 per epoch), each keyframe a full joint-parameter set (root sway/bob, torso lean, head tilt, arm angles and elbow bends, leg angles and knee bends). Poses interpolate with a smootherstep between keyframes, wrapping. The skeleton is forward kinematics: pelvis, chest, neck, head, jointed arms and legs, drawn as thick round-capped strokes with the far-side limbs at 0.5 alpha for depth.
- The virtual light: the source is a point in stage space following a seeded two-sine wander (periods ~47 s and ~39 s plus faster terms). It is never drawn. The shadow's root sits on the floor line against the wall, offset from the figure by a projection-style rule (root moves opposite the source with gain 0.9 around a base gap), its scale is 2.05x the figure's height unit modulated by source height, its vertical stretch is 1.20 nominal and grows as the source drops, and a small shear leans its top away from the source. The pool on the floor is three nested flat-alpha ellipses (no gradient) with a 0.3-alpha cyan rim stroke, positioned and sized from the same source point.
- The lag: the choreography is a closed-form function, so the shadow's pose is that same function evaluated at t - 0.5 s. No history buffer is kept; the half-second staleness is exact at every frame.
- Softness: the shadow silhouette is drawn three times with widening stroke passes (1.5x width at 0.30 alpha, 1.22x at 0.45, 1.0x at 0.80), giving a soft edge without shadowBlur.
- Detachments: seeded windows starting at 20 + k*31 + hash*7 s into each epoch, lasting 3-5 s. Inside a window the shadow root follows an eased out-and-back drift (up to 0.15 of frame width, rising slightly), with 0.7 s smootherstep envelopes at both ends; the lagged pose keeps animating throughout, only the placement leaves the figure.
- Second light: seeded windows starting at 45 + k*47 + hash*5 s, 10 s long, 1 s fades. Its source sits high at the right, so its shadow (1.8x, stretch 1.05, projection gain 0.55, tone #3A3D46) lands left of the primary shadow and crosses the figure's space.
- Interaction, all recorded as timestamped events and folded back into the closed-form state: drag moves the source (samples recorded with times; on release the source eases back to its wander over 4 s); a tap on the shadow's bounding box pins it (the frozen evaluation at the tap instant is held for 10 s, then blends back to the live shadow over 0.8 s); double-tap reseeds (new epoch: new keyframes, new wander phases, new detachment and second-light schedules; pins and drags cleared).
- Sound, lazily created on the first pointer gesture and fully typeof-guarded: the figure is a soft sine tone whose pitch follows hand/pelvis speed; the same tone feeds a 0.5 s feedback delay, the shadow's echo; a half-frequency triangle voice rides the same delay and detunes by up to 5% during detachments, resolving when the shadow rejoins. The QA renderer runs silent and the visual piece is complete without audio.
- Determinism: frame one is the synchronous draw at fixed offset T0 = 11 s (mid-phrase, shadow attached and stale by exactly 0.5 s, pool near its wander center; the first detachment is at 20 s+ and the first second-light window at 45 s+, so frame one is the pure one-body-one-shadow state). The animation loop is anchored as T0 plus elapsed time since load; the start timestamp never feeds the drawn state of the first frame. Canvas sizing uses the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; draw() pins canvas style width/height in px every frame; phone detection uses window.screen's smaller dimension (< 700 px) with the height > width * 1.15 aspect test as fallback only. No gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas, no microphone.

## Deviations

- Shadow scale and stretch: the prompt's nominal ~2.2x size and ~1.25x vertical stretch are implemented as 2.05x and 1.20 at the wander's center (both still modulated by source height) so the enlarged silhouette, raised arms included, stays inside the frame at the fixed first frame on all three QA sizes instead of cropping its hands at the top edge. The shadow still dominates the frame and overflows the right edge on phones.
- Pose history: the prompt describes a 0.5 s position-history buffer; because the choreography here is a closed-form keyframe function, the lagged pose is sampled analytically at t - 0.5 instead. The rendered result is identical, and it keeps every frame a pure function of piece time.
- Pin timing: a tap commits through the series-standard 0.35 s pending-tap window so a double-tap reseed can preempt it (the pin's effective time is the tap time itself). Recorded because the freeze starts a beat after the physical tap.
- Audio echo: the shadow's delayed voice is the figure tone through a 0.5 s feedback delay line plus a half-frequency triangle voice that detunes during detachments, rather than a pitch-shifted copy of a live performer; the 0.5 s delay and the detachment detune, the two audible mechanisms, are exact.
