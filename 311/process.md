# No.311 Embellish & Cast

After Xanthe Caesia's mould repair and embellish ritual: three stages in a row on a dark workbench. On the left stands exactly one plain commercial style vessel mould, a simple vase form in plaster white with its mould seam showing, untouched. In the middle stands exactly one copy of the same form carrying the embellish pass in ink: an added band, a treated rim, little feet and a single applique sprig, all visibly drawn on over the base form and never part of it. On the right stand exactly three slip-cast copies of the embellished form, asserted as a set of three, glazed in the first colorway triple of oxblood red, kiln grey and black, after her May 2022 set. Under each stage a small hand-lettered word, mould, embellish and cast, drawn as stroke paths only with no loaded font.

Tapping the middle stage draws on the next embellishment from an authored list of eight variants. Each variant changes the band style, the rim treatment and the feet, and carries at most one applique motif: sprig, moon, heart, spiral, flower, star, leaf and diamond in fixed order. The new pass appears with a visible draw-on animation, element by element, and the three casts on the right re-form at the same moment to match the new embellished profile in their current glazes. Tapping the right stage advances the casts through an authored cycle of colorway triples, all rotations inside the oxblood, kiln grey and black glaze families, while the forms stay unchanged. Nothing reloads.

The idle motion is a slow twenty four second loop in which soft studio light drifts across the bench, laid down as layered translucency only. The palette is only the plaster white, the embellish ink, the three glazes and the shelf ground. Frame one rests with the first variant already fully drawn on, the casts already glazed, and the light mid-drift across the bench.
