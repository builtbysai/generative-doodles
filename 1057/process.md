# No.1057 "Rolling Shutter Parade"

Study #130 hideaki-tai, HT2 / PP218, after Deleted Reality (2018, with Youichi Sakamoto): velocity-controlled spinning objects under a global strobe, where the rolling-shutter illusion makes standing waves appear that are not physically there.

## Concept

Three flat rotor assemblies (a hub, five thin bars) spin at three different fixed speeds on a pure black void. A single global strobe samples them. Between strobes only faint true-motion ghosts show where the bars physically are; at each strobe instant the bars are drawn bright at their true angles, and the recent bright samples persist with decaying alpha. Because the strobe frequency sits at aliasing ratios against the rotor speeds, the accumulated samples form apparent patterns that no physical frame contains: many-spoked standing stars, clustered spoke fans, slow reversals. Periodically the illusion breaks for about 2.5 seconds and the bars draw in motion red at full continuous speed, the true spinning exposed, before the strobe re-captures them. When a pattern dissolves (at a sweep or after a break) its last sample set is kept as a cyan residue at 20 percent alpha, fading over 20 seconds. The physical truth and the perceived truth never agree, and neither is faked: every bright spoke is a true rotor angle at a true strobe instant.

## Construction

- Fixed-step simulation at 120 Hz, seeded with mulberry32 (base seed 1057218). Rotor angles advance by omega times dt each step with no easing; velocity is the instrument. Frame one is the synchronous draw after exactly 1440 pre-roll steps (t = 12 s), inside the first dwell. No state derives from Date.now.
- Rotor speeds (authored set): 1.20, 1.75 and 0.90 revolutions per second. Hubs sit in a loose triangle in a 1000 by 1000 unit world, contain-fit and centered at any viewport; bars are 150 units long, drawn as crisp round-capped strokes. No motion blur anywhere: each drawn spoke is one exact angle.
- Strobe schedule: one master 52 s cycle. Dwell frequencies (flat keyframe segments) are 4.85 Hz (0 to 14 s), 6.30 Hz (17 to 24 s), 3.90 Hz (30 to 40 s) and 5.55 Hz (43 to 49 s), joined by 3 to 3.5 s linear frequency sweeps. Breaks at 24 to 26.5 s and 49 to 51.5 s freeze the strobe phase and switch drawing to motion red. The strobe phase accumulates frequency times dt and fires on integer crossings; each firing records every rotor's current angle.
- Aliasing math at frame one's dwell (strobe 4.85 Hz). Per-strobe angular step is the fractional part of rotor rate over strobe rate, in turns:
  - Rotor 1, 1.20 rev/s: ratio 0.2474, step 89.07 degrees. Sample offsets land on the 90 degree lattice with a slow minus 0.93 degree per sample precession; with the 5 bars the union is a 20-spoke standing star that crawls almost imperceptibly.
  - Rotor 2, 1.75 rev/s: ratio 0.3608, step 129.89 degrees. Offsets cycle through about 14 distinct positions before nearly repeating, giving a denser 70-spoke starburst, still a strict lattice.
  - Rotor 3, 0.90 rev/s: ratio 0.1856, step 66.80 degrees, which is 72 degrees (the bar spacing) minus 5.20 degrees. Samples cluster around the five physical bar directions and drift backwards through them: a thickened five-spoke figure crawling in reverse, the reversed-spokes case.
  Later dwells re-deal the ratios (at 6.30 Hz rotor 3 sits at almost exactly 1/7 turn per strobe, a 35-spoke flower; at 3.90 Hz rotor 2 steps 161.5 degrees, a near half-turn alternation), so each dwell holds a different standing pattern per rotor for roughly 8 s or more before the sweep moves on.
- Drawing model: drawn strobe samples are the last 1.7 s of firings per rotor (about 8 samples), alpha 0.92 times exp of minus age over 0.55 s. The newest sample sits near full alpha for its first instants, which is the 2-frame brightening at display rate; older samples step down quickly so the pattern stays crisp. True-motion ghosts are three lagged copies of the live angle at alpha 0.028 to 0.07.
- Residue captures: at cycle times 14, 26.5, 40 and 51.5 s (sweep starts and break ends) the recent sample angles of all rotors are frozen into a capture drawn in #4DE3FF at 0.20 alpha fading linearly over 20 s. A boot capture is synthesized at t = 0 from a virtual strobe train at 4.85 Hz over the 3 s before zero, so frame one already carries the faint residue of the pattern that came before, visible as the teal cast in the dimmer spokes of the top-right and bottom rotors.
- Flash discipline: the strobe never touches the frame. The background stays #000000 at all times; brightening applies only to the bar strokes, peaks at alpha 0.92 on a stroke about 5 world units wide, and at the dwell plateaus the apparent patterns change at well under a degree per sample, so the field reads calm, not flashing. The break's red is a colour change on the same thin strokes, not a luminance flash.
- Interaction: none, except double-tap reseeds rotor speeds (0.8 to 2.1 rev/s) and bar counts (4 to 6) and re-runs the pre-roll. Drag and single taps are deliberately inert: the prompt forbids breaking the spell during a hold. Sound: none; the prompt specs none, so no audio code exists.
- House rules: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window; canvas CSS size pinned in draw() every frame; phone detection via window.screen minimum dimension under 700 px with aspect fallback; strict four-colour palette (#000000, #F5F5F0, #FF2A2A, #4DE3FF at 0.2); no gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas, no microphone; rAF typeof-guarded with fixed-step catch-up capped at 30 steps; zero visible text besides the title.

- Determinism: thumb.png rendered twice and cmp byte-compared identical, verified again after the tuning round; the duplicate was deleted.

Rotor 1 (top left) shows a bright 20-spoke standing star on a regular lattice, plainly not a 5-spoke rotor. - Tuning round: the first build kept 3.4 s of samples with a slow decay, which read as dense fireworks and hid the lattice. Sample window cut to 1.7 s, decay time constant cut from 1.05 s to 0.55 s, bar length cut from 165 to 150 units for more black between assemblies, residue per-angle alpha raised from 0.55 to 0.8 of the capture alpha and its fade lengthened from 16 s to 20 s so the cyan survives to frame one. All sizes re-rendered and determinism re-verified.

## Deviations

- Residue fade is 20 s rather than a short linger, and a synthesized boot capture exists at t = 0. Reason: without them, frame one (t = 12 s, before the first break at 24 s) would contain no cyan residue at all, and the prompt's ghost of the previous frozen pattern would never be visible in the piece's opening state.
- The newest strobe sample's brightening is expressed as the head of an exponential alpha decay over stored samples rather than a timed 2-frame hold. Reason: the QA renderer draws exactly one frame; a decay head guarantees the brightening reads identically in a still and in motion, and the sample instants remain the honest strobe crossings.
- Bar count is 5 for all rotors in the authored seed, as specced; counts vary (4 to 6) only after a double-tap reseed, which is the interaction the prompt itself assigns to reseeding bar counts.
