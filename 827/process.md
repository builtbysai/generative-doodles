# No.827 "The Yellow Movie" — EXPANSION #101 tony-conrad, PP4

## Concept

Tony Conrad's Yellow Movies (1972–73): sheets of paper bearing a hand-brushed black rectangle that frames a field of cheap white house paint — "films" programmed to run for fifty years as the paint yellows. The piece is finished the day it ships and never changes perceptibly within a session; the work is time itself. This build is one sheet: an irregular paper sheet centered on a plain surround, a rough hand-brushed black frame, one drip at the bottom edge near the right corner, a clean white field, and a tiny gallery label at the margin stating the current frame: "Yellow Movie, frame 0.00 / 50 years." No interaction at all. No progress bar. Nothing else.

## Construction

- **Palette (strict):** paper #F7F5F0, frame black #1A1A1A, field #FFFFFF drifting toward #F5EAC8. The surround is the paper mixed 18% toward the frame black (#CFCEC9), and the label is the paper mixed 45% toward black; both mixes are recorded here per the brief's allowance for a paper-family surround.
- **Sheet:** roughly 16:10, centered, sized at 80% of the frame's smaller dimension on desktop and 86% on phones. The four corners carry a seeded jitter of ±0.6% so the sheet is irregular, not a vector rectangle.
- **Frame:** built from seeded stroke data computed once at load (mulberry32, seed 827101): three passes of overlapping round-capped line segments along the rectangle's path — a broad low-alpha underlayer, a main band at alpha 0.50–0.88, and a dry-brush speckle pass with wider perpendicular scatter. Each stroke has independent along-edge position, length, perpendicular offset at both ends, width, and alpha, so the band reads as brushed paint with bristle gaps, not a clean rect. One drip hangs from the bottom edge near the right corner (seeded position/length), ending in a small blob.
- **The yellowing (age-curve math):** the creation epoch is baked in as a constant, `Date.UTC(2026, 9, 31)` (ship date 2026-10-31T00:00:00Z). Age in years = (Date.now() − CREATION) / (365.25 days). The field color is a linear mix, field = mix(#FFFFFF, #F5EAC8, min(age/50, 1)). The largest per-channel distance is blue, 55 units over 50 years ≈ 18,262 days, i.e. ≈ 0.003 channel units per day — far below one quantization step per day, so no change is visible within a session, a week, or a month; a returning viewer next year sees roughly 1/50 of the total shift. The same scalar drives a faint yellowing veil over the whole sheet (alpha 0.10 × t) and grain speckles whose count is stepped, floor(age × 30) × 8, so the count changes only about every 12 days.
- **Label:** "Yellow Movie, frame X / 50 years." with X = age.toFixed(2), small plain sans-serif in the quiet grey mix, set below the sheet's left edge. The label value changes only when the second decimal rolls over, about every 3.65 days.
- **House rules:** canvas sized from the Visual Viewport API with resize listeners on both visualViewport and window; canvas.style.width/height pinned in px inside draw(); phone detection via window.screen physical size (min dimension < 700px) with aspect h > w×1.15 as fallback; no gradients, no shadowBlur, no ctx.filter, no offscreen canvas; no audio; the only visible text is the specced label, plus <title>.

## QA log

- Rendered with `node ~/workspace/tools/render-piece.js` at 1280×900 (qa-desktop.png), 390×844 (qa-mobile.png), and 720×720 (thumb.png).
- **Determinism:** thumb.png rendered twice and byte-compared with cmp: identical; the duplicate was deleted. Reasoning: frame one is the synchronous draw at load; all texture is seeded precomputed stroke data; the only wall-clock input is the age scalar, whose 50-year curve moves ≈0.003 channel units per day (sub-quantization), whose label is quantized to 2 decimals (stable for ~3.65 days at a time), and whose grain count is stepped in ~12-day increments — so renders minutes or days apart are byte-identical, and the frame is effectively frozen within any session, exactly as the work requires.
- **Read-back:** desktop and thumb show the paper sheet on the darker surround, a clearly hand-brushed black frame (overlapping stroke segments, dry-brush gaps, no clean vector edges), the drip legible at the bottom edge near the right corner, a clean white field, and the label reading "Yellow Movie, frame 0.00 / 50 years." Mobile keeps the whole sheet and label inside the tall frame, nothing clipped.
- **Deviations:**
  1. The prompt's technical note suggests pre-rendering the brush texture to an offscreen canvas; the house renderer cannot create offscreen canvases, so the texture is seeded stroke data drawn directly each frame instead. Same visual result, no offscreen buffer.
  2. The prompt suggests recomputing age at 1 Hz; a literal interval would hang the QA renderer process (which captures the frame synchronously and has no requestAnimationFrame), so the age is recomputed inside draw() and a typeof-guarded rAF watch redraws only when the displayed label value changes — a change that can occur at most once every ~3.65 days. In a browser the piece still tracks real time correctly.
  3. The yellowing curve is linear in age (a straight mix over 50 years) rather than eased; at ≈0.003 channel units per day the distinction is unmeasurable for decades, and linear is the honest reading of "age in years maps to a yellowing curve."
