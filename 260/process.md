# No.260 Nambu Tide

## Concept
After Stanzin Tsepel's Where Mountains Meet the Sea (Fremantle Biennale 2025): hand-woven Ladakhi nambu wool cones, off-white with bold black horizontal stripes, hang from above over a warm sand floor dotted with small white salt mounds. The cones sway almost imperceptibly, the wool breathing, almost tidal; their shadows ripple outward across the sand in slow concentric rings. Shadow is the second artwork. A seamless 30 second loop. Watch only.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, contain-fitted and centred, the page ground taking the deep shadow tone. Six seeded cones hang from the top edge in a loose central cluster, each a bellied flat shape whose wool body and four to six bold black bands are clipped inside the cone's own path, with a soft shadow flank and a lit sliver for the weave's roundness. Sway is two sine axes with periods of 15 and 30 seconds and only a few pixels of travel at the tip, so the whole piece breathes on the 30 second loop. Each cone casts a soft-edged shadow pool on the sand, three nested flat ellipses whose centre tracks the sway; two ripple rings per cone expand from the shadow centroid and fade, driven by the sway phase. Eight salt mounds cross the sand in a receding diagonal, shrinking as they go, each with a lit edge; the sand carries 1500 seeded grain points and flat depth bands. The opening clock starts at 7.3 seconds, cones hung and the first rings already expanding on the sand. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with both resize listeners, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, rAF typeof guarded, no interaction handlers, no audio, no text, no blue anywhere.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three. One fix. The cones first hung as long needle-thin daggers, more icicle than textile; they were widened at the mouth, shortened, and given bellied sides, and the flank shading was softened, so they now read as woven cones with air beneath their tips. Frame one reads as specced: six striped cones over warm sand, shadow pools with rings spreading, the salt mounds receding.
