# №8953 · Held and Shaped - process notes

- **Title:** Held and Shaped
- **Seed:** 252 (Formative Not Cohesive - Meta+Hodos)
- **Concept:** Two panels, one material. Left panel: a global pulse
  binds all dots (cohesive) while a morphing noise field colors them
  (formative). Right panel: a fixed hue gradient binds them (cohesive)
  while a traveling brightness wave shapes them (formative). Every 24
  seconds a hinge flash sweeps through and the roles trade places.
- **Technique:** Vanilla canvas. A 14x9 dot grid rendered per panel
  with shared time base; palette built from a four-stop indigo-rust
  ramp; value-noise field via layered sines for the morphing color.

## Rework 2026-09-30 (HOLD verdict: muddy render, re-inspect hinge)

- **What was muddy:** dark brown/rust dots rendered at low brightness
  on a near-black ground, so the panels dissolved into the background;
  translucent layering grayed the marks out; the divider was a faint
  line; the hinge was a weak cream-gradient sweep that read as a
  crossfade rather than an event.
- **New render:** warm paper ground (237,228,208) plus exactly two
  inks, indigo (36,49,94) and rust (179,78,28). Zero translucency:
  every mark is opaque ink on paper, so nothing can gray out.
- **Pulse-holds panel:** all dots breathe in unison (cohesive);
  formative color is quantized, a slow noise field flips whole dots
  to the other ink, so migrating rust regions stay crisp instead of
  blending into mud.
- **Color-holds panel:** fixed spatial size gradient binds the dots
  (cohesive); the traveling wave steps dot tint through four discrete
  levels mixed toward paper (formative), no alpha fades.
- **Hinge as an event:** every 24s a bright white seam with a dark
  leading edge sweeps the full stage; in its wake the dots melt to
  zero and regrow with ink, behavior, and label all traded between
  panels. Same grid, different piece.
