№8978 · Mud Map

Every palette has dead ground, the hues where color goes to mud. This wheel marks three of them, brown-olive, sick-green and corpse-cyan, and scatters a few hundred colors across it. Each trapped color draws its escape, a short arc to the nearest zone edge, and leaves more saturated than it arrived. Click the map for a fresh scattering.
