# No.293 Kokeshi Stack

## Concept
After Ruby Waters' kokeshi dolls: a tall moon-face doll dead centre on generous cream negative space. The head is a flattened sphere with a black bob hairline, arched closed eyes, vermilion blush circles and small red lips, and the face grammar never varies. The long cone body is a stack of pattern bands chosen by a seeded random pick from her set of five: stripe stack, vermilion lotus with green leaves, yellow band with black dots, vermilion chrysanthemum field on pink, and a teal wave band. A tiny painted Mt Fuji, yellow and white and orange with red dots, sits at the base and occasionally breathes a small white puff of cloud. The whole figure sways side to side like a weeble on a slow 3 degree pendulum, weighted at the bottom. Watch-only, on a 30 second seamless loop.

## Construction
Canvas 2D in a 1280 by 900 design space, centred-fitted so the tall figure keeps its proportions in the middle of the frame, with the page ground held to the head cream. The palette is held to the specced colours only: head cream, hair black, blush vermilion, body pink, band yellow, teal, lotus vermilion and leaf green; the Fuji's white is the head cream, its orange is the lotus vermilion and its dots are the blush vermilion, so nothing outside the palette enters. The head is layered flat shapes: a black flattened ellipse for the bob with two wide blunt side locks, a cream flattened ellipse for the face set into it, a stroked arc for the fringe line, a small vermilion and yellow hair ornament, two arched closed eyes with thin brow arcs above them, solid vermilion blush circles, a short nose stroke and small two-tone red lips. The body is a trapezoid cone, narrow at the neck and wide at the base, filled cream and then painted with the band stack under a body path clip recomputed each frame, with a thin black rule between bands and a black collar V at the neck. The band stack comes from a fixed seed: the shipped stack has exactly 5 bands, teal waves, lotus, dotted yellow, chrysanthemum field and lotus again, with no duplicate adjacent bands guaranteed by re-rolling any pick that matches its neighbour. Each band painter is plain data drawn directly: horizontal stripes for the stripe stack, fanned ellipse petals and leaf shapes for the lotus, a staggered dot grid for the yellow band, twelve-petal flowers with yellow centres for the chrysanthemum field, and offset rows of stroked arcs for the waves. The Fuji is a clipped cone silhouette at the base of the body with a jagged cream snow cap, a thin lotus-orange stratum and a yellow foot scattered with seven red dots, outlined in black. Motion is a fixed-step simulation: the sway rotates the whole figure around its bottom anchor at 828 design pixels, 3 degrees of amplitude on a 10 second swing, so three full swings sit inside the 30 second loop and the seam is exact. The cloud puff fires once per loop, from 19 to 24.5 seconds: five cream circles anchored at the Fuji summit that grow, rise and fade, with their offsets reseeded from the loop integer so each loop breathes a slightly different puff. Frame one opens at 0.7 seconds, with the pendulum already off-centre by about a degree and a third, so the weeble sway reads at once and no puff is in flight. No offscreen canvas anywhere, no gradients, no filters or shadowBlur, flat fills and layered translucency for the fading puff only. House rules held: Visual Viewport sizing with resize listeners on visualViewport and window, canvas pinned in px every frame, phone detection via window.screen with the aspect fallback, synchronous first frame, rAF typeof guarded, fixed seeds, no text, no UI chrome, no audio, no pointer handlers, no em dashes.

## QA log
Rendered at 1280x900, 390x844 and 720x720 from the shipped script and inspected all three; the renderer reported both resize listeners, and a grep confirms exactly the eight specced palette colours and no em dashes, gradients, shadowBlur or offscreen canvas. The seeded band stack was verified by replaying the generator: exactly 5 bands in the order waves, lotus, dots, chrysanthemum, lotus, no adjacent duplicates. Two fixes were made during QA: the bob side locks were widened and shortened so they read as a blunt bob cut rather than dangling tabs, the lotus side buds were pulled inside the cone edge so they no longer clip, and the Fuji orange stratum was thinned so the snow cap and yellow foot read clearly. Final frame one reads as specced at all three sizes: a large moon-face head with black bob, closed arched eyes, vermilion blush and small red lips above a cone body of five distinct pattern bands, the tiny stratified Fuji with its red dots painted at the base, and the whole figure already leaning about a degree into its sway on the cream ground. Nothing clips at any size; on the phone the centred fit sets the figure in the middle of the tall frame and every band and face feature stays legible.
